# Geography & Roots
Rivers: *il - Ibril, Silil
Land masses: 'ria - Essedria, Silidria
Islands: 'nis and 'ra - Hrare Nis, Alguera
Forest: Fel and Arwe - The Druidfel, Menarwe
Mountains: Fang or Ash - Mournfang Range, Argash Range
Planet: Praxia

# Seas
The Gap: Water West of Silidria
The Sea Of False Hope: Large sea south of Aventine - mariners originally thought it was sheltered. It wasn't.
The Eastern Ocean: Water East of Silidria
The Shamharan Deeps: The narrow strip of ocean below Salmuir
