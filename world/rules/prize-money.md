# Prize Money
Ship's captains are sometimes called upon to defend Aventine from pirates and opposing nations. When this happens, the captains may be compensated using "prize money" - a share of the value of any captured shipping.

The formula is this:

Captain's prize = 1/8 of total shipping value, including cargo
Captain usually provides 1/4 of that to his officers, then another 1/4 to divide among the rest of the crew.
