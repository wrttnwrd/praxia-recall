# Organization: The Shadow Veil

Leader: Guthra G'ul
City: [Aventine](/world/places/aventine.md)
Established: 3 FR

## Story
In the sewers and undercity.

The Shadow Veil is a new crime syndicate/organization operating literally underneath [Aventine](/world/places/aventine.md). 

Unlike House Belos, the Veil prefers to remain hidden. Run by the secretive Drow Guthra G'ul, they offer a variety of services and embark on a seemingly random set of missions.

But there is a method to the madness: In 4 FR, G'ul underwent a transformation, changing patron from The Great Old One to Meilikki. He's still a crime lord, but works more closely with his former Team Spear counterparts as they secure and protect Praxia.

## The Vaults
Most notable among the Veil's many services is The Vault: A series of rooms where they will, for a fee, store just about anything.

Born partly out of G'ul's dislike of the Temple of Helm, this offers an alternative for those who want to safeguard wealth and property but still be able to access it.

Each room in the Vaults has a unique protection.

## Eyes And Ears
The Shadow Veil has an above ground network of "eyes and ears" - spies and listeners that bring G'ul intelligence. 

If G'ul is seeking a specific piece of information, the network makes a series of checks, DC higher or lower depending on whether the information or person is well hidden.

20 Target is an individual well-trained in concealing themselves, but still out and about in the city
18 Target is a group well-trained, but still out and about in the city
15 Target is an answer to a specific question that is not common knowledge
10 Target is an individual or group who is not trained at concealing themselves

Every day the Eyes and Ears spend looking gives them +2 to their roll, unless the target is removed.

The network needs four successes to locate the target.
