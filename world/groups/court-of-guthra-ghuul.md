# The Court Of Guthra G'huul
Guthra started the Court in [Aventine](/world/places/aventine.md) during the Pandora Crisis. It's now a loosely-organized syndicate that rules the warrens under the city.

At its center is Guthra G'huul's inner sanctum: A small, surprisingly-lit room. It was once a dank, horrible place, but G'huul's change of patron has led him to change his ways as well.

The Court was a crime syndicate. G'huul is in the midst of transforming it into something less hostile that controls the warrens and ensures stability. Why the change? His new patron, Mystra. He now intends to use the warrens to protect [House Umbra](/world/places/house-umbra.md), the Temple of Helm, and other key locations in the city.
