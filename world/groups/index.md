# groups

* [Bluestrike](house-bluestrike.md) - The oldest house — merchants and sailors, and by far the wealthiest in Camadan.
* [Bournier Cartel](bournier-cartel.md) - The only smuggling venture on Silidria, built by Elissa Bournier from the family's shipping routes in 1 FR.
* [Cult Of Thargorax](cult-of-thargorax.md) - Thargorax's following; Agrona Sef is its known servant.
* [Cult of Callistra](cult-of-callistra.md) - A following of Callistra in Aventine; the source file is a stub with no prose.
* [Elidan's Crew](elidans-crew.md) - The band the ranger Elidan led; Drak ran with them before joining Notorious RBG.
* [Fel House](fel-house.md) - Small, secretive necromantic house that serves The Raven Queen; nearly destroyed in the Ten Years War.
* [Highfury](house-highfury.md) - Martial and pragmatic house that formed out of the war with Fel House; holds the docks, walls and military infrastructure.
* [House Belos](house-belos.md) - The crime syndicate of Camadan — it keeps crime organised so the city's commerce keeps moving.
* [House Drace](house-drace.md) - The artisan's guild of Camadan — shipwrights, armourers, pavers, and quieter work beneath the city.
* [Marta's Marauders](martas-marauders.md) - An adventuring company that disbanded after Marta Valtova died killing Gargra Longblade; the survivors still toast her yearly.
* [Notorious RBG](notorious-rbg.md) - The adventuring party — the Pentar Virones — the only ones who can open the Stygian Gate with the Quinary.
* [Parks and Recreation](parks-and-recreation.md) - The party of the Leaning Leg campaign — a camp NE of Aventine, six somewhat-competent bandits, and one good commander.
* [Silverscale Clan](silverscale-clan.md) - A semi-nomadic clan of 350 famous for riding dinosaurs and wyverns; they guard the Salmuir road in exchange for dinosaur eggs.
* [The Absolvers](the-absolvers.md) - An Aventine military formation that brings together varied talents, commanded by Gart.
* [The Aran Deones](the-aran-deones.md) - The Five First Gods who walked the Void and made the first world; the Fifth was lost when it tore apart.
* [The Aventine Guard](aventine-guard.md) - The city's guard, commanded by Lord Commander Kaelen Varrus.
* [The Biran Deones](the-biran-deones.md) - The Second Gods, who arose as Praxia formed — Amoth, Bahamut, Mystra and others.
* [The Black Helms](the-black-helms.md) - Silgorin's city watch — the party served as Black Helms in the one-shot, and take commissions from a senior commander in the fallen city.
* [The Blood Corsairs](the-blood-corsairs.md) - Raiders that sprang up around 3 FR from pirates and criminals who could not survive post-Fall; vicious, disorganised, and under Traitor Crane.
* [The Court Of Aventine](court-of-aventine.md) - The Azari court — Feyd, his wives and children, and his spymaster.
* [The Court Of Guthra G'huul](court-of-guthra-ghuul.md) - Loosely-organised syndicate ruling the warrens, being turned by G'hul into a guarantor of the city's stability.
* [The Cult Of Eight](cult-of-eight.md) - Aventine's Illithid cult, led in secret by Mevvic of House Bluestrike, with members in every major house.
* [The Cult Of The Flayed God](cult-of-the-flayed-god.md) - Amenopophis's following; they attacked the Salmuir-Aventine trade summit to be as messy and destructive as possible.
* [The Essedrian Ents](essedrian-ents.md) - The circle of ents replanting tree farms across post-Fall Essedria; Cedos leads the farmers.
* [The Haddad Combine](haddad-combine.md) - Eight Shadar-Kai followers of the Raven Queen who quietly replaced Salmuir's grey elves and work to loosen the undead's hold; they share no names and are known collectively as Haddad.
* [The Mireborn](the-mireborn.md) - A fomorian tribe driven south by the long winter; near extinction until Raban's pact made him Rave The Howler.
* [The Obsidian Wing](the-obsidian-wing.md) - Crime syndicate of Shadar-Kai under the vampire Kozani Noctis, running Shadowmuir's underworld and reaching for Salmuir's.
* [The Order Of Helm](order-of-helm.md) - The order of Helm's followers on Silidria; Valana is its lead cleric and Corwin Dunsbane a paladin lieutenant.
* [The Order Of Light](order-of-light.md) - A paladin order of never more than fifty, based in Salmuir's Outer City — "We are the Light that banishes Darkness".
* [The Royal House](the-royal-house.md) - The Azari house that rules Aventine.
* [The Sahuagin of the Dark Reach](sahuagin-of-the-dark-reach.md) - The Blood Corsairs' allies, who ambushed the Albion at the Battle of Urdun.
* [The Sequar Deones](the-sequar-deones.md) - The Later Gods, or Those That Followed — born of the Second Gods, each embodying one or more of the First.
* [The Shadow Veil](the-shadow-veil.md) - The organisation holding the Undercity vaults, watched over by G'hul's people; wrecked by Agrona Sef's Ignomancy.
* [The Sicarii](the-sicarii.md) - A guild of assassins, possibly controlled by Belos; members take monikers and surrender their names.
* [The Whitesand Clan](whitesand-clan.md) - The largest of the nomadic peoples at 1,500; agrarian traders holding the eastern bank of the Ibril and controlling its traffic.
* [The Wolfbearer Clan](wolfbearerclan.md) - A small clan of wolf-worshipping barbarians living outside the Druidfel, led by Nora.
