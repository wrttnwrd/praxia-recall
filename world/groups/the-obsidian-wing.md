# Organization: The Obsidian Wing
Nasty bunch of Shadar-Kai led by a vampire lord. Bent on taking control of [the Haddad Combine](/world/groups/haddad-combine.md) and adding the wealth of [Salmuir](/world/places/salmuir.md) to their own, then using that to expand their power in [Shadowmuir](/world/places/shadowmuir.md).

An organized crime syndicate.

Symbol: A single black wing on a gray background.

## Story
The Obsidian Wing is a crime syndicate that runs the underworld in [Shadowmuir](/world/places/shadowmuir.md), the Shadowfell mirror of [Salmuir](/world/places/salmuir.md). 

Since [The Fall](/world/events/the-fall.md), they've sought greater power and legitimacy in [Shadowmuir](/world/places/shadowmuir.md) through purchase and control of "legitimate" businesses. They want to become the power behind the throne and become the equivalent of The Keepers in Gloomwrought.

Their problem, as for most Shadowfell-based organizations, is resources. 

So the Wing seeks to take over the **Salmuir** underworld and legitimate businesses, thereby seizing Prime Material resources.

## Power

### Troops
For day-to-day operations such as collections, enforcement, and basic security, the Wing relies on a variety of Shadar-Kai, other humanoids, and ghasts.

For assassination, spying, and other stealthy work, they rely almost exclusively on Nosferatu.

Their elite fighting units are pulled from all walks of [Shadowmuir](/world/places/shadowmuir.md).

### Economy
The Wing earns roughly 100,000 gp/year. By Praxian standards, they're very small.
