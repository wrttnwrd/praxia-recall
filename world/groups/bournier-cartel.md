# Organization: The Bournier Cartel

Leader: [Elissa Bournier](/world/people/elissa-bournier.md)
City: Aventine
Established: 1 FR

## Story
Started by the Bournier family in 1 FR. In the aftermath of [The Fall](/world/events/the-fall.md), as survivors split into haves- and have-nots, Elissa saw the wealthy wanting for luxury goods. She created the Cartel from early Bournier shipping routes:

- Caravan routes from the south began carrying non-essential goods
- Shipping routes began transporting goods that should have been subject to duties or regulation - foodstuffs that were supposed to stay in their locales, for example

As the controlling interest in the only smuggling venture on Sillidria, the Bourniers have acquired incredible wealth.

## Resources
Three ships:

- The Gray Foam
- The Cat's Purr
- Helm's A-Lee

Plus a constant string of caravans.

## How It Works
Smuggled goods are brought through the warehouses, then mixed into shipments going to the recipients. So a family might buy 1000 pounds of grain from, say, the Whitesand Steppes, but then also receive two Allosaurus eggs (actually happened).

## HQ
There is no HQ per se. But most of their operations run through a barber shop on the Docks called [Uppercuts](/world/places/uppercuts.md). 

The proprietor, a lizardfolk named Gosk, lets the Bourniers use the shop in exchange for protection and a small slice of the profits. 

[Uppercuts](/world/places/uppercuts.md) is usually half-empty.

[Sandren](/world/people/sandren.md) and [Whipping Sound](/world/people/whipping-sound.md) work from there, occasionally heading to the Laughing Luff for meetings.

## Funds
The Cartel stores their wealth in a large vault under the city under the protection of [The Shadow Veil](/world/groups/the-shadow-veil.md). 

Currently stands about about 50k GP. More is hidden in investments, etc. that without serious forensic accounting will be impossible to find. But take this gold and they lose their liquidity. They're fucked.

If the party wants to end the cartel, they're going to need to either remove or render that gold inaccessible.
