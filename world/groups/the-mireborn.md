The Mireborn are a tribe of fomorians that previously lived in the cold, marshy reaches of northern [Essedria](/world/places/essedria.md). The long winter post-Fall forced them south, where they've survived by raiding and scavenging along the eastern coast.

Life has been hard. Fighting against Ents and surviving humanoids for survival, living at times on the wood and bark of trees, wedged up against the coast by the firestorm on one side and the Maelstrom on the other, the tribe faced extinction. Just six months after the Fall, 75% of the tribe was dead. The rest were starving.

Their fortunes turned when an unusually intelligent fomorian - Raban - entered a pact with the Unseelie Court. Given warlock-like psionic powers, Raban became Rave The Howler.

Rampaging up and down the coast, the Mireborn are now one of the most formidable tribes on what remains of Essedria. Of the tribal races, only the Ents and the Drow dare face them.

## Population
The Mireborn are a large tribe. Aside from dragons and other legendary or near-legendary creatures, there's nothing left on the continent that's willing to stand against them.

There are four types of fomorians in the Mireborn:

1. 15-20 Normals. [Traditional fomorians](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/17146-fomorian).
2. 5-10 [Deep Crawlers](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/3731016-fomorian-deep-crawler)
3. 3-4 [Warlocks Of The Dark](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/3731018-fomorian-warlock-of-the-dark)
4. And Rave

## Secrets
Rave The Howler isn't really Raban. He's actually an ancient Unseelie. It is said his jawbone can create one of [the Quinary](/world/things/the-quinary.md).
