## Aisha Azari
Feyd's second wife. She and Feyd love each other passionately and would do anything for each other and their children.

- Mid 30s
- Dotes on her son, Siliib, but has learned to provide a firmer hand
- Wishes she could be closer to Fidelia
- Descended from the former rulers of Salmuir
- Would prefer to stay out of the limelight
- Very intellectual, deeply studied in the religions and history of Praxia
- Teaches part time at the University
## Feyd Azari
He's a classic behind-the-scenes leader who has kept Aventine a vibrant, growing city even in the years following the Fall.

- Merchant prince of Aventine
- A handsome, dark-haired man in his 40s
- Took over after his father died
- Intensely loyal to family and those who have aided him
- Well-liked by the houses of Aventine, little-known to the rest of the city, as he prefers to be a hands-off leader, entrusting the day-to-day running of the city to the houses, who he manages and keeps under control

*The more you tell people what to do, the less they can decide for themselves. Instead, tell them what you want and let them figure it out.*

## Fidelia Azari
Eldest daughter of Feyd Azari. 

- Late teens to early 20s
- Smart as hell, studied finance, trade, weather, and some magic (no abilities, just knowledge)
- Dark, tall woman who is always composed and charismatic
- Groomed for leadership of the city
- Somewhat cool towards her half-brother and stepmother
- Her mother, Fatimah, died during the plague of -5 FR, when Fatima was only five
- May appear distant to strangers but supports her friends and family through everything
- Very serious, rarely smiles
- Engaged to the Fey Eladrin Jadari. Loves him in her own calm, deep way
- Can sink an argument with a single raised eyebrow

*I do have an opinion, yes. I thought I'd wait for you to finish babbling before I provided it.*

Gallius
Fey Summer Eladrin (Spring until recently) soon to be married to Fidelia Azari.

- Changed to Summer Eladrin after breaking w/ his parents over his engagement
- Sober, talkative, quick to anger against those who slight him or his loved ones
- Unknown age but appears to be in his early 20s
- Well mannered, but do NOT cross him
- Rail-thin, wiry, 6'5"
- Always carries a hooked, orange blade (a +1 Flametongue). When adventuring carries a longbow
- Wears rich, red brocade with subtle gold stitching you can only see in certain light
- Loves Fidelia fiercely, very protective of her, sensitive about the fact that she's human
- Stares intensely at people without realizing it
- When angered, tightens his fist on his sword hilt
- Eyes flash orange when emotional

*May I cut in?*
## Siliib Azari
Feyd's son by his second wife, Aisha Azari. 

- Mid-teens
- Pampered and spoiled early on, Siliib is maturing into a conscientious, caring young man
- Adventurous to the point of recklessness, no one can doubt Siliib's courage
- Sensitive, empathic, very good at judging others
- Hangs about with his friends, tends to talk too much at social events
- Has a tendency to overindulge in wine at social events - it's anxiety
