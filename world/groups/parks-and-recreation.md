# The Camp
Right now, your camp is a sad little collection of dilapidated tents, some pots and pans, six somewhat-competent bandits, and one good commander.

## Location
You're about 6 hours' fast walk NE of Aventine. There's a bridge

# Key members

## Leader: Jasmin Three Toes
[https://www.dndbeyond.com/profile/portentint/characters/27754065](https://www.dndbeyond.com/profile/portentint/characters/27754065)

Jasmin is the veteran of the group. She served in the Oar's Rest militia, leaving after a dispute with her commanding officer and a duel that left her missing a chunk of her foot.

She's struggled to make her way in a man's world, getting the rank she has by being ruthless, willing to fight anyone, and durable.

She wields a longsword and carries a crossbow.
