# Organization: The Order Of Light
(From Jamar)

Location: Salmuir, Outer City

Motto: “We are the Light that banishes Darkness”

Number: Never more than 50 members, as that’s all their hall can house.

How people join: Members of the Order of Light fill vacancies while they travel the world. No one can join that isn’t sponsored by a current Paladin of Light. The order prefers prospects without families so there aren’t ties to a kingdom, house, or bloodline.

Deity: Order members follow a variety of deities. The only stipulation is it cannot be an evil deity.

Important members:

- Ger’u’do Durtbourne
- [Alphonse Horn](https://www.dndbeyond.com/characters/93027552) - current lead Paladin of the Order of Light. Haughty, self-important, unbendingly moral. Horn was the Paladin that brought Rikterr into the order, and tried to act as a father figure. Rikterr cannot stand Horn, and will only visit the Order when necessary as to avoid dealing with Horn and his son, Halcyon. Alphonse is pledged to Tyr, and cut off one of his own hands as tribute. Alphonse has long white hair pulled back that is always pulled into a ponytail. His armor gleams as if it’s never seen a battle, nor any spec of dust/grime/dirt. He has brown eyes that stare through people. His weapon of choice is a mace
- [Halcyon (Hal) Horn](https://www.dndbeyond.com/characters/137285804) - Alphonse’s son, natural heir to the Order of Light leadership. Had a rivalry with Rikterr as Hal believed Alphonse loved Rikterr more. Loves being called by his full first name, so Rikterr constantly calls him “Hal” to his face whenever they interact. Hates that he is forced to worship Tyr, and is expected to cut off a hand in tribute, while Rikterr didn’t have to worship a god. Halcyon has long blonde hair, pulled into a ponytail to mimic his father. He has green eyes, a muscular figure, and beaten up armor. He carries a warhammer
- [Nathan (Nate) Dayspring](https://www.dndbeyond.com/characters/128287240) - Second in command of the Order of Light while Halcyon continues his training. Trained Rikterr and has a good relationship with him. Was the one who made sure Rikterr didn’t have to swear allegiance to a deity before taking his oath: “you are way too young and too stupid to make that decision yet”. Nathan is pledged to the Raven Queen. Nathan has a scar that runs from his brow, down his left eye, and ends at his chin. His left eye is still intact, but the pupil looks fogged over, as if his vision is impaired
- [Irene Cordova](https://www.dndbeyond.com/characters/137340569) - Found by Rikterr during one of his adventures, Irene tried to pickpocket the Paladin. Rikterr caught her, but appreciated her skills. He brought her back to the Order of Light and asked that she be brought in. The last time Rikterr saw Irene she hadn’t taken her trial. Irene plans to pledge to Tymora when she takes her oath**
