The citizens of [Urdun](/world/places/urdun.md) have been under attack by the Blood Corsairs: A group of raiders that sprang up around 3 FR, comprised of pirates, raiders, and other criminals who struggled to survive post Fall. 

The Corsairs are under the command of a woman named Traitor Crane. They have a "fleet" of small, fast-sailing vessels they're using to raid up and down the coast. They've got a larger number of fighters. They're less organized. But vicious, and no effort spent on building a self-sustaining village or city. All they're doing is raiding and killing, and the orcs are struggling to deal with them.

Traitor Crane has now switched to a strategy of tribute. The raiders come to Urdun and demand payment in the form of gold and food. If they get what they want, they leave. If they don't, they attack, burning and destroying what they can before being driven off. 

The orcs are losing this fight. They can't fortify fast enough **and** feed their people **and** grow their ability to farm and fish.


## Introductions
The players are greeted by a member of the city guard on the wall, who quickly lets them in. From there, he directs them towards Rhugorim's home (on the bluff) or the mayoral mansion (also on the bluff).
## The Players' Moment
The players are talking to Rhugorim in his home, high up on the bluffs. The docks and fishing resources are low, by the water. 

They players get to the edge of the bluff just as the corsairs are extracting tribute. Something's wrong this time, though. The corsair leader is yelling, being pushed back towards his boats by angry orcs. He launches boats, and his archers immediately start lobbing volleys of fire arrows, with casters using low-level offensive spells.

### Magic
The orcs of Urdun are not particularly strong in magic. Again, the Fall devastated their resources. 

However, they have an magical Astrolabe with two Wishes on it, provided to them centuries ago. They're now desperate enough to use it: This village is their last hope, and the corsairs will wipe it out. So, they use it.

The person using it vanishes, leaving the Astrolabe sitting on the ground.

### The Albion
400 men, including 60 marines and 30 boys, 304 feet long, she's the last of the Constitution-class frigates. She displaces 2200 tons and carries 50, 24-pound cannon as well as several smaller swivel guns.

For an hour or more, there's activity aboard the frigate but no attempts to come ashore or signal anyone. Frigate crew also spend some time fishing wounded corsairs out of the water, which makes everyone suspicious.

The captain, Samuel Cooper, rows ashore with six marines and a boatswain's mate. He's carrying several superficial wounds from splinters, and a bloody gash on his forehead. 

He and his crew are stunned by what they find, but are racing to explain what they see. For now, he knows he was delivered from a bracketing attack between two Barbary sloops to wherever this is, mid-broadside. He's lost 10 men and has another 50 seriously injured. 

He had just arrived on station off Morocco when he was set upon by five large xebecs. He dismasted two, sank a third, but then was bracketed by the last two.  

## Key Challenges

1. How do the people of Urdun use the Albion to repel the corsairs for good?
2. How do they persuade the Albion to take part?
3. How do they replace her depleted shot and gunpowder?
4. How do they repair the Albion so that she's in fighting trim, and so that she won't burn and sink the moment she returns to Earth?
5. How can the players help with this?
