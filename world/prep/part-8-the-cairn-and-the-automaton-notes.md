# 13-15 Winterclaw
The party is finally at the Wailing Cairn.

It's evening. You've just arrived, are hiding and saw Elidan and the crew ride in.

The wind is howling - HOWLING - in from the south east. It seems impossible, but it's rushing into the cave itself.

It's a nasty mix of rain and snow. In fact, if you spend the night outdoors you'll be making a CON check.

It's too cold for mud, but if you want to breathe really hard on a small patch of ground you can probably loosen some stuff up.

The area around you is dead trees, a few live ones, some rubble/ruins (per the map), rock and stone. It looks as if, long ago, something huge dragged itself across the ground, destroying everything in its path. Since then, the "nuclear winter" has frozen an area that was always cold.

# In the Chamber
See map. The Key is placed in the locking mechanism on the top of the Automaton. Jazelle will start this process either the moment an attack starts or the next morning. If the players attack, Elidan and Arvid will run interference while Jazelle runs in and activates the machine.

## The Automaton
The Primal Automaton is an octopoid that uses the stats of a reduced threat [Bore Worm](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/bore-worm). It is **resistant**, not immune, to non-magical attacks. It is still immune to poison/psychic. However, it has four tentacle attacks, can grapple and swallow prey, and has a disintegrate attack.

If the players attempt to stop the Automaton right away they will be quickly overmatched. Their best bet is to either prevent its launch or let it go and catch up later.

The Automaton inserts itself into the front of the ship, forming the tentacles and propulsion.

## The Primer: Cursed Item
In Jazelle's tent, hidden in the ground, is an actual guide - the Primer - to Lor Manadh. It was in the Automaton and Jazelle had to abandon it. Sango's passive PER will let him see a disturbance in the dirt floor. DC 20 INV/PER to see it. The primer looks like a small cube, but as soon as someone touches it, it clambers up their arm and in their ear.

Player makes a DC 18 WIS check. If they make it, they take 15 points psychic damage but receive a download showing how the Automaton docks with the Engine, as well as a kind of Engine Detector.

**However**, the Primer cannot be removed w/o Greater Restoration, and does 30pts of psychic damage every 24 hours (WIS save halves). The damage can be healed.

When the PC is within 1 mile of the Engine, damage happens every hour. Once on the Engine, it burrows its way back out of the PC's ear, causing another 30 points psychic damage.

Note: It can also be removed physically. Let the players figure that out.

This is a cursed item.
