## Planting Of The Tree
...a small vibration travels from the ground into your feet. You look down, and a small green shoot unfurls from the dark soil. The vibration deepens to a rhythmic thrumming and small pebbles begin to dance on the surface around you. 

The slender shoot leaps skyward, growing to six feet, then twelve, then fifty feet. The thrumming increases to a low rumble, and you hear shouts of consternation from around High Ward. 

The base of the shoot splits. Two, then three more stems spring from the ground, then thousands in a rippling, twining mass around the first, forming a massive trunk that expands so rapidly you have to leap back. 

Thin tendrils of branches reach through the air, mushrooming into a wide canopy. 

The tree appears to stride from the earth, one thick, gnarled root rising over the mansion, the other burrowing across the old Bournier property, then curling along the street to form a low fence. Thin, ropy filaments grow down the corners of the mansion, forming pillars and cradling the building.

Low branches flow up the trunk, reaching through the air, then wrapping around each other again and again to form smaller trunks two or three horses high that arch overhead.

With a final, resounding crack, the growth ceases.

The rumbling dies down to a low vibration. Then, silence falls. 

Neighbors have rushed out into the street to see what's happening and stop, slack-jawed, staring. Thistlebeard comes staggering out of the mansion, hands clapped around his ears.

In front of you stands a tree, 80 feet wide at the base and 70 feet high. It squats protectively yet somehow welcoming over Notorious RBG Manor on the right, and a new, cozy-looking building with large front windows and multiple chimneys on the left. Between the two is the massive trunk, itself 40 feet wide.. The canopy overhead is dense but lets clear sunlight down to ground level.
