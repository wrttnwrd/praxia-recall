# Raids
Yuan-ti raiders have been hitting shipping and caravans going to and from Salmuir, including those from Aventine.

# Monstrosities
New horrors are emerging from the Blighted Haunts

# The Sojourn
Replicates the journey 200 years ago by a team of healers from Salmuir to Aventine to treat a plague. Runs in different direction each year. This is the first year it's running since The Fall.

# The Diplomatic Summit
Aventine and Salmuir are holding a summit in Salmuir regarding the raids. Relations between the cities are solid but have been strained recently by the raids. Aventine will present a gift - a beautiful wrought-iron sculpture showing the two cities with a graceful bridge between them.

# The Wreckage Of Lor Manadh
A group of teen-agers, including the Saluman children, haven't been seen in a day and a half.

The Salumans come to RBG for help, since they know their way around. They really, really don't want their kids caught - they honestly have enough problems right now.

RBG will have to sneak in.

## What's Going On
The children snuck out to look at the wreckage of The Engine and haven't been seen in a day and a half.

They've been trapped by two Otyughs that are trying to build a nest.

## Clues
- Jacob's hat is outside the wreckage
- Artemis Hasp saw the children pull a kayak ashore on the opposite side of the harbor. It's not unusual for kids to do that. Plus, he doesn't want to share that he was snooping around the wreck
- The children took rowing gear, like life preservers
- Someone on the docks may have seen something
- The party may find the two kayaks pulled ashore on the opposite side of the harbor

## The Encounter
When RBG enters the space. the Otyughs are hidden. The children are huddled in a corner up on a rock. They're afraid to make any noise, so they won't warn the party.
