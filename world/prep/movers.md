# Amenophis - The Big Bad
**Amenophis** - The Flayed God - is the mummy of an ancient king prophesied to rise and "destroy the world to be born anew."

He was an ancient ruler of Shamharan, cast down by his own people for blasphemy after he claimed he was a risen god. His people flayed him alive, killed him. His followers stole his body and hid it in a huge temple complex beneath the delta marshes west of Salmuir. The marshes slowly became a twisted, horrible place now known as The Blighted Haunts, prowled by the Yuan-ti of the Cult Of The Flayed.

**Most** of the cult are humans. They are carefully manipulated by the Yuan-ti, who see Amenophis as a path to greater power.

Cult followers are marked by a small patch of flayed skin, usually just under the nape of the neck.

Awakened by the disturbances of recent years, Amenophis seeks to rise to godhood by emerging and conquering first Shamharan, then all of Silidria.

## His Plan
1. Solidify his cult
2. Incite conflict w/ Aventine to make war more palatable

## Restoration
First on Amenopophis's to-do list: Return to the lands of the living. To do that, he needs flesh - a lot of it. The flesh has to be bonded to his form using a magic process via the Loom (a massive construct between levels 3, 4, and 5 of the Crypt).

His living/corporeal servants use the flesh and essence of the many who lie interred throughout the crypt, feeding both into the Loom, which "stitches" material back onto Amenopophis's form. He is, for lack of a better description, being un-mummified into a new, more perfect form.

*Getting nerdy. That requires immense biomass. Each pound restored to Amenopophis requires 1000 pounds of humanoid biomass. In his final form, he'll weight just over 1 ton (2000 pounds). That's 2 million pounds of biomass. Average humanoid is 150 pounds. That's 13 THOUSAND humanoids harvested over the course of 500 years, oor 26/year if it was steady.*

There are the ghosts of **thousands** of humanoids down here. Thousands. A tiny fraction got graves or tombs. It's no wonder The Raven Queen hates this place.
