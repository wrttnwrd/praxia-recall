This is the largest workshop on the upper level. When the Crypt was locked, the maddened workers set upon each other. Now, they're dwarven wights, working for [Ammsu](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/2778556-ammsu-constructor-of-amenopophis.

The wights move from table to table, assembling flayed creations at Ammsu's guidance.

They are terrible at the work. It drives Ammsu to distraction. She moves from table to table, looking at the results of their labor, shrieking and cursing:

_No NO NO I told you to use tighter stitching, you bumbling fool!!! Why am I stuck with thick-fingered dwarves?! How does anyone call you a craftsman!!!???_

_You DOLT! You thick-skulled microbrain!!! You've attached the jaw upside-down! How will something function with a beard made of teeth! I should rip you to pieces and create something better!!!_

The dwarves appear unperturbed, but they deeply resent their taskmaster and will turn on her if given the opportunity. They can't because of a control gem set at the opposite end of the workshop. 

The gem flares brighter every time Ammsu abuses one of the wights. It has a 20 AC (small size) and 120 hp. Destroy it and the wights immediately turn on Ammsu, tearing her to pieces. After that, if they're not attacked, they wander off and eventually fall apart.

## On The Tables
Most of the tables have unidentifiable skeletons with flesh partly sewn on. The dwarves were truly terrible at their work. 

However, there are a few identifiable bodies: A necromancer of Amenopophis lies partially assembled. One of the Copybara is there, mostly stitched together. And there are an assortment of recently-living creatures, partially merged by the twisted magic of the Blight. And, scariest of all, a half-sewn-together beholder, much like the ones they fought above.

This is an important hint about the secret passage into and out of the upper level.

## On The Bookshelf
A set of books, most in good condition, including:

- <u>The Restoration Of True Life To The Dead</u> by Sissun (the hemophage dracolich they just killed). Written in draconic, the book details the process by which to build the perfect Monster. It speaks of a focus, but doesn't say what (that's the Loom)
- A book on mummification, written in Yuan-Ti
- She'chem A'pophis: Written in Common: A History - talks of a native Shamhite who became a kind of folk hero
- Written in ancient Shamarite: A book about the history of Sseth and The Raven Queen: The two were once romantically involved but fell out over Sseth's willingness to return the dead to life. They are now deadly enemies
- Various journals of Ammsu, written in a language no one can decipher (Hag). If they could, it's ramblings about the science of restoration, how her life led to this point, injustices visited upon her by the Inner Circle, etc.
