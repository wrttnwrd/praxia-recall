# Events
1. The Illithid Cult - The Cult Of Eight - knows the Necropolis is waking up. They are not affiliated w/ the Illithid behind the Necropolis, and may ruin everything. Leading the cult is Mevvic of House Bluestrike. Also involved are four lesser nobles, a member of the royal family, a Belos Lt., and a member of Fel House. They're meeting in an abandoned warehouse next door to Lumpkin's Meats, which has spawned cranium rats. Lumpkin is frantic and will find the party if he can
2. Guthra G'huul is looking for the party, although he's not sure what to do with them. He's a changed man because of his patron, but is definitely out to protect his reputation. If nothing else happens, he'll seek out the party and exchange their freedom/safety for their help investigating the various strange happenings around the city
3. Jazelle is now looking for the Oil. Artemis has it, and he's heard rumors someone's going to rob his store (G'huul has contacted him). Artemis approaches the party for help. Jazelle will send Gritch to get the potion. It's very, very unlikely the party can stop her, but Jazelle has ordered Gritch to avoid deaths

# Secrets
1. The Bourniers are furious about the theft of the necklace and are hiring a mage to track it down. Drake doesn't know what it is but he'll spend lavishly to retrieve it, going as far as hiring the Sicarii (he can't hire a Belos team because the robbery was sanctioned)
2. Guthra G'huul doesn't like the Bourniers or any of the losers up on High Ward. If the players tweak the Bourniers, chances are he'll count that in their favor
3. Fidelia Azari remembers that the party helped protect her. This may or may not matter
4. Mevvic of Bluestrike is a member of the Cult Of Eight
5. Siliib Azari (male half-elf) is also a member, as is Ket Abaf (female human) of Fel House, and Bella Blackbrew (female duergar enforcer of Belos)
6. Blong is enamored with Draknev, who she considers a fellow hunter (cough)
7. The Cult Of Eight has been meeting on the Docks in a warehouse next to Lumpkin's Meats. Their presence has spawned cranium rats which are eating meat and spoiling the rest. Lumpkin is frantic.
8. Now that the Cult of Eight has had their meeting place ruined, they're going to start meeting at the safehouse where Siliib is currently staying
