# The Warlord
Outside Aventine, deep in the hills of the Outskirts, a bandit seeks to make a name for himself. He wants to claim the region for his own, gaining control of The Shimmertrame and taking a share of the wealth that flows to and from Aventine on this major highway.

To succeed, the warlord must first subjugate the small town of Vandsund and the farmsteads and taverns around it. The characters are all that stand in his way.

**Start Date**. 20 Leaf Fall, 5 BR

## Synopsis
Rurigug, a Hobgoblin Warlord, has gathered a band of humans, hobgoblins and evil giantfolk and organized them into a small army.

Settlers of The Outskirts have seen bandits come and go. They fight the smaller bands and pay the bigger ones. Rurigug, though, is unique in recent memory, and a real threat to the region. While Aventine might intervene if he jeopardizes travel and trade or if they receive a plea for help, the city council will be slow to respond. By the time they do, it will be too late for those who live on the land.

The players may be hired by the people of Vadsund, or they may run afoul of Rurigug. At first, this will seem like simple banditry. As time passes, they will encounter increasingly dangerous opponents. Eventually, they will realize Rurigug's designs.

### Characters' Goals
Over the course of the adventure, the characters may try to accomplish the following:

* Protect Vadsund from the first and second raids
* Organize The Outskirts to resist
* Defeat and destroy Rurigug's small army
* Seek aid from Aventine
* Directly attack Rurigug

## Insignia
Maglubiyet is the god of hobgoblins, and the one that Rurigug follows. He hopes to find favor and power through his campaign.

The symbol of Maglubiyet is a simple two-headed axe. The players consistently find this symbol.

## The Thin Horn
When the players arrive, Trazzus is cleaning some broken glass from the floor of the tavern. If the players ask, he shrugs it off.


> "I run a tavern," Trazzus said with a wink, "I'm used to cleaning up a few pieces of glass. Ain't nuthin'.
>

But he looks a little unnerved.

What really happened: A hobgoblin and human showed up and demanded protection money. He refused. They then smashed a few bottles. Trazzus usually gives folks like that a knock on the head, but the hobgoblin was armed & armored, and he know the fight wouldn't go well. He reached under the bar for a crossbow he keeps there and the hobgoblin produced a buckler, blocking the bolt. That's not something he normally sees.

The hobgoblin was equally unnerved and left.

Trazzus will send the party to Vadsund to talk to the townsfolk there.

## Vadsund
Bandits just attacked Vadsund and were repelled. Everyone in town is happy with their small victory - many years they have to give in to the bandits and pay - except for Faelyn and Qinan.

If the players headed straight to Vadsund after talking to Trazzus, they arrive in the aftermath of the attack. A few townsfolk are putting out a few small fires and looting the dead bodies of five bandits who apparently attacked the gate. All are humans.

They might notice that the attackers are unusually well-equipped. If they don't, the NPCs will:

> "I haven't seen equipment this good since the orc campaigns ten years ago," Faeyln said, kicking sourly at the helm of a dead bandit."
>

## The Town Meeting
If the players arrive right after the battle, the town seems empty except for Faelyn and Qinan, who are outside the front gate poking at the dead bandits. The two leaders are about to enter the meeting and invite the players to attend, although Faelyn says he doesn't expect much to happen.

Inside, his wife is attempting to get order. Half the crowd is excited that they won the battle. The other half are frightened that the bandits would be so bold.

If the players are there, they can observe and intervene, or just listen.

## For Hire
At some point, Faelyn introduces his wife to the characters. She suggests that the characters investigate and learn about the attack after Leaffall Feast. They'll pay 200gp now, 250 after.

## Leaffall Feast
24 Leaffall is the Feast, which celebrates the harvest gets families together before Winter. The Feast is a big event anywhere, and Vadsund puts on a serious spread, with food, games, and storytelling.

Games include:

- Arm wrestling (Brezig always wins)
- Drinking (same)
- Dancing contest (combined performance checks)
- Sandwich-making contest
- Catch The Greased Dragon (greased pig in a dragon costume)

Stories are all sorts, but everyone wants Orok to participate. A small human child named Galia is fluent in sign language and will translate.

## Qinan's Suspicions
Qinan thinks Jelena may be in league with the bandits, attempting to work with them to force the town to relocate because "She's one of those druids." His bias is leading him in the wrong direction.

## The Battle Of Vadsund
Vadsund is attacked by an ogre, a hobgoblin and three bandits.

[Encounter](https://www.dndbeyond.com/encounters/9e3ba929-c829-4115-a1ac-45c4a744318f)

The bandits attack right after The Leaffall Feast, hoping to catch everyone unawares. If the party is already there, they can intervene immediately. If they aren't, they'll see smoke, just as Panker shows up, frantic.

The ogre will first throw rocks over the walls, then try to smash down the gates. He has to make three DC15 STR checks to do so. If the gates fall, the bandits rush into the town. They want to terrify the inhabitants and herd them into the common house, where they can try to extort them into joining with Rurigug.

During the attack, three other bandits slipped into the town and grabbed Galia, badly wounding her father, Arten. After the fight, the players will hear Marala screaming from their small house just outside the stockade. When they arive, they'll find Arten on the floor with a nasty wound in his side, Marala's crouched over him, clutching Galia's doll.

There's a scrawled note: "BEHAVE"

The bandits have taken her in the hopes of a more compliant town the next time they show up.

The town (if they haven't already) promise to pay the characters to get Galia back and figure out what's happening, in that order.

## Rescuing Galia
The players (presumably) head out after Galia right away. If they do, they need to track the bandits. Survival DC 15, three successes means they get the trail. The bandits are moving fast, but the players can catch them within eight hours.

They'll catch the bandits on the move. They can try to set an ambush, or just chase them down. Either way, the fight happens in a clearing.

The bandits don't have anything of value.

**If they don't catch the trail** or **they choose not to go** a messenger shows up the next day demanding tribute and that the town simply say they'll follow The Warlord Rurigug.
