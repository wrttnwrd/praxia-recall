Year: 25 BR
Location: Hull Ward, Aventine
Time: The first week of The Fading (early autumn)

Hull Ward 25 BR is a different place. A little less organized, a little more feral.

Instead of The Green Slaad is The Leaning Leg.

A new House Belos boss, Tasha Quann, is trying to assert control over the Ward and organize it into a profitmaking enterprise. She wants to use the Ward to eventually climb the ladder of power to the leadership of Belos.

Every week, an armored Belos wagon meets a riverboat from Dirion carrying gold and silver. Belos uses that to pay their employees, bribe officials, and cover other expenses.

Belos smuggles the currency from the wagon into Hull Ward using a secret entrance. So Quann is responsible for the safe arrival of the wagon.

Last week, the wagon never arrived. Quann is under pressure to recover last week's wagon and stop the raids.

First, though, she wants to make sure this week's wagon arrives. If it does, she has enough to keep everyone employed and buy herself some time.

She hires the party to do this.

## Tidbits

- Only a few people know the exact time and place of the pickups: Quann, her assistant Mevvik, the driver, and the stablehand who gets the wagon ready
- Nothing was recovered of the last wagon
- The wagon carried three guards and the driver
- It's an armored wagon with no weapons but thicker walls and an enclosed roof

## Getting The Party Started
The party has worked together before and pushed out a few ruffians who were trying to cause trouble at the Wink And Kiss. Quann decided to give them a try at bigger work.

The party's never visited the Leaning Leg. They don't have the money/inclination/whatever. Describe the POI to them when they arrive. When they walk in, Chearsto will direct them to a table and tell them to wait there.

## Competitors
Unfortunately, there's another group there that wants the work. Ranvar Flynt's gang: A group of young toughs. Tasha wants to see them face off, so she's riled Ranvar up with rumors.

Once the party settles in, Ranvar exits one of the private booths, saunters over and starts picking a fight.

The group:

- Ranvar, a [bandit](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/bandit)
- His two buddies, random thugs who tagged along for the fun

Tasha watches quietly from upstairs. If the party wins or avoids the fight, Tasha's impressed and waves them up. If they lose, Chearsto intervenes and throws Ranvar out. Tasha is a bit disappointed but likes how the party handled themselves and sees them as a far better option than Ranvar.

## The Job
Tasha explains the fix she's in. She wants the party to ride in the armored wagon to the pickup location. The hope is that the brigands will assault the cart and the players will be there to stop it.

She wants clues regarding the lost wagon, but first, she wants this wagon delivered.

(why not just use a different wagon? Belos doesn't want to be seen as hiding from banditry - it would invite more - they need to set an example)

## What's Happening
A bandit leader named Amity has formed a gang that's begun raiding up and down the Ibril. She's brought in unusual creatures to do her work. In this case, it's a band of Grung who use their leaping ability to take out the drover and quickly overwhelm the guards.

Amity promised the Grung help gaining control of their tribe, which lives further south in a small swamp about 2 days from Aventine.

## The Trap
Belos picks up the gold in a tiny hamlet north of Aventine. The barge then continues on to the city.

The ambushing party is four [Grung](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/grung): One blue, one purple, one red, and one green.

The Grung will attack at a small bridge that crosses the Ibril halfway back to Aventine. The Grung swing up from under the bridge.

They're very stealthy. Roll stealth and let the players make perception checks. If the players don't detect them, the Grung clamber up from under the bridge. A purple one wraps itself around the driver and poisons him, causing him to leap into the water.

They'll clamber up on the wagon and throw off the driver. Then they attempt to steer it away. If they can't escape, they try to roll the wagon into the river, hoping to retrieve it later.

Deadliness: Party = 4/4 = 1
Four Grung = 4 * 1/4 = 1

If it's too easy, reinforcements! More Grung arrive to throw chains into the wheels. Party members make a PER check to see what's happening.

## Figuring it out
Any party member making a nature or INT check will know what these little beasts are, and that they don't normally raid human caravans.

### Tracking The Grung Back To Amity
- They see a Grung go speeding off into the river. They can chase using athletics. The Grung moves its normal speed in the water, but that's only 25ft so the party has +4 on it. They then interrogate the Grung
- One of the dead Grung has a pouch containing a map. The map shows the ambush site. A player might notice that the map's design implies it was drawn from a particular location
- Someone in the city's been feeding information to Amity. They could try to figure that out.

### The stablehand
The stablehand is named Remy. He's the one who betrayed Belos, unwittingly. He's horribly absentminded and writes down the times and locations of all meetings, etc.

His boss was friends w/ one of the bandits. He noticed the paper, can't read a word, but recognized the Belos sigil Remy scrawled at the top and figured it might be important. He passed it along to the bandit, who passed it to Amity.

Note: If the party decides to kill Remy, make it someone else, obviously, or we'll end up crossing the streams...

# Where We're At
Party have planted fake information with the courier. They're going to meet him there.

## If they follow him
He leads them through the forest to a small, [abandoned mining camp](poi-the-mining-camp.md).

The party needs to make one DC 15 stealth check per 15 minutes they follow the courier. It's a one-hour walk back to the camp. If they fail, the courier detects them and tries to slip away, at which point he makes a stealth check, ducking around at tree or similar.

## If they capture him
Their call. He has very little loyalty to Amity and will volunteer information with little persuading. He'll reveal the location of the camp.

# Traps
Amity and her gang have set basic noise traps along the wagontrack to the cave. They are well hidden under dirt and leaves, and are DC 15 to detect.

Passive perception reveals disturbed leaves and twigs, but nothing else. They need to investigate.

If triggered, the traps rattle various bits of metal together creating a huge racket. The sentries immediately know something's up and ready their crossbows.

## Paydirt
The previous week's payroll is in the back of the cave w/ all payroll.

Amity is wielding a glaive and has a mastercrafted longsword in her loot pile. There are also 500gp in gems and coin.
