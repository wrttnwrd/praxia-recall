Siliib Azari is being held at an old inn inside the Southern Gate. Mevvic bought the inn years ago and has fashioned it into a kind of headquarters. The inn is surrounded by a hedge.

Mevvic will ABSOLUTELY cower and run if a fight starts. He's just a guy. He threw in with this crew because he felt cool. Mevvic is a genius with zero common sense and a very limited moral compass.

The Cult brought him in to get access to Siliib. They want to convert Siliib. Pharseth't wants to put him through ceremorphisis, thereby gaining a family "member" and learning all about the doings of the court.

The cult necromancer, Dustsoul, is preparing Siliib for the Illithid Parasite. Siliib has already been mind blasted and is ready for ceremorphisis.

Party needs to tell Siliib "Jenny sent you" so that he'll trust them.

Treasure: +1 dagger, 15gp.

## The Fight, Part 2
Once the party kills Dustsoul and the skeletons, the Mozgriken appears.

## Most important
In the main room is a mirror. During the fight, Dustsoul runs for the mirror (just before she dies).

If the players look in the mirror and make a 20 WIS/PER check, they'll notice there's an item in the reflection that isn't in the room. Reaching into the mirror will allow them to grab the item, which is a +1 Ioun Stone of Protection - +1 bonus to AC while wearing it, does not stack w/ a helm. There is also one Potion Of Greater Healing (4d4+4).
