I've already created a tool in ~/dynamic-dungeon to track the hunting parties.

## The Parties
Here are the hunting parties as I see it, divided up by lethality and type:

### Hunter
Lethality: Low
Count: 2

Party detects with see invisibility **and** beats Orthon's stealth: 27/14/23/31 (nat 20) respectively

1 Orthon

Task: Find the party and tag them, then run.

The orthon will use invisibility and stealth to sneak up on the party, shoot them, and then try to disappear.

### Tracking team
Lethality: Medium
Count: 4

Party gets a bad feeling on a passive PER of 19
Party detects on PER of 25

1 green abishai
5 white abishai

Task: Attack and delay the party. Signal the other teams.

The green abishai will use confusion, fear, and mass suggestion to tangle the party up. They may appear to the party in another form, or use Major Image to detour or stop the party.

### Strike team
Lethality: High
Count: 1

1 red abishai
1 green abishai
5 white abishai

Task: Capture the party to take directly to [Tiamat](/world/people/tiamat.md). Signal other teams on contact.

The strike team will attack head-on. The red abishai will direct and use their abilities to boost the others, then attack.


### Urush Gûl
Lethality: Very high
Count: 1

Party gets a bad feeling with a 21 passive PER
Party spies him in the distance with a 20 PER

1 ancient red dragon

Punished by [Tiamat](/world/people/tiamat.md) for his betrayal, Gûl is now tasked with hunting down and killing the party. Party is best off running and/or hiding. If there's a long engagement with a strike team, Gûl may show up.

Note that Gûl is at half hp right now.

Gûl will swoop in and attack, without discussion. The party may spy him circling in the distance. He is clearly wounded, with burn marks along his flanks and wings where the dragon queen sprayed him with acid.

## Teamwork
Remember that these teams will converge.

## Important points
Note that:

1. Depending on the terrain, detection may be impossible
2. Depending on the weather, same
3. These groups are connected and communicating as long as they're within a half mile of each other
4. They are **smart** and are working together
