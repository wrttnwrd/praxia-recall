10-12 Winter Claw

# The Tabernam
The Wave Serpent lands at the Tabernam.

The Tabernam is in much better shape than the last time anyone was there. Animus and his crew have made repairs. His ramps are back in place.

The main difference is Animus himself. He lost his legs in the last battle and is now on mechanical legs of his own making. He's still getting used to them.

Jazelle and her crew are making their way to the laboratory. Animus has already agreed to make a Horolith. Pharse'th is there, too, going with Jazelle partly out of curiosity and partly because she has persuaded him that releasing the Engine might help him regain his strength. He didn't know the elder brain there was dead.

When the party gets there, Jazelle is halfway down. They have taken the main lift. So the players have to either wait for the lift or climb down.

The players know the lift is running because of the guide chains - they're moving. They know Jazelle is on the lift because they can just barely see the platform disappearing from view, with mithril glittering on it and small shapes moving about.

## The Chase
Down through the levels of the Tabernam. If the players choose, they can take the access stair down to the next level and work their way through.

The players might just fly or climb straight down and meet them on the platform. If they do, they'll pop up in the workshop right when Jazelle gets there.

## The Rules
If the players choose to fight, his duergar and automata defeat them and they awake in cages in The Great Hall. They can escape, of course, by picking the locks or otherwise escaping the cages, but they're then over a pool of lava and have to figureo out a safe way to escape.

Animus has overwhelming force there and will immediately move to capture the party. The fire giant has an agreement with Jazelle and he's loathe to break it. The agreement is that he'll create the Horolith. After that's done, the party is free to do what they will. But as long as Jazelle and her team are in the Tabernam, they are under Animus's protection.

## The Dinner With The Enemy
Animus will treat the PCs generously, offering them quarters and food. They are free to explore the Tabernam, as long as they don't violate his rules. If they do, his judgment will be swift.

He invites them to dinner with himself and Jazelle. If the party joins them all, they chat with Jazelle, her wizard Aerona, and her fighter Reece. Aerona is withdrawn but can be drawn into conversation. Reece, and burly human, jumps into any discussion. Jazelle will spend the time trying to persuade the party that they can and should still work together.

If prompted, Pharse'th will make an appearance as well. The illithid looks better than the last time the party saw him. He's benefitted by Jazelle's generosity and believes the Engine is now safe, since Culudan and Zusk are dead.

## Random Encounters
The whole place is patrolled by Duergar, and there are various automatons wandering about. If the players choose to find their way through, use this table once every hour (assuming 6 hours to get down through the place):

1. Duergar patrol:
2. Duergar work party carving a new tunnel
3. Repair bot hovers nearby, tinkering with pipes or wall materials. If the players don't disturb it, it continues working and pays them no notice
