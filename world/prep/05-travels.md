# Begin 14 Winterfade
Travel from Aventine to Salmuir. The Villain's Discontent travels 200 miles/day.

Distance is 1600 miles (following the route) or 1100 miles (flying over the Sea Of False Hope).

So either 8 or 6 days, if no delays. They should arrive in Salmuir between 20 and 24 Winterfade.

The Kestrel can go 180 miles/day, but can fly over the Sea of False Hope directly, so it's roughly a tie.

Horses can zip along at 8 or so MPH if you have spares. But they can't go at night.

This is a test.

## Encounters
1-5:    All good
6-10:   Weather
11-15:  Flying creatures
16-20:  Ground event

### Weather Encounter
1d6
1:    Showers
2-3:  Winds (10-30 mph) - no damage
4:    Severe winds (31-50 mph) - lose 1/2 day travel as ship takes random damage; or, if you're really feeling like a dick, a tornado they have to get around
5:    Hailstorm - fist-sized hail crashes over the ship, holing the deck, tearing rigging. Lose 1 day travel, everyone make a DC10 DEX save or take 1d6 bludgeoning
6:    Firestorm - remnants of the Ellidrian firestorm sweep over the ship. Severe damage, lose 2 days, everyone make DC15 DEX or take 2d10 fire damage

### Flying creatures
1d6
1-3:    Nothing
4:      Two air elementals (https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/air-elemental) battling five chasme (https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/chasme) that are remnants of the devils from Avernus Brought High.
5:      Swarm of insects (over land)
6:      F'Nor and his little squadron bank across their bow, bantering and engaging in good-natured taunting

### Ground Event
If water: A pod of whales, a dragon turtle, a herd of brachiasaurus, a caravan wagon train, or a fort/town.

## Battle - 19 Winterfade
There's a crossing of the Nak'ash with a narrow bridge. All the land caravans have to get over that. Then the jungle starts. The biggest raids have been happening between the Nak'ash and Salmuir, in the jungle.

In the jungle, a mixed party of Flayed God cultists and undead, led by a necromancer, attack a large caravan. RBG can see it happening. The guards are all dead and the raiders are attacking the horses.

*This is one of the few places on the road from Aventine to Salmuir that is run down. The road enters a swamp and divides into a network of smaller trails and bridges, then rejoins into a better-groomed thoroughfare. Right now, one of the smaller paths is strewn with wrecked carts and bodies. Small cages lie open, with livestock staggering about in panic. Four large carts are on their sides. Three others are still standing. Half the horses lie dead or screaming, riddled with the same arrows that apparently slew the caravan guards and most of the travelers. Approaching from the North are five archers dressed in matching cloaks. They're pouring arrows into the remaining horses, slowly advancing on the remnants of the caravan. The survivors are huddled behind the ruins of their vehicles.*

The necromancer (Khazar - pronounced kah-ZAR) will stay well back in the trees and flee the moment things appear to go wrong, sacrificing the archers and the mummies. It's impossible to tell the necromancer's gender.

If they win, RBG finds:

1. Two survivors of the caravan: A driver (Ganden) and his wife (Emarda). They describe arrows from the trees, then chaos. They vaguely remember seeing a gray-skinned form ordering the archers. They also say the figure "waved its hand" and the zombies rose from the swamp.
2. Several surviving horses and livestock, and a bunch of injured caravan guards and merchants.
3. The zombies - they aren't typical. In fact, they're actually mummies, and any character making a DC 18 Religion check will figure it out.
4. The archers. Similar to the assassins, but their arms are flayed from wrist to elbow. They have nothing of interest
5. The Necromancer: If they somehow catch the Necromancer (unlikely) then they will find:
   1. A scroll in a language they don't understand (abyssal) - if they get it translated, it is orders describing raids on the caravans
   2. A midnight blue stone on a necklace: The stone appears to absorb the light around it. This is a piece of **inverted agate**, used by cultists to control and connect themselves to their god. The stones funnel a small amount of life force back to Amenopophis. If the wearer is not one of the anointed of Amenopophis, they gain a point of exhaustion every day they wear it. The item is cursed and can only be removed with Remove Curse, a Wish, or by destroying the item
   3. Weird jewelry: Random jade rings, etc. None is worth that much

## Next steps
If RBG decides to help protect the remaining caravan, it's a 2-day trip through the jungle to Salmuir. If not, they're about 4 hours from the city. The *Kestrel* beats them to the finish.
