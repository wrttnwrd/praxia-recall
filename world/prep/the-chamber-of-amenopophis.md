This chamber is the focal point of all of the energy harvested by the Crypt. Thousands of souls have been ground into mortar to reconstruct the Flayed God.

The left- and right-hand walls are covered with rubble: Boulders, gravel, and chunks of ceiling that collapsed during The Fall. 

The floor is cracked and damaged: There are two huge crevices, big enough for a medium creature to fall into. And there are countless smaller cracks, rendering the entire room difficult terrain.

The back wall is covered in runes that glow faintly. A similar glow emanates from the crevices. 

_If the players make an investigation check, or if you want to let them use passive perception, they see that many of the runes on the back wall match the four runes they've seen more and more often._

From the smaller fissure emerges five fleshy "hoses" that pulse beneath a sheen of magical energy. Those connect to a large shape that looks like an enormous canopic jar. The jar is also pulsing.

The jar is [Ammsu](/world/prep/ammsu.md)'s original design, brought to life by Elechar. It contains [Amenopophis](/world/people/amenopophis.md)'s until-now-senseless form. The destruction of The Loom, though, began to awaken the flayed god. If the players arrived here by opening [The Passage](/world/prep/the-passage.md), then [Amenopophis](/world/people/amenopophis.md) is fully awake but unsure why.

>> The Jar: AC 15, 200hp, resistance to non-magical damage. When broken, it emits a forceful blast of energy. All creatures in the room must make DC15 DEX saves or be flung 10 feet away, knocked prone, and stunned for 1 round. If that would throw them in the direction of a fissure, they must roll a DC15 DEX save again or fall into the fissure. A successful save pushes the creature 10 feet back, but they retain their footing, are not stunned, and do not fall into a fissure.

If the players attempt to damage or open the jar, Amenopophis explodes forth. If they do not, he will speak to them first:

_Long you have journeyed. Long have I slept. I know not why you've come, but you are not my Chosen. Why are you here?_

Amenopophis will try to reason with the players, telling his story (from his point of view) and trying to persuade them to help him. 

But he is stalling. He's preparing to use his first lair action. After about one minute of conversation, he loses patience:

_Enough! Your minds are closed. You see not the promise I bring to the living world. I have not suffered, died, and suffered yet more to be stopped by you. Come, now, and let your flesh complete me._

With that, the jar shatters. See above for effect.

## Up And Out
The entire chamber sits atop an underground lake. A single platform in the middle of the chamber is actually a cylinder of stone, 500m high (don't overthink it).

One way to leave is to activate the water ram, raising the cylinder 20 feet, then stepping into the chamber inside. The ram then carries you out of the Crypt and back to the surface, right near the Tree.

Players can simply stand on top of the cylinder, but may take damage as it rams its way through the rock.

Or, they can walk out the way they came.

**If Amenopophis thinks he's going to die, he may try to escape by activating the ram. Or Alephis may do it to betray the party and escape. Or Alephis may do it and remain below, effectively ejecting the party from his new home**.

To activate the ram, the party must press the runes on the back wall, in the correct order (as they did before).
