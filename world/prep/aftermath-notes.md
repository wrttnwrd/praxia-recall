As the players fly away from The Engine, they see disintegrate as it towards the ground. It's as if, without Baragog's power keeping it alive, the ship is dissolving. After a minute, there's nothing left except the automaton, which crashes into the forest, throwing dirt, trees and debris into the air. They feel a dull shockwave.

With the battle over, the players return to Aventine. Xaavis meets them. Most of the city knows very little of what happened (and Xaavis would prefer it that way). But the city's leaders are in RBG's debt. They ask what the party wants. Depending on the request, the leaders provide it, as well as 10k GP - enough to buy several small homes, a ranch, or very small mansion, or buy an interest in the Green Slaad.

Epilogue: The birth of Stampy jr.?
