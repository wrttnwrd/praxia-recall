# The Plot Thickens
Places:

- Oar's Rest
- The Druidfel, if the party flees there. They will meet one of Amara's priestesses, Tipulara, who knows Sango.
- Dirion
- The Whitesand Steppe

### Encounter: Bournier's Ruffians
Five bandits follow the party. They won't do anything unless they see the Timepiece. Once they do, they attack when they have the element of surprise.

The fight could happen in the Wicked Barb, but more likely outside the town.

If the party wins, they find 10 silver and that's it. If they lose, they can run and lose the Timepiece.

#### Setting: Oar's Rest - The Wicked Barb
_A small, rundown, quiet tavern in Oar's Rest. It's clean and recently rebuilt. It's busy when the boats come in. The bar is tended by a short, stocky human._


## The Wave Serpent's New Mission
They've been sent to investigate a missing merchant caravan that was crossing the Whitesand Steppes. Invited party to come along.

If they do, they'll find the wagon train, blasted, destroyed and emptied. The cargo was an incredible quantity of mithril. The mithril is long gone, but the players are surrounded by a massive Silverscale Clan force, including wyverns, who is ready to kill them before Stampy saves them.

They can go with the clan leader, Trianoma, to talk about what's transpired. She doesn't know much except that these caravans have safe passage through Silverscale lands. The raid is troubling to her. There have been rumors of magic afoot on the Steppe, though.

_Jazelle has brought in Pandora's disgraced consort (Arvid Vane) and his buddies to do some dirty work. They destroyed the caravan and transported away the mithril._

## The Battle With Arvid Vane
Arvid and his two companions - Methelbane and Sanindre - have pursued the characters back through the portal. Arvid sees where he is and immediately decides to leave, but it will take a moment for him to reach the spellstone.

Before then, he'll look around, and cast Hunger Of Hadar to stop the barbarians. It'll wreak a horrible slaughter and, depending on where the party is, they'll be caught in it, too.

If the party kills anyone, they'll find the same emblem on them.

## Where to next?
The party may want to go back and do more research regarding the Timepiece, meeting w/ Artemis at The Mekanikum.

That will bring them one step closer to Jazelle.

Other items that might move them along:

- Jazelle is among the barbarians of the Silver Scale Clan. She is using disguise self to look like one of their number. If any of the party uses an ability that allows them to see through disguise self, they'll see her. It's up to them what they do next.

- The Wave Serpent has to send a Silver Scale Clan messenger to Dirion to report. Then they'll fly back to Aventine and give the players a lift if they want.

- They might go to the Green Slaad and show the Timepiece to Roxy.

All of these things can happen in any order.

- **If the players decode the Timepiece and decide to go to the Cairn, do the side adventure with the orc tribes so they can reach at least level 6 before entering the Vale. Otherwise they'll die.**
