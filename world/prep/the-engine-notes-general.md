# Jazelle's Mansion
She lives in High Ward, at one end of the bluff, opposite the Bournier Estate. Her mansion is actually more of a large home, fairly understated (by High Ward standards).

Inside, though, her mansion is considerably more impressive. It's a collection of Orathac War artifacts, from bits of ejecta from Essedria, to charred, blackened remains of the Shards themselves, to the twisted remnants of Sulba's staff.

Also a letter from her adoptive father:

"My daughter,

I write this knowing that if you read it, I have succeeded.

You have always been a mystery to me. I brought you into my inner circle so that you might learn and become the wizard I knew you to be. You have so much potential. Please, in the new order that has arisen, do not forget that. I don't know what this transformation or Her rise has done to me. If I cannot protect you, fear not. You can protect yourself. If I can aid you, I shall.

But you must accept that not all that appears so is Evil, and that not all Evil is bad for the world and the creatures that dwell upon it.

My respect and affection always,

Sulba, Scion of Zathustra"

# The Cult Of Eight
An Illithid mindcult of various races in Aventine, the Cult of Eight worships the mind flayers as gods and looks to the day that the illithid return.

They believe the Illithid are the rightful rulers of all the planes but until recently had no connection to or power from the Mind Flayers themselves.

Recently they've connected to a lone outcast Illithid named Pharseth't. It has influenced Mevvic, the leader, conferring upon him limited powers and consuming small portions of Mevvic's psyche to sustain himself. It's been attempting to build a small "cult" while it grows in power.

The Necropolis Cult's activity has caused Pharseth't to rush its plans.

## Pharseth't
This illithid is an unhealthy shade of green, with patchy, dry skin. It has not consumed a humanoid brain in some time.

Pharseth't was cast out from its clan long ago - even it doesn't remember why. It wanders Silidria, looking for followers. Recently, it found the already-formed Cult Of Eight. The Cult was practically ready-made. Pharseth't quickly seized control of Mevvic and has begun attracting followers.

It is attempting to reconnect with its clan, sensed what's going on with the Engine, connected it back to Aventine and incorrectly thought the Cult was behind it all.

Pharseth't has set up shop in the Naphtha caves.

# The Order Of Lor Manadh
Arvid, Jazelle and a few others working to restart the Engine.

# The Perfect Timepiece
The writing says "Wake The Three" and includes a map to The Wailing Cairn and the ancient mines there. In the mines dwell the Primal Automata: Three machines that hundreds of years ago crept from the Necropolis into the mines, destroying and killing everything in their path.

The Three know where the Necropolis is. They're essential not only to starting it, but also finding and excavating it. 

# The Timetable
Jazelle has limited time to make all this happen. A conjunction happening in 60 days (30 Winter's Claw) will bring the planes closer together, allowing the Engine to more easily draw power and once again fly. If she misses that, she faces a far more difficult path.
