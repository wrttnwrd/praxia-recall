6-??? Winter Claw

Jazelle is heading to the Tabernam Animus to force Animus to forge a new Horolith. If she gets there, she can definitely make it happen. Animus has no love for Umbra and will do so willingly. It will take him a week, though, and only with the plan and mithril can he even try. He'll then need an Illithid to imprint upon it. Jazelle will have to grab Pharse'th and then head out on the Villain's Discontent.

Arvid will also be on the Discontent with the Mithril, Oil, and blueprint.

## Scenes
- The Journey North
  - Any time an incident occurs that involves the ship getting thrown around, party members must make DEX saves to avoid being pitched overboard.
  - D10 every day
    - 1-2: Nothing happens
    - X 3-4: Good weather. 1 less day of travel
    - X 5-6: Villain's Discontent sighted. Stern chase begins, starting with the two ships 1 mile apart
        1. They escape in a storm
        2. The Villain's Discontent fires and takes out a critical steering part. The Wave Serpent yaws out of control and the Villain's Discontent gets away
        3. The Discontent is damaged and forced to heave to. Jazzelle and her team teleport away (to the Tabernam, but risk damage)
        4. Nothing
        5. The Wave Serpent closes the distance by 1/2
        6. The Discontent increases their lead by 1/2
    - 7: Drak's old nemesis shows up. He's been following Drak for some time and attacks from a Wyvern. (Elidan)
    - X 8: A brass dragon flies by. It's Keanu.
    - 9: Horrendous weather adds 2 days to the trip, and a 1 in 6 chance they "run aground"
    - 10: Perfect weather subtracts 1 day from the trip
- Meeting w/ Feyndal at House Umbra
- Pharse'th's Den: If the players wait long enough (a day), Jazelle has taken him to the Tabernam. He left a few clues.
- The players realize Jazelle can still get what she needs: What do they do?

## Fronts
- Jazelle has to use the Mithril to forge another Horolith. To do that, she has to grab Pharse'th and get to the Tabernam. She'll do so on board The Villain's Discontent, a small, new pirate airship captained by Jasmine Three Toes. The players will catch the vessel as it leaves the Tabernam
- Arvid has been sent ahead to prepare the Chamber Of The Automata
- Jazelle is running out of options in the city. But Pharse'th is trying to stop the players from doing more harm and may intervene
- The Date With Hrong - it's possible. Go to the Cliff House: A magical dome on the bluffs overlooking the sea. It is *incredibly awkward*. Blong is pretty far outside her element. If the conversation turns to creatures, though, she's immediately animated.
