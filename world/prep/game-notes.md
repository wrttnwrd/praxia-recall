11/14/2022
Start Location: [The Chambers Of The Inner Circle](/world/prep/the-chambers-of-the-inner-circle.md)

Dramatis Personae: Alephis, Gimellia

Alephis sounds like a Nosferatu.

Gimellia sounds like she's talking through wet cotton or mud. She doesn't trust **anyone**, including Alephis, but can be persuaded to come along or stay behind. She won't fight unless attacked. Players can take anything from her chambers - she doesn't care.

Secrets and clues: The symbols above the chamber doors match the symbols on the circular door and the keys in each room, which also match the symbols on the map.

There is a secret door between Alephis and Gimellia's chambers. They won't explain why.

In Delteza's chambers is a small book written in orcish. The crest on the front matches the Elidan family. The book describes the family's history and their various homes around Essedria and Sillidria.

Mid location: [The Passage](/world/prep/the-passage.md)

Secrets and clues: A few tiny runes at the base of the stair indicate the button.
