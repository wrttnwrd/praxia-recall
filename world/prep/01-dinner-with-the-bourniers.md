# 35 Winterclaw
Guess who's coming to dinner?

The Bourniers have invited their new neighbors. They don't actually know the neighbors are RBG. Nor do they make the connection as to who they actually **are**.

# Characters
## Drake Bournier 
is the husband and ostensible head of the family. Drake is a boorish, low-class human being of questionable taste. He possesses a tendency to boast and believes the fastest route to importance is putting down those around him. He is **not** a good person, but he is largely harmless. His only potential for harm is his incredible wealth, which he earned smuggling during the time right after The Fall. He fancies himself a swordsman, but isn't. Consider him a level 1 fighter. He has a sallow complexion. He's medium-height, not bad looking, given to a paunch as he gets older.

## Elissa Bournier 


## Drake Jr. 
The family's bratty 5-year-old son.

## Other guests
Ebina and Rasha Saluman, w/ their children Jacob (12) and Constance (14). The Salumans are wealthy half-elves from Salmuir, clad in the clothing of their region. Hard to be certain of their age. Quiet, unassuming, don't seem to particularly like the Bourniers. Ebina is a professional bodyguard. Rasha is a professor of Finance at Aventine University and very much in demand. Jacob and Constance are clearly bored w/ the whole thing. They ask their parents and slip out before dessert. Jacob has a cool fedora-style hat of which he is clearly very, very proud.

Gideon and Wilemina Prescott. Humans in their late 20s. Wilamena is pregnant w/ their first child. They're trying very, very hard to fit in and make a good impression, but they're new at the formal dinner thing. Gideon is a well-known and respected attorney. Wilemina is a logistician (actually a spy, but who's counting).

Gertrude and Odo Rumble are two halflings from Dirion. They're retired, made lots of money as traders, crafters, and later as investors in a merchant marine. They're enjoying retirement and sponsor various events and charities. The Sojourn is high on their list.

There are six [guards](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/guard), all clad in absurdly ornate chain shirts, as well as one [captain](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/bandit-captain) named Erich. He's professional and will protect the Bourniers.

There is a long-suffering butler named Baldric, and an assortment of servants.

# The Menu
First course: Beautiful fruit, far nicer and fresher than anything they've had in years
Second course: Corn chowder
Sorbet: Lime, to cleanse the palate
Third course: A gorgeous leg of what appears to be lamb but is actually a rare deer
Dessert: Chocolate lava cakes

Beverages: Champagne (sparkling water for non-drinkers), then wine and port w/ dessert.

All served with gleaming silver and china plates.

# Conversations
- Drake Jr. tries to irritate his parents. When that doesn't work, he switches to tormenting Jacob and Constance, who tolerate it as best they can
- Gertrude and Odo listen to Drake's long, droning tale of how he grew his "legitimate" business. They may ask a few questions about boat types (did you use flat-bottomed or pontoon boats? At Dirion did you go around the falls?) that leave Drake floundering
- Elissa rather loudly asks Ebina and Rasha what they think of the rise in the Yuan-Ti raids. The obvious implication that the Salumans, being from Salmuir, know something about all this. Which they don't. Ebina might remark upon the danger posed by the Yuan-Ti. Rasha is clearly offended and asks acidly if the Bourniers know anything about the rash of burglaries in the neighborhood, since the perpetrators are clearly from Aventine
- Wilemina and Gideon will stay out of it all until Elissa makes her remarks. Then Wilemina will try to defuse the situation by commenting on the implications for supply routes. Elissa gives her a withering look
- If RBG mentions the Villain's Discontent, the Rumbles may bring up The Sojourn
- Everyone figures out who RBG **is**
