## How The Crypt Works
The Crypt is a kind of gigantic undead engine, used to restore [Amenopophis](/world/people/amenopophis.md). 

*Getting nerdy. That requires immense biomass. Each pound restored to [Amenopophis](/world/people/amenopophis.md) requires 1000 pounds of humanoid biomass. In his final form, he'll weight just over 1 ton (2000 pounds). That's 2 million pounds of biomass. Average humanoid is 150 pounds. That's 13 THOUSAND humanoids harvested over the course of 500 years, oor 26/year if it was steady.*

The flesh has to be bonded to his form using a magic process via the Loom (a massive construct between levels 3, 4, and 5 of the Crypt).

His living/corporeal servants use the flesh and essence of the many who lie interred throughout the crypt, feeding both into the Loom, which "stitches" material back onto [Amenopophis](/world/people/amenopophis.md)'s form. He is, for lack of a better description, being un-mummified into a new, more perfect form.

There are the ghosts of **thousands** of humanoids down here. Thousands. A tiny fraction got graves or tombs. It's no wonder The Raven Queen hates this place.
