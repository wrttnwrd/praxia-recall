# First Floor: Work Areas
[Menkarr](/world/prep/menkarr.md) brought in the best stonecrafters he could find: Dwarves. They did much of the work on the place.

The smaller first floor supported the laborers as they built the town-sized lower levels of the crypt.

By the time construction was complete, most of the workers had gone mad. They set upon each other, turning the work areas into an abbatoir. 

Since then, a variety of creatures have moved in or risen up from the dead:

- The ghost of Menkarr lives here. In life, Menkarr was a female yuan-ti. Her ghost still lives and "works" in her office. Ammsu despises Menkarr as weak and spineless
- Various dwarves are now wights, wandering the workshops
- Amsu, now a mummy, attempts to guide the dwarven wights as they experiment to create servants of the Flayed God. Menkarr despises Amsu for the abominations he created in life and will do anything to thwart him

## The Secret Passage
From the storerooms there is a secret passage to the surface. It's just large enough for a medium humanoid to worm their way through. 

Higher up, the roots of various trees protrude from the soil and rock. The roots are under the influence of the Blight and grab at the players, doing no damage but creating a decidedly unpleasant experience. Characters in the tunnel must succeed a DC15 CHAR save or become panicked. A panicked character goes randomly up or down the tunnel until they reach the opening, and has to wait at least 1 hour before re-entering.

## First Floor Encounters
- Random flayed constructs: Creatures, humanoids, etc. They moan quietly and wander away
- Feral flayed creatures. These are more advanced, created for combat. Ammsus let them loose.
