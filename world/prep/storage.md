Rooms that stored all the various stuff for the construction.

They contain everything from picks and chisels to stone cutters and jars of dried food, most of which is powder.

Eight Copybara escaped and are wandering the storage rooms: 100% identical undead capybara covered with matching bits of other creatures: Hands, antennae, and additional eyes. They shrink from any light source but will attack if cornered. They're weak zombies.
