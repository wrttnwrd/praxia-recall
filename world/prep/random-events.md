These events reinforce the Fivefold Brand and remind the players that Tiamat's plan is in motion.

Every day, roll a D20.


| Roll  | Result           |
| ----- | ---------------- |
| 1-10  | Nothing happens  |
| 11    | Draconic madness |
| 12-18 | Random fives     |
|       |                  |
| 20    | Five moons       |



## Draconic Madness
Any dragons or dragonfolk within 300 feet of a branded character makes a CHAR save, DC 15. On a failure, they go berserk, attacking the nearest unbranded person/creature. Attacking creature's INT/WIS/CHAR falls to 6. They can use inherent abilities, such as breath weapons, but no spellcasting, and they can communicate only in general terms.

This madness is the manifestation of a more subtle magic: The dragonfolk becomes a receptacle for the malign will of Tiamat herself. Part of the dragonfolk's essence - their "soul" - is drawn to Tiamat. If you use remove curse or another spell to counter the madness, there's a 50% chance the soul does not rejoin the body, and the hapless dragonfolk lapses into a state of near-death, where they will remain until:

1. Tiamat is killed
2. Tiamat voluntarily surrenders the soul
3. Someone casts Greater Restoration on the dragonfolk, and they win a contest of wills with the Dragon Queen:

## Five moons
Praxia normally has two moons. It captures three more asteroids, and now has five moons, each a dim shade of red, green, white, blue, or black.

## Random Fives
Randomly choose:

1. At some game of chance, the number five comes up five times. Maybe at the Green Slaad, and a fight nearly breaks out.
2. Quintuplets are born
3. Five ravens land nearby, croak five times, then fly off
4. Five-leafed clover 
5. Random spell warping - five of something
6. Colors: Something that should be a certain color ends up being red, green, white, blue, or black.
7. Five shooting stars
