# The Reproduction Pool
Players must cross the pool, jumping from stone to stone. Each stone hides small needles designed to do 1hp of damage. But each needle causes the player to bleed, and if their blood reaches the water, it instantly creates a duplicate of them that attacks.

# Patchwork Floor
This isn't a trap per se. The floor is a checkerboard of old tiles. Most are decayed and damaged. If the players step on a damaged one, it collapses, and they fall.

Some of the tiles have already cracked and fallen.

The undamaged ones rest atop pillars. The players have to figure out which are which.

They can do this by:

- Seeing which ones are slightly raised
- Finding the ones with a bit less dust (they've been stepped on more recently)
- Bypassing the entire floor

# Mimic Door
A Greater Mimic has taken up residence in the crypt. It's become a door and is consuming hapless acolytes. If the players are careful they may notice a small collection of items at the foot of the door.

# The Zombie Corridor
A collapsed corridor has various limbs and heads of zombies
