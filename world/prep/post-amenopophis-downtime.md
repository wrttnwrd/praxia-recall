## Elidan & Echo
Elidan is going to steal Echo. He has a team of four with him in Aventine. 

Whenever the party is going to be gone, he and his team will sneak onto the grounds, shoot and poison Suri, and shoot and sleep Echo. They then use a Reduction Harness on Echo and spirit him away.

Ebina and Rasha Saluman's children, Jacob (13) and Constance (15) (the ones who pet sit) saw something happen and screamed, running into the yard. Thistlebeard hears the commotion and runs outside, removing the bolt from Suri and putting a simple poultice on the wound, thereby stabilizing Suri.

Elidan leaves his crest behind.

Elidan's plan: Revenge, plain and simple. Kill Suri (he thought), take Echo, and lure Drak out of the city. Kill Drak and use his hide for another quarry. Sell Echo.

The problem, of course, is that Elidan is horribly outgunned. He has no idea what RBG has been through. Expect this fight to be messy and brief.

What he **does** have is a carefully planned ambush. If he has a few hours lead time, then he and his gang make it out of the city, just east of Diaspora. There's a small cave nestled between hills in the Shimmertrame (swamp outside Aventine). He and his gang are there. 

Echo is in the cave, still reduced, making sad noises. Three of Elidan's gang are there. Elidan is hidden nearby using Hide In Plain Sight (basically a gili suit).

Elidan will attempt to use Echo to bargain his way out. Whatever.

## Free Range Mercantile is Moving In
Grekka is in Aventine! Free Range Mercantile has sent her north to open a new location.

Problem is, small shopkeepers don't like it. Neither does House Belos, because Free Range won't need to pay protection.

If the party runs into her she'll be happy to see them, but she'll also appear worried, and she may look spooked if a medium-sized hobgloblin is skulking nearby. The hobgoblin is Glevel, a low-ranking member of House Belos.

Glevel works for Panovar Flynt, current manager of The Cauldron. He is NOT happy there and would prefer a promotion. He's taken it upon himself to "help" the shopkeepers by threatening Grekka and shutting down the operation.

The players can meet w/ Panovar, and/or Glevel. 

If they don't, there's a fire at the construction site, and RBG can intervene to put it out. They may want to wait and catch The Onion Brothers, who were hired to do the work.

Once again, Panovar is acting w/o Belos sanction. If Tasha finds out, she will be extremly angry. How that plays out is up to everyone.
