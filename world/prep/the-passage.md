Of the Inner Circle, only Ellechar ever visited the Chamber of Amenopophis.

He created The Passage to guide the rest of the Circle to Amenopophis's chamber. It winds down a spiral staircase, then through a low, straight, downward-sloping passageway that ends at an archway (no door) and the Chamber.

But Elechar never trusted the rest of the Circle. The passage is trapped. 

This trap was not designed to be fatal to undead, non-breathing creatures. Instead, it's supposed to hold his rivals in place while they called him for help. Then, when he had them at a disadvantage, he could extract concessions.

Without him, players with a passive PER of 20+ will get the feeling that something's wrong. 

A stair halfway down the spiral staircase is a trigger. Step on it, and the doorways above and below slam shut. Roll initiative.

The players hear a rushing sound as water stored for the ram instead pours into the stair. 

At the same time, gravity reverses, flinging the victims to the ceiling (which is now the floor) and water jets into the stair. 

Players must make DC 15 Nature checks. Failure means they're completely disoriented, thrown around in the water and forced to hold their breath while being thrown against the walls and stair. They will take 1d6 bludgeoning damage per round. They lose 30 seconds of breath as they're slammed against hard objects.

> A creature can hold its breath for a number of minutes equal to 1 + its [Constitution](https://www.dandwiki.com/wiki/5e_SRD:Constitution "5e SRD:Constitution") modifier (minimum of 30 seconds). When a creature runs out of breath or is choking, it can survive for a number of rounds equal to its [Constitution](https://www.dandwiki.com/wiki/5e_SRD:Constitution "5e SRD:Constitution") modifier (minimum of 1 round). At the start of its next turn, it drops to 0 [hit points](https://www.dandwiki.com/wiki/5e_SRD:Hit_Points "5e SRD:Hit Points") and is dying, and it can't regain [hit points](https://www.dandwiki.com/wiki/5e_SRD:Hit_Points "5e SRD:Hit Points") or be stabilized until it can breathe again. For example, a creature with a [Constitution](https://www.dandwiki.com/wiki/5e_SRD:Constitution "5e SRD:Constitution") of 14 can hold its breath for 3 minutes. If it starts suffocating, it has 2 rounds to reach air before it drops to 0 [hit points](https://www.dandwiki.com/wiki/5e_SRD:Hit_Points "5e SRD:Hit Points").

They can remake the survival roll each round.

Normally, the stair would completely fill, eventually drowning any creature that must breathe. Because of damage to the structure, though, the stair fills to about 2 feet from the top. Characters not in armor are extremely buoyant. Players in armor have no swimming penalty.

After 5 rounds, the victims are suspended "upside down" with their heads dangling "below" a column of water. Their feet point "upwards" towards the actual bottom of the chamber.

At the base of the stair is a single, well-hidden button about the size of the palm of a hand. 

A PC will have to find the button at the bottom of the stair (which they will float "up" to), and press it. Gravity will immediately return to normal, with water pouring rapidly from the stair and back into the holding tank. Players will can ride the water down to the base of the stair, where the doorway again opens.

If they never find the button, cracks in the structure cause water to drain out, leaving them soggy, bruised, and freaked out at the bottom of the stair. They still need to find a way out. Let them notice a small draft or something.

No living humanoid has ever been in [the passage](/world/prep/the-passage.md).  But after The Fall a number of teeny little creatures like rats slipped in. Their bodies lie at the bottom, and will get stirred up when the water rises.

Through the cracks at the base, players can glimpse the river of energy flowing from the Loom into [the Chamber of Amenopophis](/world/prep/the-chamber-of-amenopophis.md).
