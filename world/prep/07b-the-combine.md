The players meet the Haddad Combine.

Turns out the Combine is a group of 8 Raven Queen followers (Shadar Kai). They quietly replaced the grey elves and seek to loosen the hold of the undead on Salmuir. They know more about what's happening than anyone else in the city.

Saroya is the Combine's representative/contact with the city. She hadn't told the party this.

Now that Rik'Terr has become a Paladin of the Raven Queen, and the party knows as much as they do, the Combine wants to meet them.

The Combine **do not share their names.** They are known collectively as Haddad.

Many of the Combine are displeased with RBG's sudden introduction into their midst. But they see few alternatives.

Saroya invites the party to a small soiree at her home.

Once there, she ushers them into a parlor and pushes on a wall panel. It slides silently to the side, revealing a passageway.

"Understand that you are about to be welcomed into a very, very small group of people who know how it is that Salmuir continues to thrive. With this, I swear you to secrecy. Violate that oath, and I cannot guarantee your safety. All will be clear soon, I promise."

She then leads the party through the passage, which appears to be fairly new. After a few minutes, they come to a pair of heavy doors wrought of gray wood. On the door is wrought an upside-down sliver of moon with a pair of raven wings. On a religion check (advantage for Rik'Terr), the party recognizes the symbol of the Raven Queen.

_The doors shut, and the room is filled with a grey, dim light. You are standing in the middle of a ring of large chairs made of some hard-looking gray substance. Sitting in each of those chairs is a figure in a plain, formless robe. You can't see any of their faces. One, a figure in a chair that is raised slightly above the rest, nods to Saroya and says, "Thank you, Saroya 'dem. Please let us talk to your friends alone. The voice is flat but neutral. Saroya nods deferentially and leaves._

"We are Haddad. We are the proxy of our Dark Queen and serve her in this place. Speak your names."

"One of you has recently joined Her in Her trials. This is good, and we welcome you. But we must speak with you all. You approach a dangerous moment for all of us. What you do next will have consequences for all of us."

At some point in the conversation, Haddad lowers their hoods. They all have elvish features, but their skin is grey and they are almost hairless. Their eyes are so dark they look like black holes in their faces.

Haddad fears that the players will be too easily corrupted by the Blight. They put the characters to a test.
