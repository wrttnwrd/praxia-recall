Menkarr's office lies just off the first hallway on first floor work areas.

She used this office to manage the work on the Crypt. 

The bound wooden door to the office is sealed with a complex set of locks. Menkarr prized her privacy.

Inside, Menkarr continues her work. She is now a ghost. 

If the party listens at the door, they'll hear a female voice, muttering nervously.

If they enter, they see: 

The office itself is the one place on the first floor that isn't 100% utilitarian. Dried, cracking plaster walls are covered with tattered, barely-intact drawings of the city. Shelves have the disintegrated bits of various books, all in Yuan-ti, with titles like:

- The Architect's Guide To Management
- Advanced Logistics
- Wood Borers Of Praxia, Silidria Edition
- Basic Engineering

If the players make a DC 20 Investigation check, they can piece together enough of the drawings to put together a basic map of the passages and chambers of levels 2 and 3. The map was accurate when construction completed. It is not completely accurate now.

Sitting at the opposite end of the office, at a desk that leans crazily on two broken legs, is a bespectacled Yuan-Ti ghost, dressed in a wrinkled shirt and ink-smudged pants. This is [Menkarr](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/2778584-menkarrs-ghost). She is going through the motions of working on drawings, managing books, and doing all the work of a lead architect.

If she notices the characters, she looks up. If the players don't attack, she says in Common:

_Oh, it's about time. I was promised additional workers decades ago. Are you the stone masons I requested? There's still much to be done on the ramps to the undercity._

The players can try to bluff there way through the conversation, to explain the passage of time, or whatever else comes to mind.

Eventually, Menkarr may become suspicious. Or she may realize what has happened.

Either way, she occasionally mentions that she could work much better with her ruler. 

_I could finish my work if I had my ruler. But that damned psychopath Ammsu has it._

If the players ask, Menkarr tells them about [Ammsu](/world/prep/ammsu.md), who she despises. If the players offer to get the ruler for her, she's very excited, and as they leave, points out, 

_She's terrified of fire. If you could show her a flame and say Menkarr told you to, I'd greatly appreciate it._

Ammsu has the ruler in the [Flayed Workshop](/world/prep/flayed-workshop.md). If the players return it, Menkarr is released from her ties to the Prime Material Plane and fades away gratefully. 

Before she does, she tells the characters:

_Thank you for releasing me. I go to my rest. One thing - I am quite proud of the lift system in the city. Look in my desk drawer - it's really impressive._

The drawing shows how the lift system works: A complex set of pulleys and chains that raise enormous platforms up and down on inclined ramps. It also shows where the entrances to the system are, and how to use the lifts.

If the party kills Menkarr, the drawing is destroyed.
