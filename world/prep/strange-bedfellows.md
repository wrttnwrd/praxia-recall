# An Interlude
Parks & Recreation needs to get themselves set up.

1. The party needs stuff: Food, tents, cooking gear. Everything. They have to go buy it somewhere. Aventine is one possibility. If they go there, Tasha quickly finds them and wants to find out exactly what they're going. They can also head north to Vadsund and buy stuff there.
2. The party may want to build up their camp. If they leave it to their employees, it's a complete disaster. No one, as it turns out, knows how to build anything. The party can try to go find someone who can help them build.
3. If the party wants to unite neighboring farmers, they can go to the various POIs and try to help out, or they can head to The Thin Horn and meet folks there.
4. A [faerie dragon](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/faerie-dragon-younger) named Nuvor Rabbit Slayer now lives in the peat bogs (6). He uses practical jokes, telepathy and illusions to chase away farmers and anyone else trying to take up residence. Nuvor has the peat farmers terrified. They think it's a swamp drake, and Nuvor does nothing to correct them. The arrangement lets him fish and hunt to his heart's content, while also satisfying his jokester side. If the party wishes to, they can help these farmers out.
5. The hobbit farming community has an [Ankheg](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/ankheg) problem. Livestock is disappearing, leaving behind half-collapsed tunnels, as if gigantic moles have been there.
6. The party may want to try to find other bandits. There are scattered sightings in the hills east of their camp, as well as on the road to Oar's Rest. They can head out and try to happen on some. Roll randomly to find them.

If nothing else happens, a group of farmers shows up at the camp, hostile at first, then willing to talk about their problems.

There are also the three Ankheg (RIP)
