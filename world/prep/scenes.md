# Fighting Culudan
The dracolithid strikes whenever the party is alone, or as alone as possible.

Culudan is actually the Elder Brain's extension, used to roam about and attempt to hunt down the players.

Ideally, they will attack when the players are either in the air or alone in the open on the ground.

Culudan is shadowing them. Make stealth rolls every few hours against STEALTH skill. If characters' passive PER is higher, let them make a PER check. If seen, Culudan immediately attacks.

The sorcerer shows up to talk to them if they don't seek him out. Things are happening fast. He's working w/ Xavvis trying to prepare the city. He thinks there are plans for The Engine at the tower on Hrare Nis. He can send them there from a teleportation circle at House Umbra if they're interested.

# The Meeting Of The Houses
Meeting on the Palace Grounds in the Conclave Extent. In attendance:

1. Feyd Azari - the prince
2. Osdryn Bluestrike (half-elf leader) and Oriana Valtova. Osdryn wants to destroy The Engine before it arrives
3. Bataar Highfury (human) and Gart: Bataar wants to fight over the city, depending on Aventine's defenses
4. Feyndal of House Umbra and a Spellsword: Makes it back just in time after another shaky teleportation. He thinks they need to destroy The Engine before it arrives but doesn't think it's possible. He says he's found allies.
5. Tasha Quan (Belos - human) and Marceline: She'll follow the council's decision. She doesn't think Belos will be impacted that badly.
6. Parwairt (Drace - gnome) and Artemis Hasp: Parwairt want to attempt to capture and keep The Engine. Everyone else thinks they're insane. **Parwairt worked all night the night before and has a chunky, hacky-looking device that functions as an Engine Tracker. It looks for the most powerful source of psychic energy nearby. It has three hair-thin wires that together point towards the source. It will work for 36 hours, no longer - it's too delicate a machine.**
   1. Artemis has received a note from Animus: _"I adhere to my agreements. I also leave nothing to chance."_ With it is another, simpler Key. Animus created a defective Key you can insert into the Automaton to shut down the Engine and cause it to crash. Parwairt is **not** happy about using it, but if the party wants, it would be a shortcut.

Sicaari: Not attending
Fel House: Not attending

Unless the party intervenes, the council will decide to keep most resources in the city and send the adventurers to attempt to destroy Baragog and crash The Engine. Parwairt is incensed but will go along as long as they can pick over the wreckage. He provides the key.

Since The Wave Serpent isn't back (still on its way back from Feyndal's mission to the Silverscale Clan), Oriana offers to give them a ride in Marta's Fury, a smaller, simpler ship that's still capable of doing what they need.

(in Oriana's cabin is a helm with a huge slash across the top - it's Oriana's adoptive mother, Marta's helm from her last battle - inscribed on the helm is "Glorious Bitch" in Dwarvish)

# Audience w/ Vek Azar
Vek receives the players in her offices, deep under Fel House. She sits behind a simple stone desk. To her left is a shadow mastiff. The shadows behind her shift as various creatures come and go.

Vek asks the players their intentions. She wants to study the Elder Brain and will pay them handsomely for a small piece. She does **not** want the brain intact.

_Vek thinks the brain tissue might unlock greater ability to speak with the dead of abberations, opening up whole new races to the dominion of the Raven Queen._

# Chasing The Engine
The party (probably) boards Marta's Fury and heads for the Engine. They land on the dorsal and attempt access.
