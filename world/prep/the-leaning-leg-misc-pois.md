1. 1, 2, 3, 8: A group of halfling farmers who compete with each other but (mostly) get along. The Gawkrogers, Grubbs, Burrows and Goodworts grow all kinds of foodstuffs and livestock. They sell most of it in Aventine.

Gawkrogers: 6 halflings with a cozy set of hobbit holes. Led by Bildat Gawkroger.

Grubbs: Little known about them. Private, surly. Moved here recently. Led by Wandergrist Grubb. Live in huts made from sod.

Burrows: Oldest family here. 9 hobbits. Also hobbit holes. Led by a woman named Rosa.

Goodworts: Sharecroppers who just bought their way out of their contract w/ House Belos. Led by a cheerful male named Olo.

4. The Thin Horn: Very small tavern and inn owned by a half-orc named Trazzus. Trazzus is being harassed by a small group of bandits from the north.

5. 5, 7, 9, 10, A community of peat farmers. The Komar homestead (5 - humans), Brickbuckle clan (7 - dwarves), Greycarver family (9 - dwarves) and Underlakes (10 - hobbits) all protect each other while carving out a living, literally, from the peat bogs

Brickbuckle clan (10 people) is led by Armistead Brickbuckle, a dwarf who married Felicia Underlake (a hobbit). Both families now get along, as they have very little choice.

The Underlakes are hobbits (7 people) led by Melanie Underlake. Her daughter Felicia married Armistead.

Greycarvers led by Kusus Greycarver, an aging male dwarf farmer going way back. They've worked this land since he was younger - he brought them here.

The Komar homestead is a small family of five humans. They're new and just started. They most suffered from Nuvor's pranks.

6. The peat bogs

17. Helgund Ferry. A hut and a barge on a rope. For 10sp a human - Colter - will run folks across. Colter's a thoroughly average human of lower-than-average intelligence. It's very hard to persuade him to do anything other than run the barge across the rivers

18. Vadsund. A tiny, stockaded town of 15 people, including a small blacksmithy operated by Faelyn and Darunia Ilimaer (elves), a carpenter (Brezig, a half-ogre), Panker's Mercantile - a trading post operated by Panker and Wemebbaic (2 gnomes), a tiny shrine/temple of Pelor run by a half-elven cleric named Ameniel Qinan, and a half-elven Druid healer named Jelena. Jelena and Qinan do not get along. Also assorted townspeople. Characters will immediately notice the quality of construction, owing to the great work of Brezig and Faelyn.

20. Abandoned Settlement. A small settlement of 20 or so people once stood here. It's been abandoned for years.
