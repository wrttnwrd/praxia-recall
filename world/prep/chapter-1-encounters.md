# Encounter at the camp

## Notes
All Bandits are as in the book, but with 15 hp instead of 11, and AC 15 instead of 12.

Three bandit sentries sit around the firepit. If they are attacked, they immediately draw blades. If the battle goes poorly, they shout a warning and run for the cave mouth.

If they detect the party, they draw crossbows instead.

The previous week's wagon is pulled up to a tree to the left of the entrance. It is empty.

## The Fight
Inside the cave, Amity and her crew are ready.

Amity will start by trying to bargain. If the party attacks, she immediately transforms.

If the fight goes poorly, Amity tries to escape up a small tunnel she dug out the back of the cave. If she gets away, the party can try to track her into the forest.
