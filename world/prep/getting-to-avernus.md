The most obvious solutions would be:

1. Fly along the Helgoril, down the falls, and onto the River Styx, then fly along it. The Discontent could do it, but they must have a Merranaloth pilot to do it. They can hire a Merranaloth named Morugal w/ a soul coin, or by killing a particularly vile Red Abishai named Drakith, who Morugal hates because Drakith has banned the merranaloth from most of Avernus
2. Use Plane Shift to the mouth of the River Styx, then #1.
3. Use Gate to conjure a portal. They'll need a 20th-level caster. The only candidates are Vek Azar and... ? Just Vek, really, unless the players have another idea.

### Getting Paperwork: Avernus
If they decide this is the way to go, the players need to get a badge to be present on Avernus. If they don't, they'll be fighting legions of devils simply to remain there.
