# Setting
Date: 7 Deepwinter, 4 FR. Bitterly cold and clear.

# Secrets
1. The small necklace is the Perfect Timepiece. Jazelle needs it to start the Automaton but didn't know that the Bourniers had it
2. If the players steal it, the Bourniers will be furious. Drake Bournier spent a fortune on the Timepiece. He doesn't know what it is but he'll spend lavishly to retrieve it, going as far as hiring the Sicarii (he can't hire a Belos team because the robbery was sanctioned)
3. Drake Bournier fancies himself a fencer. He may confront a party member on their way out. He is a level 1 fighter, uses a rapier, and has very little skill. He's also terrified of combat
4. Attending the party is a member of the royal family - Fidelia Azari. If the party rescues her, she will remember (even if she doesn't tell the party who she is). If she's unharmed, she'll still remember the motley crew and may approach them in the future
5. Also in the Observatory is a History Of The Shards War, written by a brass dragon who followed the exploits of a group of adventurers as they faced Zathustra. The book may include hints regarding Jazelle's parentage and Sulba's true role in the war
6. Among the papers on a table in the library is a detailed map of Pharos that could help the party as they travel
7. In the library is a gleaming necklace that contains a tiny vial of water from a pool in the Druidfel. It has no special value, but was gifted to previous home's owner when she journeyed to the Druidfel to pay her respects to Meilikki
8. The Necropolis is waking up. An Illithid cult in the city with connections in all the major houses knows this, is organizing, and may ruin the Illithids' entire plan
9. Oriana Valtova skipped the party because she despises Drake, who treats his son horribly and has mocked the idea that a dwarf would adopt a human. Drake doesn't know that Marta adopted Oriana years ago
10. Jazelle is talking to Grimshaw about flying her to the Tabernam. He's the only airship captain in the city who knows how to get there
11. There's a black market in rare creatures run by Pervid Savat, who runs them down the river after purchasing them from a hobgoblin named Igmut

## The Underground
Under Aventine is a network of tunnels. These are partly drainage and sewers, partly left from the city's construction, and partly created to connect major buildings. Some of the tunnels are extremely secret, known only by unsanctioned criminals and assassins. Guthra G'hul currently controls most of the tunnels.

Known tunnels connect the drainage system of High Ward with the older temples, the Naptha Caves with Sword's Cross & Axiat (bricked up), and the Merchant Ward with Hull Ward. A tunnel runs from The Green Slaad to House Belos and out of the city.

Some of the tunnels are downright civilized, with signage and stairs. Citizens use them to move from place to place in inclement weather. Others are dripping and dilapated, barely qualifying as sewers. Players may run into typical denizens of sewers and dark places, or they may encounter the Sicarii and Guthra G'hul's retainers. Smugglers and other criminals move into and out of the city using the tunnels, so players using the tunnels near The Green Slaad may encounter them as well.

## The Catacombs And The Temple Of Shar
If the players use the tunnels to flee towards the Temple District, they will emerge in the catacombs beneath the Temple of Shar. The catacombs are damaged and haven't been fixed after the Shards War. Scraps of Death Automata litter the floor. Years later, the place is scorched and pitted from the battle that occurred. There is a tiny bit of a Shard here, too, if the players find it.

In the Temple of Shar they will meet **Isaari**, a human Priestess of Shar. Isaari will politely but firmly escort them out of the Temple, where the party will again have to contend with city guard. She can be persuaded to shelter them for the night if it suits her purposes as a Priestess.

## Hero's Square
Between High and Hull Wards is a large square. In the center is a statue of Sulba and several other fallen heroes of the Shard War.

If the players flee towards Hull Ward above ground, they'll pass through the square. There, they'll confront G'hul's patrol and, depending on circumstances, the two allosaurus.

It's random bad luck that they run into the patrol, which are three young Drow. They can try to talk their way out of it.

If they fight and it goes poorly, the allosaurus can show up.
