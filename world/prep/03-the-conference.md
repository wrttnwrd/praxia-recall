# 9 Winterfade
Weather: Overcast, not raining.

RBG is called to guard a conference (see Moleskine) from potential attack.

The trade summit takes place over a three-day period in a set of tents on the bluffs outside the city. The conference itself is quite safe. It's well-guarded, and it would be hard to slip anyone in.

However, The Cult Of The Flayed God has planned two attacks. Their goal is simple: Be as messy and destructive as possible.

## Intro
Sassiver (human, nervous) talks to the characters. He shows up at their door in disguise, invites himself in, and then explains the issue:

There are high-level, secret talks between Salmuir and Aventine on how to stop the raids on caravans. Those are under the cover of the larger merchant summit.

Xavis is focused on security for the secret talks. So Sassiver needs help on the merchant summit. He thinks the security threat to the latter is as great as the former, because an attack might erode trust and destabilize the relationship between the two cities.

Xavis wants the characters to attend the conference. How they attend is up to them (as attendees or security, or something else).

## The Actors
Five attendees are part of the Cult:

- Emcee - Elle ("they/them")

1. Ankar, merchant in the delegation, is pulling the strings. He has strong reasons to see all this fail. Most important, he's a member of the Cult
2. Mariet, a member of the delegation and merchant, is a Cult member
3. Cellos, a servant in another Mariet's delegation
4. Brundric Chaosview, an Salmurian dwarf servant
5. Aimar, a member of Aventinian security


Soraya (played by Avasarala) - delegation leader (she'll be back) - is not a cultist.

### People they know

### Sa'ad
Sa'ad is a Salmuirian monk who immediately starts to size up Sango. Said he'd love to spar sometime.

## Day One
Dinner and icebreakers.

### Awkward: The Shoe Game
It is **extremely** rude in Salmurian culture to remove one's shoes in public (except at baths).

## The First Attack: At the Lyre
The Cult has placed two jars of Alchemical Fire under the mezzanine of the Lyre. The containers are sealed with wax and lit, slow-burning wicks. Once the wicks burn down, the wax will melt and allow air into the jars. The resulting explosion will burn and blast the patrons and the building.

## At The Cauldron
Five attendees have slipped assassins into the night's events in bags of holding. The assassins have slowed their metabolisms. Once inside, the attendees will turn the bags inside-out, releasing the assassins, who will immediately attack the delegations.

## The Clues
First: The characters know where the events will be. They can inspect the buildings and be on guard.

Second:

### At The Lyre
- Two conference attendees doesn't attend the afternoon sessions on day one. They're the only ones. They say they're not feeling well and leave. If the players follow, the NPCs don't go to their tents. They attempt to sneak off. The two small jars of alchemical fire are concealed in barrels of beer being brought in for the night's fesitivities. The assassins (let's face it, that's what they are) will slip into the Lyre, drain the barrels, then set up the explosives
- As the show starts, if the players are in the kitchen (DC 15) or backstage (DC17) they may see the drained beer barrels and tracks
- During the show, players have a DC20 PER chance to smell/see the candle flames. If they go under the mezzanine, they'll need a DC17

To defuse the traps, they have to:
- Snip the wicks without disturbing the wax. Remember, if any air hits the gel, it explodes
- Move the jars/render them inert without disturbing the wax

### At The Cauldron
- Five attendees from the conference (two from Aventine, three from Salmuir) are carrying small black bags. Not remarkable except they seem to be fingering them nervously (DC20 PER check)
- At the right moment, the players see them all upend the sacks
- Then all hell breaks loose

The assassins go straight for Soroya and Elle.
