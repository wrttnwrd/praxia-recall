# 26-28 Deepwinter
RGB (using that for now) spent another day in Oar's Rest, taking part in the annual raft building competition. A quiet young boy named Bergen won the competition, defeating Grishilda (who takes herself far too seriously, apparently). Grishilda's raft may or may not have been helped on its way by an incompetent hawk...

Bergend's raft was a thing of genius, made of a set of inverted, interlocking triangles he built in an hour.

Sango purchased a glass-and-sand souvenir from Druidic Oddities. Mossy vandalized the joint. Then, Mossy and Drak headed to The Reynald Gallery, where they bought gifts for Roxy and Hrong.

The party hung around Oar's Rest for the rest of the evening. Sango told Bergend that, if he ever visits Aventine, he should visit Rastrum's Feather and Fountain. The party chatted with a few other folks, and spent more time examining the Timepiece/box. They figured out that the weird scratchings on the outside were a language called Qualith - the written psychic impressions of Illithids and other Aberrations.

RBG resolved to talk to Roxy the next time they're at the Green Slaad.

Punctual as always, the Wave Serpent arrived the next morning, and the party embarked. Grimshaw told them that merchants in the mining city of Dirion had hired the Serpent to investigate a lost caravan. The caravan was carrying a load of mithril - a priceless metal used to build weapons, armor and devices of unparalleled strength and lightness that are easily imbued with magic - to Salmuir, but never arrived.

RBG joined the crew of the Wave Serpent and followed the path of the caravan, which followed a known trade route through the Whitesand Steppes.

They found the remains of the caravan: Blackened, destroyed wagons, beasts and humanoids, still smelling of burned wood and flesh even days later. A little investigation showed the party that attackers used magic to fire the entire convoy.

While investigating, the party was surrounded by mounted warriors of the goliath Silver Scale Clan. Many warriors rode an assortment of beasts, including dinosaurs. Others flew on silver wyverns. These warriors attempted to provoke the Wave Serpent and RBG into attacking, and the crew of the Wave Serpent was getting edgy. Fortunately, a Silver Scale member recognized them: Stampy, the steed of the clan's leader Trianoma, started capering and snorting happily (as much as a 30-foot-long dinosaur can caper). Curious, Trianoma spurred her beast forward and spoke to the party.

Potential battle averted. The party returned to the Clan's camp and spoke with Trianoma of the caravan's fate. Several nights ago, sentries reported flashing lights and loud bangs from the location of the caravan. When they rode out the next morning, the caravan was destroyed.

_Worth noting: Silver Scale Clan grants caravans safe passage through the Steppes. In return, they're given trade rights with Dirion and Salmuir. They take this seriously and see the loss of the caravan as a failure on their part._

Exploring the camp, RBG found a wild assortment creatures: Allosaurs, anklyosaurs, triceretops, compys, and even a small brachiosaurus that was setting up a yurt for them.

Stampy brought Drak to see Stompy and entrusted one of her eggs to the ranger. That's a big deal. Allosaurs have tight family units but trade young to safeguard their genetic lines. Drak is now part of the family. He has to keep the egg safe until it hatches, then have a **lot** of food available when it does.

Mossy was entranced by the wyverns. She befriended a half-elven wyvern rider named F'nor and his wyvern companion, T'Cheh. They offered her a ride, which she accepted, and got the thrill of a lifetime.

The next morning, the party further investigated the caravan. Most treasure was untouched: Gems, gold, and silver was fused where it lay. Only the mithril was gone.

They found a small insignia on the charred armor of one of the attackers: A circle, possibly an eye, superimposed over a rectangle.

They also found a permanent teleportation circle. It takes a year to create a circle like this - this attack was long in the making. In the brush nearby they found a scroll of Sending (expended). Someone had hid along the route, then used Sending to tell attacking forces when the caravan approached. The attackers teleported in, ambushed the caravan, then teleported back out with the loot.

Phlebotomus studied the circle and learned the triggering incantation. After a (very) brief discussion, everyone agreed to see what was on the other side. RBG teleported and found themselves atop a stone tower far on the other side of Praxia. The tower sat upon an island in the middle of a sea. They didn't have time to figure out exactly where - they heard someone approaching and teleported back to the Whitesand Steppes.

Moments later, the circle shimmered to life, and three humanoid forms appeared.

Dramatic music. Fade to black.
