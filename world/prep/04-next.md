# 12-13 Winterfade
Weather: Overcast, slight drizzle, bitterly cold.

## Interrogation
The party has a prisoner: Elidra - an unlicensed Aventinian assassin who led the group that attacked the delegations.

If interrogated, she tells them very little.

If coerced, she'll admit:

1. She was hired by Cellos (Mariet's attendant)
2. She was under orders to kill Saroya specifically, and as many other delegates as possible
3. She was given very little time to prepare
4. They were smuggled in inside single-use bags of holding by several random attendees
5. As far as she knows she was hired by Aventine. The small flayed area on the back of her neck is a mark of her gang. Nothing more.

If the party checks, they'll notice that all assassins have that small patch of flayed skin.

## Cellos
Cellos is running, hoping to catch up with Mariet. The party doesn't know that part, but if they go looking for Cellos, he's gone, and all evidence points to a hasty exit.

He's in a carriage on the road south from Aventine. He may be hoping to meet up with a caravan or have something else planned. Honestly, he hasn't thought that far ahead.

## Last Ditch In Aventine
The Cult is frustrated and may make one last attempt on Saroya's life. The players should now understand her life is in serious jeopardy.

If they decide to try to persuade her to let them guard her, she's reticent. They might be able to manage it with a persuasion check.

If not, there will be an attempt on her life, and everyone will be told she's dead.

They'll be called to meet w/ Zaavis.

She'll be there.

RBG's new job is to return her to Salmuir alive, using the Sojourn as a cover.

The Sojourn doesn't start for two more days. If RBG wants to do some prep or work on the Manor, they can. Or we can fast forward. They'll have to tell Gertrude and Odo they're good to go.

## The Sojourn
The Sojourn starts at the little-used Southern Gate - it's the oldest gate in the city (pre-dates even the main Eastern Gate by Hull Ward). Everyone starts off their ships/horses/whatever, then races through the city to their various craft, or jumps on their horses and takes off.

At the starting line are a wild assortment of people and craft:

1. An enormous carriage with overbuilt suspension and metal wheels, apparently built by folks from Dirion
2. Horses, of courses
3. A troop of elves, apparently going on foot
4. An incredibly fast, exotic looking ship called The Kestrel (yes, that one)

Mossy will be delighted to see F'Nor and two other wyvern riders from the Silverscale clan.

Rules read out by the master of ceremonies:

- No teleportation magic of any kind
- Crews may not change, nor may mounts/vehicles. Finish with what you started
- Any route is OK
- No attempts to hinder another competitor allowed
