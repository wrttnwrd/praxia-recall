OK. Here's what's happening.

**[Tiamat](/world/people/tiamat.md)** is close to escaping the Nine Hells. Her plan was to bring RBG and [the Quinary](/world/things/the-quinary.md) to her, then use [the Quinary](/world/things/the-quinary.md) and the Five Fold Brand to destroy the Chains. She is furious at losing the Five Fold Brand and her best shot at escaping the Nine Hells, and has bent all her will towards finding and destroying [Notorious RBG](/world/groups/notorious-rbg.md). 

She's also increased pressure on her servants to complete another, darker ritual to break the Chains Of Gleynir.

Arkhan is in his tower, trying to use the Hand Of Vecna to free [Tiamat](/world/people/tiamat.md). This has been a pet project of his for a long time. Now he's the focal point of [Tiamat](/world/people/tiamat.md)'s impatience and frustration. 

Arkhan and Krull are working to give Krull full control over the Hand and also install the Eye. If he can do so, then he can use a Wish to destroy the chains.

The problem: Vecna could tear Arkhan's soul from his body and take over. That would be... bad. 

*Spoiler alert: There is no way to guarantee that won't happen. Arkhan is a coward and will never take the risk. He already wears the Hand and has the Eye, but he's too afraid to wear the Eye, even as [Tiamat](/world/people/tiamat.md) pressures him.*

Arkhan and Krull aren't that tough. The bigger problem is the entire tower, which is a maze of passages, traps, and undead.

If the players follow Bitterness, they can get into the Tower. Then they need to sneak/fight their way through.

Short of flying through the top of the Monument to [Tiamat](/world/people/tiamat.md), this is the only way into [Tiamat](/world/people/tiamat.md)'s chamber.

The Tower is a Dungeon crawl. It leads to the final confrontation with [Tiamat](/world/people/tiamat.md).

First, though, the party has to survive the [Avernus Hunt](/world/prep/avernus-hunt.md).
