# The Library

The library of Salmuir is a sprawling, two-story building that curves around a thin layer of the Crown District. It's **extremely** hard to reach if you're not a member of the upper class.

At the library, the party can:

-   Solve the cipher
-   Learn about the weird occurence with the dire troll

## NPCs

**Jenn** is a tortle library student learning the ropes. She’s assigned to help RBG with their searches. She’s 16, studying at Salmuir.

## Thutssu

The players find a new ally: Thutssu, a male Yuan Ti Pureblood who was cast out by his temple (in the Residences) for arguing against allying with Amenopophis.

Thutssu feels Amenopophis will lead to the downfall of the Yuan Ti. While most of the temples aren't inclined to join the flayed god, they're also keeping their mouths firmly shut. Most, in fact, insist Amenopophis is a myth.

He is a cleric. He is also there trying to decode the cypher and will be at the book when/if they get there.

When he sees the party, he immediately tries to hide, but does a poor job of it. He's not sure who they are or what they're doing, so he'll be cagey.

If pressed, Thutssu tells the party that he's looking for the book to take to the head of his temple in hopes of getting them to take action. He hopes the book will have proof of the rise of the Flayed God, or at least proof that Amenopophis exists.

He'll tell the party about being cast out, what he said, etc. Shekli is the one who threw him out, but is not a participant in the movement. He just feels "spreading hysteria" is foolish.

## Books

In the history section, massive numbers of books are gone. If the players ask Jenn, she will tell them it's normal. If they make an insight check DC 10 or have passive PER above 18, they'll know she's lying.

The removed books all deal w/ religion, ancient religion, and the pre-City rulers. The Haddad Combine removed them because they're concerned people are trying to return to the old ways, which honestly weren't so good.

### In Biology Section

-   Flora & Fauna Of Essedria
-   Abberant Creatures Of The Blighted Haunt (it'll have something about the troll)

### Along The Way

-   Tyrants & Trolls (banned)
-   A bored librarian shrinking various books and scrolls for easier storage and recovery
-   A visitor reading one of the miniaturized books with an enormous magnifier
-   Four massive tomes in Abyssal. These are a chronicle of one Baragog, a Bulgura who traveled Praxia a thousand years ago

## Earthquake!

During the party's visit to the library, there's a strong earthquake. It does no damage, and characters from Salmuir won't find it that disconcerting.
