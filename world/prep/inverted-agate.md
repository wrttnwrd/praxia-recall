This is a piece of **inverted agate**, used by cultists to control and connect themselves to their god. The stones funnel a small amount of life force back to Amenopophis. If the wearer is not one of the anointed of Amenopophis, they gain a point of exhaustion every day they wear it. The item is cursed and can only be removed with Remove Curse, a Wish, or by destroying the item.

This is the gem Mossy took.
