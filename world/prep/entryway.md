When the door opens, cool, dry, dusty air coughs out. 

You find a small, 10' x 10' room. The room is dry and dusty. It is pitch dark. If the dust on all surfaces is any indication, this place hasn't been touched in decades, if not longer.

 There is a single, open entryway on the opposite wall. Carved around it are ancient Shamharian runes that (with a Religion check) read "Salute Amenopophis! He who did not go away dead, but alive. He sleeps below the jungle, and when he wakes, the worthy shall tremble with ecstasy, the unworthy quake with fear."

The entryway leads to a ramp that descends into the darkness. It switchbacks at a steep, 25 degree angle. each length is 150', and it takes the party over 1000' deep into the earth under Shamharan.

The walls along the ramp are inscribed with worn runes. At the top, the runes are coherent, telling the story of how Amenopophis arrived here: His hubris, the uprising, his torture, and then the spiriting away of his body to this place.

_In -500 FR, Amenopophis started as a water seller. After predicting a great flood, he gained popularity, becoming a cleric in ancient Salmuir. His continued ability to predict the ebb and flow of waters across Shamharan led to his steady rise in power, until he came to lead a sect of followers of Sseth. Eventually, he realized his righteous cause could not be pursued in accordance with the Sacred Way Of Sseth. He left the church, starting his own, more pure way for the glory of Sseth. He built great works in ancient Salmuir and in the jungle, including the complex the players are no in. When he thought the time was right, he revealed himself to his people as a new Avatar of Sseth on Praxia. But they betrayed him, dragged him down, and flayed him alive. His loyal followers took him to (garbled writing) and preserved his life force with (garbled writing). Then they brought him to this place, so that he might sleep, recover, and return to the surface world when time is right._

As the party descends, though, the runes start to tell the story of what's coming. They become increasingly incoherent and are eventually replaced by tiny boxes in the wall. Each box contains a tiny scroll with additional writing. 

These scrolls were written by Menkarr, the architect of the Crypt. Most of them are damaged beyond repair, but see edit?usp=sharing for the full surviving writings of Menkarr.

# The Locked Door
A large, stone door blocks the entryway to the Crypt. The door was locked from the outside with a panel of knobs that have to be moved in the correct order. See the knob puzzle in Google Slides, Resources, 2.
