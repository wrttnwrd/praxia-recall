# 20 Winterfade
RBG arrives in Salmuir. They need to deliver Saroya to her residence. There, her majordomo whisks her away, but not before she offers them her hospitality.

Her residence is a wide-open structure in the tradition of the region.

# Rik'Terr's dreams
Rik'Terr has more dreams:

1. The giant figure stands over Salmuir, but the sun is behind you as you stand in the city. You see that the figure has no skin - only muscle stretched over bone. At its feet is an army of thousands. Its face is stretched into an unnatural grin as it reaches down and grabs a screaming figure from inside the walls. You see it is a half orc and realize it is Ger'u'do. The figure takes Ger'u'do in both hands and begins to twist. You hear a horrible ripping sound, then you wake up.
2. You're standing before the giant figure again. In your hands is a giant maul. At your feet is a man dressed in a simple tunic, looking at you impassively as he crouches over a large stone covered with dried blood. You know that, if you kill this man, the giant will leave. If you don't, the giant will destroy the city and everyone you care about. *If Rik'Terr kills the man, Amenopophis continues to haunt his dreams. If he spares him, he is forced to watch Amenopophis destroy the city, but in future dreams he's visited by a great mongoose.*
3. The mongoose comes to you and in a voice that isn't a voice, says, "Rik'Terr Shepherd, follower of no god or man, you are able to make the hard choice. This is good. In the coming weeks you will face that which haunts you. Follower of none, consider your loyalties and serve your conscience. Any other way lies ruin."

# Mossy's dreams
1. You stand alone on a plain. It is night, and stars stretch across the sky from horizon to horizon. The humid air gently blows past you, and the grassy aroma is intoxicating. But the hair on the back of your neck stands up. You're being watched. You see the figure of a great, golden mongoose walk from behind the only tree in sight. It sits on its haunches and stares at you, unblinking. It says nothing, and you grow restless. *If Mossy speaks, the mongoose turns and walks away. If she sits and stays patient, it walks towards her, extends a paw, and gently cuffs her in an invitation to play. If she wins the "fight," it finds her worthy and, in the next dream, will converse with her.*
2. You stand on the same plain. Before you is the mognoose. She inclines her head and rumbles a greeting. In a voice that isn't a voice, she says, "Mossy Oak of the trees of the wild and stones of the city, remain faithful to the mischief and wit of your kind. Rely on it. When the time comes, you will have to choose between your friends and victory. Remember the spark that drives you."

# 23 Winterfade
RBG spent a couple of days relaxing. They stay The Clever Luck and can explore as desired.

(Kiki prank)

Saroya sends them an invitation to meet with them.

Waiting there is Archibald Saltbaron. He is (oddly enough) a baron. Who sells salt. Some of the best salt is found in the brackish marshes SW of the city, but something's been scaring off the Demetriodons they use to pull the carts back to Salmuir. He'd like the party to go and investigate and, if necessary, deal with the problem. He'll pay the party 1000gp.

The problem is a dire troll named Gristletwitch.

They'll have to slog through the marshes near the sea. They'll go past the salt paddies and a few carts, and see the salt harvesters on their weird sticks.

The foreman at the paddies is a humorless gray elf named Daykath. She reports production is down, and they've lost at least three demitrodons. Something's been eating them, that's for certain. She can take them to the scene of the last attack.

From there, they make there way to the ruins and find Gristletwitch.

He does 22pts on Whirlwind of Claws, 5 points piercing and 5 pts poison with bite, 10 with claws.

Reward - 1500 + 250/rescued Demetriodon

## After the Battle
The dire troll was corrupted by the slow increase in power of Amenopophis. The Blighted Haunts are slowly spreading southward.

Party goes back to Archibald. He’s very grateful and pays the party in full.

Saroya’s seneschal, Aurora, looks concerned. She’s spent a lot of time in that part of the country and hasn’t seen anything remotely like that. A few trolls, but that’s it.
