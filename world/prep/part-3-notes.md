# Starting at the Safe House
The fight just ended. The party finds their loot and tries to figure out wtf just happened.

## Scenes

### Returning Siliib
Returning Siliib to the royal family: If the party returns Siliib to Fidelia, she is endlessly grateful. They can either send a message, try to find her, or something else.

If they return Siliib directly to the palace, they are immediately arrested. It will take some talking to get themselves out of trouble. The Prince's Advisor On Domestic Affairs (actually the spymaster), a tall, thin human named Xavis (Zavvis), comes to talk to them. He is **very** suspicious and protective of the royal family.

Assuming the party can assuage Xavis's concerns, he will put them up for the night, meet with them again in the morning to check against Siliib's story (most of which is true), and ask them if there's anything else they can remember. If not, he'll ask them to please keep things confidential, and give them small emblems they can show to the gates' guards so that the guards will contact him, **only in emergencies or when called**.

### The Naphtha Caves
Xavis suspects the cult is operating from the Caves and will retain the party to check it out. He thinks it's not serious. He'd like them to take a look and report back - nothing more.

He may raise this to the party during their first visit, or he may wait.

He says if they can look in the next few days he would appreciate the help.

Xavis is sizing up the party to see if they can be of aid to him and the Prince, or if they're just hacking around and trying to seem important.

Of course, Pharseth't is in the Caves. Whenever the party ventures down there, they'll encounter his cultists and then him. He may be entirely too much to handle.

#### Setting: The Naphtha Caves
Enter the caves through the stairway to the west of the wall along the high bluffs.

_Description: The wind tears at your clothing as you see the surf below breaking on the Teeth - a set of rocks below the city. You see the wreckage of elven stoneships on the rocks. You get the distinct feeling a fall from here would be fatal. The guide turns and grins at you: "Stay close to the wall and watch your footing. Don't want to lose anyone now."_

You enter the caves through a jagged crack in the cliff face. The crack is partly-melted rock on the bottom with jagged stone along the top.

_Description: Water slowly drips from the gray ceiling to a slippery floor dotted by stalagmites and sliced by channels and shallow gullies that are now empty. The caves smell faintly of tar and mold. Skeletons of small animals lie about. You can hear the sound of the surf below as it pounds on the bluffs below the city._

_Small critters squeak and run from underfoot. A PER 18 shows them to be cranium rats, but not in a swarm._

_You skirt a pool of some kind of partially-dried but still sticky tar. A rat lies dead, half stuck in the tar._

#### Encounter 1: Pharseth't's Followers
Before they can see them, they hear a small party of various races. One Duergar, two humans and an Intellect Devourer are making their way from the deeper caves out to the cliffs. They're arguing about their leader.

Human: "I dunno. Pharseth't ain't led us wrong yet."
Duergar: Something in undercommon.
Human 2: "'E says 'E don't trust no squiddies."
Human: "Shhhhh don't call him that. E'll have your brain out in a flash. Anyway, his coin is good."
Duergar: Mutters...

If the players are stealthy, they see the little party walk by. If they're not, the players hear them before they see them.

If the players lose, they awake in a small cage. The cage is locked from the outside with a large padlock (DR 18 to unlock, but requires Thieves Tools, which have been removed from Mossy's person and are sitting across the room).

If they win, they can proceed accordingly.

#### Encounter 2: Pharseth't
Cave is bathed in an eerie, sickly green light emanating from two globes (they'll die out if Pharseth't dies) bobbing gently in the center of the room. The walls are clean but covered in weird, dotted/dashed/solid lines that look almost engraved to different depths and widths. At the far wall is a row of manacles from which one human and one elf hang. Their eyes are solid white. One of them has a series of sucker marks on their face and is gibbering madly. Tending to them are two human women almost equally.

Pharseth't isn't in a fighting mood. He'll try to persuade the party to leave him in peace.

"I have no quarrel with you, or the city. I merely seek to heal and return to my own kind."

This would be easier to believe except that there are two humans chained to the wall. Their eyes are completely white - they've been mindblasted into senselessness. One of them has suckermarks on her face where Pharseth't has fed. The other is untouched but quite insane.

If the players insist on fighting, let things proceed. If they lose, they're caught and chained up to the wall preparatory to mindblasting and feeding. There are two humans tending to them while Pharseth't is away. They can talk the humans into letting them go. If worst comes to worst, the Wave Serpent shows up.

If they win, they find:

- 24 10gp gems
- Bag of Holding
- Tools Of The Fortunate Rogue: Advantage on all attempts to pick locks; once per day, automatically succeed picking a lock of DR15 or less. Only works for rogues.

### Meeting The Izzet Arcanist
Thora Ebonwalker: Dwarven woman. Robed. She'll meet the party outside the city (she's not allowed in by Belos). If it's been a day, the Bournier's little hunting party is looking for them and tries to steal the Timepiece back.

"I am Thora. I apologize for making you leave the city, but I've had... disagreements with House Belos and am not permitted within the walls."

Thora is **very** intrigued. She tells them there's some kind of mechanism in the back. There are latches.

If they use the right combination (Thora will if they don't), it reshapes itself into a box that is equally mysterious. The box appears to be iron/steel (in spite of the timepiece being gold). It has script on it they do not recognize - no one can interpret it, because it's ancient Qualith: Four parallel lines with psionic imprinting. If they find a mind flayer, it can read it for them. If they show it to Roxy, she freaks out.

The writing says "The Key Within The Key" and includes a map to Vale Gobaith and the ancient mines there. In the mines dwell the Primal Automata: Three machines that hundreds of years ago crept from the Necropolis into the mines, destroying and killing everything in their path.

The Timepiece turns into a clock that is part of the Master Key - it finds the exact right moment to insert the Master Key.
