To access level 3 [The City Of The Dead](/world/prep/the-city-of-the-dead.md), the party has to either take the lift or the stairs in the [Flayed Workshop](/world/prep/flayed-workshop.md).

The City is 100 feet below level 2.

## The Lift
During the construction, [Menkarr](/world/prep/menkarr.md) used a giant lift to raise and lower supplies. After construction, [Ammsu](/world/prep/ammsu.md) and then the Inner Circle used it to bring living, willing volunteers to the city and undercity. 

The lift is now partly operational. The problem is that it's at the bottom and needs to be raised if they're going to use it. While the lift works to slowly lower things down, the counterweight is partly broken so it can't easily be raised.

The trick is to either increase the weight of the counterweight, use brute force to raise it (DC25 STR check), or do something creative.

The players can figure out how to use the levers with a DC 20 Tinker Tools roll, or Investigation DC 20 with disadvantage, or whatever creative solution they want.

A group of 4-8 Flayed Spiders have taken up residence at the bottom. If the players ride down the lift, then the spiders hide. If they use featherfall or similar, the players surprise the spiders, which attack.

Flayed Spiders: [Phase Spiders](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/16978-phase-spider) with Undead Fortitude, DC 18 CON save vs. poison. 

At the bottom of the lift are several barrels filled with gravel, piles of unused masonry, and random stoneworking tools. There are also several rolls of bandages used to create mummies.

## The Stairs
A partly destroyed set of stairs also leads the 100 feet down to level 3. 

The stairs are missing entire 10-foot sections that the parties can leap across.

The stairs are also damaged. Players must make 4 DC 15 DEX saves to make their way down without dislodging loose stones.
