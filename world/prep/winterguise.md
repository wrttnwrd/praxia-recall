Fresh from their victory over The Engine, RBG heads back to the city.

# Events

## The Egg Hatches
If the players head to the Green Slaad right away, no need to rush them. Otherwise, while they're running an errand or speaking with someone, Blong hurries up to them. They need to get to the Green Slaad immediately. The egg is about to hatch.

She has the egg set up in a sort of metal bassinet (seriously) filled with sand over warm coals. Next to it is a kettle with small chunks of raw meat.

The egg is rocking back and forth. A tiny beak appears, then a small head with a fringe of black feathers. Out flops a gross but somehow adorable dinosaur. It squeaks and is clearly demanding food.

But there's a surprise - a moment later, **another** tiny dinosaur flops out. Twins!!! This one's a boy.

Blong tries to act surprised but she's known for a while.

The two dinosaurs are up and about within an hour. They play briefly, then lie down for a nap by the kettle.

The babies are only 6 inches long.

As they grow, they'll

They will grow to be small-ish allosaurs of "only" 20 feet in length. They'll weigh 1500 lbs each. They'll require 40 lbs of meat per day. That's one sheep for the two of them, each day, or 480 sheep/year.

## Winterguise
A week later is Winterguise. Have fun w/ this.

Hull Ward is the best place to be, and people come from all over the city to see the costumes.

House Belos funds a day of festivities, paying local businesses to open their doors.

Hadigak and his goblins are out, costumed up in particularly fearsome (and silly) fashion. They're doling out sweetpies and pastries. Remy has brewed up a pile of rock candy. Blong is distributing ale to the adults.

### Gizmo
Erik, the Belos administrator in charge of Hull Ward, comes to the PCs.

*You know something about rescuing people right? We have a problem...*

He explains that children have gone missing, and several parents are expressing concern. He's tried to find them but has no clear leads.

If the players want to talk to parents, Halik and Delia are there. Delia is clearly worried, and Halik is beside himself. They last saw their son and daughter near The Temple of Pelor.

If they ask around, the players will discover that **all** of the children who vanished were near the temple. If they think about it, they were also right near Ringling's.

If the players don't talk to the parents, they can attempt to sleuth out what happened. Or they can lie in wait and watch. They'll see a gaggle of children out front the Temple, all being entertained by the grownups.

On a high enough perception roll, they see a child almost literally vanish. Then they see the door to Ringling's open and close.

The children are stashed there, unharmed but frightened. Gizmo is hiding among the toys.
