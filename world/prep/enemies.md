# The Bandits
A small group of bandits raided Vadsund and The Tin Horn, stocking up supplies for the Winter. If the players stop in Vadsund, they'll see evidence of a fight, and the townspeople will regard them with suspicion.

The party will have to make a persuasion, intimidation, or similar check to get them to explain what's been happening.
