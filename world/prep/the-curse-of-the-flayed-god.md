**Curse Of The Flayed God**. The cursed target can't regain hit points, and its hit point maximum decreases by 10 (3d6) for every 24 hours that elapse. As the curse progresses, the target undergoes a horrible transformation. Its flesh slowly sloughs off, leaving the creature still alive but disfigured. If the curse reduces the target's hit point maximum to 0, the target is transformed into a spectre, and its flesh is turned into Thread for the Loom. The curse lasts until removed by the [remove curse](https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/remove-curse) spell or other magic.
