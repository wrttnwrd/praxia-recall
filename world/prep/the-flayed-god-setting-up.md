Fresh off their victory against Lor Manadh, RBG sets about building their lives in Aventine.

# Setting Up The Villian's Discontent
The party needs to recruit a crew and learn the world of airships. Grimshaw sends them to the Laughing Luff.

One of Lumpkin's nephews shows up to interview.

Then they meet Lysander, who brings his own crew and helps them recruit an XO, carpenter, and a couple of able seamen. The rest of the crew is easy.

Carpenter/Engineer: Alfhild - stocky Viking woman/shieldmaiden carpenter.

Gunner: Embros (EM brose) - actually on the outs with Guthra Ghul and looking for work that keeps him out of the city

XO: Fen'lal per session 1.

- First step is the raids
- Next step is to kill Saroya and make it look like Aventine did it
- Then his Cult gets to work

## The Cypher
24706CE

The cypher (old spelling) shows the location of the entrance to Amenopophis' temple complex.

Mariet scribbled it down because he doesn't know the location and wants to be there for the awakening. He's being a bad boy.

To solve it, the players will need a copy of a manuscript located in the library of Salmuir. 24706 B is the location. CE is 100 E, which is the location in the manuscript with the correct information.

A few ways to solve the cypher:

1. Take it to someone at the University (Mevvik) who may understand the number. Only one library on the continent that has that many volumes, and the rest of it fits the model: Five-digit number, letter, chapter/page.
2. Take it down to Salmuir and start showing it around
3. Or, don't worry about it. Go with the flow. They'll find their way without it if they follow Mariet, etc. etc.

The manuscript itself is a very unexciting discussion of flora of the marshes west of Salmuir. Chapter 100, letter E is a specific plant that shouldn't exist in the marsh.

Anyone w/ proficiency in Nature will immediately figure this out.

If the party can find that plant, they find the entrance.
