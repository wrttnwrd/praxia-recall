# The End Of The Beginning
By now, the characters should have discovered the lost caravan. They may have been waylaid by the Sicaari and/or learned more about the Timepiece from Roxy.

All of this should lead them back to Aventine, where they can seek more information about the Timepiece.

## Places
- The Mekanikum
- The University
- House Umbra
- Jazelle's mansion
- The Docks

## Rumors and clues:
- If they attack, the bandits were hired by the Bourniers and won't try to hide the fact
- The Sicaari thieves at the Mekanikum were hired by Jazelle
- The Rogue's Blessing: Artemis gifts it to Mossy as a thank you
- At this point Jazelle also has the map to the Primal Automata, which she's had to steal from the University because she doesn't have the Timepiece.

## The Mekanikum
The party may head here after obtaining the Timepiece in its new form, or after talking to Roxy. If they do, they'll arrive just when the place is robbed.

Five thieves remain in the shop. Two are unconscious, lying on the ground: One has a metal bird affixed to his forehead. The bird is madly pecking at his eyes. The other is pinned under Crunch's huge arm, pummeled insensible.

Artemis is also on the ground, bleeding from a wound in his chest but breathing.

The three remaining standing are reduced threat [assassins](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/assassin).

Two others have already run with the oil. The two that ran away stole the Alchemist's Oil which, if the party remembers, is what was stolen from Remy.

They're taking the Oil to Jazelle at her mansion.

_Artemis is grateful for his rescue and offers Mossy the Rogue's Blessing._

## Jazelle's Mansion
She's put herself at great risk now: She needs to get the Oil and (hopefully) the Timepiece and head for the Cairn. But she's running out of time. By now Arvid will have told her about the encounter on the Whitesand Steppes, and she knows the players are getting close.

In the mansion is also Arvid's wizard. She's there to transport Jazelle back to H'rare Nis.

Overall, aside from the one room the players have already been in, the place is a shambles. It's been neglected for months. There are cobwebs. The kitchen is a mess of half-eaten food. Specifically, they find:

- Her bedroom, which is a mess but looks like no one's slept in it in months
- A dining room covered in sheets
- A solarium that's overgrown
- Her library, which is tidier, includes a writing desk covered with plans and diagrams, several books about creation of machines and automata, all written in gnomish and common.
- Her laboratory. This is where it all comes to light. At one end is a desk that's clearly been slashed and torn by a knife. On the desk sits a letter (letter from her father), also slashed and torn but readable. In the laboratory is also a guardian: A single metal dog that viciously attacks the party when they enter.

Jazelle will try to convince the players that this is the right thing to do. She'll talk in nebulous terms about stopping the next cataclysm that House Umbra brings about, and make it clear she's going to end the House. But she'll make a good case, promising payment.

### The Timepiece
Jazelle needs the Timepiece. She still doesn't know the players have it.

The more she believes the players may work with her, the more information she'll provide. That includes the fact that she's assembling ingredients, and that she only needs one more thing: The horolith penulta. It's the final key. It was within her grasp, but she lost it.

If a player who speaks Elvish rolls a DC20 Arcana or History check, they can translate it and realize it means Timepiece or Perfect Timepiece...

### The Offer
She makes it clear that if the players choose not to participate, she'll let them go, but they must not intervene.

She offers to pay them to retrieve the horolith and help her stop House Umbra. If they say no, or they equivocate, she attempts to stop them. The players can escape (narrowly) with various automata coming out of the walls.

## Research
If the players further research the horolith, they'll trace back its origins to long before the Fall, to a time when the great Astral Dreadnaughts sailed the skies over Praxia. They'll learn it can actually control what are called Primal Automata - giant, ancient machines created by an unknown race.

They might find some of this information in the books in Jazelle's library, but that's a DC 25 Arcana or Investigation check.

They can visit the University Library. They can talk to Artemis. Or they can go to House Umbra.


## If Things Go As Planned
Jazelle sends Arvid w/ the oil and the mithril to the Cairn to start the Automata. She still needs to find the Timepiece.

Remember, she only has 30 days left to do all this.


## Other Possibilities
- If they don't do anything, they're waylaid by Bournier's Sicaari, who try to steal the Timepiece back. The "thieves" will attempt to sneak into their rooms and steal their stuff. The Sicaari are scary but not that tough - they're reduced-strength [assassins](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/assassin). The players can deal with them and then attempt to trace back who hired them.
- Or, they'll be attacked on the road outside the city if they choose to stay out there.
