It's pretty clear at this point the party is in for a rough winter if they don't get help with the camp.

If they don't, they'll lose 1d6 of their followers as the season turns bitterly cold. It doesn't feel safe, and it's not exactly comfortable.

They can make their own DC18 rolls and attempt to do the work themselves, but only if they have proficiency with carpenter's tools. Assume that they have the tools after their shopping trip to Aventine.
