You awaken to the stench of burnt wood, canvas, and molten metal. For a moment, pain floods your awareness - pressure, as if something is sitting on your chest. You tear your gritty eyes open and cautiously turn your head.

The *Wave Serpent*, or what's left of it, lies in ruins around you. And **on** you, it seems, as the mizzen mast has pinned your right arm across your ribs. 

Amazingly, you're still alive. As is some of the *Wave Serpent's* crew, if the sounds of fighting echoing around you are any indication. The vague sensation of drifting either means you're bleeding to death or the *Serpent* is still airborne.

You gently wiggle the fingers on your right hand and realize it's not broken. Then, carefully, you slide your arm out from under the mizzen. You look up. Now you see why you're still breathing. The falling mast had crushed a tool chest and come to rest across the leeward railing, pinning but not completely falling on you.

You pull yourself out from under the mast and worm through a heap of lines, canvas, and wire rigging. You emerge at the feet of one of the Serpent's marines, a wiry goliath whose name you can't recall, just as she sweeps two boarders screaming from the deck.

The deck of the powerful airship is total chaos. Grimshaw is cutting his way towards you with three other marines. Most of the ship's crew is still battling a bizarre array of creatures ranging from orcs to humans to scaled, clawed fliers you don't recognize. 

Grimshaw waves towards you, yelling something, but you can't hear over the ringing in your ears. Then there's a sharp flash, and everything goes dark.
