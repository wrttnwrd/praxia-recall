# 16-19 Winterclaw
The automaton is heading to The Engine. The players are martialing their forces.

Keep in mind:

- Jazelle is completely focused on the automaton, and will leave the players to their own devices
- However, the Elder Brain is concerned. Without Pharseth't it has to find other ways to slow down the party. It sends a Blue Dracolithid to hunt and attack the Wave Serpent. Have the party make their usual daily survival rolls.

## Opening
Wherever the players go first, then the Blue Dracolithid attacks. If things are going exceptionally poorly, someone from that place - Amara from the Druidfel,  wyvern riders, etc. - help them out.

## The Druidfel
The characters journey into the Druidfel.

If they walk in, they immediately become lost and encounter something:

- gas spores
- a mantrap
- or, if they're really unfortunate, a shambling mound

But during whatever their encounter they are fetched by S'Tuhl, who brings them to Amara's Glade.

They may also wait. That night, if the moon is full (it is), an enormous wolf comes and leads them to the glade.

### The Glade
The glade is an irregularly-shaped space in the Druidfel. It exists partly on the prime material plane and partly... elsewhere. So it's rarely in the same place, and it's very hard to find.

It's not a clearing. Instead, it's a copse of birch surrounded by larger oak trees. The larger oaks are treants.

In the center of the glade sits Amara. She presents herself to them as an elven woman, but in the moonlight she seems to drift from wolf to owl to elf.

Amara is grateful to the characters for returning the stag hide. It belonged to a young druid named Addósu.

She performs a ritual that causes a beautiful, luminous stag to rise from the hide, transform into a young male human, and then slowly dissolve into motes of light that fly out into the forest.

She bids the players to stay for one day and, if they choose to do so, she will speak to them in the morning.

*This test is patience.*

### A Note: Amara's Goal
Amara believes in three virtues that safeguard Praxia:

- Patience, which leads to growth and fruition
- Mercy, which stays the hand that might strike in anger
- Courage, to do what must be done in the face of great risk
- Hope, which keeps away the darkness

She will want to test the party for each of these virtues.

### The Trial: Courage and Mercy
There is a maddened bear accompanied by unnatural beasts () in the woods. It must be stopped.

It is in fact a Goliath Werebear:

https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/goliath-werebear (reduced threat)

Accompanied by five Will 'o Wisps.

That has been driven mad by exposure to a tiny Shard. Amara wants to free the poor creature. She will watch the battle and intervene if needed. If she intervenes, she thanks the characters for their assistance.

*This test was mercy and courage*

### The Final Trial: Hope
Amara brings the players together and has them consume spores that allow her to show them a vision:

Amara has seen this battle coming for some time. She sees a long line people of every race streaming from the burning remains of Aventine towards the Druidfel, seeking shelter and bringing ruin. Above it hovers a giant Dreadnaught that rains down arcing forks of sickly green lightning.

She explains she doesn't know how anyone can win. This creature is not of this world. How can nature stand against it when Praxia has already seen so much? The great heroes have left. Who will replace them?

*This test was hope.*

### Outcomes
**If the players complete the trial and demonstrate one or two of the virtues, Amara sends aid.**
Shebids Stuh'l to go with them (it wants to anyway). She will also watch the players and aid them at a critical moment, if she can.

**If the players complete the trial and show all four of the virtues, Amara shares gifts of power:**
Her hopes go with the PCs, and she offers them gifts: Four small, round pebbles that do the following:

- Rik'Terr - The Mote Of The Undying gives a holy warrior greater fortitude, so that they may serve as a pillar at the darkest times. +2 CON, first time knocked unconscious, you are revived with 10 temporary HP. This power recharges once per long rest.
- Drak - The Sapling Stone gives a protector of Praxia's living things agility and speed, so that they may strike precisely and spare those who fight to protect Praxia. +1 DEX, can negate one critical hit against themselves or another character in line of site as a reaction, once per week.
- Mossy - The Gem Of Restraint gives a charlatan strength of will and keen perception to stay their hand until the crucial moment. +1 WIS, expertise with Perception.
- Sango - The Seed Of Greatness protects the wearer when all seems lost, so that they may offer great fortitude. +2 on all CON saves. It appears to be a large seed the size of a gold coin. Sango also receives a bag with four seeds. Each seed is an Ironwood Healer - https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/631039-ironwood-healer.

All must be attuned by pressing them against your neck just below the ear. When you do that, it fuses painlessly with the skin.

Bearers of these stones can communicate telepathically to other bearers if within 100 feet. They do not need to be in line of sight.

### Farewell
She understands the journey the PCs are on and offers them advice:

"This thing you seek is not of Nature as we know it. But neither is it a machine. It has life, and a mind, and it feels pain, and fear. I know not how this helps you, but strike the mind and the body will fail, just like any other living being. _Plant the seed of destruction in its own mind._"

With that, Stuh'l or Algalbirch escorts them out.

## Aventine
The dracollithid failed, but Baragog has one last card to play to weaken Aventine's defenses.

### Zaavis
Zaavis wants to get the party together with key members of each house. They've been closer to the automaton, spent more time pursuing Jazelle, and know more about this than anyone.

At the meeting will be:

- Tasha Quan, who they already know
- Timogen, commander of the archers and wizards of the military (female half-orc, missing a chunk of her left hand from her battle w/ a devil during the Shard War)
- Gart, commander of the Absolvers (half-orc w/ eye patch ala General Chang)
- Bataar Highfury (thin, dark-skinned human with close-cropped hair and a neatly trimmed beard from the south)
- Osdryn Bluestrike (who shows up late w/ his son) (older half-elf)
- A Shadar-Kai w/ no name - a proxy for Vek Azar, leader of Fel House (she's never seen outside her mansion, but she's concerned that the loss of Umbra might destabilize the entire city so she sent this servant). She has pale-gray skin, no hair at all, and a white crow on one shoulder.
- Parwairt of House Drace - foul-mouthed female gnome in threadbare clothes and a filthy leather apron streaked with burn marks and chemical stains. She wants to preserve the Engine for study. She also clearly wouldn't mind if Umbra were forever wiped from the surface of Praxia.

Notably absent: Umbra, Sicaari (they're never there)

### One Last Deception
In the event of a major attack, the city's two airships, as well as the military at Axiat, would provide most of the defensive oomph (so would House Umbra, but it's too well-protected against the kinds of attacks Baragog can make from afar).

With Faendal on the Whitesand Steppes and Hexxam who-knows-where, Umbra is nearly unprotected.

Baragog will use one of its remaining drones to attempt to slip poison into the officer's larder at Axiat.

That food is provided, of course, by Lumpkin. While Baragog can't obtain sufficient quantities of lethal poison, it can get enough to sicken the entire lead officer corps. That would significantly reduce the army's fighting effectiveness.

The poison is a ground-up and treated fungus from a northern Deerhorn mushroom. The deerhorn is normally harmless. In this state, though, it triggers a long illness similar to mono, leaving the victim listless and ill. The effects can last for days:

-4 CON
-4 CHR
5 levels of exhaustion

Cure Disease can reduce the symptoms but not cure them:

-2 CON
-4 CHR
3 levels of exhaustion

The drone is a lower-level member of Bluestrike named Onyx. Onyx has two remarkable traits: First, they are the child of Osdryn Bluestrike, leader of House Bluestrike. Second, he was presumed dead after his ship, The Windcatcher, disappeared in a storm.

In truth, Onyx was snatched from the ship (which was then destroyed) and then mind blasted into a drone.

Baragog needs Onyx because the child is recognized and allowed access to any and all shipping in and out of the city.

Onyx returned to the city yesterday. Osdryn shows up at the meeting late w/ his child in tow.

Onyx is a surprisingly brawny half-elf indeterminate gender. They appear to be 19 years of age.

Any player w/ a passive PER > 15 notices something 'off' about Onyx. A high enough insight check will show they are definitely up to something.

After the meeting, Onyx will slip away, telling their father they want to say hello to everyone on the docks. Bataar has to prepare for the attack, so he can't go with them.

Onyx makes their way to the warehouses, where they are let into a warehouse w/ a military seal. Once inside, they spread powder (the fungus) near a grating. The guards appear to do nothing. That's because an Oblex came up into the warehouse and killed the guards.

If they enter the warehouse, the players immediately smell sulfur.

Onyx's last job is to assassinate their father. The assassination attempt fails, either because Bataar knew something wasn't right or because his bodyguards stop Onyx.

Bataar is heartbroken.

### The Engine
1 day before it arrives, Lor Manadh is 15 miles away in the mist, hovering, waiting for the moment when the barrier between the planes is weakest and its power will be peaking.

The weather is perfect for this. If it weren't, Jazelle would fly back over the horizon. However, a particularly keen-eyed player might notice something just out of the corner of their eye.

Baragog is NOT a normal elder brain. It is far more powerful, and The Engine acts as its lair. Everyone in the city begins acting strangely as the Baragog's lair draws closer.

If the players do **not** see The Engine (likely) then they will sense the lair actions beginning. And then, if they don't figure that out, Primp on the Wave Serpent will Send a message saying they've found The Engine and that they're on their way to Aventine.

If the players choose to wait, the Wave Serpent will arrive a few hours before The Engine. They can board and intercept The Engine well before it comes within range of the city.
