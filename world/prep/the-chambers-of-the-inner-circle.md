Four of Amenopophis's most trusted.

Alephis - Air
Azbet - Fire
Gimellia - Bile
Daleteza - Silt

They each have their own chambers. After awakening, they were supposed to return to these chambers, rest, and prepare for the work ahead.

The Fall heavily damaged the entire area. The chambers are littered with debris and, because of their prolonged sleep, the Inner Circle are weakened.

## Dalteza 
The most powerful of the inner circle. Dalteza figures to be first among the disciples when Amenopophis rises.

They are the best of the necromancers, knowing a great deal about the processes used to create the other Circle members.

Their room is simply furnished, with polished stone walls.

They have no interest in treating with the party. They will immediately attack and seek to incapacitate party members. If they succeed, they will Dimension Door back into their chamber to begin preparing their victim to create another member of the Inner Circle - this ritual takes several days.

In Dalteza's room is a small chest containing 4 Potions Of Superior Healing.

There is also is a pile of scrolls she "borrowed" from Alephis. If they look through the scrolls, they'll find 2 with "recipes" for creation of magic items: One for a suit of leather armor, one for a bow.

(dead)

## Alephis
A distant second to Dalteza, jealous of their power, hates Azbet. (Alephis took 35pts of damage in the previous combat).

Alephis is the warrior of the group, with the strongest melee and physical combat abilities.

He is also the craftsman.

Alephis will try to speak to the party. He is trustworthy as long as it suits him. If Dalteza is out of the way, Alephis may turn on the party (depending on his condition).


(Alephis will lie that he wants to stop Amenopophis)
(He has a phylactery stored in his chamber - if he died, it didn't work - it's a small, smokey-colored glass bottle)


## Azbet
Dead

## Gimelia
Horrifying to look at. Resents Alephis for his beauty, but resents Dalteza more and will work with Alephis to displace him.

Gimellia is as close to a warlock as the Circle has. She is an alchemist, as well.

Gimellia is highly intelligent but malleable, and abenstminded. She has very little desire to do anything but forward her own agenda. She feels inferior and takes it out on any hapless creature around her.

Her room has polished iron walls, to resist her bile. All furniture and other objects are pockmarked and damaged. Except the tools, scrolls, and potions, which are resting atop a special desk.

In her chamber are a set of alchemical tools, a potion of Gaseous Form, and a Potion Of Frost Giant Strength.


## Defeating The Inner Circle
If they choose to attack the Circle head-on, they have an even chance of winning. 

- Dalteza seeks to kill them
- Alephis seeks to recruit the party to help him kill Dalteza
- Gimellia attacks on site and will follow either Daletza or Alephis's lead if either is present

## The Path Down

### Loom Conduit
The players can follow the Loom Conduit directly to Amenopophis's chamber. If they do, they must make Athletics (STR) checks to struggle against the flow of necromantic energy or be diverted into one of the holding tanks. 

The holding tanks are currently empty of flesh and soulstuff. They are 6' high by 20' on a side. They may contain the following:

1. This tank is pitch dark. An [Allip](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/2560718-allip), trapped there after they attempted to share their knowledge with Ammsu, tries to share its knowledge w/ the party member(s)
2. This tank glows dimly with light from the four runes, apparently etched into the walls by Loom energy. Let the players take this where they may
3. This tank was cracked in The Fall and is half full with 3' of stinking swamp water. Players must make a CON 15 save or begin retching
4. A Sprite-like creature named Ivy has been trapped here for weeks after slipping her way down through a crack in the swamp floor and bedrock. She is starving. If a player offers her some food, she is very thankful. The player can then help her find her way out, have her accompany them, or she will find her own way
