## 20-21 Deepwinter
- first real snow of the year
- The fight at the safe house. After saving the Prince, the party met Xavis. He put them up for the night and has more questions for them.
- The party found the ioun stone, the potion of extra healing, and the Duergar Dark Blade. I don't think they ID'd everything yet.

## 22-24
Xavis asked the characters to investigate weird doings in the Naphtha Caves. They did. First, they nearly drowned when they fell through the thin crust of rock over the river. Then they had a conversation with a toad.

They ran into a party of Duergar and humans and bested them, but one of the Duergar got away.

In the course of their adventure, found a Duergar lawbook (in a library in the city).

## 25-26 Deepwinter
During their exploration of the Naphtha Caves, the party found the scene of the Battle of The Duergar Rift. In the chamber they happened on the fallen body of Marsothem, who led the clerics of Amoth against the Death Automatons created by then-mad F'lareth.

The party also visited Pharseth't's lair. They opted out of any battle (not the worst judgment ever) and had a brief conversation with them. The illithid explalined they simply wanted to build a following, regain their strength, and return to their people. There they also found a BROOCH (pronounced BROOOOOOCH) of house Highfury on one of Pharseth't's victims.

Before they left, Pharseth't told the party to "Leave the engine alone."

They returned Marsothem's holy symbol to the ruins of the Amoth temple, first meeting the Priestess of Shar and learning a bit about their very weird religion.

After this, the party went to Oar's Rest to meet Lorna, a dwarf acolyte of the Izzet League. Lorna was there to investigate the Perfect Timepiece and potentially shed some light on the item's true purpose. They hitched a ride on the Wave Serpent, landing in town during the Claw's Eve Festival (The coldest month of the year is called The Claw Of Winter). After Mossy (somehow) defeated a dwarf and a werebear in a tart-eating contest, the dwarf introduced herself as Lorna.

Lorna fiddled with the timepiece, which sprang into the shape of a large box. The box has odd, linear scratchings of irregular length and depth on the outside. They could not open the box.

The party has entered in the Deepwinter's Eve raft building contest in town. The contest works like this:

1. Everyone gets the same materials
2. You have 1 hour to build a small raft
3. Each raft will be launched into the river and retrieved with successively heavier cargo
4. The last raft to remain afloat wins

Magic is not permitted.
