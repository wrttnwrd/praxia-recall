## Soul Coins
These coins are impressed w/ the image of a dragon head (Thargorax).

1. The Bourniers have three soul coins in their possession, in the safe that's currently in [the vaults](/world/places/the-vaults.md). They found them near Porthmoor. The coins were used as payment to servants of Thargorax when they hunted for the first of the Founding Runes. The servants perished during [The Fall](/world/events/the-fall.md). If the players take the coins to [Salmuir](/world/places/salmuir.md), they'll learn all about them
2. There are also several soul coins scattered about Sillidria. You can choose to have them pop up whenever. They all lead back to servants paid to locate the Runes or do other nasty odd jobs
