The queen of dragons has no home since the fall of the Dragon Eyrie. She is bound to [Avernus](/world/places/avernus.md) with the Chains Of Gleynir, but even they demand repair else she loose them and fly free. She most recently assumed the form of Thargorax and attempted to free herself, but failed.

Know this: You can strengthen her chains of Gleynir or destroy her. Her destruction might be akin to [the fall](/world/events/the-fall.md) of Amoth. Slay her and we know not where her soul will reside: Nowhere? Elsewhere? Would she be forever released? Or banished to whatever dark corner now holds the Eyrie?

To strengthen the Chains of Gleynir, braid the roots of mountains, the breath of fish, the sound of a cat's footfall, the kiss of the unloved. Or ask the gods themselves.

## Tiamat's Goals

1. Break the Chains of Gleynir. She attempted to do this on the Fields Of Bone but failed 
2. Stay alive. Some of the most powerful heroes in all of Praxia are coming for her
3. If possible, use the battle w/ the heroes to break the Chains (assuming the heroes get there)

Note: Her goals have changed. Zariel is furious that RBG stopped her plans for Silgorin Below and demanded that Tiamat end RBG once and for all. Her goals are now to:

1. Destroy RBG
2. If possible, break the Chains
3. Not get punished by Zariel
