## Backstory
Sango went to Aventine to find missing members of the order. But he didn't do that, so he felt badly, and when he blew up, he had a bit of a breakdown.

Also, when he was in his early 20s, he was an apprentice to another druid (50 years ago). Tax collectors poisoned the village. He fled and stumbled into the order. He's now trying to hunt down the tax collector.

## What Sango's Been Up To
**Adventures of "Sango" Reed Isanador**

When Sango left RBG it was because he was shaken.  His near-death experience reminded him that life is short and he has things to do before his time is done.  When RBG decided to make the journey south to Salmuir, he knew he needed to address some of the things he had been putting off during his adventures with the group.

**1st Stop: Rastrum’s**

Sango went to Rastrum’s first.  He had to provide an update to the Order and was his first stop to gather intel.  As far as anyone at the Order knows, Reed is gathering information on the missing members of the Order.  This is where he found out about Cregyn, his unusual vision, and where he can usually be found (The Laughing Luff down by the docks).  He also made an inquiry on the Exciseman (tax collector) that had poisoned his village during his first winter as an apprentice healer-druid.

**2nd Stop: The Laughing Luff**

Having found out that there is a Tortle sailor down by the docks that has a unique vision, Sango heads to the Laughing Luff, where RBG earlier.  He found Cregyn, not hard to spot a big Tortle in a bar full of sailors, and Cregyn recognized Reed from RBGs earlier trip since they talked to Grimshaw.  And everyone knows Grimshaw.

 Cregyn was intrigued by Reed as he’s a bit of an unusual being to be an owner of an airship.  After few drinks, Reed asked about his eyes.  Cregyn was a bit suspicious, but made him a deal, if he’s provide an into to the Captain of their airship, Cregyn would tell (and show) Reed about his eyes.  This is when Reed gave him a coin with 3 constellations with shadows on it to give to Lysander to prove who he was.

**3rd Stop: The Villain’s Discontent**

Sango’s last stop in Aventine was to see Lysander and tell him he’s sending a Tortle Shipwright with Airship experience to interview for a position.  Lysander was resistant once he found out who Sango was sending him, having heard of Cregyns previous experience and cursed last flight.  Sango made Lysander a deal: just give him one trip and if it doesn’t work out then Lysander can make the call on Cregyns further employment.

  

**Oar’s Rest**

When Sango left Aventine he took a ship up river to Oar’s Rest to visit with Bergend.  He wanted to check in on the kid and see if he’d received the books he had sent. He also wanted to talk to him and his family about a “sponsorship” to study at Castra.  As with Sango’s Order, members can sponsor a student that would be free to study and learn.  There are no strings attached to do so, but they are expected to help with chores and things that the Order may need while they are there.  After, if things go well, they are usually offered a position, either at Castra (Order HQ), or as a contact in a similar manner to Rastrum.

What Bergend decides to do is up to him.

Sango left without any assurances (as is usual) other than that they’d be in contact again.
