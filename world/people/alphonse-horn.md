Haughty, self-important, unbendingly moral. He brought Rikterr into the order
and tried to act as a father figure; Rikterr cannot stand him, and visits the
Order only when necessary to avoid dealing with Horn and his son Halcyon.

Pledged to Tyr, he cut off one of his own hands as tribute. Long white hair
always pulled back into a ponytail, armour that gleams as if it has never
seen a battle, brown eyes that stare through people, and a mace.
