A classic behind-the-scenes leader who has kept Aventine a vibrant, growing
city even in the years following the Fall. Handsome, dark-haired, in his 40s.
He took over after his father died, is intensely loyal to family and to those
who have aided him, and is well-liked by the houses but little-known to the
city at large — he prefers to entrust the day-to-day running of Aventine to
the houses, whom he manages and keeps under control.

*The more you tell people what to do, the less they can decide for themselves.
Instead, tell them what you want and let them figure it out.*
