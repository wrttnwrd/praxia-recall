Late teens to early twenties. Smart as hell — she studied finance, trade,
weather and some magic (knowledge, not ability). Dark, tall, always composed
and charismatic, and groomed for leadership of the city. Somewhat cool
towards her half-brother and stepmother. Very serious, rarely smiles; she can
sink an argument with a single raised eyebrow.

*I do have an opinion, yes. I thought I'd wait for you to finish babbling
before I provided it.*
