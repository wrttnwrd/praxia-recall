[Nathan (Nate) Dayspring](https://www.dndbeyond.com/characters/128287240) - Second in command of the [Order of Light](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1-fiwaPoco4cO8i4H4mRBiQfXme0gedQQ-Fa0L1tQfpU/edit?usp=sharing) while Halcyon continues his training. Trained Rikterr and has a good relationship with him. Was the one who made sure Rikterr didn’t have to swear allegiance to a deity before taking his oath: “you are way too young and too stupid to make that decision yet”. Nathan is pledged to the Raven Queen. Nathan has a scar that runs from his brow, down his left eye, and ends at his chin. His left eye is still intact, but the pupil looks fogged over, as if his vision is impaired.

Nathan trained Rikterr, Ger’u’do Durtbourne.

Based in [Salmuir](/world/places/salmuir.md).
