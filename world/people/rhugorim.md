Rhugorim is a long-retired paladin of The Raven Queen, and one of the few mortals in recent memory to have journeyed to [Avernus](/world/places/avernus.md) and back.

He appears to be an old orc of 35-40 years. He was, in fact, born over 80 years ago. But he spent decades in [Avernus](/world/places/avernus.md), and for reasons no one understands, he did not age while there.

He traveled there in -80 FR, via the River Styx, with a party of 10 others of his order. The objective: Explore and find a way to disrupt fiendish incursions into Krag M'goth. They lost half their number in the first day, then secured a Badge Of Zariel from a messenger they killed in battle. He remembers:

- He and his team got in by hiring a Merranaloth w/ a soul coin at The Dock Of Fallen Cities. The Merranaloth offered a trade, but they didn't accept.
- They reached the Dock Of Fallen Cities through a cave at the mouth of the Helgoril (which adds up, since the Helgoril now flows backwards)
- Two of his team fell into the River Styx and emerged "blank slates." They had no memory of themselves, their friends, their skills, or their mission
- A battlefield stretching into the distance, patrolled by heavily armed and armored devils
- At one end, the Bronze Citadel. At the other, Tiamat's Monument (Tiamat wasn't a prisoner there, yet)
- Distances mean nothing. Conventional maps don't work
- The terrain changes at random times
- Downpours of burning stone and acidic blood. Ongoing battles. Explosive gases. When these downpours happen, either find a cave or use magic. Non-magical means will not protect you
- There are flora and fauna - predators, prey, plants, etc. Be careful
- Also, infernal war machines. Look out for infernal, wheeled carts of metal
- He escaped via the Bleeding Citadel
- But he is certain there are other ways
- Of the ten, three made it out

He has a map of avernus.jpg that he drew, but he warns it is woefully incomplete.

# **Rhugorim, Aging Orc Paladin (NPC) — CR 7**

_Medium Humanoid (Orc), Lawful Good (weary but resolute)_  
**AC** 18 (dented plate) • **HP** 112 (15d8+45) • **Speed** 30 ft.  
**STR** 18 (+4) • **DEX** 10 (+0) • **CON** 16 (+3) • **INT** 10 • **WIS** 14 (+2) • **CHA** 16 (+3)  
**Saves** Wis +6, Cha +7, Con +6  
**Skills** Ath +7, Medicine +5, Persuasion +6, Intimidation +6  
**Senses** darkvision 60 ft., PP 12  
**Languages** Orc, Common  
**PB** +4

### **Traits**

**Relentless (Once/Day).** When Rhugorim is reduced to 0 HP but not killed outright, he drops to 1 HP instead. (A remnant of his old orc ferocity + divine grit.)

**Fading Might.** Rhugorim’s spell save DC is **14** (was 16–17 in his prime). Damage from his Divine Smites is limited (see below).

**Lay on Hands (15 HP/day).** Same as paladin, but smaller pool reflecting age/attrition.

**Divine Sense (2/day).**

---

### **Actions**

**Multiattack.** Rhugorim makes two _Warhammer_ attacks.

**Warhammer.** +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.  
**Hit:** 9 (1d8+4) bludgeoning, or 10 (1d10+4) if two-handed.

**Divine Smite (3/day).** When he hits, he can add radiant damage:

- 1st-level: **7 (2d8)**
    
- 2nd-level: **11 (3d8)**  
    _(He no longer smites at higher levels due to physical strain.)_
    

---

### **Spellcasting (Paladin, Aged — Level 12 but diminished)**

Spell save DC **14**, +6 to hit.  
He prepares a small list he still remembers instinctively.

**1st level (4 slots):** _bless, cure wounds, shield of faith, compelled duel_  
**2nd level (3 slots):** _lesser restoration, magic weapon_  
**3rd level (2 slots):** _revivify, aura of vitality, dispel magic_  
**No 4th or 5th level spells** (he’s lost those through decline).

---

### **Auras (Diminished but Present)**

**Aura of Protection.** Rhugorim and allies within 10 ft. gain +2 to saving throws.  
**Aura of Courage.** Allies within 10 ft. can’t be frightened while conscious.

---

### **Reactions**

**Parry.** Rhugorim adds +3 to his AC against one melee attack that would hit him.

---

### **Personality & Notes**

- Once a fearsome front-line paladin, Rhugorim is now slower, scarred, and stiff-jointed.
    
- Still carries deep conviction; acts as mentor, tactician, and stabilizer in chaotic fights.
    
- In battle, he conserves spells, smiting only when necessary.
    
- His body is fading; his resolve is not.
