# Fannor and T'cheh
Fannor is the leader of the wyvern riders of Silverscale Clan. He is the stereotypical rider: Brash, with a lopsided grin and a perpetually windblown look. T'cheh (see below) is his best friend, and they share a psychic link. He is very, very protective of T'cheh and given to long conversations with the wyvern.

His steed T'cheh, if referred to as a "steed," will immediately point out that they're companions, and once they're in the air, Fannor is highly dependent on his winged friend. T'cheh is highly intelligent, with eyes that whirl different colors based on his emotional state.
