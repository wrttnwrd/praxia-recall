The Nosferatu are vampires created from Shadar Kai while in the Shadowfell. Use https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/1680934-nosferatu as a base, but:

- 250hp
- Claws do 2d12+5 (14) slashing damage
- Bite does 2d12+5 (14) but if target is missing any hp, damage is necrotic
- AC 19

No sunlight hypersensitivity.

Facts
- They possess immense strength (+5)
- They can use a form of psychic ability to render themselves "dim." They can reach out to the minds of 1d10 people within 100 feet and erase all perception of them from that person's mind. A DC 20 WIS save negates **if** there is a reason for the character to notice the nosferatu, such as the character touching the creature, the creature moving an object, or someone else affecting or being affected by the Nosferatu. Duration = 10min or concentration.
- They can move about in daylight but make all attacks and ability checks at disadvantage
- They can hide their bite wounds by licking the wound
- They are otherwise weakened when not in the Shadowfell (TBD)
- While in the Shadowfell, their creator can use a form of Psychic Whispers to maintain communications w/ up to four of its spawn.

Myths
- They turn to dust in a full moon
- They worship Shar (they don't worship anyone)
- They cannot create new vampires
- They are immortal and return to unlife shortly after destruction (people believe this because their most striking features are identical from one nosferatu to the next)
