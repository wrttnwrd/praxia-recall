Paladin of the Raven Queen. An orphan, he grew up destitute in Salmuir with
his only friends: Ger'u'do Durtbourne, a half-orc, and Schala Zeal, a half-elf.
Both he and Ger'u'do loved Schala. One night Rikterr got drunk, let his
jealousy rage, and beat Ger'u'do nearly to death. The next day he went looking
for a cliff to jump from, and the paladin Alphonse Horn stopped him — taking,
in exchange, his pledge to serve others.

He did not take a god before the Raven Queen because he never felt worthy.
His training did not remove his rage; it let him use it.

At the ambush at Mistwharf he told Mossy he loved her and triggered the
sacrifice power of The Light That Sparks, dying and coming within 15hp of
permanent death before being revivified. He has since resolved to break his
Oath of Devotion for an Oath of Vengeance. He promised the Temple of Helm the
Quinary would be returned. He was sacrificed at the fall of the chain.
