# NPC: Saroya Demiarma
Saroya is a 65-year-old diplomat from [Salmuir](/world/places/salmuir.md). She is the Chancellor of Salmuir and acts as the liaison between [the Haddad Combine](/world/groups/haddad-combine.md) and the rest of Silidria, wields immense power.

She's a brilliant politician and a ruthless advocate for her city, with a soft spot for the more downtrodden denizens of Salmuir. 

She has a cynical approach to politics that belies her idealism about the post-Fall Silidria.

## Story
Saroya started in the Outer City. Not much is known of her rise to power. One day she became the lead representative for Salmuir.

## Rumors

- Saroya had the previous chancellor assassinated
- She is a vampire
- She is a wizard and seeks to become a lich
- She is just a mouthpiece for the Combine, exercising little initiative

## Relationships
Saroya considers herself a close ally of [Notorious RBG](/world/groups/notorious-rbg.md). She dislikes Guthra G'hul, is suspicious of Feyd Azari but respects him, and detests Zaavis.
