Longtime servant of Thargorax, Agrona is a humanoid of indeterminate type, appearing generally as a gaunt, tall female in plate mail.

She is in fact a Cleric/Wizard of great power, tasked with breaking the remaining seals.

She is primarily interested in collecting soul coins and building her wealth. It is her intent to gain power through this wealth.

Agrona is utterly evil, remorseless, and unemotional. Her sole purpose is acquiring power. Thargorax doesn't trust her, but she has been a loyal servant thus far.
