Lord Commander Kaelen Varrus is a figure of imposing calm. His elven heritage is evident in the sharp, aristocratic lines of his face, the faint point of his ears, and an unnerving stillness that seems to absorb the chaos around him. His dark hair is cropped short, a practical military cut that has long since begun to show the silver of his advancing years at the temples. His eyes, a cool and piercing grey, hold the weight of two centuries of vigilance. He is lean and corded with the muscle of a lifelong soldier, his movements economical and precise. His face is clean-shaven, revealing a firm jaw and a mouth that rarely curves into a smile. He bears a faint, silvery scar that traces the line of his left cheekbone, a memento from the Third War. His uniform is impeccably maintained, the deep blue of the Aventine Guard command uniform a stark contrast to the city's pale stone, adorned only with the insignia of his rank and the city's crest.

**Personality:** Kaelen is the bedrock upon which the security of Aventine is built. He is a being of profound practicality and unwavering resolve, his humor so dry it is often mistaken for disdain. He's sharp and analytical with a clarity that can be both unsettling and reassuring. He's a creature of habit and order - almost OCD - finding solace in the rigid structure of the Guard and the predictable rhythm of patrols and duty rosters.

Fiercely loyal to the city of Aventine, Kaelen views it as a living entity that he is sworn to protect. This loyalty is not born of abstract patriotism, but from the visceral memory of its near-destruction and the faces of those who perished defending it. He is demanding of his subordinates, holding them to the same exacting standards he sets for himself, yet he is also known for his quiet, almost paternal, concern for their welfare. He is a leader who commands respect rather than affection, his presence alone enough to instill discipline and courage in the ranks.
