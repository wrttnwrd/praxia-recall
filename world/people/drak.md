A ranger who left his tribe. His arc runs from revenge as a selfish act of
anger toward vengeance as a form of justice and protection for others; he
serves Calistria, the Savored Sting, goddess of love and revenge — and is
uneasy about whether her vengeance and his are the same thing. He is
considering the Silverscale Clan as a possible new tribe, and uses his pub as
a place to find people who need help from predators and vultures.

He likes animals more than people, and is an eco-saboteur: he once foiled a
trophy hunt by convincing the hunted animals to turn on their hunters. Every
poacher died, and the man who wanted the trophies wants Draknev dead.

At Bedlam's Faire his secret was: "I loved the power I felt running with
Elidan's crew." He planted the seedling that grew into the bastion.
