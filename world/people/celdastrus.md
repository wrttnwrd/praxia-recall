An adult blue dragon who had terrorised north-eastern Essedria for
generations, held in check only by a well-organised military until The Fall.
He built an underground glass castle by melting and fusing the sand, and
ringed it in thunderstorms.

He travelled with Notorious RBG in the shape of Kergek the cartographer.
At the ambush at Mistwharf he revealed himself. Rikterr stoked the distrust
between him and Agrona Sef; when Celdastrus knocked Drak out of the fight and
looked about to kill him — against Agrona's orders to leave RBG alive — she
turned on him and disintegrated him.
