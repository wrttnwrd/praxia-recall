## Story
Whipping Sound has long been underestimated. The Kenku suffers from his race's curse, only able to mimic. But he's found a way to speak via Thieves Cant.

So, while he sounds like he's re-iterating the same phrases over and over, he's actually conversing, usually with his longtime friend and ally, [Sandren](/world/people/sandren.md).

They usually wear a dark cloak over beaten-up leather armor. They wield a shortsword, but will avoid combat at all costs.

They are the master forger of The [Bournier Cartel](/world/groups/bournier-cartel.md), easily falsifying import records and other essential documents.

Whipping Sound does **not** like the Bourniers - Elissa in particular. She treats him like shit. Given they option, they will move to another employ.
