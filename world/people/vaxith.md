_Medium Fiend (Abishai), Lawful Evil_

Infused with the essence of a Shadow Dragon by [Tiamat](/world/people/tiamat.md).

## Stat Block

**Armor Class** 22 (Natural Armor)

**Hit Points** 260 

**Speed** 40 ft., Fly 40 ft. (Hover)

|**STR**|**DEX**|**CON**|**INT**|**WIS**|**CHA**|
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|14 (+2)|24 (+7)|20 (+5)|16 (+3)|16 (+3)|18 (+4)|

- **Saving Throws:** Dex +13, Con +11, Wis +9
    
- **Skills:** Acrobatics +13, Perception +9, Sleight of Hand +17, Stealth +17
    
- **Damage Immunities:** Fire, Poison, Acid
    
- **Damage Resistances:** Cold, Lightning; Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing from all Attacks

- **Senses:** Truesight 60 ft., Passive Perception 19
    
- **Languages:** Abyssal, Draconic, Infernal, Telepathy 120 ft.
    
- **Challenge:** 18 (20,000 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus:** +6
    

---

## Traits

**Legendary Resistance (3/Day):** If Vaxith fails a saving throw, he can choose to succeed instead.

**[Tiamat](/world/people/tiamat.md)’s Shroud (Innate Spellcasting):** Vaxith’s presence subtly warps light. He can take the **Hide** action even while being observed, provided he is within 10 ft. of a shadow or a source of smoke/ash.

**Evasion:** If Vaxith is subjected to an effect that allows him to make a Dexterity saving throw to take only half damage, he instead takes no damage if he succeeds on the saving throw, and only half damage if he fails.

**Magic Resistance:** Vaxith has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

## Actions

**Multiattack:** Vaxith makes three **Vile Dagger** attacks. He can replace one of these attacks with **Master’s Pilfer**.

**Vile Dagger:** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +13 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 24 ($4d6 + 7$) piercing damage plus 14 ($4d6$) poison damage. If Vaxith had Advantage on the attack roll, the target must succeed on a **DC 21 Constitution Saving Throw** or be **Stunned** until the end of its next turn.

**Master’s Pilfer:** Vaxith attempts to steal an item from a creature within 5 feet. He makes a **Sleight of Hand check (+17)** contested by the target’s **Passive Perception**. On a success, Vaxith acquires one item the target is carrying or wearing (that is not a primary weapon or armor) without the target noticing until the start of their next turn.

**Shadow Jaunt (Recharge 4–6):** Vaxith magically teleports, along with any equipment he is wearing or carrying, up to 60 feet to an unoccupied space he can see that is in dim light or darkness.

---

## Reactions

**Uncanny Dodge:** When an attacker that Vaxith can see hits him with an attack, he can use his reaction to halve the attack’s damage against him.

**Vanish into Soot:** When Vaxith takes damage, he can release a cloud of magical ash and move up to half his speed. This movement does not provoke opportunity attacks, and he can immediately take the **Hide** action as part of this reaction.

## Tactical Notes for the DM

- **The "Shadow Anchor":** Vaxith is designed to work in tandem with Urush-Gǔl. While the dragon uses **Frightful Presence** to impose Disadvantage on the party's Ability Checks (including Perception), Vaxith moves in with his +17 Sleight of Hand.
    
- **The Escape Plan:** Once the **Astrolabe of Three Wishes** is secured, Vaxith will use **Shadow Jaunt** to move into the vertical shafts of the lair or hide within the dragon’s own shadow.
    
- **The Hook:** If the party detects him, Vaxith doesn't stand and fight. He leads them into traps or toward the dragon's claws, using his **Evasion** to ignore any area-of-effect spells the party tries to use to catch him.
