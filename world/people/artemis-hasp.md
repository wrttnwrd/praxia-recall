**Artemis Hasp** is a Gnome tinkerer wizard. Once part of [Marta's Marauders](/world/groups/martas-marauders.md), he’s the owner of The Mekanikum and a regular at [the Green Slaad](/world/places/the-green-slaad.md). He enjoys everyone’s company, trades stories with Blong and occasionally glances nervously at the stuffed Slaad behind the bar. His prize possession is a Strongarm automaton named Crunch. His favorite game is to have Crunch sit at a table with its larger arm out of view. He challenges passersby to an arm wrestling match with Crunch. When they sit down, Crunch reveals its larger arm. The construct rarely loses.
