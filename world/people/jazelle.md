A CHR 18 INT 20 artificer-wizard with dark hair, pale skin, and her father's
pale green eyes, living alone in a High Ward house whose interior is a
collection of Orathac War artefacts — ejecta from Essedria, charred remains of
the Shards, the twisted remnants of Sulba's staff.

Unlike her father she is not a seeker of power. She is on a mission, stays
behind the scenes as long as she can, and does not want to hurt anyone except
House Umbra — but will unwittingly commit atrocities on the way.
