Leader of the Silverscale Clan.

Trianoma is an older Goliath. She came to power when her predecessor stepped down. She's unusually wiry for her race, with a fine network of scars on her left hand.

She rides the allosaurus G'rondith - aka Stampy to the members of Notorious RBG.

Trianoma is a fiercesome fighter, but her greatest strength is her natural charisma and wisdom belied by her size and appearance.
