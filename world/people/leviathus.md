Leviathus, The Abyssal Maw, was attracted to the Gloomspire by the presence of Leda, one of [The Seals](/world/things/the-seals.md), then trapped in [The Whispering Abyss](/world/places/whispering-abyss.md) in The Fall. For all their power, they cannot escape the Abyss without help.

Seven years is nothing for a titan, but seven years next to the Lida Seal has driven Leviathus to the brink of madness. It will do anything to leave.

Leviathus may be willing to negotiate for help leaving the Abyss and returning to the oceans of the surface world.
