(from Jamar)

Schala Zeal was born in Salmuir to a modest lifestyle. Her family wasn’t poor, but they weren’t well-off, either. Her father was the personal blacksmith to a wealthy family, and her mother cared for Schala and her younger brother Janus.

Schala was a quiet child, mostly interacting with imaginary friends and ignoring friendships with children of her own age and even her brother. When Schala was 7 and Janus 4, Schala started showing signs of magical aptitude. Her parents made her hide it instead of seeking training for her. Schala would have occasional magical outbursts, but they were mostly small and hidden from the public.

When Schala was 10 and Janus 7, Schala’s powers uncontrollably flared up, destroying her family home and killing her parents and, she thought, her brother. Schala ran from the destruction and hid for days. She finally had to find food and headed to the nearest settlement to find some. While there, she ran into two other orphans, Rikterr Shep’ard and Ger’u’do Durtbourne. They became friends and stole and lived together for seven years. That’s when a fight broke out between Ger’u’do and Rikterr and broke apart the friendship.

Schala went off alone, trying to learn more about the magical powers that killed her family. She finally found a sorcerer named Aadrien LeBeau. LeBeau taught her how to harness her powers, but it turns out he was doing so because he’s Janus’ second-in-command. Janus survived Schala’s magical outburst and studied to be a warlock. He took The Fiend as his patron and works to sacrifice magically-inclined beings to gain more power from his patron.

After training her enough that her powers were worth sacrificing to The Fiend, Aadrien took Schala to Janus to kill her. Janus toyed with his sister for months, torturing her for the death of their parents.

The night Janus planned to sacrifice Schala, Glenn LaRana breaks into Janus’ hideout to kill him. He ends up rescuing Schala instead and missing out on his chance to kill Janus.

Ever since that day, Schala has been roaming the world looking for her brother, trying to figure out what he wants all the power to accomplish.

Schala is not a member of any organizations.
