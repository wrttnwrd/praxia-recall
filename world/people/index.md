# people

* [Aadrien LeBeau](aadrien-lebeau.md) - Sorcerer who taught Schala Zeal to harness her powers so Janus could sacrifice them; Janus's second-in-command.
* [Agrona Sef](agrona-sef.md) - Cleric/wizard of great power, longtime servant of Thargorax, tasked with breaking the four Seals; collects soul coins for herself.
* [Ahmad Azari](ahmad-azari.md) - Founder of Aventine, who journeyed north from Salmuir in -300 FR.
* [Aisha Azari](aisha-azari.md) - Feyd's second wife; intellectual, descended from the former rulers of Salmuir, teaches part-time at the University.
* [Alfhild](alfhild.md) - Carpenter and engineer aboard The Villain's Discontent.
* [Alphonse Horn](alphonse-horn.md) - Lead Paladin of the Order of Light; haughty, unbendingly moral, pledged to Tyr, and cut off his own hand in tribute.
* [Amenopophis](amenopophis.md) - The Flayed God, whose long residence blighted the Haunts and whose crypt is a vast undead engine for his restoration.
* [Amoth](amoth.md) - A Second God, slain by Asmodeus; his death caused The Fall, and his impact scarred Essedria.
* [Angus Bloodstone](angus-bloodstone.md) - A famous member of House Highfury and one of Marta's Marauders; bought The Brimming Tankard with his share of the slaad's control gem.
* [Ardmon Ignus Lacqui](ardmon-ignus-lacqui.md) - A rakshasa of the Plane of Fire, nearly immune to magic; RBG destroyed his salamanders and drove him off, winning his quarters.
* [Arkhan The Cruel](arkhan-the-cruel.md) - Guardian of Tiamat's Lair, bearer of the Hand of Vecna, working to break the Chains of Gleynir.
* [Armak Rockfist](armak-rockfist.md) - Dwarf who owns The Green Slaad in all but name; de facto leader of Marta's Marauders after Marta died.
* [Artemis Hasp](artemis-hasp.md) - Gnome tinkerer wizard, owner of The Mekanikum, once of Marta's Marauders; keeps a Strongarm automaton named Crunch.
* [Artemis Killgrave](artemis-killgrave.md) - First officer of The Wave Serpent; a half-elf fighter.
* [Asmodeus](asmodeus.md) - A Later God of faith and magic; killed Amoth, and Tiamat counts him an enemy.
* [Augustus Hale](augustus-hale.md) - Human merchant who owns Hale's Ales in Atley.
* [Bahamut](bahamut.md) - A Second God; his temple in Aventine is a leaping spire of white stone under Elenara Silverleaf.
* [Bataar Highfury](bataar-highfury.md) - Human leader of House Highfury, the martial and pragmatic house that holds Aventine's walls, docks and military infrastructure.
* [Blong Hrusto](blong-hrusto.md) - Giff manager and bartender of The Green Slaad, quietly angling to own it; now also manages RBG's bar at Wyldwood.
* [Brugilia Broadstorm](brugilia-broadstorm.md) - Zerla Pelletweaver's significant other, of Nigh Darûl.
* [Callistra](callistra.md) - A Later God embodying chaos; a cult in Aventine bears her name.
* [Calyx](calyx.md) - Ship's mage of The Villain's Discontent.
* [Cedos](cedos.md) - The ent who leads the farmers replanting Essedria.
* [Celdastrus](celdastrus.md) - An adult blue dragon who impersonated the cartographer Kergek, then was disintegrated by his own ally at Mistwharf.
* [Cetranah](cetranah.md) - Duchess of the Summer Court; Gallias's mother, who retained Diskuss to break the engagement.
* [Corwin Dunsbane](corwin-dunsbane.md) - Paladin lieutenant in the Order of Helm; a tall, tough-looking man in plate mail.
* [Cregyn](cregyn.md) - Tortle of Notorious RBG; his life partner Taiga is expecting, and he has five years to live because of the Voice That Calls.
* [Dalyor](dalyor.md) - Gray elf sailmaker of Porth Môr, made modest by The Fall.
* [Debelar](debelar.md) - The bastion's butler — an ancient goblin of indeterminate gender who gets along with Thistlebeard for reasons no one understands.
* [Diskuss](diskuss.md) - Jester of the Gloaming Court, disguised as a gnome waiter, who enchanted Fidelia at the wedding.
* [Dolmand Trollflayer](dolmand-trollflayer.md) - City Jarl of Nigh Darûl.
* [Drak](drak.md) - Ranger sworn to a goddess of vengeance, moving from revenge-as-anger toward vengeance-as-justice; an eco-saboteur who worships Calistria.
* [Drake Bournier](drake-bournier.md) - Bournier who unwittingly hired RBG to move the family safe into the Undercity vault after they burned his mansion.
* [Drakith](drakith.md) - A particularly vile Red Abishai who has banned the merranaloth from most of Avernus.
* [Eldari Belos](eldari-belos.md) - Founder of House Belos, which has overseen Camadan's "legitimate" crime since -300 FR.
* [Elethar](elethar.md) - Female elf of 1500 years who leads the refugee community of Taelian.
* [Elgonna Hand](elgonna-hand.md) - Bugbear who manages the affairs of Atley from the Jarl's mansion.
* [Elidan](elidan.md) - A ranger who dealt with the unseelie fae and was killed by RBG in a box canyon outside Aventine.
* [Elidra](elidra.md) - An unlicensed Aventinian assassin who led the attack on the trade delegations, hired to kill Saroya Demiarma.
* [Elissa Bournier](elissa-bournier.md) - Founder and head of the Bournier Cartel; tortured and murdered by Agrona Sef on 27 Stormclaw 07.
* [Ephiane](ephiane.md) - The elven wizard who slaughtered the druidic orcs, re-buried the skystone, and raised Nova Zuros — then brought it down.
* [Erzohd Baendi](erzohd-baendi.md) - A Silver Dragonborn therapist specialising in group, trauma and magical disorders.
* [Essie Darksinger](essie-darksinger.md) - Ancient, evil hag; proprietor and prisoner of Bedlam's Faire, who tests visitors with her own tarot deck.
* [Faendal](faendal.md) - A sorcerer of Aventine, counted an ally by Gritch.
* [Fannor](fannor.md) - Half-elf leader of the Silverscale Clan's wyvern riders; shares a psychic link with T'cheh.
* [Fatimah Azari](fatimah-azari.md) - Feyd's first wife and Fidelia's mother; died in the plague of -5 FR.
* [Fenlal](fenlal.md) - Executive officer of The Villain's Discontent — a half-elf, formerly of the Surprise; no intuition and a chip on his shoulder, but supremely competent.
* [Feyd Azari](feyd-azari.md) - Merchant prince of Aventine; a hands-off leader in his 40s who rules through the houses.
* [Fidelia Azari](fidelia-azari.md) - Feyd's eldest, groomed for leadership; her mother Fatimah died in the plague of -5 FR when Fidelia was five.
* [Flux](flux.md) - First God of chaos and change.
* [G'rondith](grondith.md) - Trianoma Shef's allosaurus — "Stampy" to Notorious RBG.
* [Gallias](gallias.md) - Fey Eladrin, Summer since breaking with his parents; married Fidelia Azari at Midsummer 07.
* [Gargra Longblade](gargra-longblade.md) - Bandit leader who transformed into a green slaad and was killed by Marta Valtova; now stuffed behind the bar.
* [Gart](gart.md) - Half-orc commander of the Absolvers, and a famous member of House Highfury; wears an eye patch.
* [Ger'u'do Durtbourne](gerudo-durtbourne.md) - Orphan of Salmuir raised by monks; Rikterr's estranged childhood friend, now of the Order of Light hunting the new Domain of Dread; has only recently learned of Rikterr's death.
* [Ghorza Rockfang](ghorza-rockfang.md) - New shaman leader of the orc clans around Dirion; has united them and presents a major danger to the region.
* [Glenn LaRana](glenn-larana.md) - Broke into Janus's hideout meaning to kill him and rescued Schala Zeal instead.
* [Godtfred Frey](godtfred-frey.md) - Dirion's stables owner — in fact a deep scion enslaved to Sycorax, careful to cover his tracks.
* [Gosk](gosk.md) - Lizardfolk barber who lets the Bourniers run their operations out of Uppercuts for protection and a slice.
* [Grandma Valar](grandma-valar.md) - A green hag who keeps a barge on the River Styx, selling potions to travellers.
* [Grimshaw Shadowplank](grimshaw-shadowplank.md) - Foul-mouthed captain of The Wave Serpent, given to awful puns.
* [Gritch](gritch.md) - Born Cora; an Aasimar assassin of the Sicarii who takes her targets' left thumbs.
* [Guthra G'hul](guthra-ghul.md) - Master of the warrens beneath Aventine; founded the Court during the Pandora Crisis and is turning it from crime syndicate to guarantor of stability.
* [Hadigak](hadigak.md) - Gruff half-orc who owns the Hull Ward Bakery and is fiercely protective of his goblin staff.
* [Halcyon Horn](halcyon-horn.md) - Alphonse's son and heir to the Order of Light; resents that Rikterr was excused the oath to a god he himself is forced to swear.
* [Helm](helm.md) - God of guardians; his followers hold the line between the Material plane and the others.
* [Heribert](heribert.md) - The other of Lumpkin's halfling nephews; they're not much, but they try.
* [Hexxam](hexxam.md) - The true leader of House Umbra; stopped Guthra G'hul from killing RBG and explained the Stygian Gate to them.
* [Irene Cordova](irene-cordova.md) - A pickpocket Rikterr caught and brought into the Order of Light; plans to pledge to Tymora.
* [Iric](iric.md) - Elf druid of The Druidfel whose scrying located Elidan for the party.
* [Iridian Drace](iridian-drace.md) - Founder of House Drace in -150 FR, though rumour traces the house's roots to the earliest dwarven kingdoms of Essedria.
* [Janus](janus.md) - Schala Zeal's younger brother; survived her outburst, took The Fiend as patron, and sacrifices magical beings for power.
* [Jazelle](jazelle.md) - Illegitimate daughter of Sulba, bitter about what magic did to her father and was denied her; she means to end traditional magic, starting with House Umbra.
* [Kaelen Varrus](kaelen-varrus.md) - Lord Commander of the Aventine Guard; 203 years old, bedrock of the city's security.
* [Kendra Cloudreaver](kendra-cloudreaver.md) - Bugbear who keeps the Church of Amoth open in Atley though she has lost all her powers.
* [Kergek](kergek.md) - An aging halfling cartographer found alive in Celdastrus's lair, now travelling with the party to map post-Fall Essedria.
* [Keyleth](kiki.md) - Mossy's faerie dragon, called Kiki for short; left in Aventine with Roxy for safety, and dreaming badly since the Brand.
* [Kozani Noctis](kozani-noctis.md) - 400-year-old vampire head of the Obsidian Wing, and Saroya Demiarma's Shadowfell mirror; remembers nothing before Shadowmuir.
* [Kru](kru.md) - Female tortle master shipwright of Porth Môr; staid, sober, and just wants to build cool ships.
* [Leviathus](leviathus.md) - The Abyssal Maw — a kraken drawn to the Gloomspire by the Lida seal and trapped in the Whispering Abyss by The Fall; seven years beside the seal has driven it to the brink of madness.
* [Lida](lida.md) - First God of life and naturum; it was she who most wanted a second world made.
* [Lucretia Borova](lucretia-borova.md) - Owner of The Flagrant Farce playhouse.
* [Lumpkin](lumpkin.md) - Rotund halfling entrepreneur behind Lumpkin's Meats and Lumpkin's Fine Foods.
* [Lysander Stormchaser](lysander.md) - Aaracocra captain of The Villain's Discontent; cocky as hell.
* [Ma'ayaat](maayaat.md) - Leader of the Sicarii — a childlike human girl, and in fact a changeling.
* [Magon Ezodar](magon-ezodar.md) - Owner of The Wink And Kiss.
* [Marceline](marceline.md) - Tall, wispy half-elf who runs Ringling's Collectibles; a famous member of House Belos.
* [Marta Valtova](marta-valtova.md) - Leader of Marta's Marauders, who sacrificed herself to kill Gargra Longblade; her greatsword hangs in The Green Slaad.
* [Mevvic](mevvic.md) - House Bluestrike member and secret head of the Cult of Eight; a genius with zero common sense and a limited moral compass. Died during The Engine.
* [Mist](mist.md) - First God of magic and arcanum; she wove the four Seals and set them along the Ley Line.
* [Morugal](morugal.md) - A merranaloth who can pilot a craft down the Styx, hireable with a soul coin — or by killing Drakith, whom he hates.
* [Morwenna](morwenna.md) - A vampire whose lair under Atley reached the skystone; her feedings steadily emptied the town until RBG killed her.
* [Mossy](mossy.md) - Tabaxi thief of Salmuir, searching for her lost friend Twig; the Trickster Tabaxi whose theft set Agrona Sef hunting.
* [Mystra](mystra.md) - Guthra G'hul's new patron, and the reason the Court is turning from crime toward stability.
* [Nathan Dayspring](nathan-dayspring.md) - Second in command of the Order of Light; trained Rikterr and spared him an early oath. Pledged to the Raven Queen.
* [Neomak](neomak.md) - One of three werecreatures in the Silverscale Clan; a werebear goliath barbarian.
* [Nora](nora.md) - Human barbarian who leads the Wolfbearer Clan.
* [Nosferatu](nosferatu.md) - Vampires made from Shadar-Kai in the Shadowfell; the Obsidian Wing's assassins and spies.
* [Obaris](obaris.md) - A human wizard contaminated by long contact with the Gurges and the River Styx.
* [Opius](opius.md) - The Villain's Discontent's wizard, who works the ship's spell cannon.
* [Oriana Valtova](oriana-valtova.md) - Tiefling captain of Marta's Fury and pirate hunter for hire; Marta Valtova's adopted daughter and Grimshaw's protege.
* [Osdryn Bluestrike](osdryn-bluestrike.md) - Half-elf leader of House Bluestrike, the oldest and wealthiest of Aventine's houses.
* [Panovar Flynt](panovar-flynt.md) - Belos lieutenant, once the Green Slaad's manager; now owns The Cauldron and wants the tavern back.
* [Parwairt](parwairt.md) - Foul-mouthed gnome leader of House Drace, the artisans' guild of Camadan.
* [Plo](plo.md) - Ancient male tortle who runs Porth Môr's sanctuary for predators left without territory by The Fall.
* [Pop](pop.md) - Nervous gnome chemist of great renown behind Pop's Blasting & Oddities; Artemis Hasp's competitor and frenemy.
* [Ragnar Ansson the Young](ragnar-ansson.md) - Jarl of Dirion — a good guy, fighter/bard.
* [Rave The Howler](rave-the-howler.md) - An ancient Unseelie wearing the shape of the fomorian Raban; led the Mireborn until RBG killed it for its jawbone.
* [Rhugorim](rhugorim.md) - Long-retired orc paladin of the Raven Queen; one of the few mortals to have journeyed to Avernus and returned.
* [Rikterr](rikterr.md) - Paladin of the Raven Queen; orphan of Salmuir; sacrificed himself at the fall of the chain in 027.
* [Roxlycheh](roxlycheh.md) - An immature beholder who lost most of their eyestalks in an explosion and now hosts at The Green Slaad as "Roxy".
* [Sandren](sandren.md) - Bournier Cartel operator at Uppercuts who dislikes working for the family and runs a forgery side business.
* [Sango](sango.md) - An aging druid-monk of an order in the valley; poisoned tax collectors destroyed the village he served, and he hunts one still.
* [Sar](sar.md) - A Later God embodying chaos.
* [Saroya Demiarma](saroya-demiarma.md) - 65-year-old Chancellor of Salmuir and the Haddad Combine's liaison to Silidria; a ruthless advocate for her city.
* [Schala Zeal](schala-zeal.md) - Sorcerer whose uncontrolled power killed her parents at ten; roams the world hunting her brother Janus, who tortured her.
* [Sea Gristle](sea-gristle.md) - Gnoll of the Bournier Cartel, ordered to get rid of RBG; confessed the plot after Drak nearly killed him.
* [Siliib Azari](siliib-azari.md) - Feyd's son by Aisha; mid-teens, maturing out of a pampered start into a conscientious young man.
* [Sulba](sulba.md) - Jazelle's adoptive father, a wizard of the Orathac War whose staff survives in twisted remnants in her collection.
* [Surtur](surtur.md) - Power of the Plane of Fire, and — it turns out — once an avid D&D player.
* [Sycorax](sycorax.md) - The power to whom Godtfred Frey is enslaved.
* [T'cheh](tcheh.md) - Fannor's wyvern companion — not his steed, as T'cheh will point out; eyes whirl colours with mood.
* [Taiga](taiga.md) - Keeper of a forge in Porth Môr; donates to the Drissel Home alongside Cregyn.
* [Tasha Quan](tasha-quan.md) - Half-elf rogue of House Belos; in 25 BR a rising boss holding Hull Ward, by -5 FR head of the House.
* [Thargorax](thargorax.md) - An aspect of Tiamat cast through the Stygian Gate and imprisoned on Avernus for two millennia; a being of pure hate.
* [Tharian Thunderhammer](tharian-thunderhammer.md) - Dwarven leader of the Whitesand Clan.
* [The Fifth God](the-fifth-god.md) - Lost to time when the first world tore itself apart; the other four still seek them.
* [The Golden Mongoose](the-golden-mongoose.md) - The presence that visits Rikterr and Mossy in dreams, counselling conscience over loyalty and mischief over caution.
* [The Raven Queen](the-raven-queen.md) - Goddess served by Rikterr and, secretly, by Fel House.
* [The Twins](the-twins.md) - Two dinosaurs hatched at the Green Slaad from the egg Blong kept in a bassinet of sand over warm coals; kept at Wyldwood.
* [Thistlebeard Kragwok](thistlebeard.md) - The bastion's chef — zero manners, fantastic feasts, and the best bacon in the entire land.
* [Thud](thud.md) - Half-ogre bouncer at The Green Slaad.
* [Thutssu](thutssu.md) - A Yuan-Ti pureblood cast out of his Salmuir temple for arguing against allying with Amenopophis.
* [Tiamat](tiamat.md) - Queen of dragons, bound to Avernus by the Chains of Gleynir since the First War; she assumed the form of Thargorax to free herself, and failed.
* [Traitor Crane](traitor-crane.md) - Commander of the Blood Corsairs.
* [Trianoma Shef](trianoma-shef.md) - Older goliath leader of the Silverscale Clan; rides the allosaurus G'rondith, whom RBG call Stampy.
* [Turbin](turbin.md) - One of Lumpkin's two glum-looking halfling nephews, sent to the city to learn a trade.
* [Twig](twig.md) - Mossy's best friend from Salmuir, possibly aboard the Bluestrike merchant vessel Snapjaw.
* [Tymora](tymora.md) - The god Irene Cordova plans to pledge to when she takes her oath.
* [Tyr](tyr.md) - God to whom the Horns of the Order of Light are pledged; tribute is a severed hand.
* [Urush Gûl](urush-gul.md) - An ancient red dragon sworn to Tiamat, ordered to weaken RBG but not kill them; he gave them the Searing Brand and crippled the Discontent.
* [Valana](valana.md) - Lead cleric of the Temple of Helm; a muscular half-orc who takes the front line and delegates the politics.
* [Vaxith, the Hand In The Dark](vaxith.md) - An Abishai infused with the essence of a Shadow Dragon by Tiamat; shadowed RBG since Urush Gûl and is now at Tiamat's side, planning to lead the next assault on them.
* [Veda](veda.md) - First God of faith and divinity.
* [Vek Azar](vek-azar.md) - Powerful necromancer who leads Fel House; short of temper, protective of her power, and not evil.
* [Veles](veles.md) - Duke of the Summer Court; Gallias's father.
* [Venkat](venkat.md) - Founder of the Temple of Pelor in Hull Ward.
* [Veyrin Toldain](veyrin-toldain.md) - High Hierophant of the Temple of Amoth, driven mad by hearing his god die; murdered three of his own clerics with the Hand of Amoth.
* [Vogrel Stonebeard](vogrel-stonebeard.md) - The scholar whose lecture is the best-known profile of Thargorax.
* [Voleth of the Fog](voleth-of-the-fog.md) - A top-tier ferryman of the Styx, who would carry the party to the Dock of Fallen Cities for two soul coins or a theft from Obaris.
* [Vyrassinth the Whispercoil](vyrassinth.md) - Green Abishai in Avernus scheming to rise to first among Tiamat's servants; will flee rather than risk herself.
* [Whipping Sound](whipping-sound.md) - Bournier Cartel operator at Uppercuts, treated like dirt by the family; runs a forgery side business with Sandren.
* [Wom Taits](wom-taits.md) - Ancient dwarvish bard with a grating style that has found its fans; performs at The Green Slaad.
* [Xavis](xavis.md) - Feyd's Minister Of Domestic Affairs — in fact his spymaster.
* [Zariel](zariel.md) - The fallen angel who rules Avernus; counted an ally by Tiamat.
* [Zerla Pelletweaver](zerla-pelletweaver.md) - Maker of Nigh Darûl who set RBG trials and forged The Hand That Strikes; lives with Brugilia Broadstorm.
* [Zheilhurd Oregazer](zheilhurd-oregazer.md) - Librarian of Ironstead, who showed RBG the -2000 FR woodcut of the five heroes.
* [Zurhdal ket Mak](zurhdal-ket-mak.md) - Commander of Urdun's 200-strong militia.
