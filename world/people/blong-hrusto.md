Blong Hrusto is the Giff manager and bartender of [the Green Slaad](/world/places/the-green-slaad.md). Given the chance, she will tell long, detailed stories of various events she's witnessed, sometimes relevant to the conversation, sometimes not. Blong owns a bar in nearby Oar’s Rest, but moved to the big city to expand. She is saving up to buy a new bar, but would prefer find a loophole in her contract that lets her take ownership of [the Green Slaad](/world/places/the-green-slaad.md). Armak knows what Blong is up to, but finds the steadfast Giff charming and thinks he might someday sell to her. Most people assume Blong owns the tavern. She does nothing to dispel the notion. Neither does Armak. Blong keeps a musket under the bar at all times. Blong has the stats of a [Giff Shock Trooper](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/2821168-giff-shock-trooper) but wears leather armor (AC 14).

Blong has recently become the manager of [Notorious RBG](/world/groups/notorious-rbg.md)'s bar at Wyldwood.
