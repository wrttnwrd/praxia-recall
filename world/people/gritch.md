Cora was born to an Aasimar family in [Aventine](/world/places/aventine.md), but was exposed to evil when Narinda Shadomantle brought the dread ring to the place.

Her horrified father caught his daughter torturing rats on the floor of the family’s sleeping room. Even at the age of 9, it was clear Cora was a psychopath. Her parents turned her over to the Zhentarim’s assassins as the only alternative to killing her. They renamed her Gritch.

Gritch excelled as an assassin. Her utter lack of empathy made her ruthless and decisive.

She appears fearless, and spends all of her waking hours honing her body and mind for what she considers her art.

Gritch is 5’ 9”, utterly unattractive and wiry, weighing 120 pounds. She possessed amazing flexibility and dexterity, is one of the best bowmen in [Aventine](/world/places/aventine.md), and a deadly two-handed dagger fighter. Recently, she’s been training as a monk, adding unarmed combat and climbing to her arsenal.

Even assassins need some restraint, though, and Gritch has problems from the start. On her first job, she silently slaughtered five guards before killing her target. She argued her mission was easier without the guards. 

Since then, she’s worn an amulet that keeps her focused on prey and less likely to stray. It changes her alignment from chaotic to true neutral. The guild has promised to kill her if she removes it.

She prefers to immobilize targets with a poison-tipped arrow, then move in to finish them off and remove some proof that her job is done. Unlike most assassins, she has a calling card: She removes every target’s left thumb. No one knows why, or what she does with the thumbs.
