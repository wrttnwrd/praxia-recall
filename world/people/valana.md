Lead cleric of the Temple of Helm

Valana is a muscular half-orc woman of about 5’8”. With close-cropped hair and tattoos up one side of her face, she doesn’t look like a priestess. She breaks the mold in many ways, often taking the front line in battle, eschewing the political role of her station and delegating it, and spending more time tending to her flock.

She wields a glaive and no shield. Her armor is simple, without adornment.

_I know not what tomorrow brings. Only that if it is ill, we will protect the innocent from it._
