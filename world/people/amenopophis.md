# Amenophis - The Big Bad
**Amenophis** - The Flayed God - is the mummy of an ancient king prophesied to rise and "destroy the world to be born anew."

He was an ancient ruler of Shamharan, cast down by his own people for blasphemy after he claimed he was a risen god. His people flayed him alive, killed him. His followers stole his body and hid it in a huge temple complex beneath the delta marshes west of Salmuir. The marshes slowly became a twisted, horrible place now known as The Blighted Haunts, prowled by the Yuan-ti of the Cult Of The Flayed.

**Most** of the cult are humans. They are carefully manipulated by the Yuan-ti, who see Amenophis as a path to greater power.

Cult followers are marked by a small patch of flayed skin, usually just under the nape of the neck.

Awakened by the disturbances of recent years, Amenophis seeks to rise to godhood by emerging and conquering first Shamharan, then all of Silidria.

## A Tragic Villain
Amenopophis was once Shechem A'Pophis: A lay preacher who brought help to his people. He was prideful and arrogant, but basically out to do good. Over time, influential members of the Church Of Sseth and Salmuir decided he was too powerful and engineered his removal from the church under a dark cloud. That led to his life as Amenopophis and eventual undoing.
