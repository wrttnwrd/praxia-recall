A Tabaxi thief from Salmuir. She carries the Seals and wields the Eye That
Sees, with which she caught Rave The Howler invisible and preparing an ambush.

She is looking for Twig, her best friend from Salmuir, who may be aboard the
Bluestrike merchant vessel *Snapjaw*.

Under Essie Darksinger's tarot trial her secret was: "I let another Tabaxi
take the fall and get killed for one of her crimes."
