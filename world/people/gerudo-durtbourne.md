Ger’u’do Durtbourne was born in Salmuir to a poor lifestyle. He has no idea who his family is: his first memory is being tossed off the back of a wagon in front of a monastery. When he arrived, he didn't have a name. The monks looked at a list of names from their texts and found one as strange as their new charge: Ger’u’do. They gave him the surname Durtbourne because that’s what he was: born from the dirt.

Not knowing how old he was when he arrived, he has no idea how old he really is. The monks started counting his birthdays from when he showed up at their door, spending 10 years with them. Then, on what the monks would’ve called his 11th birthday, Ger’u’do ran away.

Stumbling through the woods for days, Ger’u’do finally found a small settlement. While there, he ran into two other orphans, Rikterr Shep’ard and [Schala Zeal](/world/people/schala-zeal.md). They became friends and stole and lived together for seven years. That’s when a fight broke out between Ger’u’do and Rikterr, and the friendship broke apart.

Ger’u’do spent a few days convalescing from the fight, then returned to the monastery. He’s been there ever since, honing his skills, until one day, he receives a letter from a familiar name...

Rikterr Shep’ard…

Ger'u'do is now a member of [the Order Of Light](/world/groups/order-of-light.md) tasked with investigating the new Domain Of Dread ([Shadowmuir](/world/places/shadowmuir.md)) alongside [Nathan Dayspring](/world/people/nathan-dayspring.md). Because of that, he hasn't seen the letter.

Ger'u'do and [Schala Zeal](/world/people/schala-zeal.md) were briefly lovers and are now good friends, but he hasn't seen her in years.
