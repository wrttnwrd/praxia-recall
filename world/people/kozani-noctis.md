Kozani Noctis is a 400-year-old vampire. She does not remember anything before her arrival in [Shadowmuir](/world/places/shadowmuir.md), where she became the head of [the Obsidian Wing](/world/groups/the-obsidian-wing.md). 

Ruthless and cunning, Noctis has one goal: Accumulate wealth and power. She quickly built the Wing into the top criminal organization in Shadowmuir. Her ultimate goal is to take over the entire underworld, then legitimize the Wing. To do that, she's turned her eye to Praxia and the city of [Salmuir](/world/places/salmuir.md).

If the party spends much time around her, they'll notice similarities to Saroya:

- Dress: Darker, more monochromatic versions of the draping fabrics and styles Saroya favors
- Appearance: A younger Saroya, tall, straight-backed, black hair
- Mannerisms: Swears, short-tempered, brilliant

## Her Story
Before The Fall, Kozani was a vampire of middling reputation and power with control over a small area in the foothills of The Spine. The Fall released energies that generated a new Domain Of Dread (Shadowmuir) and flung her and her tower there. She has no memory of this.
