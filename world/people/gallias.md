Sober, talkative, quick to anger against those who slight him or his loved
ones. Appears to be in his early twenties. Rail-thin, wiry, 6'5", always
carrying a hooked orange blade — a +1 Flametongue — and wearing rich red
brocade with gold stitching visible only in certain light. He loves Fidelia
fiercely and is sensitive about the fact that she's human. His eyes flash
orange when he is emotional.

He changed from a Spring to a Summer Eladrin after breaking with his parents
over the engagement.
