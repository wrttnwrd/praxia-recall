Urush-Gul is an ancient red dragon w/ the following changes:

1. AC 28
2. 754 hp
3. Meteor Swarm once/day, not Fireball
4. Breath recharge on 4-6

Urush has been tasked by [Tiamat](/world/people/tiamat.md) to do one thing: Weaken the party. Make them more susceptible when she springs her trap.

[Tiamat](/world/people/tiamat.md) has been scrying the HELL out of the party. Which they'd know if they were watching, but they weren't.


**Note - next time Mossy uses the goggles, give her a chance of seeing the scry**.



## When the players meet Urush

>"You've brought them. Good. You seek the Queen? I will not stop you. In fact, I will help you. But I shall exact a twofold price against your pride. In exchange, I shall grant you the 'Protection of the Red' and carry you to [Avernus](/world/places/avernus.md) myself. Refuse, and face the might of [Avernus](/world/places/avernus.md) and the Queen unaided."

If the party chooses to go along, they must grab and hold the obsidian pillar for six rounds. Any possessions they have not specifically resistant to fire will be destroyed, so they'll need to leave their stuff. 

## What Urush Can Do
### Long Shot: Discharge the Astrolabe
Urush will attempt to use [Vaxith, the Hand In The Dark](/world/people/vaxith.md) to swipe the Bag Of Holding, bring him the Astrolabe, and then replace it.

Urush can do that while the party grabs the obsidian pillar, if they do that. That's easy. 

Or, he can use the Smoldering Haze ability when he destroys the Discontent, then have [Vaxith, the Hand In The Dark](/world/people/vaxith.md) move in. 

Once Urush has it, he will use the following Wish:

"The bearer of this item will, without harming [Tiamat](/world/people/tiamat.md), weaken the chains of Gleynir, bringing them halfway closer to destruction."

This limits the potential for disaster, aids [Tiamat](/world/people/tiamat.md), and burns the last Wish.

### Damage the Discontent
Urush will attempt to destroy or disable the Discontent, leaving them to travel overland to [The Gurges](/world/places/the-gurges.md). Then they must either take his deal or burn the resources to get to [the Gurges](/world/places/the-gurges.md).

Urush will either:

1. Drop 4 Meteor Swarms on it
2. Drag the ship to the ground
3. Breathe fire, destroying the ship's rigging

Remember that the ship is **already damaged**, down 100hp.

### The Searing Brand
**Then**, Urush offers the Contract Of safety. He will take them to [Avernus](/world/places/avernus.md), but they must accept the Red Brand. 

> "You think you're safe because of the mumblings of an old man? [Avernus](/world/places/avernus.md) has changed. The fires will burn you alive you before you can speak. My brand is the only thing that will keep your flesh on your bones. "

Party gets immunity to fire damage, but gets -2 to all saves while w/in 100 miles of [Tiamat](/world/people/tiamat.md).
