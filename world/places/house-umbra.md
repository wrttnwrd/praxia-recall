# House Umbra
Location: ??
Famous members: Sulba, Hexxam, Faendal
Leader: Faendal
Founder: Mellificent Umbra, -850 FR

_You arrive in front of a kind of manor house with narrow slits for widows and solid stone walls. Around the house is a simple wrought iron fence. Squatting on the lawn two enormous stone dogs._

_A path leads past the gates and the dogs to the front of the building. There is no front door._

_The house has an ugly, blackened scar along the front. The scar continues along the ground to the fence, where you can see a large section is recently replaced. Several workers are rebuilding the wall._

Visitors to House Umbra cannot make their way more than 5 feet up the walkway without being teleported back outside. A 15 PER check will reveal a series of teleportation squares encircling the building. The squares are 5ft wide and can be jumped across.

Crossing the squares without permission activates the two dogs, which are actually gargolyes. They interpose themselves between the interloper and the building, never letting the characters pass. If the characters attempt to move closer, the gargolyes attack.

As you walk up the central path, the front wall of the manor house shimmers, revealing a stone door.

The foyer is immense, as is the rest of the building, which is a pocket dimension. It contains training areas for each school of magic, two dining halls, meeting rooms, sleeping quarters, an armory and other assorted rooms and services.

The library is famous for its magical knowledge throughout Praxia. Two others surpass it for mundane knowledge, but none for arcane. It houses thousands of magical tomes and is tended to by Nizorum, a wizened old man of indeterminate age. No one can remember when he started working there.

## Inside The House
Inside the house is a demiplane of far larger dimensions.

### Security

#### Sensors
Melificient Umbra created sensors throughout the house. The art of creating them was lost over the centuries, but they still work. The leader and anyone else selected by the leader can focus their attention on one of the sensors and see what's going on in a particular part of the mouse.

The eyes possess Truesight.

#### Locking Doors
Various doors can only be opened with passwords or keys. The keys are possessed by whichever house member owns/has permission to enter that area.

#### Gargoyles & Armor
Positioned throughout the house are gargoyles and animated armor. If attacked, any house member can summon 1d4 gargoyles or 1d4 suits of armor.

### Puzzles

#### Mirrored Storage
Particularly precious items are behind locked doors, then stored in a mirror that shows a perfect reflection of the room **except** for the item in question. Find the item in the mirror, find the location in the real room and the item will appear.

#### Painted Lock
_A wizard with a great appreciation for art put this as the lock to their chamber_

A key sits next to a door w/ no keyhole. Also sitting there is a small bucket of paint. Paint the keyhole on the door. Roll at least an 18 Performance check. The keyhole matches the key, you can insert the key in the lock.

#### Weeping Angels
Because why not.

#### Water Gauntlet
In the basement. Damp room. Hatch leading to Hexxam's chambers (?). There's a statue of a hand at one end of the room. A gauntlet mounted in front of the hatch. Insert your hand. Water starts flooding the room. You can't remove your hand.

Imitate the gesture of the statue and you'll stop the water, drain the room, and open the door.

#### Giant Key
Enlarged key. Tiny lock. Use reduce on the key.
