# The Leaning Leg
The largest tavern in Hull Ward (and the only inn, unless you count the Wink & Kiss). It's also Tasha's base of operations.

(Someday the Leg will become the Green Slaad, but not yet)

People entering the tavern for the first time through the ironbound door immediately take in:

- The strong aroma of smoke from a fire and from pipes
- A slight haze of the same
- A low murmur of conversation
- A large, open room with a well-used hearth at each end. To the right, a flight of stairs goes up to a landing and hallway. The hallway leads to guestrooms and Quann's office. The walls curve upwards towards a peak that's hidden by the floor above. Large timbers support the floor.
- A simple, highly-polished oaken bar - the only well cared for piece of furniture in the place - tended by a tall, rough-looking woman.
- Behind the bar, a timbered wall lined with kegs and a few bottles of whiskey and other liquors
- Furniture that's clearly been repaired a few times.
- Tables are nicked and stained by years of hard use.
- Two private booths in the far corner where patrons can conduct business

The bartender is **Chearsto Barrion**. She's a barbarian of the Silver Scale Clan, come to Hull Ward to seek a different path in life as an enforcer. Tasha has her working here because she keeps the place quiet.

With Chearsto there, the bar doesn’t need a bouncer. It does have a nondescript, aging Dwarf bard named **Brewkettle**. He has a so-so voice, but some very unusual music and he's well-liked in Hull Ward. He tends to play three or four instruments at once (Think Tom Waits.)

Also in the bar:

* A cutpurse named Panovar Flynt
* A young hobbit entrepreur named Lumpkin
* A hangdog half-orc fighter named Zet who's always looking for work
* A female card player named Aqua who's actually fey and uses her luck to win consistently without cheating (or is it cheating?)
* A waiter - Jorge, a skinny, suspiciously hairy bloke with long fingernails

## Experiences
Brewkettle takes the stage to play a set.

## Games
**Fast Eights**. A game where players control tiny carts drawn by simple automata (they won't work if removed from the course). The automata run around a figure-eight course. Players control the speed of the automata using a small two-button wand. Go too slow and lose. Too fast and you crash off the course. Players roll DEX, WIS or INT ability checks to control by skill, intuition, or insight. Each roll is a contest. First player to get four successes wins
