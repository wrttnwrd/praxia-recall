The Plane Of Fire near the Prime that contains Praxia isn't as hostile as other fire planes. 

In the center of the plane is the Pillar Of Flame, which is actually a globe-shaped of blue-hot fire **so** hot that it distorts the air around it and appears to be a column. Nothing can live in there, except Kossuth.

Important locations include Wrathskald, the Cinder Plane, the Sea Of Lava, and the City Of Brass.

The City Of Brass floats above the desert, drifting around the disk-shaped plane.

The most powerful beings on the plane are:

- Surtur - Demi-god of fire giants, trapped here by Tiamat
- Kossouth: Lord of flames, lives in the Pillar Of Flame
- Imix, Prince of Evil Fire Creatures
- Marrake al-Sidan ben Lazan - Sultan of the Efreet. Rules the City Of Brass from the Charcoal Palace
