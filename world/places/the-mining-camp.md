# The Mining Camp
Years ago, a few farmers found a vein of copper and dug into a rocky hillside, hoping to strike it rich. They broke into a natural cave but quickly exhausted the vein, moving on and leaving behind a haphazard mine and a falling-down camp.

Since then, it's served as home to bandits, travelers, and various forest denizens.

The only outside evidence of the camp is a few stones arranged in a firepit and logs set up as benches. A closer look will find animal bones, an old latrine (long unused), some rusted/smashed dishes, pots and pans, and areas where brush was cleared out.

The cave opening is concealed by brush and requires a Passive Perception of 14, or a Perception check of 12 or higher.

Inside the cave the walls are earth and rock. The floor is stone, with patches of dirt and clay. It's dry and cold and smells vaguely of earth. Roots from outside trees wind their way through small cracks. There are no natural light sources.

The cave extends 75' in from the opening and is 100' wide.

## Random Ideas
- Bandits have taken up residence and are waylaying travelers on the nearby road
- Gnolls have taken over. Outside the cave mouth it reeks of carrion, and the old encampment is littered with carcasses. Inside, a gnoll chieftan plots a bold attack on the nearby farmsteads
- A family of Kenku have taken shelter from viscious bugbears
- A dryad, treebound to the massive oak growing over the cave, attempts to heal after nearby farmers hacked branches off her tree
