Population: 3500
Location: North-western [Silidria](/world/places/silidria.md), on the Gap
Climate: Cold, wet, windy. Temperate as of 6 FR

Urdun is a very young orc city built by settlers from Krag M'goth. The elders of Krag M'goth had long planned to expand southward. [The Fall](/world/events/the-fall.md) and cooling climate made it that much more important. 

The city is built in two levels: Residences, artisans, etc are up on a bluff. The docks, heavy industry, etc. are down on shore. 

The city survives on fishing and farming as the first post-Fall warming has permitted. Orcs are adept hunters, fishers, and farmers.

They have a full compliment of blacksmiths, etc.

The city is not well-fortified, as it's too new. Work is ongoing on a 10-foot high wall near the docks.

The populous is 80% orc, 15% half-orc, 5% others.

The city has a 200-person militia commanded by Zurhdal ket Mak. The militia carries basic shortbows, swords, and axes. Most wear padded or leather mail. These are well-trained but only equipped with basic equipment because of post-Fall chaos.

The city's mayor is Voda - female orc - erinya is wife

Inn: Storm's Respite

They're mostly followers of Luthic, with warriors following Gruumsh.

The city is fairly wealthy.

It sits at the northern end of the Raskul Reach, a deep-water bay. A very narrow isthmus shields them from the sea, but does allow attackers to sail up to the mouth of the bay unseen (unless they're on watch).

## Enemies
[The Blood Corsairs](/world/groups/the-blood-corsairs.md) are competing with Urdun for fisheries, and see Urdun as an easy source of wealth.
