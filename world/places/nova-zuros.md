## Origin
Thousands of years ago a small skystone fell in Eastern Essedria. It embedded itself deep in the bedrock of the continent.

A millenium later, the druidic orcs of the continent unearthed the stone and unlocked its properties: The Stone could reverse gravity, lifting heavy loads free of Praxia's surface.

The orcs sculpted the stone, creating small pieces that can be found to this day in airships, carrying disks, and small magic items.

But the elves learned of the stone, and with them, the wizard Ephiane. She had bigger plans. Slaughtering the orcs, she re-buried the stone, then, with the help of a small army of pechs, excavated an enormous island around it. Elves and Orcs alike watched, stunned, as an gargantuan chunk of Essedria itself lifted ponderously into the air. 

Over the next 100 years, Ephiane built a magnificent palace on the island, which she named Nova Zuros. 

But Ephiane didn't fully understand the stone. Her tampering went too far, and Nova Zuros plunged from the sky. Ephiane and all the denizens of the island perished in a natural disaster that killed thousands more. 

The elves found the stone under the rubble, and bound it to the bedrock using powerful earth magic. They prepared to do more, but the first Orc/Elf war broke out. The orc hosts drove to the very gates of Kah Dorei itself. In the struggle for survival that ensued, the stone was forgotten.

There it remained, until the vampire Morwenna happened upon it while expanding her lair under the town of Atley. Working with a few of her grey elf allies, she researched and learned the nature of it and the elemental magic holding it in place.

*Morwenna used Atley as a kind of feeding ground.*

Uncertain how she might use the stone, with a lifespan measured in centuries, Morwenna decided to wait. She kept the knowledge close but, like Ephiane, worked with the local Pech tribes to free what remained of Nova Zorus. Over the years, the Pech helped her find a way to crack the earth magic. That, too, she kept close.

Until the Fall. When Amoth plunged to Praxia, the fireball engulfed most of Essedria. Morwenna, facing certain death, ordered the Pechs to cut the island loose. Nova Zorus shot back into the air, taking with it Morena, Atley, and all manner of creatures that made their home in and around the vampire's lair.

Damage was extensive. Parts of Morwenna's lair collapsed. The town of Atley was nearly leveled. The lake, carried with the rest of Nova Zuros, somehow remained intact, a perpetual waterfall cascading over the rocky extreme of the island. 

Morwenna secured her survival for a time, but faced a new problem: The Stone's strange effects made it nearly impossible to leave the island. A dwindling food supply. The town of Atley, only 2500 souls to start, couldn't sustain the vampire and began to shrink. Morwenna supplemented her diet with livestock and other creatures and still, the town shrank. Within a few years, the town was only 500 people. 

She is now abducting children from the town and raising them like cattle, hoping to create breeding pairs and increase population. But she's also working w/ the Pechs, researching how to get Nova Zuros down to the surface without destroying her lair and herself. 

## Morwenna
She once had a small group of spawn and other servants. Starvation forced her to consume most of them, except as noted below.

She is a [Vampire Spellcaster](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/17207-vampire-spellcaster) with an AC of 19. After years of skulking about, her stealth is mind-bogglingly good: +14, with Skulker. She also has Misty Step at will.

## Species

- Orcs, bugbears, humans, elves, and a smattering of dwarves now live in Atley
- Orcs live outside the town
- Also on the surface:
	- A pack of Barghests
	- Deer, etc.
	- A small community of harpies who lurk about the Endless Falls
- A wide variety of creatures live in the warrens around Morwenna's lair: 
	- Xorn
	- A hermit named Soo has an intelligent pet ooze named Amazon Slime because of a helm and stabbing sword in the ooze. The hermit knows of the trap at the doors to Morwenna's lair, and also of the hoard mimics (although she forgets those 50% of the time). Soo is also the owner of the Gem Of Soo, which she lost some time ago. If it is returned to her, she provides instructions regarding the caves, a partial map, and a diamond worth 500gp.
	- A chimera and gorgon wander the place
	- A small family of hoard mimics sleeping next to the [dwarven guard ghosts](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/175975-obzedat-ghost). They pretend to be part of the hoard. They often feed off the remnants of the ghosts' victims
	- Dwarven guard ghosts: Use [Obzedat Ghost](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/175975-obzedat-ghost). These were guards left long, long ago when the island first flew. They perished in the island's first fall but remain bound to the area. Players can attempt to persuade them that there's no need to remain.  
	- Pechs live in the tunnels of Morwenna's lair. They have an uneasy relationship with the vampire, trading their work on the Stone for their continued survival
