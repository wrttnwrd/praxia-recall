# Social Structure
See The History Of Salmuir And [Shamharan](/world/places/shamharan.md). Pre-Fall the city's residents lived in a rigid caste system with grey elves at the top, natives near the bottom, and the few Drow below even them. The lowest castes held the worst, dirtiest, most dangerous jobs, from mucking out dinosaur stalls to cleaning the city's always-backed-up sewer system.

Post-Fall, most of the city's lower castes live in the outer two rings: The docks and warehouses, and what remains of the wealthiest neighborhoods.

Higher castes live in the remaining rings, mixed except for the innermost ring, where the palace and very wealthy live inside higher walls and more elaborate buildings.

There are two groups that live outside the caste system: The druids, and the Yuan-ti.

After [the fall](/world/events/the-fall.md) of the grey elf ruling powers, a little-known group known only as [The Haddad Combine](/world/groups/haddad-combine.md) has taken over. The Combine is a mix of races, but little else is known about them.

## [The Haddad Combine](/world/groups/haddad-combine.md)
Turns out [the Haddad Combine](/world/groups/haddad-combine.md) is a group of 8 Raven Queen followers (Shadar Kai). They quietly replaced the grey elves and seek to loosen the hold of the undead on Salmuir and the surrounding area. They know more about what's happening than anyone else in the city.

[Saroya Demiarma](/world/people/saroya-demiarma.md) is the chancellor and the Combine's representative/contact with the city. She hadn't told the party this.

Now that Rik'Terr has become a Paladin of the Raven Queen, and the party knows as much as they do, the Combine wants to meet them.

The Combine **do not share their names.** They are known collectively as Haddad.

## Law Enforcement And Crime
For much of its existence, Salmuir had relatively little crime. The grey elves were ruthless enforcers of the law, and life outside the city was almost impossible.

Post-Fall, it's a more dangerous place, but still nothing like [Aventine](/world/places/aventine.md). There is a thriving underground market, the Cult of the Flayed God, and a handful of small-time criminals. Everyone's simply too busy surviving.

Salmuir is patrolled by a City Guard now in the employ of [the Haddad Combine](/world/groups/haddad-combine.md). They enforce the laws fairly and professionally.

It's also patrolled by various Druid groups. No one's certain what they do, or how they fit into the local laws. They've so far been harmless.

# Architecture
Salmuir sees a short, intense rainy season but, for the rest of the year, is hot and humid. Most buildings are cone shaped with long chimneys. The chimneys funnel heat out of the structures - the taller the chimney, the cooler the interior. The wealthiest homes have chimneys towering 50-100 feet in the air.

Lower caste homes are built of clay brick, wire, and light timber from the surrounding jungle. Higher caste homes are built of limestone and timber fused by magic wielded by the city's Druid caste.

### The Mists
Once home to the homes of the very wealthiest of Salmuir's residents, it is now a wreckage. The area is enveloped by a cloud of moisture, pollution, and the smoke of small fires that can't travel away in the heavy air. It is now called only The Mists, and few venture there unless they must.

# Education
There is no one “University of Salmuir.” Instead, there are dozens of small, specialized schools focused on individual trades.

# Food
If you're from the north, Salmuir's typical fare goes from mildly exotic to downright bizarre. The diet is heavy in fresh fruits, olives, and cucumbers (including for breakfast). Thin, lightly-leavened breads and baked goods are eaten throughout the day.

There are literally dozens of varieties of chocolate from the region's coca plants, and spices abound. Food is flavorful and lively.

The most popular drink in the city is a bitter beverage called *mirum*. Mirum is similar to coffee, usually served with cinnamon and sugar and sometimes with alcohol. It's highly caffeinated.

## Alcohol
Chilled beer and wine, rather than room temperature. Wine with fruit in it. And a strong, sharp liquor created from the local palm trees called Varnat (Var-NOT) that is extremely bitter and very strong. First time you drink it, roll a DC 15 CON check. If you fail, you spit it out. Natives chase it with sweet fruit juice or wine.

## Proteins
Fish and various serpents are most of the diet. Fowl and other meat are luxury items.

## Recreation
The proliferation of fungus combined with centuries of experimentation have yielded several recreational drugs:

- Orugar: Hallucinogens consumed by the upper and middle classes
- Elfcap: A milder, homebrewed version of Orugar
- Wrack: A powerful psychotic with a wide range of hilarious and dangerous effects. Outlawed in Salmuir
- Mercy: A powerful sedative used for medical procedures

# Points of Interest

## The Clever Luck
Location: The Outer City
Actually pretty high end. This is a hotel/tavern run by an older tabaxi named **Clever**. He may remember Mossy as he came across her years ago when she was shoplifting.

The Luck is a delightful, 90' vertical chaos of rooms, ropes, stairs and ladders. It's only 100 square feet at the base. Guests can get a private room if desired but typically sleep in one of the many hammocks, containers, or open tubes/rooms that fill the place.

Clever serves a variety of drinks and food, all of which are quite good. He also provides Elfcap if desired.

Business partner/wife: Vanity Luck

## The Bathhouse
Next door to the Luck. Magic water of prestidigitation.

## The Library Of Salmuir
Location: The Crown District
There is nothing like the Library anywhere else on Silidria. A massive, curved limestone building rivaling the Library of Alexandria, it has hundreds of thousands of tomes, scrolls, maps, and other material. It's carefully organized using a numbering system.

It's maintained by a large staff, all in robes. They carry special glowing fungi to avoid setting their precious charges on fire.

**Many books at the Library are shrunk down for easier storage. Players will walk past many tiny shelves with lots of these miniature tomes. There are enormous magnifiers throughout the library so people can read the reduced documents.**

- Reading rooms have silence spells on them
- The library is always open: 24/7

Chief Librarians: Eldara, Menolly, Indira
