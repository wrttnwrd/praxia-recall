## Description
Duergar city in the Underdark.

5 days' ride from [Ironstead](/world/places/ironstead.md).

Old, ornate architecture and gray-ish lighting that's efficient but unflattering.

## Details
City Jarl: Dolmand Trollflayer
Zerla Pelletweaver lives here with her significant other, Brugilia Broadstorm

!nigh-darul.png

## Details
A series of cable cars allows shortcuts across the city.

The "bottom" of the city is covered in water. This is from [the Fall](/world/events/the-fall.md), but the Duergar have built a clever system of boats and barges to capitalize on it.
