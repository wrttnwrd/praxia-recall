# POI: Uppercuts

## Story
Gosk started Uppercuts as a place for sailors to get cheap, easy shaves and cuts. He's better known for his range (he can style fins, hair, and scales) than for skill. Uppercuts is rarely full. 

It's a run-down place, quiet, with no employees. There's an always-stuffed swear jar to the left of a rickety door, a small table under the front window, and two barber's chairs. 

On the sole counterop is a collection of scissors and less-prosaic implements Gosk uses for his various clientele.

## Smuggler Headquarters
The [Bournier Cartel](/world/groups/bournier-cartel.md) operates out of a small room at the back of the shop. The room is well-hidden behind a shelf (DC 20 to find). There, [Sandren](/world/people/sandren.md) and [Whipping Sound](/world/people/whipping-sound.md) handle the day-to-day, tracking routes and managing orders.
