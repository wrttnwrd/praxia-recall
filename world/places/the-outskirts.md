Located just outside [Aventine](/world/places/aventine.md), The Outskirts includes patches of forest, bog, swamp and low hills. It's important for two reasons.

First, it straddles The Ibril. If someone controlled The Outskirts, they could threaten barge traffic.

Second, The Shimmertrame, the biggest land highway in Pharos, crosses the area. From it, you can head up the Ibril overland, into the Whitesand Steppe, or all the way to [Salmuir](/world/places/salmuir.md) and [Shamharan](/world/places/shamharan.md).

Small-time warlords and brigands have fought over this region for decades. When transportation is threatened, [Aventine](/world/places/aventine.md) sends out the army. But settlers and others in the Outskirts are mostly left to survive on their own.

## The Region
See above and below. The Outskirts is a mix of low hills, forest, swamp and grasslands.

In addition to humanoid inhabitants, a variety of natural creatures make their homes here. There's also a scattering of fey, giantfolk, and a small tribe of lycanthropes. A troop of gnolls has recently tried to move in but is meeting the resistance of the lycanthropes.

### Environment
The Outskirts shares its climate with [Aventine](/world/places/aventine.md), but its distance from The Gap means the weather is more consistent. It's a bit less windy, but gets slightly hotter in the summer and colder in the winter.

The Outskirts is a busy place. Roll a random encounter for every twelve hours the party is here.

### Politics
[Aventine](/world/places/aventine.md)'s houses all exert some influence here, but those who live in The Outskirts and don't threaten travel are left to their own. House Belos transports contraband through the hills and (if they're feeling bold) on the Shimmertrame.

## Travel
The Outskirts starts where [Aventine](/world/places/aventine.md) ends. Adventurers can walk from [Aventine](/world/places/aventine.md) to the northernmost borders of the region in two days, and to the east in two days. Roads vary from well-cared for (The Shimmertrame) to barely foot paths though the bogs and woods.

## Places
Random important places.

### The Farmsteads
Tiny farmsteads set up by families who struck out from [Aventine](/world/places/aventine.md) or the neighboring towns in search of a better life.

1. Gawkrogers: 6 halflings with a cozy set of hobbit holes. Led by Bildat Gawkroger.
2. Grubbs: Little known about them. Private, surly. Moved here recently. Led by Wandergrist Grubb. Live in huts made from sod.
3. Burrows: Oldest family here. 9 hobbits. Also hobbit holes. Led by a woman named Rosa.
4. Goodworts: Sharecroppers who just bought their way out of their contract w/ House Belos. Led by a cheerful male named Olo.

### The Peat Farmers
Tiny huts and determined people seeking fortunes from peat farming. It's hard work, but peat fetches a fine price in [Aventine](/world/places/aventine.md) and the smaller villages. The bogs are dangerous and often unstable, so the farmers live in sod huts on stilts.

5.  The Komar homestead (5 - humans), Brickbuckle clan (7 - dwarves), Greycarver family (9 - dwarves) and Underlakes (10 - hobbits) all protect each other while carving out a living, literally, from the peat bogs
6. Brickbuckle clan (10 people) is led by Armistead Brickbuckle, a dwarf who married Felicia Underlake (a hobbit). Both families now get along, as they have very little choice.
7. The Underlakes are hobbits (7 people) led by Melanie Underlake. Her daughter Felicia married Armistead.
8. Greycarvers led by Kusus Greycarver, an aging male dwarf farmer going way back. They've worked this land since he was younger - he brought them here.
9. The Komar homestead is a small family of five humans. They're new and just started. They most suffered from Nuvor's pranks.
10. Unknown, was the haunt of Nuvor Rabbit Slayer, a well-intended but mischevious faerie dragon.

### 18. Vadsund
A tiny, stockaded town of 75 people, including a small blacksmithy operated by Faelyn and Darunia Ilimaer (elves), a carpenter (Brezig, a half-ogre), Panker's Mercantile - a trading post operated by Panker and Wemebbaic (2 gnomes), a tiny shrine/temple of Pelor run by a half-elven cleric named Ameniel Qinan, and a half-elven Druid healer named Jelena. Jelena and Qinan do not get along. Also assorted townspeople. Characters will immediately notice the quality of construction, owing to the great work of Brezig and Faelyn.

Darunia is the defacto leader of the town.

* [Faeyln Ilimaer](https://www.dndbeyond.com/profile/portentint/characters/29045673): Elf Fighter Lvl 2. Faelyn is a scarred veteran of the Orc Wars.
* [Darunia Ilimaer](https://www.dndbeyond.com/profile/portentint/characters/29045937): Elf Bard Lvl 2. Darunia is a bard who has traveled as far as [Shamharan](/world/places/shamharan.md), and met Faelyn there.
* Brezig: Half Ogre Fighter Lvl 1. Brezig is a monster of a man, nearly the size of an ogre. He wears a set of tools and wields one of his hammers in combat.
* Panker & Wemebbaic: Gnome Artisans
* [Amenial Qinan](https://www.dndbeyond.com/profile/portentint/characters/29045380): Human Cleric Lvl 1. A haughty cleric of Pelor, Amenial has seen very little of the world. He wields a hammer and mace.
* [Jelena](https://www.dndbeyond.com/profile/portentint/characters/29046192): Half-Elf Druid Lvl 1. A half-elf druid with a shocking head of red hair, she left the Druidfel to explore the world and currently lives in Vadsund, making a living as a healer. She's mischevious and prone to teasing. which is one reason Qinan doesn't like her.

Other towns people: Just people.
