# Porth Môr
Coastal village: Population 2,500

Situated on a bay can only hold maybe 3-5 ships at a time.

Porth Môr is best known for making durable seafaring ships of small and medium size (can't build big ones as the port is not very big).  Part of their secret to the ships it the lighter in density wood they harvest from nearby trees that is also strong enough to build ocean bound ships.

Mostly Tortles, but being a port town there is a variety of races that do live there.  Maybe 50%-60% Tortles.

Porth Mor is mostly inhabited by Barbarian Tortles whom mate for life.

## Shipworks
The main industry is shipbuilding. Timber is brought in from the surrounding jungle, shaped into timbers, dried, and then made into the ribs of each ship. Planks then form the hull. The main shipwright is a female tortle named Kru. She's a very staid, sober woman who just wants to build cool ships. Her employees include a female apprentice Loxodon named Susnol.

Sails are stitched from a hemp-like cloth made in the village. The sailmaker is a gray elf named Dalyor. The Fall was a brutal wakeup call for him, and he's now a modest, craftsman who enjoys his work. He employs a mixed group humans and tortles.

## Foods
Porth Môr is reknowned for their citrus pies and baked goods, including a crusty, chewy egg bread.

## The Orphanage
The Drissel Home for Beautiful Children.

An orphanage down the road from Taiga's forge. Most of the children that live there are between the ages of 6 and 16 and became orphans after the Fall.

Cregyn and Taiga donate to the orphanage on a regular basis.

In addition to the tortles, there are a Tabaxi - Carabel (fur is patchy and missing in places w/ lighter skin left underneath) - and Loxodon child at the orphanage.

## Animal Sanctuary
Plo, an ancient male tortle, cares for a number of predators that were left without their territories during the Fall. Included are:

- An extremely grumpy giant crab named Tripod who lost all but three legs and hasn't grown them back
- A pair of giant toads named Bully and Wart
- A gigantic flying dinosaur with a nasty disposition. Named Thresh
- A 30-foot-long python (no name)

## Eateries/Inns
There isn't any inn or tavern per se. Meals are taken in a large communal hall in the center of the village. There are a few small eateries on boats in the harbor.
