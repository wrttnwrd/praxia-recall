# About H'Rare Nis
It's a group of four small islets separated by narrow bands of water. On one of them is a tiny, unnamed village that springs up right after the Gith hatch and leave, then is abandoned every 80 years before "the raiders" return.

Why? The island drifts through prime whaling grounds that would be extremely dangerous by boat. Villagers make a narrower crossing from Sillidria, then return 80 years later when the crossing narrows again. IN the interim, they can hunt from the stable "boats" that are the islands.

On the largest islet is the village. The only thing worth noting is a large-ish hut (being generous) called The Wilted Urchin. It serves watery beer and every kind of cold fish imaginable. The only hot dishes are whale bits and some kind of barely-edible bread-like thing.

In the middle of the village is a huge rendering pot that reeks of whale blubber, oil, and rotting meat.

H'rare Nis sits atop the Githyanki creche.

# H'Rare Nis Structure

## The Warrens
Under the tower is a series of caves that form the Githyanki creche. You can enter the caves from the bluffs around the island. That means **not** having to deal with the guards.

There are two ways into the tower from the caves: A secret door that opens into a secret room on the second floor, and a hatch that opens in the middle of the ground floor.

### The Tunnels
The tunnels are warm and dry. Significant effort has been made to keep the creche safe for the young.

H'Rare Nis's unique properties mean the island hovers over warm geothermal fonts much of its 100-year cycle. This warms the tunnels, which are cleverly designed to push cold air out.

### The Hatchery
A large bowl-shaped cavern with sand at the bottom. The cavern is at the bottom of the warrens. The sand is hot to the touch. There are a few remaining egg shells, but that's it.

Stepping onto the sands triggers an accidentally-created trap: The Fall cracked the basin on which H'Rare Nis rests. The sand in the hatchery is in place, but only barely. Any medium-sized creature stepping into the bowl will crack the floor. The sand will start draining out.

Every round, trapped characters must make a strength saving throw (DC 15) to avoid being pulled under the sand and eventually deposited in a deeper area of the warrens that connects directly to the Underdark.

Two failures means the character is pulled under. If this happens, normal drowning rules apply. The player must continue making STR saves. If they fail two more, they are pulled into the deeper warrens and take 3d6 bludgeoning damage in the process.

A character can try to help a trapped adventurer escape the sandfall: They can throw a rope (advantage), or leap into the trap (substituting their save for the trapped character, but at disadvantage as they try to haul two people out).

## First Floor: Entryway
Per the map, a narrow flight of stairs up to a locked portcullis. Human guards stand by with crossbows. If the players come up from the warrens they'll emerge from a grate in the floor, across from the guards.

## Second Floor:
Quarters, per the map. A desk with some papers - random notes and letters scrawled in Infernal (Arvid's second language) that talk about the deal he's struck w/ the Githyanki, including payment schedule. Also a calendar showing when the hatchery will next be used (97 years from now, or 101 FR).

Three of the Githyanki are here, practicing swordsmanship in close quarters. The players may attempt to slip past if they so desire.

## Third Floor:
The library and orery. Everything is trashed by water damage. The desk is locked and contains the schematics.

On the desk are numerous writings about The Engine, most of which confirm the characters' knowledge. The writings are in Jazelle's hand and trail off into insane scribbles after some time.

One side of the desk is a Mimic which, if someone touches any part of the desk, springs to life, thrashing about and making noise. If the Gith are still alive, this will bring them.
