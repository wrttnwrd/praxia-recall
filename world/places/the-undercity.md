# POI: The Undercity

Nation: Camadan
City: [Aventine](/world/places/aventine.md)
Established: 

!undercity-vaults.png
## Story
During the construction of Aventine's High Ward and other areas, the dwarven builders installed a series of tunnels and rooms under the city. 

This complex is mostly dry, clean tunnels and rooms used to store construction materials and equipment during the build. Some tunnels are now treacherous, badly damaged during The Fall. Others are in good shape.

[The Shadow Veil](/world/groups/the-shadow-veil.md) is based in the Undercity, as are [the Vaults](/world/places/the-vaults.md).
