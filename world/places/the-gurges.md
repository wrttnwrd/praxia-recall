The Gurges: The falls from the end of the Helgoril to The Dock Of Fallen Cities. 

Related: [Silgorin](/world/places/silgorin.md)

Gurges effects do not apply in the town of Silgorin.

Treat the Gurges as a Lair. There are random small buildings and ledges, and folks traveling through the area come under certain effects:

## Lair Traits
The following effects are active throughout the tower unless otherwise noted.

### The Call of the Styx
The thunder of the falls carries fractured memories and half-forgotten names.

- Any creature that **starts its turn within 20 feet of an open edge, shaft, or balcony** must succeed on a **DC 17 Wisdom saving throw** or suffer disadvantage on its **next attack roll,skill check or saving throw** before the end of its next dungeon or combat turn.
- On a failure by **5 or more**, the creature is also **Frightened** of the edge until the end of its next turn.

Creatures immune to being frightened still suffer the disadvantage.

---

### Necrotic Saturation
Negative energy bleeds upward from the Styx and soaks the stone around The Gurges.

- When a creature regains hit points from a spell, it regains the **minimum possible amount** for each die rolled.
- Undead have **advantage on their first attack roll each round**.
- If a creature is returned to life by *revivify* or similar magic, it gains one **short-term madness** (DMG) as fragments of foreign memories cling to it.

---

### The Land Leans
All surfaces near  subtly twists toward the falls.

- Ranged weapon and spell attacks made beyond **30 feet** suffer **half cover**, unless the attacker is firing downward.
- A flying creature that ends its turn in the tower must succeed on a **DC 15 Strength (Athletics) or Dexterity (Acrobatics) check** or drift **10 feet toward the nearest open edge**.

---

## Lair Actions
Once per dungeon turn, the falls takes one action. The same lair action can’t be used two turns in a row.

---

### Bonewake Surge
Fragments of bone and funerary debris erupt from the stone floor.

- The lair creates up to **three 10-foot-radius areas** of difficult terrain it can see.
- A creature that enters one of these areas for the first time on a turn or starts its turn there must succeed on a **DC 18 Dexterity saving throw** or be **Restrained** until the end of its next turn.

---

### Styxward Pull
The waterfall surges unnaturally, drawing creatures toward oblivion.

- One creature within **60 feet of an open edge** must make a **DC 14 Strength saving throw**.
- On a failure, the creature is pulled **15 feet toward the edge**.
- On a failure by **5 or more**, the creature is also **knocked prone**.

A creature that goes over the edge does not immediately die; instead, it vanishes into mist, roar, and memory (see *Falling into the Gurges*).

---

### Echoes of the Drowned
Spectral figures rise from the mist, whispering accusations.

- Up to **two creatures** the lair can see must succeed on a **DC 15 Wisdom saving throw** or become **Haunted** until the end of their next turn.
- While Haunted, a creature has:
  - Disadvantage on Concentration checks
  - No benefit from Inspiration
  - Hallucinatory visions of the dead addressing them directly

---


### Negative Energy Backwash
A surge of necrotic force rolls upward from below.

- Each living creature of the lair’s choice takes **1d10 necrotic damage**.
- Undead instead regain that many hit points.
- The lair’s master regains **10 hit points per creature damaged this way** (maximum 30).

---

## Environmental Features

### The Open Shaft
The falls descend a huge open shaft.

- The shaft drops hundreds of feet into roaring mist.
- Creatures falling into the shaft experience:
  - Fragmented memories not their own
  - Visions of past failures or lost loved ones
  - The sensation of being judged

A creature that falls may:
- Land on a narrow ledge
- Be intercepted by Styx-touched undead
- Be carried into the River Styx itself

---

## Falling into the Gurges
A creature that falls into the waterfall is not immediately slain.

Possible outcomes (DM choice or roll):
- The creature survives but returns **changed**
- The creature is lost and presumed dead
- The creature becomes a future antagonist
- The creature returns bearing knowledge it should not have

---

## Regional Effects
The region within **1 mile** of the Gurges is warped by its presence.

- Corpses do not rot normally
- Voices echo when no one speaks
- Creatures dream of falling water and forgotten names
- The dead sometimes answer questions they were never asked
