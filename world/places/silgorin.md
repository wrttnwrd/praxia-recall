## **Silgorin**

Perched on the basalt rim above **[The Gurges](/world/places/the-gurges.md)**, **Silgorin** is a place where elves and devils coexist. Though only 7 years old - Silgorin formed right after [The Fall](/world/events/the-fall.md), it seems far older. Magic and temporal forces at [The Gurges](/world/places/the-gurges.md) affect the passage of time.

It is all that remains of the original city of Silgorin, high seat of the Temple of Amoth, now known as [Silgorin Below](/world/places/silgorin-below.md).

The devils are mostly Arcanaloths, Abishai, Imps, and other devils focused on acquisition of souls. The devils of Silgorin are generally there because they're down on their luck and looking to gain favor. They're ruthless opportunists, willing to live even alongside elves if it means they can gain a modicum of power.

The elves here tend to be jaded pragmatists. They're here to restrain the devils. Many are also here to research [The Gurges](/world/places/the-gurges.md) and see if they can figure out a way to stop them.

Read to the players:

> As you approach from the East, all around you is black, glassy stone and coarse bedrock - the ruins of Amoth's fall. A roaring fills your ears. It is the Gurges: All the waters of the Helgoril, draining from the great ocean, thunder out of sight through the massive crater created in the great god's fall. You're still a quarter mile away, but you can see the Western rim of the crater, shrouded in mist. 
Perched on that rim is an improbable city: A single stone edifice, clinging both above and below the top of the crater. The buildings almost blend into the surrounding stone. It appears hacked from the very bedrock itself: A cluster of buildings, towers, streets and stairs that crawl down the cliffside and across a series of spans that leap across the chasm of the Gurges. At the near end of the spans are docks, and at those docks hover unnatural craft: These are the vessels that ply the waters of the River Styx by plunging down the falls.
A little distance from the city, across a flat expanse of rock, a single, forbidding tower seems to lean towards you. The Tower Of Obaris, the necromancer, menaces everything it sees.

### **The Culture of Silgorin**

- **Twin Councils** govern the settlement —  
    the **Ivory Chorus** (elves of containment)  
    and the **Brazen Compact** (devils of contract ).  
    Each may veto the other, which keeps the town balanced on a knife-edge.

- Children born in Silgorin — tieflings   


### **Why They Stay**

- The elves believe Silgorin is the **last promise** they owe to the Helgoril — to watch the place where the river ends, even if it now ends in Hell.
- The devils believe Silgorin is a **foothold**, a laboratory of mortal weakness and metaphysical pressure centered on a wound created in [The Fall](/world/events/the-fall.md). Some also believe they can expand and extend their power here, potentially recruiting troops to the Blood War and [Avernus](/world/places/avernus.md).

### **Tensions & Fault Lines**

- Some devils lobby to move further onto [Essedria](/world/places/essedria.md), with others arguing they're already overextended
- Some elves research ways correct the flow of the Helgoril and close [The Gurges](/world/places/the-gurges.md)
- Some tieflings want the town to tear away from both cultures and become their own
- Looming over the town is [The Tower Of Obaris](/world/places/tower-of-obaris.md), from which Obaris, a twisted elvish necromancer, exerts her influence. Obaris doesn't **control** Silgorin. She's not sufficiently engaged for that. But she is its most powerful resident, and denizens on both sides fear and resent her.

### What about time?
It's only 7 years since The Fall. How did this city spring into being, complete with children born of elf and devil?

The city's proximity to the River Styx and Avernus means time passes differently here. The 7 objective years since The Fall passed as 50 subjective years in Silgorin. That doesn't mean time is always passing faster here. It's just... malleable.

## People
1. Valthus The Quill - elven lawyer in [Silgorin](/world/places/silgorin.md). She wants the best possible deal, and profit
2. Voleth Of The Fog - stealth boat driver on the Styx. [Merrenoloth](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/2560873-merrenoloth), but with 250 hp, AC 20, all save DCs 18, saves are +9, skills are +6, resistances are to magical and non-magical, prof bonus +6. Oar attack does 25 fire damage. Creepy AF. Long arms that seem to stretch out. 
	1. Voleth either wants a soul coin, or they want the party to steal a soul coin from the necromancer Obaris. They won't say why, but Obaris obtained a ride down the Styx, then refused to pay. Voleth isn't strong enough to exact punishment.
	2. Voleth is **the captain** to hire. They're stealthy, powerful, and they've never, ever lost a passenger. Nor are they types to lawyer up. 
3. Essentius The Ferryman - a lower-level Merrenoloth who can take the party along the river. They'll do so for 1000 platinum or a soul coin. Use Essentius and the party will face skill checks on INT, WIS, and Survival to successfully hire and navigate the Styx.
