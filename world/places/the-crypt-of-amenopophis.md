His living and corporeal servants feed the flesh and essence of the many
interred throughout the crypt into the Loom — a massive construct between
levels 3, 4 and 5 — which stitches material back onto his form. He is, for
lack of a better description, being un-mummified into a new and more perfect
form. There are the ghosts of thousands of humanoids down there. It is no
wonder the Raven Queen hates the place.
