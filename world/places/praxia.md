## The Founding
Four primal forces woven together comprise the fundamental elements of Praxia:

Divine (power from the gods)
Arcane (the magical world)
Nature (the natural world)
Flux (chaos)

Each competes with the other. None are good or evil. 

Read [the Tome Of The First Gods](/world/things/tome-of-the-first-gods.md) for the full mythology.
## Dimensions
Praxia is 24000 miles in circumference.
It is roughly 80% water, as far as anyone knows. 

## Calendar
Fall Reckoning (FR) where 0 is The Fall.

BR is Before Fall. 350 BR is 350 years before The Fall.

Praxian culture refers to the event as The Catastrophe because most don’t believe it was the Fall of Amoth, instead attributing it to a natural disaster or one intentionally inflicted upon them by the gods. But they still use FR.

Weeks are 7 days long. Months are 4 weeks. There are 10 months.

### Holidays
2/35 (Winterclaw 35) - Winterguise - celebration of getting through the coldest parts of winter. Costume parade through Hull Ward. Some costumes designed to scare children. If the children get scared, the "guisers" give them sweets to make them feel better. Of course, the kids run around squealing and pretending they're scared...
