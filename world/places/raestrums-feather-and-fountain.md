# Rastrum's Feather & Fountain

## Stationary Store & Tattoo Shop
Dark lacquered unassuming 2 story building with big windows on each side of the recessed doorway. Located down a infrequently used alley, across from a Butcher Shop. (diagon alley-esq)

## Wooden Sign
Wooden Sign hangs outside depicting an Inkwell and a Fist gripping a rolled-up Scroll and a dripping Quill. It also has a small forest silhouetted by a moon behind it in the corner.
## Tattoo Chair
Wooden Leather-covered Tattoo Chair in one of the front windows.

## Stationery Supplies
Cabinets line the right and left walls (glassed upper cabinets, drawers below) containing Ink, Parchment, Writing Supplies, a few common Books, etc. and there is a small table in the other window opposite the Tattoo Chair.
## Betula Scrolls
The Feather & Fountain specializes in hard to find Betula Scrolls made from Betula "living wood" birch trees. These scrolls are made from the bark of Betula trees and can only be harvested once per year, in the Spring, without killing the tree it comes from. The bark is more durable than normal parchment or paper and prized by Academics and Mages alike.
Unseen Servant: An unseen servant is tied to the shop and performs small in-store servces like copyng books and fulfilling tattoos.
## "The Scrolls"
There are 3 ornate scrolls behind the counter. One can copy text from a scroll or book nearby into it's page. It will disappear after a little bit. Another you can write and receve messages from the Order on. The 3rd scroll...?

## Retail Counter
Retail Counter in back next to a chello and a swinging door leading into the backroom/hallway.

## Backroom
Backroom contains some additional supplies for the store. There are also stairs leading up to living area on 2nd floor, and a hidden set leading down to the basement.

## Nib's Bedroom 
(2nd floor): Nib has a simple bedroom on the 2nd floor with a bed, small table and chair, and wardrobe.

## Library
(2nd floor): A simple room with bookshelves on 3 walls, and a simple table with 2 chairs along the 4th wall, 2 brown leather chairs in 2 corners opposite the room from the table.
## Spare Bedroom
(2nd floor): There is a room on the 2nd floor with a window overlooking the storefront and ally with 2 small beds, a table and chairs.

## Stockroom
(basement): A stone walled basement room filled with shelves, extra/rare supplies and 2 doors.

## Music room
(basement): The stone walled music room (keeps sound down to a minimum) containing a chair, ornate chello and a few other music instruments.
## Office/Vault (basement)
The Office (vault) contains a desk, 3 safes, and The Big Book with pages made from Betula "living wood" birch tree bark..

## Nib Rastrum
Proprietor: Humanoid??, Male. His arms are a little longer than average, and he has slender rough hands, with callused fingers and ink-stained tips.
Tattooist: Small tattoos on his exposed arms and hands.
Musician: An proficient musician and composer, he is practiced in several instruments and specializes in string instruments.
Order Member: Member of the Order of the Unsune Silvanus
Mage/Bard??: Cantrips: Mending, Prestidigitation, Vicious Mockery, L1: Comprehend Languages, Detect Magic, Dissonant Whispers, Identify, Speak with Animals, Unseen Servant
Helper: Nib employees a helper to deliver orders and run errands.
