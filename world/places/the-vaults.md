# POI: The Vaults

Nation: Camadan
City: [Aventine](/world/places/aventine.md)
Established: 5 FR

## Story
Created in [The Undercity](/world/places/the-undercity.md) by [The Shadow Veil](/world/groups/the-shadow-veil.md) (see page for more details), with some help from Feyndal and House Umbra.

The vaults are a series of rooms, each with its own unique set of safeguards. 

The closest entrance is [Uppercuts](/world/places/uppercuts.md), through a secret door in the back.
!undercity-vaults.png
### Entryway
Accessing the Vaults themselves isn't trivial. They're deep within [The Undercity](/world/places/the-undercity.md), protected by [The Shadow Veil](/world/groups/the-shadow-veil.md). Guthra G'ul takes his clients very seriously, and the entryway has four guards, plus two Earth Elementals sleeping under the floor. 
!Pasted image 20230220163806.png

If someone triggers the alarm, more senior members of The Veil will arrive within a few minutes. 

There are also two Earth Elemental Guardians patrolling the walls, floor, and ceiling. If anything attempts to sneak in, their tremorsense ability detects it.

!Pasted image 20230327165603.png
### The Mimic
The most important vault (party unknown) the first on the right as you enter the vaults. The door is actually a [Gargantuan Mimic](https://www.5esrd.com/database/creature/mimic-gargantuan/).
!Pasted image 20230219121245.png

At least one person entering the chamber must have a Veil Medallion. Otherwise, the first person to attempt to open the door is immediately attacked.

This vault has a small table at the center. On the table is a small locked, trapped chest. In the chest is a small deck of cards. The deck is a Deck Of Many Things.

If the alarm is triggered for this vault, there is an immediate, overwhelming response of at least 4 Drow guards. Every 4 rounds, another guard appears. After 12 rounds, G'ul himself appears. 

### Bournier Cartel Treasure Room
This treasure room holds the [Bournier Cartel](/world/groups/bournier-cartel.md)'s ill-gotten gains.

Complex trap.

- If you don't have a Mediallion, once you enter the room, the following happens (roll initiative):
	- Alarm activates
	1. Initiative 20: Doors slam shut and lock. Two locks, DC 25 each lock
	2. Initiative 20: Jets activate. (hissing sound) 
	3. Initiative 20: Party turns slightly blue (wears off in an hour). DC15 PER to notice
	4. Initiative 20: Hissing sound increases
	5. Initiative 20: Hissing sound gets even louder
	6. Initiative 20: The entire party is turned bright, flourescent blue for one week. Only a remove curse or greater restoration can remove the color before then
	7. Initiative 20: Jets reactivate with sleep gas. 300 HP worth
	8. If the guards at the guard station are still there, they show up after 10 rounds (1 minute)
	9. If not, then a larger force shows up after 2 minutes

#### Disabling The Trap
1. Open the Doors. Opening the doors is the quickest way to circumvent the trap, but they are warded with magic. To open the doors, a character must first succeed on a DC 20 Wisdom ([Perception](https://www.dndbeyond.com/compendium/rules/basic-rules/using-ability-scores#Perception)) check to find the locking mechanism. A successful DC 20 Intelligence ([Arcana](https://www.dndbeyond.com/compendium/rules/basic-rules/using-ability-scores#Arcana)) check is then required to disable the sphere of force that surrounds the lock ([dispel magic](https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/dispel-magic) is ineffective against it). Success on a DC 20 Dexterity check using thieves’ tools picks the lock. Finally, a successful DC 20 Strength ([Athletics](https://www.dndbeyond.com/compendium/rules/basic-rules/using-ability-scores#Athletics)) check is needed to push the door open. Each check requires an action
2. Disable the jets. There are six jets, two on each wall. A player can make a Thieves Tools roll (DC20) to close ONE jet. Tinkering can do the same. Must be proficient, or at disadvantage
3. If the players damage the jets for more than 50hp, the jets immediately vent, filling the room w/ whichever gas is currently being administered

### Other Rooms

The rooms hold everything from books to gold to:

1. Several people who were turned to stone by a Gorgon. They're there for safekeeping until the family can raise the gold to re-animate them
2. Someone's doll collection (seriously)
3. A gorgeous sailing yacht
4. Four empty power units similar to what drives the Villain's Discontent
5. The mimic

All rooms are triple-locked, requiring three DC 20 thieves tools checks to open. Then any traps must also be disarmed.
