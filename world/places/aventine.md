# Aventine

Population (0 FR): 500,000 plus 15,000 elven refugees.
Economy: Roughly 31 million gp/year (in circulation)

Founded by Ahmad Azari when he journeyed north from [Salmuir](/world/places/salmuir.md) in -300 FR

Current prince: Feyd Azari
Daughter: Fidelia Azari

City Standard: White ship on a blue background.

## The Houses
The seven houses extend their influence across all of Camadan but are based in Aventine.

### The Royal House
Feyd Azari: Prince
Fidelia Azari: His eldest
Siliib Azari: His son by his second wife
First wife: Fatimah Azari - died during a plague
Second wife: Aisha Azari (still alive)
Domestic Advisor: Xavis (Zavvis) (tall, thin human wearing simple, rumpled robes - actually the spymaster) (beautiful teeth)

### Bluestrike
Location: Merchant Ward
Famous members: Oriana Valtova, [Grimshaw Shadowplank](/world/people/grimshaw-shadowplank.md), Eledra (Drow), Mevvic, Lumpkin
Leader: Osdryn Bluestrike (half-elf)
Trappings: None

The oldest of all of the houses, Bluestrike was formed by the wealthy half-elf founders of Aventine. Bluestrike is a house of merchants and sailors. They comprise most of the Aventine merchant marine and traders. Shrewd businessmen and explorers, Bluestrike can be found in most major cities around the world. Bluestrike is by far the wealthiest house in Camadan.

_Mevvic was the secret head of Aventine's Illithid Cult, the Cult Of Eight. He died during the-engine_.

### Highfury
Location: Axiat
Famous members: Gart, Angus Bloodstone, Marta Valtova
Leader: Bataar Highfury
Trappings: A brooch or pin with a crossed sword and axe overtop a shield

House Highfury formed out of war with Fel House. Kragen Highfury united the other houses against the necromancers, eventually defeating them at the battle of Swords’ Cross in Aventine itself. 

This house is both martial and pragmatic. They form alliances as they feel necessary to preserve the balance between all houses. They have financial interests in the Aventine docks, maintenance of the city walls and most of the military infrastructure. Most of the highest officers in the Aventine military come from House Highfury, although they have to surrender house affiliation when they assume their posts.


### House Umbra
See separate file.

### House Belos
Location: Hull Ward
Crime syndicate of Camadan.
Famous members: Tasha Quan, Panovar Flynt, Marceline
Leader: Tasha Quan
Trappings: Gray cloaks

Started in -300 Fall Reckoning by Eldari Belos, House Belos oversees "legitimate" crime in Aventine and throughout the kingdom of Camadan. The House is particularly influential in Aventine. Belos is key to the smooth flow of commerce within and through the walls upon which city depends.

Belos does what the city guard cannot: They keep crime organized, protecting citizens from turf wars and violent crime while managing an appropriate level of graft and larceny.

House Belos does not sit on the council, but makes their wishes known via proxies and communication with the other houses.


### House Drace
Location: The Docks
Artisan's guild of Camadan
Famous members: Artemis Hasp
Leader: A foul-mouthed gnome named Parwairt
Trappings: Blue Apron With The Aventine Crest

Iridian Drace founded this house in FR -150, although rumors trace its roots back to the earliest dwarven kingdoms of [Essedria](/world/places/essedria.md).

Craftsmen to a one, House Drace is smaller than most. Their members do everything from building ships and creating armor to laying pavers and working on the city walls. They do more complex projects under the city as well. 

## Other Organizations

### Fel House
Location: Underground in the Docks neighborhood.

Since the war with the other houses in BR 95, no one really knows much about this house. Rumors abound of dark doings: Aventinians mutter darkly of necromancy, Set worship and sacrifices. But Fel House serves The Raven Queen.

_About the war: In BR95 Vek Azar and her acolytes attempted to create The Monster. Other houses found out and began a ten year campaign to destory Fel House. Eventually, Azar struck a truce w/ the others and ceased her work._

It is run by the powerful necromancer Vek Azar. She has kept her house small and secretive to avoid a repeat of the Ten Years War. While short of temper and extremely protective of her power, she is not evil.

Vek is a thin, white-skinned human woman with the emblem of the house tattooed on her left wrist. She has short, silver hair. She wears a choker of small black stones and several plain silver bands around her fingers. Her ears are pierced and adorned with multiple simple studs and chains.

The building is a simple, one-story house with a larger complex of offices deep underneath. Players descend into the main areas via a float tube.

The entire building is decorated in grays and blacks. Raven symbology is everywhere.

Leader: Vek Azar

Trappings: A small tattoo of the head of a raven

## Military
The Aventine military is a standing army of 10,000. It can raise an army of another 50,000.

The city-state is a major military power.

It’s split into the navy and land army.

The navy includes 50 ships of war of varying sizes that carry an average of 50 people each, including marines.

The army is divided as follows:

* The Magicians Guard are warlocks, sorcerers and wizards numbering 100
* The Clerics Guard are Clerics. Total: 500
* The rest are as follows:
  - 2 legions of roughly 4500, each led by a Major
  - Each legion is separated into 10 cohorts of 400. Each cohort is led by a captain
  - Each cohort is separated into 5 centuries of 80 each. Each century is led by a Lt.
  - Each century is separated into 10 units averaging 8 people led by a sergeant.
  - The remaining people are leadership, etc.

Different cohorts have different purposes: Mounted, light, heavy, etc. In combat, various magicians and clerics get assigned to cohorts.

The Absolvers bring together varied talents.

### Axiat
Places of government, the central hospital, and the Brimming Tankard.

Also the central guard, and Aventine University.

#### The Palace
More a municipal complex The Palace does include the Prince's living spaces, as well as the Conclave Extent (the meeting area of the eight houses).

##### The Conclave Extent
A large circular chamber with a central speaking podium. Each of the houses has a reserved spot.

#### The University Of Aventine
A center of education for all of [Silidria](/world/places/silidria.md), the University is a major institution and the focus of Axiat. Its proximity to the government and hospital make it easy for students to study with the best of the professions. The various houses recruit from there.

The campus currently includes 800 students and 300 staff (teachers and others). It can hold up to 2,000 students and 800 staff. It's comprised of five "quads" surrounded by a low, 4-foot wall that serves as a border more than a security barrier. The gates are always open. Just inside the gates is small administration building. There is a basic mess hall and rooms for up to 500 students. Most live in apartments in other parts of the city. The rest of the campus is comprised of classrooms and laboratories.

**There is no magical training at the University.** Students can learn medicine, mathematics, history, writing, and other mundane topics.

Trades are generally taught by artisans in the city, and by the major houses.

The school has seen better days. The grounds were once meticulously kept lawns, trees and meandering paths. The post-Fall climate has reduced most of this to mud, a few stunted trees and crushed rock walkways to keep pedestrians' boots relatively dry.

The University also one of the greatest surviving libraries in the world. While it doesn't rival House Umbra for magical knowledge, it surpasses all others for history.

The only competing library is the Remnishir in Salmuir.

Teachers wear robes of deep blue to match the standard of Aventine.

The University is tightly woven into the fabric of the city politically, economically, and diplomatically. While Aventinian leadership tries to stay hands-off, they still intervene if matters on the campus go awry. One example was the student demonstrations against House Umbra in 3 FR, when students threatened to leave the campus and attempt to storm the House. The city guard stopped the students at the University gates.

Before [The Fall](/world/events/the-fall.md) University tuition was 2,500gp/year - a princely sum. Since [the Fall](/world/events/the-fall.md), the Prince has financed the school from the city coffers and offering students a stipend of 20gp/month. feeling that they need to train a new intelligensia and concerned that many youths were staying home to help support their families. Admissions, however, are difficult, and students must a pass a battery of exams to be accepted.

A center of higher learning for all Silidria. A cluster of stone buildings at the southern edge of Axiat. Utilitarian. Big library. No magic education or tinkering.

### Taelian (TAIL-e-in)
Also called Diaspora by some in the city.

No longer an encampment, this is a large community outside the walls of Aventine with its own small standing militia, temples, a market, and the other trappings of a community of 10,000+ citizens.

Taelian presents some unique challenges for the rest of Aventine. Without it, it faces an influx of ten thousand refugees. With it, they have an unsecured small city immediately outside their walls. Enemies can assemble there, recruit, or use the city as a base of operations.

The elves are more than capable of keeping Taelian secure, but decades of mistrust between elves, humans and half-elves mean the neighbors are still uncomfortable with each other.

Led by Elethar (female elf, 1500 years old)

## The Underground
Under Aventine is a network of tunnels. These are partly drainage and sewers, partly left from the city's construction, and partly created to connect major buildings. Some of the tunnels are extremely secret, known only by unsanctioned criminals and assassins. Guthra G'hul currently controls most of the tunnels.

Known tunnels connect the drainage system of High Ward with the older temples, the Naptha Caves with Sword's Cross & Axiat (bricked up), and the Merchant Ward with Hull Ward. A tunnel runs from The Green Slaad to House Belos and out of the city.

Some of the tunnels are downright civilized, with signage and stairs. Citizens use them to move from place to place in inclement weather. Others are dripping and dilapated, barely qualifying as sewers. Players may run into typical denizens of sewers and dark places, or they may encounter the Sicarii and Guthra G'hul's retainers. Smugglers and other criminals move into and out of the city using the tunnels, so players using the tunnels near The Green Slaad may encounter them as well.

You enter the caves through a jagged crack in the cliff face. The crack is partly-melted rock on the bottom with jagged stone along the top.

The caves smell faintly of tar and mold. Skeletons of small animals lie about. You can hear the sound of the surf below as it pounds on the bluffs below the city._
