## **[Silgorin](/world/places/silgorin.md) Below**
[Silgorin](/world/places/silgorin.md) Below is the original city, a largely destroyed ruin suspended above the battleground of Avernus. It is a blasted ruin.

> **Flavor Text** A blasted ruin of a city lies before you. Lit by the infernal fires below and the ghastly skies of Avernus above, shrouded in vapor from the River Styx and The Gurges, the city is shrouded in dim, orange-red light that barely illuminates the crumbled buildings and rubble-strewn streets. All around you echoes the sounds of combat, and as you watch, swarms giant, insectoid fiends do battle in the sky, and well-organized units of devils cross swords with hordes of gibbering demons.
> Six massive, iron chains, studded with wicked spikes, stretch from the city to the surface of Avernus. Each chain has a winch attached to the surface of the city where swarms of Lemures turn huge, groaning cranks. Slowly but inexorably, [Silgorin](/world/places/silgorin.md) Below is being drawn into the River Styx.

During The Fall, Asmodeus had Zariel trap the city and prevent it from plunging into the River Styx. He needs souls for the Blood War, and Silgorin could supply thousands.

The city is now chained to the surface of [Avernus](/world/places/avernus.md) by huge chains. The chains are comprised of 10-foot-by-30-foot iron rings bristling with blood-soaked spikes. They're attached to enormous winches turned by [swarms of Lemures](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195226-swarm-of-lemures). 

Demonic forces have figured out Zariel's plan and are attacking the winches, attempting to reverse Silgorin's fall while they exterminate the residents of the city.

The city has turned into a battleground, with demons attacking the citizenry and devils attempting to corrupt those same citizens while also battling the demons. It's complete chaos.

Zariel has recruited Enthraxis, an [Ultraloth](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195244-ultroloth) general, to lead infernal forces in [Silgorin](/world/places/silgorin.md) Below. Enthraxis's job is to (ironically) defend the mortals while corrupting as many of them as possible. They have employed two [Bone Devils](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5194930-bone-devil) as lieutenants, who then lead regimented units of [Bearded Devils](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5194920-bearded-devil).

## Layout

The city is a hub-and-spoke with The Commons at the center. The largest "spoke" was once a great boulevard, now strewn with rubble of collapsed buildings. At one end of that boulevard was the Temple Of Amoth, which cracked in half when the city was dragged into Avernus. That is now the site of the Temple of Asmodeus. 

Opposite the Temple of Asmodeus is the City Armory.

The Commons was a park with a huge tree at the center of the city. The tree is now a [Gulthias Blight](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195067-gulthias-blight).

Streets are a series of concentric rings with other "spoke" streets extending through them. Where well-organized, cleanly-layed-out neighborhoods once lay is only ruin. 

From above, the city appears to be a floating irregular disk of stone with cracked and crumbling stone at the edges and the six massive chains attached to huge bolts evenly distributed around the perimeter.

## POIs
Temple of Asmodeus: Built on the remains of the Temple Of Amoth. It is a well-organized set of buildings constructed by swarms of devils shortly after [the Fall](/world/events/the-fall.md). They are the only buildings that don't appear damaged or scarred by the chaos around them.

City Armory: The only other structure still standing. Many of the city's original residents have taken shelter here, hoping against hope for rescue. 

The Commons: A wide open space that lies at the "hub" of each "spoke" in the wheel-shaped layout of the city. The most intense devil-demon fighting is happening here.
