The Whispering Abyss is a deep, still, saltwater lake located just west of [Ironstead](/world/places/ironstead.md) and Nigh Darul, in the Underdark.

The lake is extremely salty, and things float easily. 

It once connected directly to The Gap by an underground river that opened into the surface sea, but was cut off during [The Fall](/world/events/the-fall.md).

That trapped [Leviathus](/world/people/leviathus.md), The Abyssal Maw. [Leviathus](/world/people/leviathus.md) was first attracted to the area by the presence of one of [The Seals](/world/things/the-seals.md) (Life, or Lida). The Fall has trapped it there. It is a [https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/16940-kraken](Kraken) lost to legend and time. 

For all their power, [Leviathus](/world/people/leviathus.md) cannot escape without help.
