# places

* [Atley](atley.md) - A dwindling town on Nova Zuros, steadily diminished by Morwenna's feedings; most of it now abandoned.
* [Aventine](aventine.md) - City-state of 500,000 plus 15,000 elven refugees, founded -400 FR by Ahmad Azari; seat of the seven houses.
* [Avernus](avernus.md) - The layer of the Nine Hells closest to the Prime Material, ruled by the fallen angel Zariel; entered by the Fields of Bone portal or by sailing the Helgoril down into the Styx.
* [Axiat](axiat.md) - Aventine's government district — the Palace, the central hospital, the guard, and the University.
* [Bedlam's Faire](bedlams-faire.md) - A faire whose proprietor, the hag Essie Darksinger, is also its prisoner.
* [Camadan](camadan.md) - The kingdom containing Aventine; ruled from the city by the Azari princes.
* [Dirion](dirion.md) - A Norse-influenced stone town of up to 10,000 upriver of Aventine, living on quarry, masonry and timber.
* [East Godelis](east-godelis.md) - A barren island whose western slope holds the cave stair down to the Underdark; site of the Chaos/Flux seal.
* [Essedria](essedria.md) - Continent devastated by Amoth's impact; ash waste, ent tree-farms, the Field of Bone.
* [H'Rare Nis](h-rare-nis.md) - Four small islets that drift through prime whaling grounds; their village is settled and abandoned on an eighty-year cycle as the crossing from Silidria narrows.
* [Hale's Ales](hales-ales.md) - A small, quiet tavern in Atley with four rooms to let; everyone there seems on edge.
* [House Umbra](house-umbra.md) - The house of magical knowledge; holds Seals for RBG and the Tome Of The First Gods.
* [Hull Ward](hull-ward.md) - Aventine's oldest neighbourhood, built from the upturned hulls of river barges; policed in practice by House Belos.
* [Hull Ward Bakery](hull-ward-bakery.md) - Hadigak's bakery, partnered with Lumpkin to sell meat between two slices of bread.
* [Ironstead](ironstead.md) - Deep gnome city besieged by Duergar who want the Tome Of The Ancient Gods removed from its library.
* [Krag M'goth](krag-mgoth.md) - The orc city whose elders planned the southward expansion that settled Urdun; the Avernus expedition went to disrupt incursions into it.
* [Lumpkin's Fine Foods](lumpkins-fine-foods.md) - Lumpkin's Hull Ward expansion, just inside the Eastern Gate, adjoining the old butcher shop.
* [Lumpkin's Meats](lumpkin-s-meats.md) - The old butcher shop in Hull Ward, run by Lumpkin's family for generations; supplies most of the meat for the palace and the military.
* [Lumpkin's Warehouse](lumpkins-warehouse.md) - Lumpkin's warehouse on the Docks, where supplies come off the keelboats and traders before reaching the Hull Ward shop.
* [Merchant Ward](merchant-ward.md) - The merchants' quarter; House Bluestrike's seat.
* [Mistwharf](mistwharf.md) - Ruined town where Agrona Sef and Celdastrus ambushed RBG and Rikterr first died.
* [Nigh Darûl](nigh-darul.md) - Duergar city in the Underdark, five days' ride from Ironstead; Zerla Pelletweaver lives here.
* [Nova Zuros](nova-zuros.md) - A flying island raised around a skystone by the wizard Ephiane, which later fell and killed everyone on it; Morwenna lairs beneath Atley on top of it.
* [Oar's Rest](oars-rest.md) - A settlement counted among Aventine's allies; Blong Hrusto kept a bar here before moving to the city.
* [Porthmoor](porthmoor.md) - Tortle shipbuilding village of 2,500 at the southern tip of Silidria, renowned for its durable small ships and its citrus pies; site of the Arcane seal.
* [Praxia](praxia.md) - The world. Its calendar resets at The Fall, dividing history into BR and FR.
* [Raestrums Feather And Fountain](raestrums-feather-and-fountain.md) - A landmark of Aventine, named among the city's principal sites.
* [Renney's Remedies](rennys-remedies.md) - Purveyor of potions and salves in Hull Ward.
* [Ringling's Collectibles](ringlings-collectibles.md) - Magic items, scrolls and sundries in Hull Ward; a 5% chance any given item is stolen.
* [Salmuir](salmuir.md) - City of 130,000 under the Haddad Combine; pre-Fall a rigid caste system with grey elves at the top.
* [Saltreach](saltreach.md) - The town at the edge of the Fauces.
* [Shadowmuir](shadowmuir.md) - A Domain of Dread created by The Fall — the Shadowfell mirror of Salmuir, run by the Obsidian Wing.
* [Shamharan](shamharan.md) - The nation on Silidria whose capital is Salmuir; the Blighted Haunts lie in its jungles.
* [Silgorin](silgorin.md) - A city of elves and devils clinging to the basalt rim above the Gurges; seven years old but seeming far older, because the Gurges bends time.
* [Silgorin Below](silgorin-below.md) - The buried original city — once the high seat of the Temple of Amoth.
* [Silidria](silidria.md) - The continent holding Camadan and the city-state of Aventine.
* [Sword's Cross](swords-cross.md) - Home to part of Aventine's standing army; site of the battle that ended the war with Fel House.
* [Taelian](taelian.md) - A community of 10,000+ elven refugees outside the walls, led by Elethar; also called Diaspora.
* [Temple of Pelor (Hull Ward)](temple-of-pelor-hull-ward.md) - Recently founded in Hull Ward by Venkat, tolerated by the residents.
* [The Bastion](rbg-bastion.md) - RBG's manor, pub and stables grown from a seedling Drak planted in the Post-Thargorax downtime; much of it lies in an extradimensional space.
* [The Blighted Haunts](the-blighted-haunts.md) - A blighted stretch of Shamharan's jungle outside Salmuir, under regional effects created by Amenopophis's long residence.
* [The Brimming Tankard](the-brimming-tankard.md) - A tavern in Axiat, owned by Angus Bloodstone.
* [The Cauldron](the-cauldron.md) - A pitfighting arena in Hull Ward, now owned by Panovar Flynt.
* [The Church Of Amoth (Atley)](church-of-amoth-atley.md) - Kept open by Kendra Cloudreaver, who has lost all her powers but hopes Amoth returns.
* [The Crypt Of Amenopophis](the-crypt-of-amenopophis.md) - A gigantic undead engine built to restore Amenopophis — a pound of him for every thousand pounds of humanoid biomass, fed through the Loom.
* [The Dock Of Fallen Cities](dock-of-fallen-cities.md) - Where the Gurges empties into the River Styx; travellers must bribe a Merrenaloth to go further.
* [The Docks](the-docks.md) - Aventine's working waterfront; home to House Drace and, underground, Fel House.
* [The Drissel Home for Beautiful Children](drissel-home.md) - Porth Môr orphanage for children left parentless by The Fall; Cregyn and Taiga donate regularly.
* [The Druidfel](the-druidfel.md) - A forest region; Iric is its druid, and the Wolfbearer Clan lives outside it.
* [The Elemental Plane Of Fire](elemental-plane-of-fire.md) - Where RBG traded a globe of misty stuff for iron worked on the Plane of Fire — an ingredient of the Eye That Sees.
* [The Fauces](the-fauces.md) - The mouth of the Helgoril.
* [The Field Of Bone](field-of-bone.md) - The plateau where the Stygian Gate lies and where Thargorax means to force a final confrontation.
* [The Flagrant Farce](the-flagrant-farce.md) - A playhouse in Hull Ward owned by Lucretia Borova.
* [The Gap](the-gap.md) - The stretch of screaming winds and uplifted ocean floor between the continents; the Maelstrom circles above it.
* [The Gloomspire](gloomspire.md) - Underdark spire on the shores of the Whispering Abyss; held the Lida (Life) Seal.
* [The Green Slaad](the-green-slaad.md) - The largest tavern and only real inn in Hull Ward; named for the stuffed slaad behind the bar.
* [The Gurges](the-gurges.md) - The falls where all the waters of the Helgoril thunder into the crater of Amoth's fall and down to the Dock of Fallen Cities.
* [The Helgoril](helgoril.md) - The great river winding to the Field of Bone; Rave The Howler's territory lay along it.
* [The High Ward](the-high-ward.md) - The wealthiest neighbourhood, home to most house leaders.
* [The Leaning Leg](the-leaning-leg.md) - The pre-Fall name of the largest tavern in Hull Ward, and Tasha Quan's base of operations before she took House Belos.
* [The Maelstrom](the-maelstrom.md) - A huge chaotic storm circling above the Gap, spawned by The Fall and Agrona Sef's destruction of the Arcane Seal.
* [The Mekanikum](the-mekanikum.md) - Artemis Hasp's shop of gadgets, traps and mechanical weapons in the Zuppek Bazaar.
* [The Mining Camp](the-mining-camp.md) - An exhausted copper mine and falling-down camp six hours NE of Aventine, since home to bandits and travellers.
* [The Mournfang Range](mournfang-range.md) - Mountains north of Aventine near the orc lands, where the Lor Manádh lies dormant.
* [The Naphtha Caves](naphtha-caves.md) - Tar caves entered through a crack in the cliff face below the city walls.
* [The Outskirts](the-outskirts.md) - The ground outside Silgorin, where Urush Gûl set the party down.
* [The Palace](the-palace.md) - Municipal complex holding the Prince's living spaces and the Conclave Extent where the houses meet.
* [The Temple District](temple-district.md) - Most of Aventine's places of worship, excepting the Temple of Helm in Hull Ward and a small Pelor temple in the High Ward.
* [The Temple of Helm](temple-of-helm.md) - The main place of worship for Helm's followers, in Hull Ward; expects the Quinary returned to it.
* [The Tower Of Obaris](tower-of-obaris.md) - A half-collapsed necromantic tower leaning over the lip of the Gurges; its only living resident was the wizard Obaris, killed there by Notorious RBG on 15 Fading 07.
* [The Undercity](the-undercity.md) - The inhabited depths beneath Aventine, watched by the Shadow Veil.
* [The Underdark](underdark.md) - Post-Fall, primarily the realm of the Duergar, plus a small community of strange Tabaxi and the svirfneblin artificers of Ironstead.
* [The Underground](the-underground.md) - The tunnel network beneath Aventine, most of it controlled by Guthra G'hul.
* [The University Of Aventine](university-of-aventine.md) - 800 students, no magical training, and one of the greatest surviving libraries in the world.
* [The Vaults](the-vaults.md) - The Shadow Veil's massively trapped vault complex in the Undercity; RBG failed to rob it and Agrona Sef succeeded.
* [The Wandering Emporium](the-wandering-emporium.md) - A travelling emporium in Avernus run by Ardmon Ignus Lacqui, who took it over after Notorious RBG destroyed him and shut down TEMU.
* [The Whispering Abyss](whispering-abyss.md) - A deep, still, intensely salty lake west of Ironstead and Nigh Darûl, cut off from the Gap by The Fall.
* [The Whitesand Steppes](whitesand-steppes.md) - Steppe country of the Silverscale Clan.
* [The Wilted Urchin](the-wilted-urchin.md) - A largish hut on the biggest islet of H'Rare Nis, serving watery beer and every kind of cold fish imaginable.
* [The Wink And Kiss](the-wink-and-kiss.md) - A sleazy-but-safe house of ill repute and bar in Hull Ward.
* [The Xorn Cave](poi-the-xorn-cave.md) - A cave of the Leaning Leg campaign's territory north-east of Aventine.
* [The Zuppek Bazaar](zuppek-bazaar.md) - A riot of shops and stalls between the Docks and the Merchant Ward; site of the sinkhole Agrona Sef caused.
* [Tiamat's Lair](tiamats-lair.md) - Atop a tall mountain at the centre of Avernus, found by moving to the Pillar of Skulls.
* [Uppercuts](uppercuts.md) - A front for the Bournier Cartel; its hidden back room is the cartel's main office.
* [Urdun](urdun.md) - A young orc city of 3,500 on the Gap, settled from Krag M'goth after The Fall; militia of 200 under Zurhdal ket Mak.
* [Wyldwood](wyldwood.md) - Site of Notorious RBG's bar, now managed by Blong Hrusto.
