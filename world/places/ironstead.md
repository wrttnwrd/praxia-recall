# Ironstead: A Sverfneblin City in the Underdark of [Praxia](/world/places/praxia.md)
Duergar city: Nigh Darul

## Overview

!Ironstead.png

**Ironstead** is a vibrant Sverfneblin (deep gnome) city nestled deep within the Underdark of Praxia. Despite its subterranean location, the city exudes a surprisingly cheerful atmosphere, a testament to the hardworking spirit of its 2,500 inhabitants. Ironstead is famed for its exceptional craftsmen and craftswomen, who are regarded as some of the best in all of Praxia. The city also boasts a small but significant library, housing ancient texts of great value.

When players enter:

*Even up close, the wall appears almost perfectly smooth and made of shiny, black stone. A door as wide and high as a small airship silently slides open so flawlessly you almost think it's magical, but it's not. You can see a perfectly-machined track and gears built into a mechanism that moves a massive but balanced door to one side. Unexpected bright, yellow light spills from within. You stand for a moment, wondering what to do, and a massive, humanoid stone shape emerges from the ground. It grunts and motions you in.*

*You pass through the wall and the door whispers shut, leaving no apparent seam. To your left and right are small gatehouses. You're standing at the base of an enormous stone formation. A cave vaults hundreds of feet over your head. Terraces in the wall hold buildings of varying size and shape that appear to be carved from the very stone of the cave itself. In front of you is a small, motley collection of buildings that appear to be homes. A river flows from the rock at your feet and across the town "floor."*

*All around you are the sounds of industry and activity: The flow of water over several mill wheels, hammers on metal, a low murmur of voices. In front of you stands three heavily armed and armored gnomes flanked by two more of the stone figures. "I am general Kraal Stonecrusher. Before I take you to see Mayor Cobaltbone, I'd have you answer a few questions."*

### Unique Features

- **Location:** Situated at the end of a large cave, Ironstead is fortified with a smooth stone wall stretching from the cave's floor to its ceiling, providing formidable protection against intruders.
- **Tome of the First Gods:** This is a sacred and dangerous artifact within the city. Bound in enchanted paper and written in a mix of Abyssal and Celestial languages, it reveals the secrets of Praxia's formation and the four Seals. The Tome also details the use of Soul Coin to crack these Seals, making it a highly guarded and potent source of knowledge.

## Notable Inhabitants

### Kelschunk Cobaltbone - Mayor

- **Description:** Kelschunk Cobaltbone is a wise and charismatic leader. Known for her deep understanding of crafting and trade, she has been pivotal in establishing Ironstead as a center of exceptional craftsmanship. Her leadership has also ensured the city remains a cheerful haven in the gloom of the Underdark.

### Kraal Stonecrusher - General

- **Description:** General Kraaldrunk Stonecrusher is a formidable figure in Ironstead's military. He is known for his strategic acumen and unyielding bravery. Under his command, the city's defenses have been bolstered, ensuring that the secrets of the Tome and the safety of the citizens are well-guarded.

### Zheilthuld Oregazer - Librarian

- **Description:** As the keeper of the city's unique library, Zheilthuld is a guardian of knowledge. He is deeply versed in ancient lore and languages, making him one of the few who can understand the complex writings within [the Tome of the First Gods](/world/things/tome-of-the-first-gods.md).

### Other Key Figures

- **Khelwars Soilleg:** An expert miner and geologist, vital in navigating and exploiting the resources of the Underdark.
- **Baldig Rubbleseeker:** A renowned architect, responsible for the city's unique structural designs and fortifications.
- **Tacktug Cobalttrader:** A savvy merchant, key in establishing trade relations with other Underdark communities and surface dwellers.
- **Ghulschend Goldcarrier:** The head of the city's treasury, managing the wealth and economic stability of Ironstead.
- **Thekners Tinchin:** A skilled craftsman, known for her innovative designs and contributions to Ironstead's reputation.
- **Snaickil Groundholder:** An agricultural expert, responsible for managing the city's food supply through underground farming techniques.
- **Kiggeg Lumpsmelter:** A master blacksmith, famous for her exquisite metalwork and weaponry.
- **Wirdrin Oregazer:** A seasoned explorer and scout, providing valuable information about the ever-changing landscapes of the Underdark.
- **Thelwald**, another librarian - infected.

## City Dynamics

Ironstead is a bustling hub of activity, with each inhabitant playing a vital role in its functioning. The city's economy thrives on its reputation for high-quality crafts and trade. The presence of the Tome of the First Gods adds a layer of intrigue and danger, as the knowledge it contains is both powerful and coveted by many. The city's leaders and citizens are thus ever-vigilant, balancing their daily lives with the responsibility of guarding this ancient and mysterious artifact.

Sverfneblin are not known as a fun-loving group. Food is plain, entertainment minimal. But there is joy here: A population who loves their work and labors unceasingly to perfect their craft. There is also a sense of responsibility. These people are guarding something, and they know it.

## Defense
The gnomes aren't much at combat, preferring their arts. However, they've allied with several Galeb Duhr and earth elementals, as well as three Xorn. They have substantial magic and mechanical defenses as well. It's enough to keep roaming Drow out, and they have an uneasy truce with the Duergar that's lasted decades.

## The Main Tower
At the top of the cave, the main tower houses much of the population and many offices. It also has a small dungeon, kitchens, and the library.

## Library
The library is barely that. It's a small room deep within the main tower. Old Zheilthuld Oregazer works within, poring over ancient texts. Chained and bound to one wall is [the Tome Of The First Gods](/world/things/tome-of-the-first-gods.md).
