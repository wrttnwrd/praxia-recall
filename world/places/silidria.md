Post-Fall, Silidria is the most populus of Praxia's continents.

Originally home to orcs, giants and goblinoids, the humans and elves have increasingly settled in two kingdoms, Camadan and Shamharan, and two cities within those kingdoms, Aventine and Salmuir.

# Pre-Fall
Before the Fall, native Northern civilizations of Silidria warred with each other and the humans and elves. But humans and half-elves allied with the orcs and hobgoblins, driving the Gray Elves back South to Shamharan and the city of Salmuir. The coalition races co-existed somewhat peacefully until the Fall, with humans and half-elves keeping to their cities and towns, and the orcs controlling the mountains and northern steppes.

# Post-Fall
The War For The Shards united the peoples of Silidria, but this broad alliance is strained. Harsher weather and scarcer food have driven all the races closer together. Tens of thousands of elvish refugees live outside Aventine, and refugees have swelled Salmuir to the breaking point.

Humans and half-elves seek to expand into the wilds, where orcs and goblinoids are finding life increasingly difficult.

Communications are open and leadership are trying to cooperate through difficult times, but cracks are showing, and skirmishes have erupted between human and orcs, goblinoids and humans, and orcs and goblinoids.

# Nations

## Camadan
The major human & half-elven kingdom of Silidria. It includes Aventine, Dirion, and most villages and territory up and down The Ibril. Cities and towns within Camadan trade with each other, the orcs and goblins to the north, the barbarian tribes of the Whitesand Steppes, and the peoples of Shamharan farther south. Lands include the foothills of the Mournfang Range, the grasslands along the Ibril, and the forests on either side. With access to the Gap Sea to the west and ample sheltered harbors and bays, Camadan possesses great wealth.

Capital: Aventine
Population: 2.5 million
Primary races: Human, half-elf, elf, orc, goblinoid
Geography: Forest, foothills, mountains, grasslands, some marshes

## Shamharan
A smaller nation at the southern tip of Silidria. It includes Salmuir and a scattering of small villages. Shamharan is self-sufficient, but cities and towns trade gems, dyes, alchemical ingredients and delicacies with Camadan to the north and with the now-rebuilding peoples of Essedria.

Shamharan is also home to the world's only wild dinosaur population. These beasts are tamed and used for transportation, construction, hunting, and sport. They're also traded to the north and Essedria.

Capital: Salmuir
Population: 800,000
Primary races: Human, half-elf, goblinoid, Yuan-Ti
Other races: Tabaxi, Tortle, Lizardfolk, Tiefling
Geography: Marsh, jungle, forest

It is ruled by the Haddad Combine, which has held control since 0 Fall Reckoning.

### History Of Salmuir And Shamharan
Thousands of years ago, before the Fel-Moril died and the Humans and Orcs first went to war, a reptilian race known as the Yuan-Ti settled in the South of Silidria. They may have fled the Elves of Essedria, or crossed from the other planes.

Little is known of their civilization, except that when the first Humanoids migrated south - humans and orcs - they were met with hostility and driven back North.

A few hundred years later, the first Elven stone ships arrived in Shamharan. The Elves built a new city atop the ruins of Old Salmuir. For several hundred years, they prospered, trading rare dyes and metals with the humans, half-elves, and orcs to the north. They also sent huge cargoes of artifacts, plant life and wildlife across The Gap, back to their home cities in Essedria.

Other races that lived in and around Shamharan began to move to the city: Adventurous Tabaxi moved in from the deserts to the East and North. Tortles came from the islands and atolls to the South. And the Yuan-Ti, barely seen for centuries, began to set up temples and small communities in the city.

But the Gray Elves of Southern Essedria dictated the culture. They held Salmuir in an iron grip, and their hatred of the dark-skinned Drow gave rise to a brutal caste system: Lighter-skinned races and individuals enjoyed rights and privileges that others did not. Orcs, Drow, Sverfneblin (deep gnomes) and Lizardfolk had no rights. Darker-skinned humanoids exist somewhere in between.

In -500 FR, these humanoids migrated North, establishing the city of Aventine. For 20 years, Aventine drew thousands of lower-caste and half-caste Salmuirians. The Gray Elves saw this and moved North, briefly seizing control of the young city and reimposing their strict hierarchy. Their grip was broken by the famous Bluestrike rebellion, led by Malindra Bluestrike. With it, half-elves and humans permanently took control of Aventine.

Forced to trade with their half-elf and human neighbors to the North, the Elves of Salmuir had to make concessions. The caste system evolved at a snail's pace over a century, then over decades. Still the Elves had control.

Then The Fall struck. The Essedrian Firestorm engulfed the Gray Elf kingdoms in a few days and with them, all support for their Sillidrian colony. The mysterious Haddad Combine now rules the city. The city is still recovering from the catastrophe. Old power structures contend with new ones. Biases remain strong.

### Shamharan Geography
*Taken from the journal of an unknown explorer who came south from the Mournfang Range.*
Salmuir rests on a marshy delta created by the Nak'ash and several other, smaller rivers. Those rivers are the only reason anyone would have ever settled there.

Hot, humid, insect-infested, the marshes around the city yield very little of value. Giant lizards and other creatures roam the mucky grasses, and ruins of another older empire pepper the entire region.

The city, by comparison, is (was) beautiful (the Fall badly damaged it). Built on a small rise in the marshes, it's dry and, thanks to unique architecture of its buildings and streets, cooler than the surrounding region.

The elves excavated a deep harbor that's embraced by two long, thin arm-like jetties. The network of rivers that feed the delta were channeled into a set of six curved, concentric canals that ring the city. Over the canals are pedestrian and vehicle bridges. The vehicular bridges are broad and strong enough to accomodate some of the city's larger suarian denizens. Each canal is joined to its concentric neighbor by a series of locks.

The city is on a very small rise, the palace at the highest point in the center, the docks and warehouses dotting the outermost ring. Before the Fall, there was a ring even further out where the wealthiest lived, enjoying the breezes and views of the jungle and harbor.

The city is sheltered from most weather, but the the Fall overwhelmed it. Giant waves destroyed most of the outlying ring. All that remained was stinking debris and ruined homes choked with grey elf bodies. It is being restored by the lower castes who are moving into the area.

Each ring is encircled by a wall, with the outer walls the lowest and inner walls built to a height of 20-30 feet.

#### Surrounding Environment
The delta and surrounding region are 70% covered with 1-2 feet of water. There the forests are lush, swampy, and humid.

Farther from the delta the land turns first to marshland, then forested foothills. Even farther East is are the southern foothills of the Mournfang range and, East of there, desert.

It all looks beautiful from the air. If you're not familiar with the region, though, everything there seems designed to poison, injure, or digest you.

#### Flora and Fauna
Within 20 miles of the delta the forests are enormous trees with gnarled, twisted root systems that dive down through swampy water, with a forest "floor" of fresh water plants, vines, and algae. There are enormous, brightly-colored mushrooms and fungi, vines, flowers, and leafy plants that smell like the three-week-old corpse of a large mammal.

Denizens of the region include dinosaurs, lizards, fowl, and insects of varying sizes and disposition. Most are harmless, some are annoying, and a few would delight in crushing, stinging, or otherwise rendering you inert for future liquification and consumption.

If you don't know what to look for, it can be a really icky place. It does have its own beauty, but as far as I can tell everything that wouldn't disgust you is designed to lull you into a false sense of security.

I can't wait to get back to the mountains. Sure, there are Frost Giants, but you can see them coming.

## The Whitesand Steppes
Not actually a nation, the Whitesand Steppes is a flat, is home to various tribes of humans, orcs and other races. Some of the tribes are loose confederations. Others are tight-knit societies built around smaller cities.

### The Silver Scale Clan
Leader: Trianoma Shef
Notables: Wyvern Rider Fannor and his steed, T'Cheh
Races: Goliath, half-elf, human, lizardfolk
Population: 350 (including 25 wyvern riders)

Silver Scale is a small clan known for its unique alliance with the largest of Silidrian beasts, the dinosaurs. Elite warriors ride allosaurs, and they have a small wing of Wyvern riders. Brachiosaurs help assemble buildings.

The clan is semi-nomadic, traveling up and down the trade road to and from Salmuir. They have an agreement with most of the merchant houses to guard the road. In exchange, they receive dinosaur eggs they hatch, raise, and tame.

### The Whitesand Clan
Leader: Tharian Thunderhammer (dwarven)
Notables: Neomak, werebear/goliath barbarian
Races: Human, dwarf, halfling, goliath
Population: 1,500

Named after the Steppes they call home, the Whitesand Clan is the largest of all the nomadic peoples. A large, mostly peaceful tribe. They are nomadic only in name, with small bands ranging across the Steppes every year.

The clan holds a strategically-important location on the eastern bank of the Ibril, between Kerwick and The Dunbarrow.

Their community is a single large, semi-circular series of yurts that have been partly covered with sand, then planted with dune grasses. They dine in family groups or, at specific times, as an entire tribe.

They're agrarian and traders, living on trade and other contracts with the towns and cities up and down the Ibril. They do have a formidable military dedicated to protecting and controlling traffic on the Ibril. This has made them particularly important in the economy of Camadan.

Travelers from the clan ride horses or walk.
