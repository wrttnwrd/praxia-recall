Medium-sized town: 10,000 people max
Economy: Poor to mid
This town is a mix of dwarves, humans, half orcs, gnomes, and a few elves. It's mostly stone, hacked from the granite and limestone of the quarry.
The economy is driven primarily by stonemasons, the quarry, and timber. Food comes up the river from Aventine and from Weatherbee.
Norse-influenced.
Walled in with low, 10' high fortifications. The city has a central well that draws from the lake.
