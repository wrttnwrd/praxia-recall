Spawned in the aftermath of the Fall and the destruction of the Arcane Seal by Agrona Sef, this is a huge, chaotic storm that circles through skies above The Gap. It winks in and out of existence. Sea- and air-based beings and craft have a chance of being sucked in, in which case they're stuck there for 2-4 days.

Draw four cards from the Deck of Many Things (see Book of many things page 13) every day.
