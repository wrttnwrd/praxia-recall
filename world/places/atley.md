## Origin
Small community on [Nova Zuros](/world/places/nova-zuros.md). Was 2500 before the Fall. Since Nova Zuros rose, it's been steadily diminished by Morwenna's feedings. 

Atley shows signs of having once had more residents. Now, most of the town lies abandoned.

Almost every surviving group in the town is a family of some kind. 

The following still exist, and might be useful to the party:

1. Hale's Ales: A small tavern owned by Augustus Hale, a human merchant. Offers modest food and drink at reasonable prices, and has four rooms to let. It's quiet but cheery. Everyone seems... on edge
2. The Church Of Amoth: Still open and run by Kendra Cloudreaver, a bugbear. She knows Amoth is gone. In fact, she's lost all her powers. She keeps the church open in the hopes that Amoth will return. A few people straggle in and out. You get the sense they're covering their bets as much as anything
3. The Jarl's "mansion." A modest two-story home at one end of the town. From here, Elgonna Hand (another bugbear) manages the affairs of the town. It includes direct access to the laboratories
4. The city gardens: A lush garden on the east side of Lake Zuros - completely overgrown
5. Orik Broadfist, an orc blacksmith. He can do basic repairs, some 
6. Samuel Shortfoot, a human, married to Nory Shortfoot, a halfling. They can introduce the party to the townsfolk.
