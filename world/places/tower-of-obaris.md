# The Tower of [the Gurges](/world/places/the-gurges.md) (of Obaris)
*A necromantic lair perched at the brink of an endless cataract pouring into the River Styx*

The Tower of [the Gurges](/world/places/the-gurges.md) stands half-collapsed and leaning, anchored into black stone at the very lip of a colossal waterfall. The waters plunge for miles into mist and screaming darkness, eventually feeding the **River Styx** far below. The roar never stops. Neither do the whispers.

This tower is not merely near death — it is *aligned* with it.

Its only living resident is [Obaris](https://homebrewery.naturalcrit.com/share/Dn7c5DwsYDkY), an human wizard contaminated by long research and contact with [The Gurges](/world/places/the-gurges.md) and the River Styx.

---

Obaris is on a mission to create and possess her monster and in so doing attain immortality. In the course of her work, she's created all manner of abominations that now act as guards.
