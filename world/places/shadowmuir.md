# Point Of Interest: Shadowmuir
Shadowfell mirror city to Salmuir.

Formed as a new Domain Of Dread when [The Fall](/world/events/the-fall.md) hit.

Because of the differences in the passage of time, Shadowmir has been around for 100 years.

Based loosely on Barovia and Gloomwrought. Keepers are [the Obsidian Wing](/world/groups/the-obsidian-wing.md). Obsidian Wing led by Kozani.

It is a city of at most 1000 people.

Home of [the Obsidian Wing](/world/groups/the-obsidian-wing.md).
## Story
Before The Fall, Shadowmuir didn't exist in this form. Energies released by [The Fall](/world/events/the-fall.md) created a Domain Of Dread and established Shadowmuir at its center. Before that, Shadowmuir was a functioning-if-drifting Shadowfell city with no connection to a larger area. Kozani's arrival changed all that.

Most residents of the city are from the Prime Material but don't remember how they got here, why they're here, or anything of their past lives. Instead, they're left with a vague sense of longing and a desire to leave the place. Which they can't.

**Some** residents - those who had suffered harshly on [Praxia](/world/places/praxia.md) - are reasonably happy here and, for reasons they can't explain, fight any effort to return.

## Geography
Right now, Shadowmuir comprises 75% of this domain. Characters who attempt to leave Shadowmuir by land or sea will travel for less than a day, then find themselves back in the city with little or no recollection of what they encountered.

Those who somehow travel beyond there are cast out. DM's discretion, but a few ideas are:

- Players are cast back into the Prime Material Plane at a random location
- Players are cast to a random plane, such as Limbo
- Players are attacked by ...

## Locations Of Note

### Kozani's Tower
On the edge of Darktome Cross, it dominates the skyline.

### Darktome Cross
A sort of temple district that extends to the edge of the Gray Haze Yards and the Palace District.

The temples of Shar, Vecna, and The Raven Queen are all here. Their close proximity leads to a few weird effects:

- The cobblestone streets echo with voices when there's no one nearby
- There's a 1 in 10 chance that any non-divine magic cast here won't work

### Palace District
Players will find the Palace District occupied only by a few tattered beggars. [Kozani Noctis](/world/people/kozani-noctis.md) wiped out the leadership when she arrived.

**Headstone** is a burnt-out hulk of a building. Noctis imprisoned all of the city leaders here and burned the building down when she arrived. You know what it's called because someone scrawled the name on the one still-standing wall.

The one thing that **is** still active here is the barracks, which now houses Kozani's 50 or so Shadar Kai troops.
### Trade District
Most of the shops and markets. 

The **Bone Bazaar** is a marketplace. It includes the **Cold Grin**, a magic shop operated by a ghost, and the **The Last Stop**, a healer's shop operated by a Shadar Kai named **Eggseth**. The **Sallow Sip** is a food and drink establishment that's both a restaurant and a market.
### Gray Haze Yards
The dock area and the surrounding housing and shops. 

The dock and the vessels were pulled here when the domain formed - they and their crews are trapped. The crews spend much of their time drunk at Cinderhaven and around the docks. They don't know why, but they badly need to leave this place. Every now and then, a few brave ships run the maelstrom but disappear. There is no way out of the bay.
#### Cinderhaven
A bar and lodgings. From the outside, Cinderhaven is a 5-story spire towering over other buildings in the Yards. Inside, it's something else entirely.

Cinderhaven is what you'd imagine if a punk bar merged with the Clever Luck. Stairs spiral crazily up at weird angles. Pulsing, bass-heavy music constantly plays. And the entire bar is lit by strobing black and white lights. The lighting turns everything to shades of gray.

Run by Dante and Dumas, Duergar twins stuck here when the dark domain formed. They are utterly humorless. They pay protection to [the Obsidian Wing](/world/groups/the-obsidian-wing.md) but they don't like it. They know who [Nathan Dayspring](/world/people/nathan-dayspring.md) and  Ger’u’do Durtbourne are and hide that fact from The Wing, at some risk to themselves. The sign is a large black X on a gray background.

Dante and Dumas are, at their hearts, revolutionaries. They want Shadowmuir in the hands of the "working folk." They may not have thought it through, though. Like all Duergar, they're driven by rules, and they crave stability. The Shadowfell has brought out their more chaotic impulses. All information they provide about the city is woven into a fabric of revolutionary rhetoric where the Obsidian Wing are parasites that will suck Shadowmuir dry and move on. Dante and Dumas have made a life here, and they have no desire to see it destroyed.
##### Mosh Pit
The center of the bar portion of Cinderhaven is a huge dance floor and mosh pit.

Players can join the seething mass of humanoids on the floor. 

*You're immersed in a sea of sweaty, secreting humanoids frantically dancing.*

If you want to make this more of a thing, have players make Athletics or Performance checks, or something similar. DC 15. If they fail, they get a few bruises. If they succeed, they win a few new friends.
##### The Bar
The bar is a curved stone surface that levitates above the floor. It is always staffed by two shadar kai and either Dante or Dumas.

If this is their first time in the bar, the players will find Ger’u’do Durtbourne there, nursing a tankard of ale. Ger'u'do is actually keeping an eye on [the Obsidian Wing](/world/groups/the-obsidian-wing.md) members in the private room upstairs. He hopes to capture and interrogate one of them to learn some way into the castle, so that he can kill Noctis.
##### Private Rooms
High above the mosh pit are private rooms. The rooms are open to the bar so that guests can watch the action. There are curtains for privacy, if desired.

Either Dante or Dumas are in the center room, entertaining members of the city's Obsidian Wing elite or an affiliated group.
##### Lodging
Cinderhaven has twelve simple guestrooms and one Grand Suite at the very top of the spire.
##### Stable
Two stablehands, random collection of riding beasts.
### Hungering Bay
Turgid black waters dominated by a huge, churning maelstrom. If you look carefully you may see immense shapes moving just under the surface.
### The Wards
A sort of residential district filled with misshapen houses.

Included in the Wards are the following key locations:
#### Chemistry House
This is actually two houses owned by [the Obsidian Wing](/world/groups/the-obsidian-wing.md). The houses are joined by tunnels. The Wing uses them to create the tainted Elfcap and Orugar they're now exporting to Salmuir. 

At any given time, the houses contain 5-10 **guards**. These are downpowered [Shadar-Kai Shadow Dancers](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/2560910-shadar-kai-shadow-dancer). They have only 50 hp, and the saves against their chains are only DC 10, but versus INT not DEX.

If a fight starts, one of the guards triggers an alarm that sends an immediate message to Headstone. It takes 10-20 more guards 4-5 minutes to arrive. These guards include at least one gang leader (a [Gloomweaver](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/2560909-shadar-kai-gloom-weaver))

The houses also contain 3 **chemists**. These are Shadar Kai alchemists. As [Duergar Alchemist](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/273947-duergar-alchemist) but with:

- Advantage vs charm and immunity to sleep
- - 50hp
- All attacks do 2x damage

Finally, there are 10-20 civilians, helping blend ingredients, run errands, and handle the other day-to-day work. If there's violence, they flee at the first opportunity.

*Note that RBG destroyed the chemistry houses and the cellars on 4 Highsun 7.*
