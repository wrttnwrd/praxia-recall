(black cat living in bar - maine coon - male - hates Kiki - Cat)

Created from a seedling planted by Draknev during [Post-Thargorax](/world/prep/post-thargorax.md) downtime. 

Facing it all from the street, the manor is now the right-hand building. The pub is on the left. The tree drapes protectively over it all and actually forms the pub, with their laboratories, etc. located per below.

But much of the bastion is in an extradimensional space that can be entered from two locations

1. A shed at the back of the twins' paddock, which opens into a larger stables and run. There's a door that, if you aren't allowed to enter, will open onto the back of their paddock. If you're allowed, you speak the word and poof, it opens into the stables and play field.
2. A back door to the pub. The door normally opens onto the patio. But if you've been given permission, you can open it, speak the word, and step into the rest of the space, including the laboratory.

To use either location, the user must:

1. Be allowed via ritual by Drak or another member of RBG
2. Know the location of the entryway

The players will know they didn't install those doors.

Whenever a player uses those doors, they need to roll to make sure no one spots them. Depending on the time of day that's a DC 10 (off hours) or DC 20 (peak hours).

When someone re-enters this plane from the demiplane, they merge seamlessly into the flow of other creatures and there's no problem.
