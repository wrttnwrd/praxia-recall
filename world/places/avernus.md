Avernus is the layer of the Nine Hells closest to the Prime Material. All Forgotten Realms content applies.

[https://dungeonsdragons.fandom.com/wiki/Avernus](https://dungeonsdragons.fandom.com/wiki/Avernus)

## Gaining Entry

You can't just walk into Avernus. 

Or rather, you can:

1. Via a portal on the Fields Of Bone
2. By sailing up the Helgoril, which descends via a great falls into the River Styx and lands at the Dock Of Fallen Cities. From there, the players need to bribe a Merrenaloth to transport them
3. Potentially via a massively powerful travel spell like Plane Shift

But once there you need permission to be there. If you don't have permission, legions of armed and armored devils stand between you and, well, anything. They'll tear any unwelcome visitors apart, then drag their souls to lower levels of the Hells, or fling them onto the fields of Avernus for the demons.

Damned souls enter Avernus through the River Styx.

You can enter from below via the tallest metal spire of Dis. It pierces the floor of Avernus near the Pillar Of Skulls.

## [Tiamat](/world/people/tiamat.md)

[Tiamat](/world/people/tiamat.md) was imprisoned on Avernus as punishment for her role in the First War. She dwells in [Tiamat](/world/people/tiamat.md)'s Lair, perched atop a tall mountain in the center of Avernus. You can find it by moving to the Pillar of Skulls. 

## Who's in charge?

Zariel, a fallen angel.
