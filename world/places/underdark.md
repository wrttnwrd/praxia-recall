Post-Fall, the Underdark is primarily the realm of the Duergar. Half-lucky, half-skilled, the dwarves' cities are located in particularly robust areas of the underground world, and they have the skills to dig out and adapt to a far less stable post-Fall world.

Also there is a small community of particularly strange Tabaxi.

Also there is a single, well-defended city of sverfneblin artificers: Ironstead. Ironstead is at the base of the  Hrare Nis and Aventine entrances.
