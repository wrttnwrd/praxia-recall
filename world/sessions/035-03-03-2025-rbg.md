The portal opened on 28 Summertide. There is a 25% chance it is still open enough to let the characters through.
## Mythic places & Info

### The Temple Of Shar
The destroyed temple is in the ruins of The Mists.

_Inside, the temple reeks of mold and rotting vegetation. The only light filters in from the open doors. It is stiflingly humid, and the floor is saturated with salt water and slightly springy - it gives slightly as you walk. A thin mist of water vapor rises from the floor. Everywhere you  see the circle-in-circle symbol of the Goddess Of Loss. Scattered about are tents, sleeping bags, cooking utensils and other detritus of the less-fortunate. There are also pieces of furniture that don't belong in a temple of Shar. Clearly, people have taken advantage of what shelter the ruins can provide. Those folk are not here now, though. At least, most of them aren't. Spread around the dais are the head, torso, and limbs of several adults (probably human). Elsewhere in the temple are two other intact bodies._

If the players inspect, the bodies have been drained of blood and have broad, ragged wounds on their throats.

If they enter the basement:

_The basement is cold - almost frigid - but still damp. As you descend the stairs, a breeze hits your face. Across the room, a large standing mirror glows with blue-gray light. The breeze seems to come from the mirror itself. Just ahead of you, five figures move towards the opening._

These are the [nosferatu](/world/people/nosferatu.md) and two [Gloom Weavers](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/2560909-shadar-kai-gloom-weaver)

All the nosferatu wants to do is escape back to [Shadowmuir](/world/places/shadowmuir.md). It only attacks if the characters try to stop it. If the players arrive at night, it will take 4 rounds for the portal to fully open. If they arrive during the day, the portal is closed, and the nosferatu and shadar-kai aren't there yet.
## Events

### The battle in the temple
If the players go into the basement, they'll find the mirror, still glowing and active. The mirror is bathed in blood, using Sanguinomancy to hold the aperture open. The amount of blood required was absurd. Almost every corpse upstairs.

### The jump to [Shadowmuir](/world/places/shadowmuir.md)
*You feel a wrenching drop so abrupt it's as if you stepped off a cliff. There's no warning. The smell of lightning and gunpowder fills your nose. You wince, expecting an impact. Instead, you find yourself standing on solid ground surrounded by a swirling sea of gray mist. Slowly mist resolves into solid shapes, and you're standing in a gloomy, dimly-lit room. Stairs lead up. There are no other visible means of egress.*

They exit upstairs into an intact version of the Temple of Shar. 

*You emerge into a well-lit area. But the light is different somehow - it's directionless and washed out. There are colors, but muted. Most things are shades of gray. Sound seems almost... muffled.*

If the players were pursuing Malachai and the gloomweavers, they see them fleeing up the stairs.

Once on the street, they're in Shadowmuir. At this point, the city is business as usual. Kozani has no idea the heroes have come through because she has yet to hear from Malachai. 
## Secrets

1. [The Order Of Light](/world/groups/order-of-light.md) sent Ger’u’do Durtbourne  through the portal 28 days ago. The Combine knows this but no one's heard from either person
2. Malachai won't leave without Skith. So he and the other Gloomweavers are waiting in the temple, hoping he'll show and that they can still use the portal
3. The players can also get to Shadowmuir from **Yggdrasil** or the Infinite Staircase, or a caster of sufficient power can send them
