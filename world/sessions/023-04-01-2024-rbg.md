## Recap
You traveled South, towards the Helgoril. 

You met a gnome on a large snail and helped him destroy a plague wasp nest. You then discovered his name was a godawful ripoff of a meme, but chose to leave him alive. (remember "Teeroooyyyyyy Jenn-Kin!)

While on watch one night, Mossy happened to use the Eye That Sees and caught Rave red-handed, invisible, preparing to do something awful. 

In the fight that ensued, you killed Rave and created the Mouth That Calls.

In the process, you completely short-circuited a complex plan the DM spent two weeks developing, killed a major BBEG, and generally sowed chaos throughout the land.

*Somewhere off screen, Agrona Sef probably said, "I AM VERY DISAPPOINTED!!!!"*

You are now 1-2 days' hike from the Helgoril, where you're supposed to meet the Villain's Discontent and decide the next leg of the journey. You can try to find the Hag Of Fate and have her weave the Door That Opens, or continue on to the Fields Of Bone.

## The Plot
Hunting for Rave The Howler

The party comes ashore north of the mouth of the Helgoril, in what used to be a dense, lush forest. It's now a twisted landscape.

Kergek recommends proceeding south from here. That will bring them to the Helgoril, which winds its way to the Field Of Bone. It also puts Rave The Howler's territory between them and the Helgoril.

## Scenes & Settings

1. Recovery
2. Troy, son of Jenn, a small gnome riding a large snail, comes upon the party. He is a bard explorer, looking for great stories. He's preparing to wipe out a large plague wasp nest. He doesn't look ready for it. If the players offer to help, he'll ask them to sit down w/ them to plan. First time they look away, he leaps on his snail and spurs into action, shouting "Troy Jenn-kin!"
3. An area where the ground has been scoured clean. There is only bare rock
4. Their first look at the Helgoril from high on a hill. It's about 30 miles away. It's a torrent, flowing the wrong way. The river winds into the distance, and it's hard to see where it ends up
5. The trap: Second night of travel, Rave will, using combat invisibility, use his Paralysis Hex on the party. DC 20 INT save. Then he will have them carried to the village. He works for Agrona Sef. He doesn't know about Kergek. Agrona doesn't think he'll succeed. She wants the party to create another part of the Quinary, then ambush them
6. The battle: At the Mireborn camp, there are three fomorians, one Noble, the surviving Deep Crawler (still hurt) and Rave (Fomorian Warlock but add paralysis, improved invisibility, eldritch blast, Arms Of Hadar, 300hp, repelling blast, 18AC). Rave's Blood Rune is inscribed on the back of his hand




- [ ] Kergek is actually Celdastrus - the blue dragon they encountered a while back - in the service of Agrona Sef and Thargorax. He's there to sow doubt in the party and, more important, help set the party up for a raid by the Sef. If he can, he'll guide the party to the mouth of the Helgoril, the largest river on Essedria. The river is a perfect spot for an ambush, and, since its flow reversed in the Fall, a great way for Sef's forces to run if they succeed (or fail). He will help the party defeat Rave The Howler, though.
- [ ] The Sef is collecting herself for an attack at the mouth of the Helgoril
- [ ] Even in lead-lined boxes, the Seals cause mild effects. The players will notice some of the primordial stuff growing out of the Leda box, and there will be a tiny area of chaos around the Flux box, where weird things happen: Bugs avoid the area, dust collects faster, rats are belligerent, and any devices/other items decay/break more often
