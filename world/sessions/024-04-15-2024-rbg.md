## Recap

## The Plot
Betrayal

The party is headed south towards the mouth of the Helgoril (maybe).

They don't know this, but Agrona Sef awaits them in the ruins of Mistwharf. 

## Scenes & Settings

### Getting lost
On their way down to the Helgoril, they end up in a twisted maze of gullies and broken ground. Make three Survival checks w/ disadvantage.

### The Tortured Plain
They cross a blackened plain. Large, molten chunks of ejecta fell to Praxia here. The ground is a cracked, blackened crust of rock that flowed and solidified. 

Here and there you see the stony outline of a tree trunk where melted rock literally rained from the sky, burned away the tree, and cooled so quickly it preserved the outline. Where it was windy and cold a few minutes ago, here it is stifling. 

As you move across the plain, you see a few dank, turgid pools of water. Other than the trees and the pools, the entire plain is flat and featureless. Everything was melted and reflowed into a level surface. Or so you think. 

After several hours, a thick, choking fog forms and it's hard to see more than a few feet in front of you - consider the area heavily obscured as you stumble your way forward, making group Survival checks to stay on course. Then you detect a large shape ahead. 

It's a house, completely encased in rock. You move slowly into a town - more houses come into view. Then you stumble over something on the ground. You look down, and there's a humanoid form, also encased in a thin crust of black rock. 

Its arms are upraised as if warding off a blow from above. As you move through the town square, you find more and more twisted, blackened forms. These people had very little time and never made it to shelter.

### The town of Mistwharf
Eventually, the Tortured Plain gives way to a series of hills. Cresting one of those hills, you're looking down on a huge landslide that apparently engulfed a town. 

Agrona Sef is waiting on Sillidria for a signal from Celadastrus. On his signal, she will teleport to the temple and attack.

Their objective: The Seals. Celdastrus was not going to attack the Discontent on his own. Even if he did, Agrona wouldn't have allowed him to get the Seals w/o her. They hate each other. The plan is to attack the party while they're on the ground, then go up and deal w/ the Discontent. Then grab the Seals at their leisure. Divide and conquer. If it all goes wrong, Agrona may try to get up to the Discontent



- [ ] Kergek is actually Celdastrus - the blue dragon they encountered a while back - in the service of Agrona Sef and Thargorax. He's there to sow doubt in the party and, more important, help set the party up for a raid by the Sef. If he can, he'll guide the party to the mouth of the Helgoril, the largest river on Essedria. The river is a perfect spot for an ambush, and, since its flow reversed in the Fall, a great way for Sef's forces to run if they succeed (or fail). He will help the party defeat Rave The Howler, though.
