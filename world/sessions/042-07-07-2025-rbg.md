## Outline

### Getting To Avernus
The most obvious solutions would be:

1. Fly along the Helgoril, down the falls, and onto the River Styx, then fly along it. The Discontent could do it, but they must have a Merranaloth pilot to do it. They can hire a Merranaloth named Morugal w/ a soul coin, or by killing a particularly vile Red Abishai named Drakith, who Morugal hates because Drakith has banned the merranaloth from most of Avernus
2. Use Plane Shift to the mouth of the River Styx, then #1.
3. Use Gate to conjure a portal. They'll need a 20th-level caster. The only candidates are Vek Azar and... ? Just Vek, really, unless the players have another idea.

### Getting Paperwork: Avernus
If they decide this is the way to go, the players need to get a badge to be present on Avernus. If they don't, they'll be fighting legions of devils simply to remain there.

They can get this badge a few ways:

1. From Surtur, a Fire Giant demigod who despises Tiamat and would love to see her dead. Animus could potentially connect them. Surtur has several badges obtained from messengers he's killed. Surtur will consider it, but will require payment, or a promise.
	1. To travel to and survive on the Plane (not just in the City Of Brass) the players will need protection
2. They can capture and kill one of Zariel's messengers and steal their badge. That would be extremely dangerous, as they'd have to convince anyone that the badge is legitimate, as is their right to possess it. But Zariel's messengers frequent parts of Silidria. The players would have to hunt one down. Easiest way to do that is to find someone who deals w/ devils.
	1. In Aventine, they work with a professor and researcher at the University - Lyra Thorne - who trades with them for research insights. Her soul is long forfeit, but she is not a warlock.
	2. Magog actually deals with a devil who helped them become a lich 
3. The Pit Fiend Volmoch possesses the hammer and anvil used to create the badges. They could attempt to sneak into his stronghold on Avernus (he is one of Zariel's chief lieutenants) and use the hammer and anvil.
4. Or find another enemy of Tiamat, enemy of Zariel, or another interested party who can secure one

## Recap

*19-21 Highsun, 07 Fall Reckoning.*

After the revelation of the Five Fold Brand, RBG spent a few days researching and planning.
### Keeping Loved Ones Safe

First, Mossy decided that, no matter what you all do, Kiki will stay in Aventine with Roxy and the dragonborn bosun's mate from the Villain's Discontent. The Twins will stay at Wildwood, with Kiki keeping an eye on them (it's only a few minutes from the Green Slaad as the faerie dragon flies). Thistlebeard and his friends will keep an eye on things, too, as will this capybara.
### Parental Revelations

At the end of the game, Cregyn dropped a bombshell: Taiga, his life partner, contacted him to tell him she's pregnant. 

*Female tortles become "pregnant" when their body tells them it's time. "Time" can be for a number of reasons. The female lays the eggs, which can lie for as long as 30 days. In that time, a male has to fertilize them. The eggs then incubate and hatch after about six months.*

What this means for Cregyn's role in the group remains to be seen.
### Research, Planning, And A Little God Talk

RBG started with some research, learning that:

1. Tiamat is one of the most vengeful, petty demigods. She is a prisoner in a fortress called Tiamat's Lair on the plains of Avernus, which is the top layer of the Nine Hells
2. You can't teleport there. You'll need to use Plane Shift or Gate. You're not sure Plane Shift will work. Gate would, but would require a 20th level caster. There are two or three folks you know who might be able to do this, and you only know of one or two: Vek Azar of Fel House, and possibly Hexxam of House Umbra (less likely)
3. You can also sale up the Helgoril to Hell's Falls, then follow those to the River Styx and sail to Avernus
4. Regardless, Avernus is a war zone. It's the front in the Blood War: Demons continuously try to infiltrate Hell via Avernus. The devils there are well trained and organized. Mortals **do** travel the Plains, but not without a Badge Of Zariel - an amulet the tells Avernesian devils you belong there
5. Those badges are in very short supply. Drak communed with Callistra (or Callistra's herald, anyway), who told him that Surtur, a fire giant demigod, has several and will barter for them
6. Tiamat and Bel ran Avernus for some time. Then something went wrong, and Asmodeus transferred control of Avernus to the archdevil Zariel

Mossy reached out to Nikthim, who responded via cat puke, saying yes, he'd love to help play a nasty joke on Tiamat.

### Propaganda

Mossy and Rikterr bumped into Salib Azari who, it turns out, is studying "mimetic transmission" at the University of Aventine. They've asked him to prepare and launch a disinformation campaign against Tiamat. He agreed.

### The Plan (I think)

I *think* RBG plans to go talk to Animus (the fire giant) about meeting with Surtur and getting a Badge Of Zariel from him. That would require a trip to the Elemental Plane Of Fire, which is not trivial. You've been to the City Of Brass, but the City is specifically designed to accommodate mortals. The rest of the Plane of Fire is extremely, well, fiery. You'll need some kind of special protection to go there.

Regardless: Right now the plan is to get a Badge Of Zariel, then somehow go to Avernus and confront Tiamat.

First, though, you're going to spend a couple of weeks creating some stuff at Wildwood. I don't have the specifics on that, so I expect you'll share w/ me tomorrow.
