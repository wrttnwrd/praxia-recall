## Recap
You wrapped up the conversation at House Umbra, which included a conversation with an extremely freaked-out Eldrin Valthor about The Ritual Of Binding. The Ritual, encoded into the language of the Tome Of The First Gods, provides a way for a sentient being to return the Seals to the First Gods.

You learned a few other options for keeping the Seals out of Agrona Sef's hands: Destroy them (could be bad) or just try to hide the (could work). 

You unlocked the Flux globe and retrieved that seal, then put it in a lead-lined box. You now have TWO seals, both in lead boxes. You left the Tome with Hexxam at House Umbra.

You went to Nigh Darûl, met Zerla Pelletweaver, and completed a series of trials. She then created The Hand That Strikes.

She also gave you information on The Mouth That Calls, saying that it requires the jawbone from a creature named Rave The Howler that haunts the peaks on the Eastern shores of Essedria.

## Scenes & Settings

### Nigh Darul
A 4-5 day journey from Ironstead.

There, they can find Zerla Pelletweaver and learn the secrets of the remaining blueprints. She can also create the Hand That Strikes, and provide some ideas where to go next.

- A Hag named Essie Darksinger lived on Essedria in an ancient forest. She can make the Door
- A creature known as Rave The Howler lives high in the mountains above the Eastern Shore of Essedria. Her jawbone can yield the Mouth That Roars

Zerla could use Mossy's help creating the Hand, if she's inclined.

Also in the city is a Duergar named Tysmora. She apparently possesses a length of Kraken gut. But she won't part with it unless someone helps her retrieve a small chest that's been hidden at the opposite end of her family vault for centuries. It's the balance and ruin room from the Deck (page 29)

### Tavern
The Stoic Forge

### Proving Themselves
Zerla wants the players to prove they're worthy of The Hand. A priestess of Gond will put them through a Trial By Ordeal. 

The PCs must succeed in a series of contests:

1. Lifting heavy objects: An automaton is there. It has a 24STR. There are various gadgets that can be assembled into a device that can aid them. Objects are huge blocks of stone
2. Target shooting: A Duergar produces a crossbow with a ridiculous scope and easily hits the center. Again, there are tools, but they can try a shot
3. Cross The Bridge: Cross a bridge to the next contest. Coming with them are three drow with clear symbols of Lolth on their armor. The bridge shakes violently, pitching one of the drow off. What the players do at that point may show their valor/kindness/reverence for all life
4. Final contest: The puzzle
