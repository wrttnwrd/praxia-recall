## Recap
After some discussion, RBG and the Discontent began the trip to a little-known island in the middle of The Gap. 

You decided to keep the current Seal on the Discontent as one of the safest places it can be.

During the journey, experimentation with the Discontent's new Spell Cannon bore fruit.

Upon landing on the island, RBG began their trip into the Underdark.

Saw their first Duergar and Steeder, had a conversation w/ some naked mole rats. Stopped there.

## The Plot
The party has to decide what to do with the Flux Seal.

Depending on where they go, they may learn more at the Temple of Helm. Or they may head into the Underdark.

Best options for hiding the Seal are to either:

1. Take it to the temple
2. Keep it
3. Remove it to another plane: Tiamat is a demon. The devils have a strong incentive to keep it safe

## Secrets and Clues

- The party has a 2-week lead on Agrona, particularly since Hexxam was able to slow her down. Unfortunately, the wizard was sucked into a pocket dimension along with the Sef, so he's out of commission for the time being. If Agrona escapes first there will be no one to stop her
- There's an ancient text hidden in Ironstead. It's actually a copy of what Agrona Sef has. It tells the story of the Seals, and contains the binding ritual. The Tome Of The First Gods
- The Seal is nearby Ironstead
