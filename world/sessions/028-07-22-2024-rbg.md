## Scenes

### Captured!
At dusk, RBG spots a floating island in the distance. As they're figuring out what they're seeing, something grabs their ship, dragging it towards the island. It's Morwenna, using a beefed up Control Winds to draw the Villain's Discontent to the island.

### Confrontation
At some point, the players are asking around and notice someone with an air of authority staring at them. It's Ezekiel, a "priest" of Morwenna. He may interrupt, which makes whoever the party was talking to extremely nervous.

## Nova Zuros


### Location: Atley
There's a sign outside the town that reads "Atley - Population: 2500"

A town located on the surface of Nova Zuros. Atley shows signs of having once had more residents. Now, most of the town lies abandoned.

There are many more children than adults (adults offering more food)

The following still exist, and might be useful to the party:

1. Hale's Ales: A small tavern owned by Augustus Hale, a human merchant. Offers modest food and drink at reasonable prices, and has four rooms to let. It's quiet but cheery. Everyone seems... on edge
2. The Church Of Amoth: Still open and run by Kendra Cloudreaver, a bugbear. She knows Amoth is gone. In fact, she's lost all her powers. She keeps the church open in the hopes that Amoth will return. A few people straggle in and out. You get the sense they're covering their bets as much as anything
3. The Jarl's "mansion." A modest two-story home at one end of the town. From here, Elgonna Hand (another bugbear) manages the affairs of the town. It includes direct access to the laboratories
4. The city gardens: A lush garden on the east side of Lake Zuros

### Location: The Nova Zuros Laboratories
Morwenna - https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/17207-vampire-spellcaster - has spent time rebuilding the laboratories to suit her purposes.

She doesn't understand the stone and has given up trying. Now, her time is spent finding ways to extend her food supply. As it stands, she only has a year or so left, at most.

She's tried a host of experiments. None of worked. Now she's reduced to feeding on pigs and other livestock, all raised w/in the laboratory.

1. The barghest room
2. The quipper pool
3. The mimic hoard
4. The pig chamber (breeding)
5. Cow chamber (breeding)
6. Sand pit - giant constrictor snakes

Remember: She's been paid w/ a soul coin by Agrona Sef to stall the party as long as possible.
