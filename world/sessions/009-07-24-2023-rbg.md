## Recap
Party figures out there's the one ley line. They can't move w/o it. Airships tie to the line and use it to move.

You went to see Animus, who agreed to refit the Discontent for this new environment.

During your visit, you also went to the City of Brass and obtained (in exchange for a strange globe of misty stuff) iron worked on the Plane Of Fire.

You now have the first two of the Quinary, and a slick new-ish airship! 

## The Plot
The party has killed Elidan. As they were returning, however, Agrona Sef cracked the first seal (outside Porthmoor), disrupting many forms of elemental magic and causing the Discontent to bank and yaw sharply.

Cregyn rolled well and helped right the ship, jury-rigging sails and a kind of "air rudder" to steer.

## The Hard Part
The party can go in at least two very different directions:

### Modify The Discontent
They can modify the Villain's Discontent to operate in a post-elemental world.

Then, they can fly down to Porth Moor, or try to get ahead of Agrona and stop her, or start getting the Quinary assembled.

1. If Quinary + flying, then they'll end up heading to H'Rare Nis and making a short jaunt down into the Underdark, or seeing if Animus can do something, or something else. Whole trip might be 8 days + the refit
2. If PorthMoor, flying is clearly faster (and safer), but they can't go against prevailing winds. It would also take at least 4 weeks to get to Aventine.


**Regardless**, refitting the Discontent means going to Animus. He's the only person who can do this. Cregyn and Fenlal both know this. Cregyn's rig means a 4-day week trip to the Tabernam.

Costs are as follows:

1. Engines: 7500 GP
2. Better rigging: 5000gp GP
3. Improved cannon and ballistae (to replace the BFG, which no longer works):
	1. Hookshot cannon: 2500GP
	2. Spell cannon: 3500GP (lets wizard cast and amplify spells)
	3. Ballista: 500GP
4. Reinforced/matte hull: 5000gp - takes HP to 600, makes ship harder to detect

Or, the whole thing for 15,000GP.

Party paid the 3750.

Animus will take payments: 25% up-front, 75% in a year.

Animus now has a bookkeeper: A young, nerdy orc named Mogak.

*Mogak is a scrawny orc artificer with an incredibly agile mind. She wears a monocle and a small abacus around her neck and has a set of tools clipped to her jeans. Yes, jeans. She takes a lot of notes about everything, and tends to ask a lot of questions. She's going through puberty, which in orcs means her voice cracks constantly.*
### Head Straight Into The Underdark
They can attempt to head through the Underdark, which would allow them to get to the next Seal and go to Gracklstugh, where they could get a few of the Quinary built.

This is a dangerous journey. It COULD pay off in a shorter transit time.

Their guide, regardless of where they enter, will be a dwarf named Garsir Rockreaver. He's younger, with a lengthy beard and curly, lush eyebrows, prone to puns.


### Travel times and options

1. Fly to Tabernam: 2 days
2. Refit: 3 days then all travel times return to normal (wind-dependent)
3. Fly to West Godelis: 1 week (Seal and Underdark entry); OR
4. Fly to H'rare Nis (underdark entry, but challenging): 5 days; OR
5. Fly to Porthmoor: 1 week
6. Walk to Aventine: 4 days
7. Walk Underdark to Gracklstugh: 1 week (if OK)

Really, it evens out. Walking the Underdark is sure to lead to big slowdowns - no one's done the whole route since The Fall. Flying has its own dangers, but they have a good crew and a fast ship.

## Secrets and Clues

- Sef cracked the first Seal, Divinity, blotting out the stars and breaking the ley lines. Ships using elemental nacelles no longer have propulsion, and navigation has become harder.
- The second seal, Nature, is on West Godelis. It will take Agrona at least a month to get there and crack the seal. The party can learn this from a variety of sources, including Hexxam, Feyndal, and others. If they don't, Hexxam will use Sending or similar to get them a message
