## Recap
So, the plan was:
1. Set fire to the Bournier’s mansion
2. Which would make the Bourniers want to store their stuff in their vault
3. Then pretend to be some movers who would help move the stuff into the vault
4. It actually WORKED. The building rolled a 1 on its save and went up like a torch.
5. The party saved this safe from upstairs, and Drake was so grateful he paid them to move the safe into the Undercity Vault
6. They get in, with Drake, escorted by guards (they’re all in disguise)
7. Creygn is hiding inside a bag of holding, because why not
8. Once in there, though, they can’t get rid of the guards. All they want to do is make the guards go away
9. Drak decides to create a distraction by summoning 8 giant centipedes
10. Cregyn pops up out of the bag, right in front of the drow guards
11. Who then attack. They’re total punks - not strong at all. The party puts them down w/ non-lethal damage (or lethal w/ undo)
12. Not realizing that the medallions have to be in the possession of a CONSCIOUS individual
13. Door slams shut, gas starts. Cregyn is outside w/ one remaining drow
14. The party DECIDES TO KEEP STUFFING GOLD INTO THE BAG while listening to the hissing of the gas
15. Cregyn gets the third Drow guard to open the door
16. Door opens, hissing is louder
17. Everyone turns bright blue (except Cregyn)
18. They CONTINUE STEALING STUFF
19. Sleep gas comes out, Mossy and Rikterr fall asleep
20. Bright blue Drak is dragging the unconscious bodies of bright blue Mossy and bright blue Rikterr members out of the vault, along w/ a bag of holding stuffed w/ stolen gold, just as Guthra G’ul shows up w/ 25 guards
21. Guthra G'ul knows what they've done for the city, and in their defense, he actually invited them to do this. He shakes his head sadly, makes them put the treasure back, and then throws them out
22. Drake wakes up and is told that he was attacked, the movers died, and the Vault's defenses are impenetrable.
