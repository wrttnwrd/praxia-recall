This takes place outside/inside [Ironstead](/world/places/ironstead.md), depending on what the players decide to do.

## The Plot
The city is surrounded by a small Duergar force. They aren't attacking. They aren't letting anyone in or out, either.

### What's Happening
With the cracking of the first Seal, the Duergar felt that the Tome of the First Gods must be better protected. They knew the location through their spy network.

The Tome is indeed very, very dangerous. But it would be very difficult for them (or anyone else) to find their way in.

## Conditions


### 1.  The Duergar
Maermura, from last game, is in the blockading force. The force is led by Gald Proudguard. It includes fifty Duergar on Steeders and 300 foot soldiers. 

Also has a drow elf there - Lelouch - advisor.



### 2. In [Ironstead](/world/places/ironstead.md)
 The sverfneblin is a male named Thelwald. Each blames the other for "trapping" them here, even though that's absurd. They're both speaking Undercommon.


## Recap
RBG approached Ironstead, only to find the city surrounded by a small army of Duergar. 

RBG met with the Duergar, who agreed to allow them to approach Ironstead but explained that they feel the Tome Of The Ancient Gods, currently in the possession of the Deep Gnomes, is far too dangerous to leave there.

They'd appreciate the party's help obtaining the Tome.

Once in Ironstead they had a few very good persuasion rolls and are now speaking to Zheilhurd (Zayal-herd) Oregazer in the library.

We left off just after Zheilthurd showed them the woodcut dated -2000FR that shows a Tabaxi, a paladin in plate, a tortle, a wizened monk, and an elf.
