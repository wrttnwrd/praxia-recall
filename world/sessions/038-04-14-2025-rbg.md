## Outline
The fight in the Wards.

Ghouls:
50hp
Bite: +8, 10 damage + 2 necrotic
Claw: +8, 10 damage, DC 10 CON save or paralyzed

Tinkerers have more hp and can do 20 fire damage on a failed save, DC 12 dex save. But they'll be slow to use them because they're afraid of blowing everything up.

The players can exit the tunnels at any drain. Or, they can attempt to follow all the way from the Wards to the Temple of Shar, where there's a secret door.

Duergar alchemists have 80hp. Attacks are +8, and all do 2x damage.

### The Secret Doors
Each house has a secret door hidden under a brazier. The players may find them by checking for secret doors. Or:

- Tinkerers run for those doors if they're attacked
- Ghouls periodically emerge or descend through the doors

## Mythic places & Info

1. Under The Wards - the drug labs, where an assortment of kobolds and ghouls are running things. There is also one gloomweaver. If it looks like a cakewalk and you want to make it more interesting, throw in the gargoyle.
2. There's a major shipment ready to move out via the portal in the temple of Shar. The tunnels lead there.
3. Kozani Noctis is constructing a new portal. She (thinks) that if she can get twisted Elfcap into enough people in Salmuir, that'll cause a cataclysmic event and allow her to create it
