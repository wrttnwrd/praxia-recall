## Scenes
Through the dungeon.

### The Stone Room
Pechs and others have been accumulating the odd flying stone in this room. 

Morwenna's theory is that she can collect the stone here, which is approximately the center of the island (weight-wise) and then steadily dispose of it, allowing the island to settle to the ground.

The Room Guard is there with his pet. 
### Rescuing Dura
The young orc girl is sitting at the end of a table that's covered in sweets.


### Location: The Nova Zuros Laboratories
Morwenna - https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/17207-vampire-spellcaster - has spent time rebuilding the laboratories to suit her purposes.

She doesn't understand the stone and has given up trying. Now, her time is spent finding ways to extend her food supply. As it stands, she only has a year or so left, at most.

She's tried a host of experiments. None of worked. Now she's reduced to feeding on pigs and other livestock, all raised w/in the laboratory.

Remember: She's been paid w/ a soul coin by Agrona Sef to stall the party as long as possible.

## Rescuing The Wave Serpent
You see a thin trail of smoke against the sky. As you move closer, you begin to make out the details of an airship. But something is wrong. It's listing heavily to port, and smoke is coming from belowdecks. The mizzen mast is dangling over the side, and most of the ship's rigging lies in ruins. There's a gaping hole in the stern on the port side, and the ship is not moving.

Written on the ruined bow you can still make out the ship's name: The Wave Serpent.

There's little movement on deck.

## Meeting w/ Grimshaw
Grimshaw and a sizable chunk oof the crew are still alive. He explains what happened:

The ship was set upon by some kind of scaled, clawed fliers and a mixed group of orcs and humans, all led by a warrior "in dark armor, wielding a sword the size of a fucking goliath."

He says she led the first attack, then stood back while her troops did the fighting. 

He lost sight of Sango. When the old man reappeared, the lead warrior was on him in a second, immobilizing him with some kind of spell. Then she vanished.

The winged creatures left, and Grimshaw and the crew managed to kill the rest of the attackers.

*Agrona has taken Sango to the Fields Of Bone. Once there, Sango escapes. She's... disappointed. Sango will try to find the party.*
