## Scenes & Settings

### Meeting Kergek 
Somewhere along the way, the party meets Kergek, a sane Derro sage. Kergek will place himself in the players' path somehow.

Kergek the cartographer is a tall-ish (3'5") Derro dressed in a traveler's tunic and leather armor. He has a weatherbeaten backpack. From the backpack hangs an assortment of climbing tools, rope, and map cases. The map cases contain up-to-date detailed mapping of the Essedrian coastline.

He presents a figure who can aid the party: He knows [Essedria](/world/places/essedria.md), having spent time there mapping the changed continent. He has a great love of the creatures there, and considers himself a steward of the slowly-returning biome. 

Kergek is a N-E-R-D, with a snorting laugh and a tendency to provide ridiculously detailed descriptions of things. He hates to admit he doesn't know something.

### Crossing The Gap
It's a 2-week journey across The Gap if they take their time and are careful. If they want to rush, it's 10 days, but encounter rolls go up.

Encounters this time:

5 in 20 (10 in 20 if they hurry)

1: Rope dangling from open air. Climbing it will reveal an open space, in which resides Kergek (if they haven't encountered Smoke and Gale yet)
2: [The Maelstrom](/world/places/the-maelstrom.md). Take a card.

### Attacked!
The Discontent is attacked by confronted by two Djinni - Smoke and Gale - who demand payment for safe passage. The two travel in a huge Cloud Barge pulled by a trio of air elementals.

In their employ is a terrified-looking Derro (Kergek, see above). He'll look pleadingly at the party as he spills drinks and generally screws up his job. 

Smoke and Gale will happily trade - a handful of gems, gold, or something else will buy "passage" but are also OK fighting. They'll run if things get serious.


## Secrets & Clues
Important tidbits:

- [ ] Kergek is actually Celdastrus - the blue dragon they encountered a while back - in the service of Agrona Sef and Thargorax. He's there to sow doubt in the party and, more important, help set the party up for a raid by the Sef. If he can, he'll guide the party to the mouth of the Helgoril, the largest river on Essedria. The river is a perfect spot for an ambush, and, since its flow reversed in the Fall, a great way for Sef's forces to run if they succeed (or fail). He will help the party defeat Rave The Howler, though.
- [ ] The Sef is collecting herself for an attack at the mouth of the Helgoril
- [ ] The [The Maelstrom](/world/places/the-maelstrom.md) will die away if the Seals are destroyed or borne away
- [ ] Even in lead-lined boxes, the Seals cause mild effects. The players will notice some of the primordial stuff growing out of the Leda box, and there will be a tiny area of chaos around the Flux box, where weird things happen: Bugs avoid the area, dust collects faster, rats are belligerent, and any devices/other items decay/break more often
