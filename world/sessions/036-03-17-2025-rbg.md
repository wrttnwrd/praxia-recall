## Mythic places & Info

### Cinderhaven
Dante and Dumas's club. See [Cinderhaven](_sourcebooks/pois/Shadowmuir#Cinderhaven) 

### Darktome Cross
The players start here. Ger'u'do leads them through the West edge of the Cross, South to the water, and then along the water to the Yards.

On the way, they pass:

- A huge, imposing tower. That's the Temple Of The Raven Queen, which is the primary religion here
- An equally huge, imposing obelisk with a detailed engraving of a hand with an eye in its palm. This is the temple of Vecna, which is the other primary religion here
- The Temple of Amoth, with supplicants in tattered robes, flagellating themselves and weeping. The now-Cult of Amoth whip themselves daily, hoping their suffering will bring their fallen god back and with it, their power
- Other makeshift temples thrown up by worshippers, none of whom remember why they built them
- That includes a Temple of Nik'Thim, in which rests a joke book that looks much like the one Mossy left in Salmuir
- What appears to be a small child, sitting on the side of the street, head in hands. Everyone gives the child a wide berth. It is actually a hungry Sorrowsworn that, when disturbed, turns and hisses at whoever is nearby. It won't try to feed if there are multiple people about

### The Gray Haze Yards
As the players turn East, they'll pass:

- Their first views of Hungering Bay
- Docks crowded with ships that aren't being loaded, unloaded, or crewed
- A small group of drunken sailors, remarkable by the fact that they're sitting gloomily rather than shouting and laughing

### Random Encounters
Along the way, the players may run into (D10):

1. 2-3 Obsidian Wing members: Two Shadar Kai and one human, all bearing cudgels. The members will immediately confront the party and demand payment. If the players destroy this "patrol" Kozani will figure out they're there that much faster. Ger'u'do will try to get the players to avoid a conflict
2. Obsidian Wing members shaking down a local business. Same problem as #1, but in this case it's two dwarves and a halfling rogue, all trying to get protection money from a small food shopkeeper
3. A shadowy copy of Celdastrus, flying overhead
4. A rogue who tries to pickpocket one of the characters (preferably Mossy). The rogue is a Shadar-kai teenager named Vada. She could be of use to the players, if desired. She's very well-connected in the non-Wing underworld and also has distant ties to the Temple of Vecna
5. Five Vecna worshippers out proselytizing. 
6. + = nothing


## Events

### Getting To Cinderhaven
The characters probably don't want to go blundering around the city. Fortunately, Ger'u'do is there. See above.
### Meeting Ger'u'do
Ger'u'do Durtbourne was waiting for Malakai to emerge from the Temple. When he sees the characters he rushes to them. He wants to get them out of sight as quickly as possible.

*Hurry, he says. Time for questions later. Now we need to get you out of here and somewhere safe. Whatever the hell that means in this place.*

- He and knows that Kozani has already taken control of much of the Orugar and Elfcap business in [Salmuir](/world/places/salmuir.md). He doesn't know why
- He knows a great deal about how this place formed, its denizens, and why so few really want to be here
- He **does** know that [The Obsidian Wing](/world/groups/the-obsidian-wing.md) is using a set of buildings in The Wards for some unknown purpose, and that she's gone to considerable lengths to hide/protect it. He's also seen traffic moving from those buildings to the Temple Of Shar

Then, she's going to use the chaos to take control of The Outer City, the Residences, and The Trade District. If she succeeds, a lot of people will die. Ger'u'do doesn't know this.
