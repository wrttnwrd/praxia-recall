## Recap
Kergek's transformation into Celdastrus and the appearance of Agrona Sef had RBG fighting for their lives. Fortunately for them, the whole was **not** stronger than the sum of its parts. The distrust between Celdastrus and the Sef, stoked by Rikterr, caused Celdastrus to ignore Agrona's wish that the party not be killed.

It appears Agrona had instructions to retrieve the seals but leave all members of RBG alive. Celdastrus had no such orders or chose to ignore them.

When Celdastrus knocked Drak out of the fight and (in Agrona's eyes) was about to follow up by crushing the ranger's unconscious form, she took action. For two critical rounds, Agrona lured, then disintegrated the adult blue dragon.

But Agrona wasn't done. A few more rounds passed and the entire party was looking, well, pretty rough. Drak had gone down once (not dead) and looked like he might go out again. I believe at least two other PCs were close to unconsciousness. The Sef wasn't doing so great, either. 

Bruised, cut, and partly fried, Rikterr fixed Mossy with a steady gaze, said, "I love you," and activated the sacrifice power on The Light That Sparks. The party healed, and Rikterr's lifeless form tipped to the ground.

Wounded, frustrated, and unable to secure any of the seals, Agrona teleported away.

It's D&D, though, so Rikterr's back, revivified, with some explaining to do...

Key moments: 

- Well-timed magicks from our resident tortle
- A big-time heal from Rikterr
- Drak (nearly) sacrificing his life to keep Celdastrus busy for two rounds
- Mossy being Tabaxi and driving Agrona to distraction, forcing the Sef to move around the battlefield, driving her towards Drak
- **Rikterr using the sacrifice power and coming within 15hp of permanent death**. 2X the damage healed came to (if my math was right) 276 points. Rikterr's total HP? 140. 2x your total = death



## The Plot
On the banks of the Helgoril, the party is ambushed by [Agrona Sef](/world/people/agrona-sef.md) and Celdastrus.

They know that Mossy has [the seals](/world/things/the-seals.md). 

Neither trusts the other. If battle ensues (it will), they each wait for a chance to betray the other.

If Agrona is about to win, Celdastrus will try to stop her and seize Mossy. If Celdastrus is about to win, Agrona will do the same.

Agrona is much, much more powerful than Celdastrus. But she has been ordered: All of RBG must make it to the Fields Of Bone. So she won't kill them. She'll grab the bag and disappear.

Thanks to Celdastrus, Agrona knows exactly who has the bag. She immediately tries to trap Mossy in a Force Cage. Then she casts Enemies Abound on Drak.
