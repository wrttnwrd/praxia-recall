## Recap
The party learned about the ley line, got the Shadow Cloth, and learned about the Underdark.

They now understand the seals, and that Sef is probably going to try to break those seals.

They met w/ Iric, who used powerful scrying magic to locate Elidan. They've designed an attack plan. They're heading his way.

## The Plot
Repairs are underway in the Bazaar, and in the Shadow Veil's complex. The party isn't directly involved with either but can be if they'd like.

RBG is now trying to figure out next moves. Among options are:

1. Seek out the pieces for the first few of the Quinary
2. Find the first Seal (near Porthmor)
3. Do something completely different

On 09 Thaw 07, though, the stars over The Gap go out. The compass on The Discontent begins spinning crazily. 

A little research tells the characters that this would mean a ley line has broken. Disrupting the flow of divine energy to the ley line would do this.
