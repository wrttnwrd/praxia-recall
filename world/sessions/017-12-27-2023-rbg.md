This starts in [Ironstead](/world/places/ironstead.md). It may move on from there, depending on what the players decide to do.

## The Plot
RBG, after defeating an attempt to steal [the Tome Of The First Gods](/world/things/tome-of-the-first-gods.md) from the library in Ironstead, helped rescue Thelwald's family from Agrona's hirelings. Now, they either need to travel to the Gloomspire and attempt to recover the next of the Seals (Lida) or head to Nigh Darul and learn what they can there.

## Travel
RBG will be traveling West from Ironstead. The terrain is near-featureless desert with the cave walls and ceiling almost hidden in the distance. There are occasional small glades of mushrooms, ponds, and larger rocky features.

## Encounters:

**CR 13 Balanced Underdark Encounter Table**
Roll d6. On a 4-6, roll below:

1. Giant Cave Toad (CR 13) hides in the mud under a small pond
2. Sandstorm. Lose 1 day. Make a DC 20 survival check. If succeeded, find shelter, and all players make a DC 15 CON save or gain a point of exhaustion. If failed, as previous but save at disadvantage and take 2d6 damage as sand and rock scrapes and batters the party
3. Displacer Beast Pride (CR 13) - they're hunting and will avoid the players if left alone
4. Two Behirs - https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/16804-behir - (CR 13) - they're hiding in a rocky area and attack as the players move through a ravine
5. Roper and Darkmantle Combo (CR 13)
6. Froghemoth (CR 13)
7. Three Vampiric Mist - https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/2560946-vampiric-mist- (CR 13) - attack during the night
8. A Bulette Pack - https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/16818-bulette - 4-5 Bulettes ambush the party after RBG intrudes on their mating grounds, a sandy area with no nearby rocks or shelter - (CR 13)
9. A pack of Flumphs are drifting by and warn the party about a dangerous area ahead where several travelers have gone missing (the Aboleth lair). Give the players a roll when they pass the trap.
10. Storm - if within 6 miles of the Whispering Abyss, the players experience a violent thunderstorm that obscures everything. Hail and rain pelt the party, and travel becomes impossible. As the storm diminishes, the players must make a DC 25 Survival check to not get lost. If they fail, add 1 day to the journey.

## Scenes & Settings

### The First Abberation
An Aboleth - https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/16762-aboleth - has taken up residence a mile from the Gloomspire. It enslaved a Sverfneblin (long dead) to build it a complex trap. Anyone falling into the trap is dropped from the surface into an underground lake, where the Aboleth attacks. Players w/ a passive PER > 18 notice something off about the ground up ahead.

If the players notice the trap, they avoid it and pass what appears to be a small pond. The pond is actually a narrow opening at the top of the underground lake where water has pushed up to the surface. The aboleth will use its lair and regional affects to attack the party.

Reason: The Aboleth, in spite of its great power, has fallen under the influence of Leviathus. It feels compelled to defend this area. It also seeks a way out of its prison and up to the surface.

### The Gloomspire & The Whispering Abyss
A 2-3 day journey from Ironstead.

Conditions: As the players near the Gloomspire, the "weather" worsens. First, it's a low, wispy fog. Then it's a thick fog. Then rain. Then a driving rain, and finally, as they get to the Spire, it's a raging thunderstorm with high winds. This is all thanks to Leviathus.

## In The Gloomspire
Attracted and charmed by Leviathus, five Deep Scions - https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/2560769-deep-scion -  have taken up residence. All have 120hp, do 14pts/claw attack, 16/19 Battleaxe damage. All have Psychic Screech but require a DC 18 WIS save, not DC 13. They are led by one greater Scion, which is also a Kraken Priest with 150hp (https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/2560858-kraken-priest).

They are all concealed in the cave and are ready. One of them followed the party down through the Spire. 

Their job: Prevent the removal of the Seal or, if that becomes impossible, summon Leviathus.

If the battle appears to be going badly, the priest will attempt to flee or otherwise warn Leviathus. Play accordingly.


## Secrets & Clues
Important tidbits:

- [ ] [Agrona Sef](/world/people/agrona-sef.md) is free, but she is too wounded and exhausted to confront the party. She's attempting to stop the party from behind the scenes, but because they're in the Underdark, it's very, very difficult
- [ ] [Leviathus](/world/people/leviathus.md) **might** be willing to make a deal, **if** the party is willing to help it leave The Whispering Abyss
- [ ] The Duergar are tailing RBG to guarantee safety of [the Tome of the First Gods](/world/things/tome-of-the-first-gods.md). If the players get into too much trouble, they may help. At any time, you can have the players make a DC 25 Stealth check. The Duergar are **very** good at hiding, and this is their natural terrain



## Recap
Well, **that** was interesting.

The players had a brief shopping trip at Eggelthrak's Artisanal Goods. The proprietor is the only Duergar ever known to have a sense of humor. Because of that, he had to move and now lives in Ironstead. 

RBG stocked up on potions and other goodies, then set out for the Gloomspire, Tome in hand, paw, and claw. 

_Reminder: The Gloomspire rests on the shores of an underwater lake called The Whispering Abyss. You don't know much beyond what Zeilhurd - the Ironstead librarian - told you, but before the Fall there were communities on the shores of the Abyss. They're now gone._

The party saw a pride of displacer beasts. They also made quick work of a family of vampiric mists. Both are typical for the region.

The next morning, though, they encountered a group of Flumphs that warned RBG about something bad up ahead. A short time later, the ground turned from the rocky, dusty moraine of the Underdark to a slimy, seaweed-covered marsh. 

Sulomorn The Tidebender lay in wait. As RBG approached, an aboleth emerged from the slimy water ahead. Rikterr did Rikterr, charing the beast and beating it about the head and shoulders (it doesn't have shoulders - or a head, really). 

But something wasn't right. Rikterr couldn't seem to connect, and the aboleth seemed to be pulling its punches.

After several rounds of futility - enabled by a catastrophically bad series of saving throws - the  party realized they weren't fighting the real Sulomorn. They were battling a Phantasmal Force duplicate. It took several rounds for everyone to figure out they weren't fighting the **real** enemy. 

By then, the real Sulomorn grabbed Rikterr and dragged him under the surface of the inky pool in which the creature was hiding. Cregyn and Mossy followed soon after.

Drak spent most of the combat trying to calm the Twins. Once they were settled down, he turned towards the suddenly-quiet battleground, only to see ripples as Mossy's head disappeared into the waters of Sulomorn's slime pool.

Fade to black.
