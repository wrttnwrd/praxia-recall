## Recap
They told Celdastrus about Thargorax

## The Plot
With a newly-upgraded Villain's Discontent, RBG needs to figure out their next move.

With Hexxam and some of Team Spear headed off to slow Agrona, RBG can fly straight to Godelis to find the 2nd Seal, which hangs on the edge of the Underdark.

Travel time to Godelis: 5 days

## East Godelis
There is no town or city on East Godelis. It's a fairly barren piece of rock with a small tuft of forest on the steeply-sloping western side of the island, which is sheltered by the equally steep Eastern side of West Godelis.

There is a large cave entrance in the western slope. That leads to the Underdark. "Leads to" is a kind interpretation: There's a treacherous, 2-mile-long stair that adventurers can take down to the entryway to the Underdark. It's treacherous. Characters either need to successfully make multiple DEX/athletics checks, use magic, or find another clever way to navigate.

But as they draw closer, the players find two things:

1. The seal, as it turns out, is massive. It lies partly buried at the entrance to the cave, dangling over the stair. It's mostly dug out. But it weighs almost a ton and RBG can't just carry it away. They **do** need to either take it away, or hide it so thoroughly that even Agrona can't find it. They're going to need to use the Discontent or some other means
2. Lysander explains that someone has  area's defenses are considerable and clearly recently beefed up. As it stands, the Discontent could not safely hover long enough to lower gear, get connected to the Seal, raise it back up, stow it, and then be on their way. They'd be taking heavy fire from casters and ballistae the entire time. A few well-placed hits from ground emplacements and the Discontent could be badly damaged

Let the players discuss their options.

### Significant Defenses
If they decide on a frontal assault:

_Almost as soon as you're low enough to see individual people moving about, the ship starts taking fire. A series of massive ballista bolts fly past the prow as another embeds itself in the ship's hull with a massive WHUMP that you feel in the soles of your feet. A moment later, a series of flaming spheres, probably from a catapult, fly over the port side of the ship. One grazes the ship's rigging and sprays burning tar or a similar substance across the deck. An unlucky crew member topples over the size in flames and screaming._

The Discontent might make it down, and the players might deal w/ the defenses. It'll be a near thing, and the Discontent will likely take heavy damage and be unable to lift the Seal.

Provide lots of chances for them to retreat.

## The Meat Grinder
If the players decide to land and attack, they enter a meat grinder:

1. At the start, 2 troll mutates (https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/3731058-troll-mutate) and random guards, etc.
2. But creatures start coming out of the underdark
3. At round 3: A patrol of three Duergar Warlords (https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/2560798-duergar-warlord)
4. At round 6, a patrol of five Duergar Warlords
5. At round 10, a Troll Titan (https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/2560777-dire-troll)
6. If the party makes it that far, they've killed all the monsters, the workers are milling about horrified, and they can do as they wish

## Getting Sneaky
The party can also sneak down, disable the ballistae, and then quietly loop cables around the Seal and use the Discontent to swipe it.

If they do, they'll certainly end up fighting, but they'll have cover.

## Also: Crossing The Line
On Day 3, the Discontent will cross the Equator. Aside from Cregyn, this is the first time the party has crossed the Equator on a ship. 

The players are called above decks because of an emergency. Give Cregyn a chance to realize what's going on.

Once up there, they see that the Discontent is hovering mere feet over the ocean. The entire crew arrayed around the gangway. Fenlal is wearing some kind of scaly garment and wielding a sad-looking pitchfork. He is flanked by two sailors draped in seaweed.

Fenlal turns to the two sailors and says (this is ridiculous) and then declares: "Let those of you who have not yet crossed the ley line step forward." and then "Poseidon greets you! You are now part of an illustrious cadre who have sailed across the ley line. You must be initiated. First: Do you swear upon whatever gods, friends, relatives, or foods you hold dear to never reveal this ritual to the uninitiated?" then "Second: Look to your right. And your left. And in front. And behind. This is your home. Do you swear to protect it and those upon it?" Then "Good. Now, it is time for the ceremony!"

They must:

- Wear funny hats made from bits of rope wrapped around spare feedbags from the twins
- Eat a disgusting brew of vinegar, cinnamon, and fetid shark
- Sing a song (+5 w/ the crew if they actually sing)

Afterwards is a fair amount of celebration.
