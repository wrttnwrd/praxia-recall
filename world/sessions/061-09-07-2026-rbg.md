## Politics & Prey

### Strong Start
See Secrets And Clues. Because of Rikterr's sacrifice, the curse of the Five Fold Brand is broken. But so is the quest. Zariel has ordered Tiamat to put a stop to RBG's assaults on her domain.

Tiamat dispatches her Abishai, who attack at the very start of the game, swooping in on the party. The attackers are:

1 [Green Abishai](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/2560838-green-abishai), leading
5 [White Abishai](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/2560959-white-abishai)

See the [Tiamat's Wrath encounter](https://www.dndbeyond.com/encounters/44282e7a-eef6-43b1-9b31-99484f1792ed).

If the battle goes horribly wrong, Bel's troops show up:

5 Barbed Devils
1 Bone Devil
1 Ice Devil named Antipathy

After the fight, Antipathy talks to the survivors:

> "My lord Bel would speak with you. He holds no love for your enemies, and has a proposition. Come."

If the players follow, they meet Bel. If they don't, Antipathy lets them go on their way, but Bel offers no further help.

### Conversation With Bel
Describe entrance to the Forge: Huge iron gates that open to let the players into a volcano. Lava is everywhere, and the heat is blistering (over 120 degrees). 

> Extreme heat rules apply. DC 5 the first hour, +1 every hour after. Failure means you get a level of Exhaustion. Roll every hour.

The lava is fed through tubes to various forges. At each forge a Fire Giant toils, creating weapons for the front lines of the Blood War. Most of the weapons are huge, meant to be wielded by Large or larger creatures. They include javelins, swords, armor, shields, and spears.

Suspended above a central magma pool is a terrible machine. You're not sure what it does, but it looks awful. This is Bel's latest project, and it's meant to help him overthrow Zariel. 

> The machine is a particularly potent Hellfire Engine, CR 25. But it's not yet operable.

Bel has a simple offer for the party:

> "Your friend's sacrifice broke the curse upon you. But Zariel had plans for the thousand souls of Silgorin. She is enraged, and she will tolerate Tiamat's scheming no longer. She has ordered the dragon queen to kill you. The attack you just faced will be the first of many."

Travel above ground is now least incredibly dangerous. Devils aren't a monolithic entity, so it's not like **every** devil is hunting for them. But Tiamat's abishai are casting a wide net.

> "Zariel stole my throne. Tiamat is her most powerful ally, and without her, I can re-take my rightful place as Duke Of Avernus. We can help each other."

Bel offers to provide the party a guide - an imp named Bitterness ("Bitter, for short"). The guide will help them by steering them through the complicated, twisting nature of Avernus's surface to Arkhan's Tower. There, if they can defeat the tower's denizens, they'll have a shortcut to Tiamat's Monument. This assumes the players still intend to confront the dragon queen.

If the players attack Tiamat, Bel will commit his forces to attacking the abishai. He will **only** do this if the players attack the dragon queen. A group of devils attacking the abishai is unremarkable, but Tiamat will figure out who's responsible. If she and Zariel both retaliate, Bel could suffer. If Tiamat is rebound or dead, though, Bel only has to contend with Zariel.

What does Bel get out of this? Tiamat won't end up dead. But she'll end up greatly reduced in power, and Bel will have an opportunity to decimate her Abishai troops. That would level the playing field with Zariel.

## Secrets and clues


1. Zariel is enraged that the party ruined her plans for Silgorin Below. She has told Tiamat that enough is enough, and it's time to end this. 
2. Bel has gotten wind of this and will do everything in his power short of direct confrontation to help the party in their quest
3. Demons and devils in Avernus can listen in on the party line. The party discovers this when Vaxith or someone else lets slip something the party said over the line but not out loud, or when Enthraxis reveals it just to fuck with them
4. Killing a god greatly increases your power. That's why Asmodeus killed Amoth
5. There are powerful guardians over Tiamat's Lair, including one with the Hand Of Vecna. He - Arkhan The Cruel - is the one who works to break the Chains Of Gleynir
