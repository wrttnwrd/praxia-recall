### The Temple Of Shar
The destroyed temple is in the ruins of The Mists.

_Inside, the temple reeks of mold and rotting vegetation. The only light filters in from the open doors. It is stiflingly humid, and the floor is saturated with salt water and slightly springy - it gives slightly as you walk. A thin mist of water vapor rises from the floor. Everywhere you  see the circle-in-circle symbol of the Goddess Of Loss. Scattered about are tents, sleeping bags, cooking utensils and other detritus of the less-fortunate. There are also pieces of furniture that don't belong in a temple of Shar. Clearly, people have taken advantage of what shelter the ruins can provide. Those folk are not here now, though. At least, most of them aren't. Spread around the dais are the head, torso, and limbs of an adult male (probably human). Elsewhere in the temple are two other intact bodies._

If the players inspect, the bodies have been drained of blood and have broad, ragged wounds on their throats.

If they enter the basement:

_The basement is cold - almost frigid - but still damp. As you descend the stairs, a breeze hits your face. Across the room, a large standing mirror glows with blue-gray light. The breeze seems to come from the mirror itself. Just ahead of you, five figures move towards the opening._

These are the nosferatu and two [Gloom Weavers](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/2560909-shadar-kai-gloom-weaver)

All the nosferatu wants to do is escape back to Shadowmir. It only attacks if the characters try to stop it. If the players arrive at night, it will take 4 rounds for the portal to fully open. If they arrive during the day, the portal is closed, and the nosferatu and shadar-kai aren't there yet.

## Events

### The barroom brawl
It's chaos. 

Every round, all players roll a d4. On a 1, roll on the random table.

Felbrow and his gang will form a wedge and try to get out. Felbrow is a bandit crime lord. His two bodyguards are hobgoblin warlords armed with cudgels (1d8 damage) and lower-quality armor (leather, AC 16).

### The Chase
Fake Skith will try to escape the brawl as quickly as possible. He will run out of the bar towards The Outskirts.

If they're in the bar, and he takes damage, he'll use Misty Escape to get to the door and run.

Along the way, he will do **anything** he can to lose his pursuers. Points where he can try something:

1. Ducking down the narrow alleys as they move from the Outer City towards the Mists - the players can try to follow, or they can make a survival check to try to catch him around the other side
2. Vaulting up on rooftops. He can leap up to a rooftop and try to lose the players that way. He'll choose a narrow, hard-to-navigate area. If a player follows him, he'll cast Darkness, then attempt to trip the player off the roof - DEX/athletics so D20 + 8 w/ disadvantage for darkness. If a fail, the player falls 20 feet (2d6).
3. If he's being pursued by only one player, he'll try to use confusion or fear. 
4. He might run into a building w/ a window, then banf through to another location.
5. At the wall between the Outer City and the Mists, he'll vault over
6. If he's close to the Temple, he'll use arcane gate to teleport, but that has to be line-of-site, so the players will see it happen

Reminders: 
- Only dash 3+CON modifier times, DC 10 CON save after that, point of exhaustion on fail.
- Skith has a +6 modifier because of his increased speed. So his checks are at D20 + 10 (yes, that's right)
- Skith has advantage on chase checks

## Recap For 1/25

### Salmuir By Night, Part 2
*Misinformation, detective work, and barroom brawls.*

On their way to visit the Library Of Salmuir, Notorious RBG noticed a young waif handing out leaflets. She's standing at the gates between the Trade District and The Residences. Mossy grabbed one of the leaflets - it was an embarrassingly poor drawing of stick figure vampires battling other stick figure humanoids, being staked with toothpick-shaped weaponry. The leaflets claim that the Haddad Combine are actually vampires who are using the city for food and making the case for new leadership.

A small bribe of pocket bacon loosened the girl's tongue. She told Mossy her name is Thalia. She can't read the pamphlets herself (she can't read) but tells RBG she was given the pamphlets by a guy who works for a Wrack dealer named Felbrow. She also tells them where she picked up the pamphlets.

Mossy purchased the remaining pamphlets and sent Thalia on her way with the promise of a job at the Clever Luck.

Leaflets in hand, RBG headed up to the Library. There, they saw a familiar face: Jen, formerly a library intern, now librarian, helped the party dig up information about the Nosferatu and the Shadowfell. She also helped RBG narrow down the source of the leaflets.

A quick sojourn into the Outer City (during which Drak showed off the Twins' future as circus performers) gave RBG a chance to scare the bejesus out of some drug dealers got a location for Felbrow and let Rikterr get in touch with his murder hobo side. The Church Of The Raven Queen got an unlooked-for but welcome donation later that day.

Returning to the Palace to investigate the scene of the Haddad murder, RBG learned that the area is extremely secure, but the guard present the night of the murder (Skith) isn't there. 

They met with the Haddad and learned a great deal about Salmuir's rulers, their motivations, and why someone might murder them.

Then, RBG headed back down to The Outskirts to visit some drug dealers. They visited a bar (The Cheeky Monkey), found Skith and Felbrow, attempted subtlety, failed, and are now embroiled in a bar fight.

That's where we'll pick up next time.

19. There is likely a portal in The Mists. Portals open in locations of great death and destruction, and they tend to open on the new moon. Which, as it happens, is tonight.
