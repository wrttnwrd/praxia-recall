## The Battle To Save Silgorin Below

### Strong Start
They're outside the bunker. **Remember encounters**.

1. Urguros
2. Enthraxis's death
3. Weather
4. Time slip
5. Rogue Lemure
6. Nothing

### Potential Scenes

1. Confronting Urguros
2. Time loop
3. Enthraxis's death: You round a corner and see the remnants of a recent battle. 
4. The first chain room: The room is filled with a deafening mix of screeching metal, clanking chains, and the acrid smell of metal. It's stiflingly hot in here. There's a single chain anchored to a huge cleat. Imps and wretched lemures swarm over the cleat, applying oils and casting spells to keep it in place. The PCs can easily scare off the Imps and Lemures. Mossy can figure out how to destroy the cleat, too. If they do, the entire city lurches sickeningly.
5. Weather

## Secrets and clues

1. Vaxith, The Hand In The Dark, has been following them since they left Urush Gul. He'll show himself if the party gets into big trouble or, if Mossy dons her goggles, she'll see him on a DC 25 PER roll
2. The Chain Devil known only as The Binder is in charge of winching the city down into The River Styx. The Binder's machinery is in a central location under the city
3. Demons and devils in Avernus can listen in on the party line. The party discovers this when Vaxith or someone else lets slip something the party said over the line but not out loud, or when Enthraxis reveals it just to fuck with them
4. Killing a god greatly increases your power. That's why Asmodeus killed Amoth
5. There are powerful guardians over Tiamat's Lair, including one with the Hand Of Vecna. He - Arkhan The Cruel - is the one who works to break the Chains Of Gleynir

## Fantastic Location: The Suspended Machine
>You're standing on a metal catwalk. In front of you, two enormous gears turn slowly, taking up tension in eight enormous chains that loop through the gears and down to the surface of Avernus hundreds of feet below. The River Styx oozes past, a purple ribbon. The din of the chains is deafening. Sparks fly off of the gears as the arcane machinery does its work. As you watch, the gears ratchet tighter. The chains grind another link and, with a screech, the entire city ratchets downward 20-30 feet closer to the surface and certain destruction. 
>
 There's been a battle here. Demon ichor pools everywhere, intermixed with the ashes of dead devils. Four dark, chain-wrapped figures confront you. They immediately attack.

The chain devils  will attack on sight. If they're prepared, then the Abishai (Othaz The Blue) is invisible when the party first arrives. 

Each gear has 200hp and a damage threshold of 50. They are be hard to destroy. An artificer can do it with a DC 20 roll, though. Make them describe how they do it.

The gears are hot to the touch. A fail will cause 2d10 hp heat damage (not fire).

Othaz is in a tough spot. He's responsible for the chain machinery. But he's also responsible for ensuring the party makes it to Tiamat. He'll probably stay invisible as long as possible, attacking in an attempt to drive off the characters. He'll use Wall Of Force to divide the party or to protect the gears, use Charm to try to get them to stop, and find any other possible trick to get them away. Failing that, he'll run, using invisibility to escape, and let the party complete their mission. Better Zariel's wrath than Tiamat's.


## Specific encounters

### Enthraxis's Death
> Piles of ash and pools of ichor are everywhere, as are blast marks and arrows. In the center of it all, an ultraloth lies on the ground, dying. It is Enthraxis. (they can talk to him if desired, or ask his corpse questions - he dies soon)

Enthraxis may reveal:

1. That powerful demons and devils can hear what the party says via telepathy
2. That Arkhan The Cruel is at Tiamat's Lair
3. The nature of the chains
4. That Urguros is still out there, rampaging through the city



### Battle w/ Urguros
Urguros, having routed Enthraxis and his troops, confronts the PCs.

*A huge demon with a boarlike head thuds to the ground and stands on hooved legs. With breath that reeks of rotting flesh and sulfur, it snarls at you. 
"You. You think you've saved them when all you'v e done is damned them. **We** will save them, by ending their miserable lives. Only then will they be rid of this place."* 

This fight is pure chaos. The players should have almost no problem dealing with the attack, but be sure to describe the quasits swarming on the players, dretches flinging themselves at them, and Urguros staying back.
