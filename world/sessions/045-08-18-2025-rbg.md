## Outline

### Ardmon Ignus Lacqui
#### The battle against Ardmon Ingus Lacqui
Ardmon has been using TEMU as a way to sow a little chaos and curry favor w/ Tiamat by screwing w/ Surtur.

He normally resides in the 9 Hells in the Iron City (in Dis).

Players will notice his hands are upside-down.

Quirks:

- He has several small fire elementals who take messages to and from the shop floor, and he's constantly interrupting conversations to check in with them
- He has a harried female salamander who is constantly trying to keep him organized
- He constantly brags about how some cool thing he has he got for free because of who he is
- His office is festooned with photos of him with what the players assume are famous people from the plane of fire, including Surtur

- In a fight, he never shuts up

Company name: Thal'keth Erimir Malfeth Urzith (TEMU) or "The Eternal Market Reigns Supreme"

## The Battle
https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195178-rakshasa

The very first thing Ardmon does is Baleful Command. The next round, if BC doesn't reset, he'll turn invisible, skulk around, and try to surprise a character.

Ardmon will do everything he can to imprison the party. If party members "die," they come to awareness in a prison located under the TEMU building. He thinks he might be able to use them to rise in caste - if he can get the party to work for him, he'll gain power and move up ranking. 

Additional salamanders will appear every 10 rounds, unless Ardmon dies. **If** Ardmon dies, the salamanders just stop what they're doing and start arguing about next steps.

If Ardmon dies, he'll reappear in the Iron City. He doesn't take any of this personally. In fact, he finds the party stimulating. The party may see him again if they head to Avernus.

### If they're imprisoned
The players wake up without their gear, bound and gagged. 

The area they're in is climate-controlled, which is why the players are alive in spite of the lack of amulets of comfort.

Ardmon's assistant, an impeccably dressed, halfling-sized Magmin, asks the players to wait a moment. They leave the room, returning w/ Ardmon.

Ardmon has a simple proposition: Ally with him, help him get TEMU to the prime material plane. If he can do that, he'll gain tremendous power in the circles of the Rakshasa.

He'll give the players back their gear, **if** they agree:

1. To help him secure a foothold on the Prime Material Plane
2. And behave themselves
3. In exchange, he'll give them their stuff back, and let them finish their mission so they can get the Badge Of Zariel


### After
Ardmon's assistant, Veerna, approaches the party and tells them that, if they'd like to accompany her, Ardmon had specific directives for whoever defeated him.

If the party wants to explore the building, they'll find:

1. Ardmon's quarters. Palatial quarters comprised of a living room, bedroom, and a balcony overlooking the Cinder Wastes and the column of fire at the center of the plane.
	1. In his bedroom, his bed seems... weird. If they pull back the sheets, the mattress is comprised of dead and living insects
	2. In the living room, a meticulously set dining table with large, cushy chairs
	3. In the kitchen, a wide array of knives and saws, clearly designed for large corpses
	4. Photos of him with what the players assume are famous people from the plane of fire, including Surtur
	5. His book, Tapping The Fire Within: My Journey From Serf To Baron, features prominently on a coffee table in the living room
2. In those quarters is:
	1. The Wand Of Fate
	2. A Bag Of Colding, which can hold 100 pounds of foodstuffs, keeping it fresh. Right now, it holds 100 pounds of human flesh. It's sitting on a shelf in the kitchen
	3. Random bracers
### Loot: For Mossy
Ardmon Ignus's prize possessions are a Wand Of Fate (in a case in his quarters) and a set of +2 Molten Bronze Skin (half plate, worn) that gives the wearer fire resistance and doesn't impose disadvantages to Stealth or rolls.

## Recap
RBG battled Ardmon Ignus Lacqui, defeating his salamanders and scaring him off. It was a tough fight, owing in part to the fact that Rakshasa are nearly immune to magic and magical effects. 

However, you filled him arrows and bludgeoned him with axes, and he had to made a quick run for the door.

Wrathskald will be in a much better place without having to compete with knock-off competitors from Thal'keth Erimir Malfeth Urzith (Ardmon's company). You have no idea what that means, since you don't speak Abyssal.

Once he was gone, his strange assistant walked you upstairs. Lacqui, apparently, had stated that whoever defeated him would get access to his quarters.

### A little information
Also, Drak's detective work bore fruit: You now know there **is** someone on Praxia who has gone to Avernus and returned to tell of it. He's an orc shaman named Rugorim.

According to the tale Drak heard, though, Rugorim would have to be at least 150 years old. The typical orc lives 30-40 years. 

Rugorim is rumored to live far up on the NW coast of Silidria.
