## Recap
The *Villain's Discontent* made landfall north of the Helgoril, over what was once a dense, lush forest. The Fall and the firestorm that followed reduced the area to ash. RBG traveled through a graveyard of blackened trees, gray, dead ground and bare rock flung hundreds of miles in the ejecta of Amoth's impact.

On Kergek's recommendation, you traveled south, headed for the banks of the great river. That's Rave The Howler's territory and gives RBG a chance to hunt for the creature.

*Talking to Kergek, you realized no one really knows **what** Rave is, or how long he/she/it has been roaming Essedria. It might be hundreds of years. It might be a few. What you **do** know is Rave once haunted northern Essedria but may have been driven south by the cooling climate.*

The landscape wasn't just ashen waste, though. You found mysterious patches of greenery dotting the wastes: Vines, ground cover, and even small trees clustered around small springs and other water sources. That didn't make sense: You would expect more random, shrunken growth across the region as plants reestablished themselves. 

Turned out these small oases weren't accidents. A small circle of Ents were planting and tending to tree farms all across Central-Eastern Essedria, hoping to speed the forest's recovery.

RBG spoke with one of the ents. You offered to help. The ent was skeptical - you are awfully fleshy for such work - but they gave you tend seedlings to plant across Essedria.

You spent an unexpectedly restful night amidst the greenery, then set out the next day.

Partway through the day you nearly ran afoul of a small fomorian hunting party. Some really good dice rolls on your part and some **really** bad ones by the fomorians kept you hidden.

You stopped in another of the ent glades to rest for the night. But four fomorian deep crawlers attacked. They pulled themselves over rocks and trees on twisted limbs and **tried** to use their psychic attacks on the party. But with some great teamwork, RBG killed all but one of the warped, boil-covered giants. Mossy failed her roll and got hit by the curse, but only at the end of the battle, and only after she'd stuck daggers in every chunk of fomorian flesh she could find.

After that, you all decided to have a nice nap.

Note: Cregyn is still on the *Villain's Discontent*, helping with repairs. He can return to the party any time.


## The Plot
Hunting for Rave The Howler

The party comes ashore north of the mouth of the Helgoril, in what used to be a dense, lush forest. It's now a twisted landscape.

Kergek recommends proceeding south from here. That will bring them to the Helgoril, which winds its way to the Field Of Bone. It also puts Rave The Howler's territory between them and the Helgoril.

## Scenes & Settings

1. The scorched forest
2. The fomorian procession: If someone's alert, they feel/hear heavy footfalls. If not, Kergek may. A group of fomorians walk slowly by, carrying several carcasses of large fish. If anyone speaks Giant, they're talking about the catch and mention "how Rave will like it." If the party stays hidden, they move past
3. The Ent tree farm / The oasis
4. Mireborn ambush: Rave has been tracking the party. He sends four Deep Crawlers to seek them out. Rave isn't smart, but he's cunning, and he wants to see how dangerous RBG is.




- [ ] Kergek is actually Celdastrus - the blue dragon they encountered a while back - in the service of Agrona Sef and Thargorax. He's there to sow doubt in the party and, more important, help set the party up for a raid by the Sef. If he can, he'll guide the party to the mouth of the Helgoril, the largest river on Essedria. The river is a perfect spot for an ambush, and, since its flow reversed in the Fall, a great way for Sef's forces to run if they succeed (or fail). He will help the party defeat Rave The Howler, though.
- [ ] The Sef is collecting herself for an attack at the mouth of the Helgoril
- [ ] Even in lead-lined boxes, the Seals cause mild effects. The players will notice some of the primordial stuff growing out of the Leda box, and there will be a tiny area of chaos around the Flux box, where weird things happen: Bugs avoid the area, dust collects faster, rats are belligerent, and any devices/other items decay/break more often
