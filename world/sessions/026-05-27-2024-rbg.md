## Recap
After the battle with Celdastrus and Agrona Sef, RBG got some much-needed rest, repaired the Villain's Discontent, and headed NW in search of Celdastrus's lair.

Celdastrus built his home in what was once the dry, sandy plains of north eastern Essedria. He used his break to melt and fuse the sand into an underground glass castle of sorts. He also surrounded it in thunderstorms, so it wasn't that hard to find.

In the low-risk dungeon crawl that followed, RBG found:

- An enormous kinetic sculpture of a dragonfly
- A kind of art museum complete with an animated statuette that provided a description of the most famous works
- A copy of The Histories Of The Seguar Deones (the gods that followed/the later gods). That history speaks of how the heroes (Pentar Virones), after the battle at the Second City, seized the Stygian Gate for themselves and attempted to summon Thargorax to Praxia. It doesn't say what happened. There was a woodcut showing this: !ianjlurie_In_the_style_of_an_old_woodcut_five_heroes_stand_in_f_96272276-b354-4e30-8324-64d554ecea50.png
- And, they found the real Kergek! The aging halfling cartographer has offered to travel with the party and provide what knowledge he can, all while he improves his own maps of post-Fall Essedria. Kergek reveals that he knows an underground route to an ancient drow temple that lies at the Eastern edge of the Fields Of Bone. It's a very long trip, and might be better taken by airship. But the unpredictable weather makes travel by air extremely risky

Now what? You can seek out the Hag and try to create the Door That Opens. Or you can head to the Fields Of Bone and see what's happening there. Or you can open a nice corner store in the ruins of the town where you faced Agrona Sef. 

## The Plot
Open world. The party may:

### Recuperate
The _Discontent_ sustained some cosmetic damage Lysander would like to repair. The party is very beat up. It might be a good idea to rest up, heal, and consider next steps

### Go After The Hoard
Celdastrus's lair is in the plains 200 miles NW of Mistwharf. The dragon had been terrorizing the entire region for generations, held in check only by a well-organized military. With [The Fall](/world/events/the-fall.md), nothing restrained his greed or cruelty.

If the party wants to find the lair (and the hoard), they can:

1. Find some small bit of Celdastrus. He wore a set of toe rings, and one of them survived the disintegration. They could use locate object to try to find its twin, but they'll have to get close
2. Think about where blue dragons typically live, and what this part of [Essedria](/world/places/essedria.md) was like pre-Fall. The only place a blue dragon would've been comfortable was the dry, grassy plains NW of there
3. Do something I don't expect

The entrance to Celdastrus's lair is **not** well hidden. It is surrounded by crashing thunderstorms, making it easier to spot the region.

The hoard contains 3500gp, 10500gp in gems, and many, many objects d'art. Celdastrus had particular tastes and didn't see accumulating gold as a meaningful pastime. 

Overall, the players can loot about 12k gp in treasure.

### The Lair
Celdastrus fused sand into glass to create a lair with gleaming walls and ornate floors. Many rooms are elaborately furnished.

#### The Hoard
Celdastrus fancied himself a collector of art. Different parts of the lair have carved gemstones, glass, paintings, sculptures, etc.

In particular:

1. At the entrance to his lair is an enormous kinetic sculpture of a dragonfly. If the players are cautious, they'll detect something very large moving just inside the door that appears to be a creature. The dragonfly is a beautiful, metal sculpture 7 feet wide by 8 feet long. Its wings flap slowly and realistically, antenna bobbing gently
2. The hoard display room is dominated by a 1/2-scale glass sculpture of Celdastrus himself
3. In his personal chambers, Celdastrus kept a copy of The Histories Of The Seguar Deones (the later gods). It speaks of the same war between the Second and Later gods. But it has an entire section written in a cypher of some kind. If translated, that section speaks of how the Five Heroes (Pentar Virones), after the battle at the Second City, seized the Stygian Gate for themselves and attempted to summon Thargorax to Praxia. It doesn't say what happened. The art depicts the heroes in front of the gate with a five-headed serpent coming through. 4. In the hoard display room is a glass bust of a bespectacled female human with her eyes closed. If any players come close, it suddenly opens its eyes and says, "Welcome to Lord Celdastrus's galleries. If you face me towards an object, I shall describe it." #### The Treasury
This is where the money is:

- 3500 gp
- 10500gp in various blue gems
- A beautiful blue horn that, if gently blown, produces a tiny thundercloud, complete with thunder, lightning, and hail. Worth 5k GP in the hands of the right buyer

- A number of tomes on becoming a lich. Nothing valuable or secret

#### Traps and Dangers
1. The entire lair has a fine tracery of silver lines in the floor, walls, and ceilings. Celdastrus's inner chamber has a large, silver bell-shaped object into which all those lines feed. This was his defense system. If he breathed lightning into the bell, the entire lair became electrified, sending a 7th level lightning bolt (7d6) throughout
2. The treasury room has a glyph on the door. 45 seconds after the party enters, the room fills with electricity, causing 50pts of lightning damage to everyone present. Dex save 17
3. There are random sinkholes throughout. Every 30 feet traveled, roll percentile. < 25% means there's a sinkhole of 1d6X10ft depth. When the players walk over it, the floor starts to buckle. DEX save DC 12 keeps them from breaking the floor surface and falling in. Standard falling damage, but feel free to add in collapse/suffocation
4. Celdastrus maintained a nest of giant centipedes and other insects. Any time you fall through a sinkhole there's a chance you'll disturb a nest. In that case, hundreds of 2-foot-long centipedes scramble over you, inflicting 4 points of poison damage
#### Surprise!
Surprise! There really **is** a Kergek, and he really **is** a cartographer. He's a halfling, though, and very old. 

Kergek has his own study and bedchamber next door to the treasury and just off of Celdastrus's personal chambers. Celdastrus kept him in his employ to create beautiful maps. In particular, he has a map of Praxia that he's been working on for years. He'll provide that to the players.

There are various others that are difficult to identify. At least one appears to be a map of the Inner and Outer Planes. There is also a map of The Abyss (not from first-hand experience). 

Kergek refers to Celdastrus as "that old scamp" and says he "wasn't that hard to live with, once you got past the random electrocutions." He does say the dragon appreciated art and often "recruited" artists like himself.

Kergek **also** knows that the private entrance connects to a system of tunnels leading to the Essedrian Underdark and a drow temple (see Team Spear histories) on the Eastern edge of the Fields Of Bone. It's a 1500-mile hike.
