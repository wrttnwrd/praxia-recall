## The Albion
Start w/ the battle on the Albion. 

First scene: Crew cheering as one of the xebecs loses its mast and drifts away, unable to aim its guns. Another xebec is already a hundred yards away in the same condition. You see the bow of another, tipped up from the surface. The other two, though, are bracketing the Albion. The Albion has lost its mizzen. Six guns are off their carriages, and the ship is listing to port badly enough that it can't elevate those guns. That's where one of the xebecs is trying to board.

1. Every round, have something happen to each character. 
	1. Gun loading is in three steps - sponge/load, aim and then fire. Each requires a roll.
	2. The topman will need to fix something and get shot at by a musket (d8)
	3. Bosun is in the fray, repelling boarders and trying to push off the xebecs so they can get another broadside off
	4. Pritchard has a steady stream of casualties: RP these
	5. Marine is shooting at people
2. Be sure to narrate what's happening - the smell, noise, splinters. This is chaos beyond what characters experience in a typical D&D fight
3. Describe the xebecs, and the attackers (diverse, wearing barbary clothes of varying types, skilled). This is a hard fight. Make it clear they may or may not win this.

### Random battle encounters

**1. Barrett’s Bayonet (Silas Barrett – Marine)**  
A corsair vaults the rail and charges an officer. Silas can intercept with a bayonet thrust (melee +2, DC 12). If he fails, the officer is cut down.  

**2. Pike’s Steady Shot (Josiah Pike – Sharpshooter)**  
A pirate sniper in the rigging is picking off crew. Pike can line up a “steady shot” (ranged +4, DC 13). Success: sniper falls. Failure: another crewman drops dead.  

**3. Carter’s Cannon Jam (Obadiah Carter – Gunner’s Mate)**  
A cannon misfires with powder hissing dangerously. Carter can clear it (DEX +3, DC 12). If he succeeds, next shot deals +5 damage. If not, it explodes (1d6 damage nearby).  

**4. Gideon Rally (Gideon – Bosun)**  
A deckhand panics as boarders swarm, dropping his line. Merritt can bark orders (CHA +2, DC 12). Success: the crew rallies, all allies gain advantage on next roll.  

**5. Worthington’s Quick Thinking (Elias Worthington – Midshipman)**  
Captain Cooper bellows contradictory orders in the smoke. Worthington can reinterpret (INT +3, DC 12). Success: smooth maneuver, morale rises. Failure: confusion gives enemies +2 on next action.  

**6. Pritchard’s Field Dressing (Jonah Pritchard – Surgeon)**  
A cannonball smashes through the deck, crushing a sailor's (Jeremiah Cutter - 25 years old, religious, wiry) leg. It's a horrific wound. For now, Jonah has to stop the bleeding (WIS +4, DC 14) and determine if amputation will be necessary later. Or amputate then (WIS +4, DC 18)

**7. Ransom’s Rigging (Levi Ransom – Topman)**  
A sail catches fire, spreading fast. Ransom can swing down and cut it loose (DEX +4, DC 12). Success: deck saved. Failure: fire damages rigging.  

**8. All Hands on Deck (Any)**  
A group of pirates boards en masse (1d6). Marines can fight, Gunner’s Mate can swing axe/pistol, Topman can drop from rigging, Bosun can rally. Everyone has a way to shine here.  

**9. Splinter Storm (Surgeon / Marines)**  
Musket volley shatters railing, spraying splinters. Everyone rolls CON save (DC 12). The surgeon can give one ally a reroll. Marines can cover allies with shields/bodies.  

**10. Grapple Cut (Ransom / Gideon / Barrett / Merritt)**  
Corsairs throw grappling hooks. Ransom can cut them loose from above (DEX DC 12). Barrett can hack ropes with bayonet (STR DC 12). Merritt can direct men to heave them off.  

**11. Hero Shot (Pike / Worthington)**  
Enemy lieutenant appears, leading a boarding party. Pike can try a single perfect musket shot (ranged DC 13). Worthington can rally sailors into formation (CHA or INT DC 12). Success = lieutenant down, orcs cheer.  

**12. Powder Panic (Carter / Merritt / Pritchard)**  
A spark ignites near the powder store. Carter can smother it (DEX +3, DC 13). Merritt can rally men to haul barrels. Pritchard can calm a panicked sailor to stop him from fleeing with a lit torch.  

**13. Lucky Shot (Carter)**  
Carter notices the crew on one of the surviving corsairs aiming a swivel gun directly at his crew (WIS DC 12). He can warn the crew and dive for cover, or try to aim and fire Reason on the fly (Cannon attack, AC 19). 

**14. Don't slip (anyone)**
A puddle of blood trips you up. DEX DC 14
