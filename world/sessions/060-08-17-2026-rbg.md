## Recap
You walked away from Rikterr's sacrifice in silence. Behind you, the fallen chain sent a slow, viscous ripple billowing up and down the River Styx.

Voleth offered passage downriver for two soul coins. Drak argued for walking, and for finally using the vault beetle. He lost the vote 3-1. Sango's tiebreaker was a natural 20, so at least the dice were emphatic about it.

Before you shoved off, an enormous raven landed on the quay, hopped onto Sango's shoulder, and whispered something nobody understood. Sango now carries the Raven Queen's Boon. He rolled it: at any point in the next 28 days he can summon four Shadar-kai Soul Mongers to fight alongside you. These are the same assassins that gave you fits in the Shadowfell, only I'm not powering them down this time. Each one can cast Finger Of Death. Don't steal Sango's snacks.

On the first night, everyone's chest started to burn. Vaxith, The Hand In The Dark, was perched on the stern. "Just a messenger," he said, and flew off to tell his queen you're proceeding at pace. Mossy flipped him off. When you looked down, the grey star was gone, replaced by healed skin. With Rikterr's death there are no longer five of you, and everything tied to the number five has come undone: the curse that drove nearby dragons mad appears to be broken, and the Quinary have lost their sacrifice power. Somewhere in her lair, Tiamat's green head is very disappointed.

The rest of the trip was Avernus being Avernus:

- Sango watched bearded devils burning down leathery trees that drop abyssal chickens. You stopped, collected one, and cooked it. Best chicken any of you have ever eaten. This is canon, not something I made up. Drak saved one for Sarah.
- Voleth confirmed what the Styx does: touch it and you forget everything that makes you you. Greater Restoration within 30 days fixes it. After that, nothing does. Mossy bottled some anyway. It's good for 24 hours.
- Mossy also collected three vials of demon ichor and dipped five arrows for Drak. She rolled a natural 1. Drak touched an arrowhead, tripled in weight, and stripped naked in front of everyone to get out of his mithral before it crushed him. Cregan whittled a suggestion box, then cast Greater Restoration. Drak hates this game.
- Grandma Valar, a green hag running a barge called The Pus Purveyor, sold you a potion of stone giant strength (Cregan), a potion of gaseous form (Drak) and an elixir of health (Mossy) for 750 gold each. You did not give her your names. Good.
- A meteor storm forced you ashore. Two hours in the vault beetle listening to fist-sized rocks hammer the roof. When you came out, the ground was pumice and the nearby demon patrols were ash.
- Two kenku, Chukka and Klonk, were stranded with a 6,000-pound bone-and-iron war machine. Mossy found the stripped gear and fixed it in about a minute. They offered a soul coin and said there's more where they come from, then roared off toward their fort.

Then the fog rolled in. Greenish, thick, full of ghostly shapes. Cregan put up a Tiny Hut on the boat, and three hydroloths came out of the Styx after your memories. They turn invisible, they hit hard, and they burn very nicely. You killed all three. But at least one of you failed a save against a hydroloth's mind-wipe and came away with a hole where a memory used to be.

Distances, by the way, are completely fucked. The map is accurate. The miles are not.
