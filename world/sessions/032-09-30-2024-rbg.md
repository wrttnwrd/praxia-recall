## Endgame
RBG knows Sango is back.

Agrona is headed for the Field of Bone and the pit. Once there, she'll wait for RBG. 

Her job now is to get the Pentar Virones to the Field Of Bone. Hopefully all her manipulations to date will help her do that. If not, she's got Sango as bait (she thinks). 

RBG, in the mean time, may decide to rescue Sango, or do something else.

At the start of the session, Agrona is holding Sango near the Pit. He's tied up, but he's attempting to escape and he may manage, because she's communing w/ Thargorax. 


## Setting
This all takes place on the Field Of Bone. It's called this because The Fall revealed the very bones of Praxia. It's like Yellowstone, but really awful.

From a distance it looks like a giant pall of steam. As you get closer, you see all the fumaroles, etc.

This makes for a few interesting effects:

- Perception (sight) checks are at disadvantage, unless you've got Truesight up
- Gasses - random roll to see if a geyser or mud pot erupts. If so, DC 10 CON save or poisoned for 1 minute
- Tremorsense is less predictable, with perception checks required and made at disadvantage
- Perception (smell) checks are at disadvantage
- Vision is obscured beyond 30 feet
- If players attempt to rest while on the surface, they need to make DC 12 CON saves each night or pick up a point of exhaustion

## Important
This session starts a running battle that lasts until the battle with Thargorax is resolved or the players flee. They won't get a chance to easily rest.

### Scrying Sango
As the spell takes effect, you see two vague figures outlined against a wall of yellowish steam. You zoom in closer and get a better view.

Sango lies on his side on gray and white, slick stone. He is bound and gagged and has been badly beaten. There's a welt on the side of his head, one arm is clearly broken, and he appears unconscious.

Nearby Agrona Sef sits, unarmored, sword at her side.  Where her head should be is a shadowy, semitransparent mass you can't quite make out. She is crosslegged, apparently staring into the depths of an enormous pit nearly 50 feet in diameter. From the pit pulses a dark, reddish mass of semi-opaque energy. The mass pushes against an unseen barrier as if it's trying to surge from the pit. For a moment - just a moment - you can imagine an enormous neck pushing and straining as if lifting a massive stone. Then you can almost make out a wing. Then dozens and dozens of small, glowing eyes.

Then you notice: Sango isn't unconscious. He's working slowly at his bonds, trying to get his hands free. So far, the Sef hasn't noticed. 

Next to Agrona lies the remnants of three bodies: Charred robes and armor. Shadowy outlines of skeletons.

And a lead box, broken open. At Agrona's feet lie two circular objects. The Seals.

## Agrona
Agrona now has the entire Quinary **and** the Seals, all in one place. If she can control them all, she can cast them into the Pit and release Thargorax. If she lives and kills the entire party, she'll do just that, but a few things are working against her:

- RBG is stronger now
- She's been weakened in her battle with Umbra's guard

Once bloodied she'll use ignomancy to collapse the ground around them, turning the area into a series of treacherous pits.

It's safe to assume she won't survive the fight. If she goes down, the scene changes:

 *For a moment, silence falls. Agrona's armor lies, broken and empty, on the hard ground of the Field of Bone. The pit, previously a seething red mass, glows with a golden light that brightens until you must look away. When it subsides...*

## Mechanic Of Final Fight
Thargorax has only one way to escape: Shatter the Seals with the Quinary. With Agrona down, she has to somehow get the items within reach - she's stuck in the partially-opened Gate.

To do that, she's summoned a reduced-threat Balor. 130 hp, no Fire Aura, 

To do that, she must 

- Somehow rip the Quinary from each party member or
- The Balor has to do the same. 

The Balor will focus 100% on getting the items. It will fly around, waiting for a chance. It will not attack party members.

The Balor can do it with its whip. It will use the standard disarm maneuver:

1. Attack roll contested by acrobatics or athletics
2. Disarm. When the item is dropped, grab it with a second Whip attack at disadvantage (because it's on the ground)

The hard part will be the Light That Sparks. It's a ring. The Balor will have to hit +2 Rikterr's AC to sever the arm, or +4 to sever the hand, then grab it as an object. It has to use the sword to do this - the whip won't work.

Remember that all players have the fate dice. But Essie may use those rolls to work **for** the party. When that happens, give them a little description:

*You have a brief flash of a bony green hand stretched over a bowl of water. In the bowl is an image of yourselves, (describe what's happening). The hand waves, and for an instant, fate twists and changes, thwarting the (creature) as it (tries to do something).* Once per player.

Or:

*You have a brief flash of a bony green hand stretched over a bowl of water. In the bowl is an image of yourselves, (describe what's happening). The hand waves, and for an instant, fate twists and changes, aiding you as you (action attempted).* Once per player.

If things go really, really wrong, have the Discontent show up on the scene. It can't do much. Even the ballistae will have little impact on Thargorax. They can distract the Balor, or they can crash into Thargorax.

*I understand now. I cannot hope to reason with you. Fools. Unseeing fools. I will see you suffer for an eternity. You will see me again. You will see my servants again. You have your lifetimes. I have forever.

*Now, return home and see what your insurrection has wrought.*
