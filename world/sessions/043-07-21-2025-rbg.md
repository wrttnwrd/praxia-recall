## Outline

### 12 Days At Wildwood
First off, they're spending 12 days at Wildwood, working on items, etc.

During that time, be sure to roll on the [Random Events](/world/prep/random-events.md) table.

If things go smoothly, nothing happens, and they get their work done.

### Meeting w/ Animus
Animus explains the party needs to visit Surtur's palace on the border of the Cinder Wastes, south of the City Of Brass. So they can teleport to the City of Brass and arrange transport from there. 

Since the City of Brass is habitable, and populated by Efreet and Azer, they should be able to arrange something. He explains that they'll need to obtain something to protect themselves from the environment outside the City, too.

Animus has a teleportation circle that links to a circle in the City of Brass, so the players can easily get there. He can have a friend of his, an Azer named Searcrest, act as a guide. He has a special compass that will point them in the right direction, too.

### In the city
They'll learn that Surtur lives in a palace outside a village known as Wrathskald, on the southern edge of the Cinder Wastes. From there, he keeps an eye on the fire giant kingdom.

Ways to get there:

1. Book a carpet (1 day journey)
2. Join a caravan (3 day journey)
3. Rent steeds - firehorses (1 day journey, extremely dangerous)
4. Take a fireship (1/2 day journey): Captain Emberforge, an Azer, can take them on his ship, the Fireheart. It's crewed by other Azer

Protections:

- "Potions" of chewable gum
- Amulets of Comfort
- A flamewarden - a specialized mage who concentrates on protecting them

### Surtur's favor
Surtur will give the party a badge, but they must first do a thing for him. They need to help the mayor of Wrathskald.

Wrathskald's economy is powered by trade of fireproof metals and gems fashioned into equipment, and magics: Adamantine, diamonds, etc, as well as fire protective magic and equipment. They have renowned crafters and artificers.

If the players go meet with the mayor - Ullos, a bespectacled, older (?) fire giant - he explains the problem. Recently, the market's been flooded with second-rate goods that sell at steeply discounted prices. 

He has sample goods: A suit of armor that's been partly melted, a sword that shattered, and a potion that doesn't completely protect the user.

This has all been caused by a group of salamanders to the East, in the salamander kingdom. They've found ways to create inferior items at massive scale, then sell them off for far less. 

If the players go look, they'll find machines and magic that copy legitimate items and create fakes using inferior materials.

The salamanders will NOT bargain. The players have to wipe them out.

The salamanders are led by a scheming [Rakshasa](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195178-rakshasa) named Ardmon Ingus Lacqui (AI Lackey) - a  devil who spends most of his time mentoring his sycophantic followers. 

He and his group operate out of a large, plain, obsidian building. If the players enter through the front, there's a large reception area and an enormous rendition of a machine. That's the device they're using.

Quirks:

- He has several small fire elementals who take messages to and from the shop floor, and he's constantly interrupting conversations to check in with them
- He has a harried female salamander who is constantly trying to keep him organized
- He constantly brags about how some cool thing he has he got for free because of who he is
- His office is festooned with photos of him with what the players assume are famous people from the plane of fire, including Surtur
- His book, Tapping The Fire Within: My Journey From Serf To Baron, features prominently on his desk, the tables outside his office, etc.

Company name: Thal'keth Erimir Malfeth Urzith (TEMU) or "The Eternal Market Reigns Supreme"
