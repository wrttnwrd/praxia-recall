2. Random fives: Ravens lead Rikterr to their nests. Each nest has precisely five eggs
3. At Rhugorim's request, the party goes to visit the children they helped save while the children are at school. As the characters move through the classroom, the children all say thank you very politely. With a perception check, though, the players notice they're all drawing the same thing. A five-headed dragon w/ the word "SOON" written in abyssal

## Events

1. Meeting [Rhugorim](/world/people/rhugorim.md): The aged orc is injured but healing. He's a bit sheepish after running off mid-battle. He knows what happened and is sure it wouldn't have affected him years ago. If the players wait a couple days, they'll see him in bed, recuperating. He's been doing some reading, reviewing the journals of his fellow party members. In particular, there's a story of them witnessing [Tiamat](/world/people/tiamat.md) and her minions handling an attack from some kind of demonic mercenary company.
2. Zurhdal and Voda (militia commander and mayor, respectively) are available to explain the town's status: They're going to invest more effort in cutting off the bay. If [the Villain's Discontent](/world/vehicles/the-villains-discontent.md) can help, that's great
3. RBG has to work out their shit. They're heading into the fight of their lives. [Rhugorim](/world/people/rhugorim.md)'s explanation should be enough to make this clear
4. Why isn't [the Quinary](/world/things/the-quinary.md) working correctly? Rikterr's dream
5. Voda wants to talk to the party about the Astrolabe of Three Wishes. After what happened to Erinya, it's painful to look upon. She cannot repay the party for what they've done - they saved the entire town, which is the orc's hope for the future. If they would accept the Astrolabe, though, perhaps they can put it to better use.

## Rhugorim's notes
I found these notes. They'd long since passed from memory, but the Abishai you fought tickled the back of my brain.

"Today, we observed a separate battle above the desperate, roiling struggle of the plains. A detachment of powerful fiends sought to storm Tiamat's Lair. That itself was not unusual. But these creatures breached the upper gate. When they did, the Dragon Queen herself rose from the ruin. Even chained, she towered over them. 

She let out a great roar from each of her hideous heads, and everything within earshot was struck with fear. Her assailants, and the combatants nearest the mountain fared worse: Most of them fled, shrieking, demons and devils alike ignoring each other to slither, run, and fly away as fast as they could.

But some - the strongest lieutenants of whatever demon prince sent them, I assume - remained. I say this: I despise these malignant creatures more even than I do devils. But if I could have pitied any of them, it would've been these. They didn't stand a chance. Most were dead in moments, consumed by cold, poison, fire, acid, and lightning. Spells did not affect her. Weapons did little. Those she didn't destroy with her breath weapons she tore to pieces.

It was over in moments."

## Recap
The Battle Of [Urdun](/world/places/urdun.md) was a victory for the orcs, for RBG, and for [the Albion](/world/vehicles/the-albion.md). 

[The Albion](/world/vehicles/the-albion.md) has returned to Earth. Rearmed and repaired, she weathered the Xebec assault and won the day.

The orcs, having dealt [the Blood Corsairs](/world/groups/the-blood-corsairs.md) a resounding defeat, can look forward to some time to rebuild and improve their defenses. 

RBG helped defend the harbor, and Rikterr stopped a Blood Corsair attempt at hostage-taking, saving [Rhugorim](/world/people/rhugorim.md)'s life and ensuring his bravery wasn't in vain.

But the battle raised key questions for RBG: 

Who were the mysterious Abishai that waylaid Rikterr at the end of the fight? What was their goal? 

What's to be done with the Astrolabe Of Wishes?

What's RBG's next steps?
