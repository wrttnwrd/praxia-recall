## Recap
The party encountered Agrona Sef. She cast Geas on Mossy and forced her to find her in Hull Ward. 

Combat ensued. Agrona took a great deal of damage, but knocked Drak unconscious (Drak only just survived)

Agrona leaves w/ the coin and immediately cracks the first seal.
## Strong Start
- Agrona is after Mossy, and she's using the Soul Cage to find her. She's going to lay a trap.
- G'hul comes for them 

[Agrona Sef](/world/people/agrona-sef.md) thinks that Mossy has the [soul coins](/world/things/soul-coins.md), because she can't detect them in [the Vaults](/world/places/the-vaults.md). She'll intercept the party near the mansion or elsewhere in the city, drop everyone and then interrogate Mossy.

If Agrona finds out the coins are in [the Vaults](/world/places/the-vaults.md), she goes straight there. But Elissa only knew of Mossy's ongoing thefts and told Agrona Mossy has them.

Agrona will try to find a place the neutralizes the party, then lure them there. She is waiting, invisible, outside the hotel. She will cast Geas on Mossy, sending her to the alley. Mossy goes there without saying much to the party (leave that up to Jamie). One in the alley, Agrona will try to torture Mossy into turning over the coin.

If they all end up in the alley:

_The alley is engulfed in a thin, wispy fog. At the opposite end stands a tall figure in black armor. What lilttle skin you can see is red. Their eyes are black, empty sockets sunken into angular features. They're holding an immense sword that glows with a dull, purple light. When you look carefully, you're sure you can almost see a pair of diaphonous, black wings that hover behind them.

_As you enter the alley, you feel a chill, and a tall, white wall of ice slams down, sealing you in. A voice whispers from the armor: "Come. Join us. I have questions."_

### If they go into [the Vaults](/world/places/the-vaults.md)
_G'hul waits for you at the door to [the Vaults](/world/places/the-vaults.md). "We had an agreement, and after your, attempt, I chose to let you go and honor that agreement. I am no  genius, but I don't think you'd be foolish enough to come back and try again. Am I correct?" You've never seen this side of G'hul before. He is pure menace. The shadows seem to enfold him as he leans, too casually, against the wall. He is flanked by humanoids of varying species. None are guards - three are armed with various blades and crossbows. A fourth is clad in leather armor and carries only a staff._

Depending on how the party answers:
_G'hul seems to relax a bit, but he's still clearly furiously angry. He stalks down the long hall to the individual vaults as if expecting you to follow. He's deceptively fast, and you have to walk fast to keep up. He stops in front of the Bournier vault. The door and wall are simply... gone. Not destroyed - it's as if a giant knife carved a ragged hole where the forbidding door once stood. There is no debris. 

_Inside, the room is littered with the dead bodies of G'hul's guards. The left-hand wall is cracked and broken as if something burst out. On the floor beneath the damage is a pile of earth and enormous guantlets. Apparently, someone did what you could not - they broke into the vault._

With investigation, it's clear that the invader opened the chest. There's nothing obviously missing. G'hul says an enchantment on the room clearly shows that **something** was stolen. Probably something very small.
