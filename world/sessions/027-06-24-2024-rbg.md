## Recap
Secrets were told. Fate was set (sort of).

RBG went in search of the hag who could create the Door That Opens. They found her: Essie Darksinger, ancient, evil hag, proprietor and prisoner at Bedlam's Faire.

Essie put the party through a series of trials drawn from her own specialized Tarot deck. 

At one point, the party had to reveal secrets about themselves:

Drak: "I loved the power I felt running with Elidan's crew"
Rikterr: "I hate the man who made me a Paladin"
Mossy: "I let another Tabaxi take the fall and get killed for one of her crimes"
Cregyn: "My god killed my children"

As a final price, Essie demanded a bit of fate from each character. Game mechanic: Players each rolled 1d20. DM recorded it and may use that roll as desired at some future time.

In the end, Essie created the Door: A piece of relatively nondescript black fabric that, once unfurled, appears to open a portal to another plane. That's as far as RBG got in their investigation.
## The Plot
Open world. 

## Option 1: Going to meet Essie Darksinger
The players step into a round, oddly green patch of grass and trees, almost like the oases they'd seen, but the lighting is weird. Once they do, the circle vanishes and they're standing at the entrance to an outdoor fair. 

Two moons whirl overhead at impossible speed, waxing and waning.

Everything is gray, white, or black.

In front of them is an entrance to the fair with a sign above it that reads "Welcome to Bedlam's Faire."

### Location: Bedlam's Faire
A riotous collection of tents, weird shops, and twisted, strange-looking plant life. 

The faire is run by Essie Darksinger, a Hag of Fate who has long kept the secret to creating the Door That Opens to herself.

The main currency here is **names**.

- Erga's Sweets: All sorts of tasty treats
- The Thrown Goblin
- The Hungry Nut 
- The Face Rental booth
- Mockingbird

### Scenes:

#### Meeting Essie the first time
"Welcome To Bedlam's Faire, dears. Please, take a card. When all these cards are gone but four, meet me by the darken'd door."

Drawing a card causes an incident.

Each time an incident is complete, a disembodied deck of cards appears.

The deck dwindles each time.

A few random cards:

Corpse: Three humanoids - a human holding her own head, a tiefling with an axe in their chest, and a dwarf stuck with dozens of arrows - are in a room arguing. They don't notice the players unless they do something to get their attention. They explain they killed each other and their souls are trapped. They'd be very appreciative if the players, should they find a small, lead box, drop it into any water, fire, etc. they can find. 

Gem: "Only the bold will wear the gold." Random magic item, cursed. Party member must wear it to move on to the next step.

Monstrosity: "They are not what they seem." Player 1d4 is in a room with another player 1d4. They begin conversing. One of them is replaced and attacks. If the actual player loses, they gain 1 exhaustion.

Pit: It's a pit. 

Prison: "Gain your freedom with a secret." Each party member is locked in a gibbet over a pool of fire. They're being lowered in. Each must tell a secret before all are freed. If they don't they stay there, burn, and come back with 2 points of exhaustion.

Rogue: PER. Rogue w/ 25 sleight of hand steals an item. If not caught, the party doesn't know, and I remove one item from each PC's inventory inventory.

Stairway: Recite a numeric sequence: 0-100, by 5s to 50, then by 2 to 70, then by 1s to 100. But a bunch of gnomes start shouting random numbers at you.

Well: An open field on a beautiful, perfect day. There's an idyllic well. Next to the well is a table, two chairs, and a small, lead box. Sitting in one of the chairs is a satyr. He motions you over and gestures for someone to sit. "Do you like this box? Would you like to see what's in it? Go ahead. Ah ah ah - before you do, know this: Open it and reap your reward. Cast it into the well for another's gain. Or leave, and go unharmed."

#### Meeting her at the end
Backs to them, sitting at a desk, beautiful black hair cascading down the back of her chair.

Then she turns and unfolds into an 8-foot-tall monstrosity with long, oddly-shaped limbs. Her face is... colorless. There's no other way to describe it. Not pale, not gray. It's absent of color.

She will create the Door, but requires a gift. First, she has the party draw a card.

Depending on the card, she wants:

1-5: Time. A year of each of their lives
6-10: Color. The color of part of their bodies. Everyone gets -1 CHAR for 1d20 days (Greater restoration to restore)
11-15: Fortune. Everyone rolls a d20. I keep that roll to use at my discretion, any time
16-20: A name, from each.

She collects whatever she takes in four vials.
