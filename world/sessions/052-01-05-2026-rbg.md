He's renowned for his ability to pilot through [the Fassus](/world/places/the-fauces.md) and is the only pilot to have NOT lost a boat in three seasons. His vessel, the Spray Skimmer, is a unique boat of his own design: Narrow, with a heavy bottom and a huge, outsized rudder, he uses it to catch the Silver Gilled Lungfish whose meat is prized highly. Torsten wants to do impossible shit. That's it - he's wildly unpredictable, brilliant, and driven by a desire for fame and fortune.
