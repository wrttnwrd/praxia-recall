## Strong Start
The bully: [Kozani Noctis](/world/people/kozani-noctis.md)'s lieutenant, Gorric Malvane, is sent to Cinderhaven to demand that they turn over everyone who participated in the raid.

Gorric arrives at the club looking for Dante and Dumas. They find Dante behind the bar and, without preamble or any effort to move to a quieter location, demands answers. When Dante isn't forthcoming, Gorric makes it clear: Turn over the responsible parties, or lose Cinderhaven.

As emphasis, Gorric scoops up a nearby patron and throws them over the bar, then grabs Dante and slams him down on the bar for good measure. While they have him pinned, Gorric starts pouring bottles of liquor on the helpless Duergar, who's no match for Gorric, and couldn't retaliate and risk the bar regardless. Gorric is laughing cruelly while doing this.

The players don't hear any of this, but they see it. Gorric's hard to miss. 

If the players sneak over to listen in, when they get there, Gorric is saying, "...a lot of them. They're here somewhere, and they can't hide forever. You can tell me who they are, or Kozani can tear this place down, brick by brick. Immediately. Your choice, little grey man. We already ignore all sorts of nastiness from you two, don't we?"

If they intervene, Gorric fights. They're not going to chat, and they have no idea who RBG is. The players should be able to dispatch them pretty easily, or just chase them away.

If the players don't intervene, Gorric works themselves into a frenzy and starts lashing out at patrons and wait staff alike.

If they die, one of Kozani's spies takes word to her. If they live, they flee to Darktome's Cross and Kozani's tower.

Either way, Kozani finds out, and the attack starts momentarily.

### Gorric
Gorric is a massive [nosferatu](/world/people/nosferatu.md) who, even among their kind, is a nasty piece of work. They wear a tuxedo-style outfit that's made outlandish by their fangs, gigantic musculature, and tendency to idly finger their fangs in public. 

Gorric has a long scar across their face from a wound received pre-embrace. It makes them look like they're sneering. Which they often are.

They enjoy hurting and scaring people, and they go out of their way to do it.
