## Recap
You spent a short time at Wildwood. Drak found a door that opened to a strange, almost marshmallow-like substance. On the door, written in primordial, were the words "not done yet." 

You arranged to fly [Saroya Demiarma](/world/people/saroya-demiarma.md) to Salmuir. During that time, you did a little quick refresher on Salmuirian history and the Haddad Combine. You also learned that Saroya is now the Chancellor of Salmuir. Once you were underway, she explained that someone was trying to destabilize the city through disinformation and harassment. Then, a short time ago, a member of the Haddad Combine was murdered, right in their residence in the Palace She wants RBG's help stopping the plot (if there is one) and figuring out who killed the Combine member, preferably before news spreads and the city starts to panic.

On your journey, you spotted a burnt area of the Whitesand Steppes. The burn matches the symbol on one of the Seals - the one that Agrona cracked during your final battle. The fire is centered precisely over the Crypt Of Amenopophis, which (you realized) was where Agrona found the Life Seal.

Once in Salmuir, you briefly investigated the murder: First, Saroya showed you propaganda leaflets circulating through the city: The leaflets suggest that the Haddad Combine can't be trusted. These leaflets include the imprint of a single black wing. 

You inspected the dessicated corpse of the Combine member. As you were poking around the dead Shadar-Kai, though, the corpse sat up and attacked, vomiting foul-smelling blood all over Drak and ruining his outfit. You dealt with the creature without much trouble. 

Rikterr determined that the creature was a nosferatu - a vampire indigenous to the Shadowfell. You all spoke to the Priestess of the Raven Queen, who told you more about the Shadowfell and suggested you visit the Library Of Salmuir to learn more about nosferatu.
