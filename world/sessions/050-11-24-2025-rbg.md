## Outline
[The Blood Corsairs](/world/groups/the-blood-corsairs.md) are on their way to [Urdun](/world/places/urdun.md) with a fairly large fleet, including casters. The party is preparing defenses.

The players have another 24 hours before [the Blood Corsairs](/world/groups/the-blood-corsairs.md) reach [Urdun](/world/places/urdun.md). Possible courses of action:

- Create more enhanced gunpowder for [the Albion](/world/vehicles/the-albion.md)
- Set up defenses
- Do a decapitation strike on the Corsairs

The Albion is undergoing fast repairs. They've repaired rigging and mast, re-connected the helm, and (maybe) have more powder.

## Corsair attack plan
The Corsairs are all about terror. They're going to land, kill, and destroy as much as possible. They think that'll terrify the orcs into submission.

The sahuagin have scouted for them. They know where the Albion is. They don't fully understand it. They know what's happened to the first unit of ships they sent, too.

The Corsairs plan to:

1. Have the sahuagin scout the bay
2. Once they see [the Albion](/world/vehicles/the-albion.md), they'll attempt to take the ship w/ the sahuagin
3. And sail their fleet around [the Albion](/world/vehicles/the-albion.md) to destroy [Urdun](/world/places/urdun.md) w/ fire arrows and magic at range, then land and sack the city
	1. This takes advantage of the orcs' lack of a navy
	2. And avoids immediate melee with the orcs, which they might lose
	3. Instead, they soften them up first
4. Then leave, having established their dominance and forced the orcs to submit tributes

This isn't subtle. [The Blood Corsairs](/world/groups/the-blood-corsairs.md) don't know exactly what they're heading into. 

Traitor Crane is cautious. She's not in the attack.

## Encounters

- The deck of [the Albion](/world/vehicles/the-albion.md)
- [Urdun](/world/places/urdun.md) (docks): Fight on the docks themselves between Corsairs and the party
- [Urdun](/world/places/urdun.md) (town): Battle at the shelter at the top of the town
- The Reach: Naval battle/battle on a boat or two 

### The attack on the Albion
A unit of sahuagin attack the Albion:

1. One[ High Priestess](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/316516-sahuagin-high-priestess)
2. 10 [warriors](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195190-sahuagin-warrior) but with 40hp - 5 more warriors arrive every 4 rounds until the priestess is dead or 40 warriors, whichever comes first
3. One [Deep Diver](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/316511-sahuagin-deep-diver)

The warriors will attempt to drag crew members under while the priestess uses bless, guiding bolt, spiritual weapon, and fear. The Deep Diver will attempt to charm anyone in the water but won't surface.

If the Albion fires her guns, the sahuagin figure it out and immediately attack the gun crews.

### The attack on the fleet
No idea how this is going to shake out. but it's a fleet action. 

Corsair ships will come in close and launch fire arrows soaked in oil, as well as oil jars from catapults/fire bolts. If they ignite the docks, the orcs can't set up to fight and the corsairs can pick them off. If the docks remain, the orcs can form up and there's no way the corsairs can come ashore.

6 X [corsair longships](https://www.dndbeyond.com/vehicles/longship?srsltid=AfmBOorN9kzl_WKVumXSlyASe81OHd9shaGuEUCrmeAlIeCl0IUAp7CT)

**[Orc firefighting squad](/world/lore/orc-firefighting-squad.md) must be protected.**

This is a mass combat. 150 or so corsairs storm ashore. 

**[Orc firefighting squad](/world/lore/orc-firefighting-squad.md) must be protected.**

### [Rhugorim](/world/people/rhugorim.md)'s last stand
A small group of corsairs and sahuagin have traveled around the back of the town and up to the ruined temple to kidnap the children. They hope to use them as bargaining chips. [Rhugorim](/world/people/rhugorim.md) and two other warriors are holding them off. The two warriors are wounded, and [Rhugorim](/world/people/rhugorim.md) bleeds from what appear to be a thousand cuts.

Raiders: 

1X [drow assassin](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/273152-drow-assassin)
3X [sahuagin champions](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/316122-sahuagin-champion)
3X [bandits](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5194912-bandit-captain) who are badly overmatched

### The ambush
[Vyrassinth the Whispercoil](/world/people/vyrassinth.md) has been stalking the party for some time, spying on behalf of [Tiamat](/world/people/tiamat.md). If any party member is isolated (Rikterr) she senses an opportunity and attacks.

Vyrassinth wears light armor and a red cape which is a Cape Of The Mountebank. She'll use the cape to slip away if necessary. She has an AC of 21. She won't put herself in serious danger and will run if she thinks she's outclassed.

She'll start with a sophisticated attack: She'll use alter self to make herself look more like a dragonborn than a devil. She'll swoop down, killing the assassin and casting mass suggestion to get everyone to scatter. Then she'll cast charm. If it works, she'll immediately tell them in a rush that their friends are in trouble, and to follow her.

If it fails, she'll attempt to bluff, and try charm again.

## After the battle
Depending on how things go, Traitor Crane may simply count her losses and move on, raiding other towns as she rebuilds her forces. Or, she may decide to attack again.

This will depend on whether RBG reveals themselves in full force. Crane will have **nothing** to do with the *Discontent* and the party if she realizes who's there. 

If Crane decides to leave everything, then the Albion will vanish.
## The Albion's Return
The *Albion* returns to the same moment in time they left + 5 seconds. The Xebecs are completely flummoxed as she leaps forward under sail, then engages them ship-to-ship, destroying them one at a time.



## Recap
From 11/24:

As the Corsair fleet approached Urdun, RBG put a plan into action. Sango and Drak positioned themselves to help defend the mouth of the bay and [the Albion](/world/vehicles/the-albion.md). Mossy prepared to do arson from [the Villain's Discontent](/world/vehicles/the-villains-discontent.md). Rikterr organized the defense of the docks. The crew of [the Albion](/world/vehicles/the-albion.md) took up their posts, slow matches burning over guns, prepared for battle.

Ever hear the expression "Everyone's got a plan until they're punched in the face?"

The Praxian version is "Everyone's got a plan until a bunch of Sahuagin show up, the monk gets banished, and the ranger is fighting a bunch of underwater shock troops."

The Corsairs allies are the Sahuagin of the Dark Reach. They've ambushed [the Albion](/world/vehicles/the-albion.md). 

In the fray, the DM got a **very** good roll and Sango got a **very** bad one. Sango is now hanging out in a small demiplane until someone can break the sahuagin priestess's spell. That's left the capable-but-mundane crew of [the Albion](/world/vehicles/the-albion.md) to fight a completely unfamiliar foe on their own. A well-placed fear spell scattered the gun crews, but Obadiah and the Reason crew arestill at their posts, about to fire their first broadside into the approaching Blood Corsair craft.

Drak is busy tangling with weaker-but-annoying sahuagin under [the Albion](/world/vehicles/the-albion.md). He's killed several, but more are coming.

Mossy is gleefully raining fire and ballista bolts on incoming corsair vessels.

Rikterr waits on the docks. He and the orcs are tense. They can see [the Albion](/world/vehicles/the-albion.md) and the rest of RBG are engaged, but they also see corsair vessels rushing towards them...
