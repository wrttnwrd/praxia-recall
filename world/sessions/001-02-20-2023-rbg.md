## Recap
RBG went to Uppercuts, meeting Sandren and Whipping Sound. They were hired as crew on the Gray Foam. They did NOT know that Sea Gristle was under orders to get rid of them. Sea Gristle didn't know that Elissa's real plan was that the entire ship get destroyed. 

Elissa tipped off Bluestrike in the hopes that one of the airships would trash the Foam and kill the party along with it.

But it was a clumsy attempt. Voltova saw the party and held fire. Drak nearly killed Sea Gristle, who then confessed the entire plan (except the destroying his ship part). 

The party now plans to do a heist, robbing the Bourniers.
