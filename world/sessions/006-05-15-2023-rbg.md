## Recap
Recap from last game: You made your way (go ahead, sing it) through the city, slowly triangulating the epicenter of the mysterious earthquake. RBG quickly discovered it wasn't a quake - it was the use of some kind of powerful magic that created a huge sinkhole under the Zuppek Bazaar. Thankfully, there were few dead, and the party saved several lives. The entire incident occurred, as you discovered, when Agrona Sef used a magic called Ignomancy to destroy part of the Shadow Veil's vaults and steal items from the Bournier's treasure trove: The remaining two soul coins. (You're not 100% sure where the Bourniers discovered these coins just yet)

Agrona's spell did extensive damage to the Shadow Veil complex. Gh'ul is incensed and out for blood. His first instinct was that RBG was somehow responsible. Fortunately, his good friend, Hexxam, true leader of House Umbra, was there and hit the pause button before wholesale slaughter ensued.

From Hexxam, you learn:

 1.  RBG is at the center of some confrontation between the Prime Material plane and the Abyss that has been centuries in the making.
 2. The confrontation involves the Stygian Gate, about which you know little
 3. RBG has heard the name "Thargorax" a few times. Translated, it means "Many headed dragon" (thargo + rax)
 4. Which apparently means Tiamat, once queen of the Abyss, fallen into disrepute in recent centuries following and then completely displaced by the recent battle for the Stygian Gate
 5. "Sef" means "servant" - a little-known servitor of the Abyss's royalty
 6. Apparently, Agrona is ONE of the Sef, but the only one recently seen
 7. The soul coins are a valued form of exchange in the Abyss and The Nine Hells, often provided to the Sef by their masters
 8. There are a set of four seals across the surface of Praxia
 9. Breaking all four seals unlocks the Stygian Gate, but not in the little, let's-open-a-tiny-door way that a Balgura tried to do several years ago. It would rip open a massive rift between the planes
 10. The Gate can be used as a bridge between planes. In particular, it can be used to connect Avernus (the battlefield between Hell and The Abyss) to the Prime Material plane
 11. If a demon lord/queen (like, say, Tiamat) were in the right place, they could leap to the Prime Material Plane
 12. Which is definitely Not A Good Thing


## The Plot
An enormous earthquake appears to have struck. People are terrified, thinking [The Fall](/world/events/the-fall.md) is about to repeat. Some rush to temples, others flee the city.

The "quake" was actually Agrona using _ignomancy_ to crush the area around The Vaults, gaining access to the Bournier's chambers and fetching the other two soul coins. With that done, she immediately heads for the second Seal.

Agrona's spell did extensive damage to the Shadow Veil's complex. Many of Gh'uls troops are trapped. Some have been crushed.

At some point, they'll come upon the epicenter and see the sinkhole that opened under the Bazaar.

_As you walk towards the Bazaar, you get a feeling of wrongness, as if the ground is pitched slighly in the wrong direction. You hear what sounds like a mob, and the sound of crashing stone and metal, as if a riot is underway. When you turn the corner, though, you see the reality: A gaping hole has opened under the center of the Bazaar. At the edges, the outer walkway sags down into what is now a 50-foot-wide maw. Buildings and tends surrounding the square lean crazily towards the abyss. Some are slowly caving in. Under it all you hear the screams and cries of people as they lie broken, crushed, or trapped, or as the building they're in slowly teeters towards destruction. You're assaulted by a wave of dust, then the stench of blood from crushed humanoids and animals alike._

Various scenes confront the characters:

1. A single guard lies nearby with a broken leg. He's tried to move towards a small nearby bakery. In it are Merana and Evenar - two elves recently moved into the city. Cradled between them is their young daughter, Tiana. Tiana is either dead or dying as she cradles a small doll in her arms. 
2. A building is swaying dangerously. There are at least 5 panicked people inside. Skill check DC 20 against whatever the players decide to try to stabilize the structure. If they instead decide to hustle people out, then it's a DC20 persuasion, intimidation, or other check to get them out
3. A merchant is desperately clinging to her tent as it dangles over the hole. She won't let go. If no one helps her, she's pulled in


Any character w/ a passive Perception above 15 immediately notes that the hole looks directly into part of the Shadow Veil complex. If they slide down into the sink hole, they see this:

_The vaults lie in ruin. Most of the doors are covered in rubble. The contents of those rooms may still be safe. The Bournier's vault, though, is blasted open. It lies directly under the crater._

If the players check carefully (DC25 INV), they'll find that a small box has been ripped open. In it is a velvet pad, and the pad has two circular impressions in it.
