## Recap
RBG went after Elidan. They split the party, with Cregyn and Rikterr entering the box canyon from ground level and Mossy and Drak sneaking up from above. 

Elidan didn't pack much of a punch, but he had time to prepare the ground: By making deals with the local unseelie fae, he got a small army of angry beasties to harass and injure intruders while also alerting him.

Drak and Mossy arrived at their post unscathed. Rikterr and Cregyn fell into a pit trap and were attacked by a swarm of tiny Darklings while two Quicklings raced back to alert Elidan.

After that, though, the mission went as smoothly as could be hoped: Elidan 0, RBG 1.

Cregyn proved himself a bit of a hand-to-hand monster, pinning the fallen ranger and allowing Drak to get the killing shot. 

The party reboarded the Villain's Discontent just in time for this:

*You feel as if something's just... wrong. As if you've forgotten something essential at home and can't quite recall what it was. 

*A moment later, a soft, low, keening wail begins, not coming from anywhere in particular, but everywhere at once. It's that much more disturbing because it remains just at the outer range of your hearing, distant and forlorn. 

*You glance up, seeking the source of the sound, and see a curtain of darkness spreading across the night sky. The stars wink out, one at a time, as if you're in a clear jar and someone's poured a thick, viscous liquid over the top. Familiar constellations vanish. Then one moon. Then the next. The curtain descends to the very horizon.

*The keening fades and you stand, rooted, helpless, knowing that something truly cataclysmic has happened.

Then the entire Villain's Discontent pitched sharply to the right. 

Fade to black.

## The Plot
The party's seeking out Elidan. They've found him, thanks to Iric's scrying. 

## The Big Bad: Elidan
There's a small cave nestled between hills in the Shimmertrame (swamp outside Aventine). He and his gang are there. 

Four of Elidan's gang are there. Elidan is hidden nearby using Hide In Plain Sight (basically a gili suit).

*Remember Elidan has an Oversized Longbow - 2d8+5 damage*

https://www.dndbeyond.com/characters/48082821

With him are four other hunters like https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/149873-ziraj-the-hunter but human, only 100hp, and not with the oversized bows.

Also with him is a Fallen Druid. !Pasted image 20230629150656.png
But with Spike Growth spike-growth, Animal Friendship animal-friendship, Earthbind earthbind, Heat Metal heat-metal, Plant Growth plant-growth

Elidan and the Druid have put up concentric rings of traps. As the players move in, the traps are more difficult to detect and evade.

Elidan has also struck bargains with evil fey in the area. They won't fight, but they help enhance traps.

#### Outer ring
18 PER
15 DEX
Goal: Detect
Four whisperwhistle cross bows, all pointed inwards, attached to a snare. If the players see the snare, they can find the traps.

Some of the (Mites)[https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/588989-mite] that Elidan has retained stay close by, inflicting disadvantage on DEX saves.

#### Second ring
18 PER
18 CON
Goal: Detect, damage

There are also (Darklings)[https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/17231-darkling] in the area. If the players kill any, they flash brightly.

#### Third ring
15 STR
18 PER
Goal: Impede, detect

The ground is covered with sticky, thick vines. At first glance they look not quite right. They're coated with a sticky solution. STR 15 to get loose once you're trapped. It's impossible to get loose without making noise.

Once trapped, the players are swarmed by the Darklings. The swarm has 100hp. Each turn the swarm makes 2 attacks, each hit inflicting 1hp of necrotic and 1hp of standard damage. Players w/in the swarm are at disadvantage on concentration checks. Every time the players do 10hp damage to the swarm, one of the darklings dies with a small flash.

**If this trap is not triggered, the Darklings show up 2 turns into combat.**

There are (Quicklings)[https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/17299-quickling] here who immediately dash for the camp when they see the characters. 

#### Inner ring
24 PER
24 DEX
Goal: Impede/damage/detect

A low fog clings to the ground, concealing several large pits. The pits are only six feet deep, but if the players fall in:

10 (Reflections)[https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/1240437-reflections] have emerged from the Underdark and patrol the area. They appear as 2-dimensional Derro and are hidden inside the pits. They immediately start attacking the players and again, Quicklings zip into the camp.

!Pasted image 20230629160243.png

Or, they trigger a net trap that opens, releasing a swarm of tiny fey locusts that sting and bite, doing the same strength drain.
