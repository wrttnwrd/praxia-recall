## Strong Start
Depends on where the players start.

If they successfully hide and then emerge, they will be summoned by the City Guard for reasons they don't know. Once there, they will be informed that [Elissa Bournier](/world/people/elissa-bournier.md) has died.

An evil cleric named [Agrona Sef](/world/people/agrona-sef.md) has tracked the soul coins (see the soul coins [entry points](/world/prep/entry-points.md)) to the Bourniers, showing up too late to recover them. She has performed Legend Lore and was told a Trickster Tabaxi Lit A Fire In My Bones. It didn't take much work for her to figure out the rest.

She tracked down Elissa, interrogated her to find out about Mossy, and then killed her. She then trapped Elissa's soul using Soul Cage for the next 8 hours. She plans to use Elissa's soul to confirm when she finds Mossy and her companions.

If they fail to hide, they're brought before the City Watch or whoever else and may be accused of murder by an investigator named Gavin Horn. 

They can always run - Gavin and the Watch just aren't that up to the task. If they do, though, the city will dispatch more, uh, substantial folks. 

**Also**, if Agrona somehow figures out the vaults, and can't detect the coins, she's going to go down there. That'll get Guh'l looking for the party, because they're the only other ones who went into the vaults.

### If they see Elissa
_The penthouse at the Emerald Harpist is opulence made real. Five rooms include a master and guest bedroom and a richly-appointed dining room. Furniture that would almost fit in a museum adorns the seating area. A blaze cheerily crackles in the huge fireplace, and wide, almost floor-to-ceiling windows look out over the foggy harbor.

_Which only makes the scene that unfolds before you that much more jarring. Seated on the couch, facing away from you, appears to be a woman with black, gray-streaked hair. Her arms are draped over the back of the sofa in a pose made a chilling mockery of relaxation by the fact that they bend backwards at the elbow_

_As you move around to the front of the sofa, you see a woman, head thrown back, face frozen in a rictus of pain and terror. Her legs are broken in multiple places, she's streaked with black burns of some kind, and her ribs appear caved in. Her eyes, rolled partway back in her head, are milky white. You realize you are looking at the tortured, lifeless form of Elissa Bournier._

### If they go into the Vaults
_G'hul waits for you at the door to the Vaults. "We had an agreement, and after your, attempt, I chose to let you go and honor that agreement. I am no  genius, but I don't think you'd be foolish enough to come back and try again. Am I correct?" You've never seen this side of G'hul before. He is pure menace. The shadows seem to enfold him as he leans, too casually, against the wall. He is flanked by humanoids of varying species. None are guards - three are armed with various blades and crossbows. A fourth is clad in leather armor and carries only a staff._

Depending on how the party answers:
_G'hul seems to relax a bit, but he's still clearly furiously angry. He stalks down the long hall to the individual vaults as if expecting you to follow. He's deceptively fast, and you have to walk fast to keep up. He stops in front of the Bournier vault. The door and wall are simply... gone. Not destroyed - it's as if a giant knife carved a ragged hole where the forbidding door once stood. There is no debris. 

_Inside, the room is littered with the dead bodies of G'hul's guards. The left-hand wall is cracked and broken as if something burst out. On the floor beneath the damage is a pile of earth and enormous guantlets. Apparently, someone did what you could not - they broke into the vault._

With investigation, it's clear that the invader opened the chest. There's nothing obviously missing. G'hul says an enchantment on the room clearly shows that **something** was stolen. Probably something very small.

Agonar Sef is actively seeking the soul coins. When she fails to find them all, she starts going after the Bourniers, then she'll go after the party based on the fact that they stole the safe. They may not know this until they find out Elissa is dead.
