## Recap
Drak survived a touchy negotiation with Urush Gûl, but the dragon, enraged that the party did not arrive precisely as demanded, showed the vicious, petty nature of his kind, crippling [the Villain's Discontent](/world/vehicles/the-villains-discontent.md), leaving much of the airship's crew badly injured or dead and the craft unable to fly.

Gûl then took the party to his lair and explained his predicament: 

[Tiamat](/world/people/tiamat.md) wishes to escape and return the Prime Material Plane to its former glory as the domain of chromatic dragons. She would wipe out most other sentient species, leaving the plane untainted and dragons free to roam as they desire.

Urush Gûl, though, prefers having humanoids and others to serve him. If [Tiamat](/world/people/tiamat.md) wins, his life becomes... boring.

But he has sworn fealty to [Tiamat](/world/people/tiamat.md), and even imprisoned her power is such that he cannot disobey her. She demands that he weaken RBG before they arrive in her citadel, but **not** kill them. 

Gûl, therefore, must thread the tiniest of needles, delivering a weakened party to [Silgorin](/world/places/silgorin.md) and [The Gurges](/world/places/the-gurges.md) for the next stage of their journey, but also granting the party a boon that ensures [Tiamat](/world/people/tiamat.md) doesn't get her way.

When he hears their plans to [travel](/world/lore/travel.md) through Avernus, though, he scoffed: 

> "You think you're safe because of the mumblings of an old man? [Avernus](/world/places/avernus.md) has changed. The fires will burn you alive you before you can speak. My brand is the only thing that will keep your flesh on your bones. "

He offered, and RBG accepted, the Searing Brand. With it, he granted RBG **immunity to fire damage.** He also took an unspecified price.

He then transported the party to [the outskirts](/world/places/the-outskirts.md) of the town of [Silgorin](/world/places/silgorin.md), perched high above [The Gurges](/world/places/the-gurges.md). Here, RBG will attempt to find passage through the falls to the River Styx.

### People & Places

- [The Gurges](/world/places/the-gurges.md): A roaring cataract created by the Helgoril River as it pours through the crack created by Amoth's fall to the River Styx. This is the most obvious way to physically travel to Avernus
- [Silgorin](/world/places/silgorin.md): A small city clinging to the upper rim of The Gurges
- Urush Gûl: A particularly nasty, powerful ancient red dragon
- Vaxith, The Hand In The Dark: A Black Abishai who was present when the party accepted the Searing Brand
