## Notes
RBG is headed to [Avernus](/world/places/avernus.md). Assuming they've decided to head to [the Gurges](/world/places/the-gurges.md).

Red is 5 rounds away

### Essie's warning
Essie Darksinger contacts Rikterr, warning him that [Tiamat](/world/people/tiamat.md) is arranging ambushes all along the way across The Gap and to [the Gurges](/world/places/the-gurges.md). Be ready.

There are three options:

1. Take the shortest route possible, heading straight West across the Gap. In that case, there's an ambush of Abishai waiting for them on the barrier islands East of [Saltreach](/world/places/saltreach.md)
2. They can head north or south, then across the Gap. 
	1. If they go North, Niithrax The White is waiting for them at The Battered Reach with specific instructions from [Tiamat](/world/people/tiamat.md): Do damage. Slow them down. 
	2. If they go South, Urush-Gûl (ancient red dragon, 730 hp, also has Earthquake once/day) is waiting on The Shattered Isle (which they are NOT happy about) and Vorthaxus at Blackspray. Same instructions.
3. If they go East, flying over the Ingluves, there's no one waiting to stop them. But The Spine is home to a tribe of Cloud Giants. In the Spine they'll meet a [Cloud Giant Smiling One](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/2560760-cloud-giant-smiling-one) and a [Cloud Giant Of Evil Air](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/3730995-cloud-giant-of-evil-air), Trigvag and Xelas. They're a mated pair who take cruel delight in torturing anyone who attempts to cross the spine by air or land.


4. [Vyrassinth the Whispercoil](/world/people/vyrassinth.md) has been scolded. Her job is to **weaken** RBG, not **kill** them. [Tiamat](/world/people/tiamat.md) wants them to make it to her so that she can use their power to destroy the Chains of Gleymir.


## Places

1. Emberbridge: A small settlement built spanning the Helgoril, about halfway from the ocean to the falls
2. [The Gurges](/world/places/the-gurges.md): The falls from the end of the Helgoril to The Dock Of Fallen Cities
3. The Fauces (Fass-uss): The "mouth" of the Helgoril
4. [Saltreach](/world/places/saltreach.md): The town at the edge of The Fauces
5. [Silgorin](/world/places/silgorin.md): The abyssal city perched at the edge of [The Gurges](/world/places/the-gurges.md), populated by elves and devils who live in a strange detente
	1. Torn Asunder: The Inn built into a broken ship on [the Gurges](/world/places/the-gurges.md)
	2. The Precipice: The cantina where they can meet Voleth.

## People
1. Valthus The Quill - elven lawyer in [Silgorin](/world/places/silgorin.md). She wants the best possible deal, and profit
2. Voleth Of The Fog - stealth boat driver on the Styx. [Merrenoloth](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/2560873-merrenoloth), but with 250 hp, AC 20, all save DCs 18, saves are +9, skills are +6, resistances are to magical and non-magical, prof bonus +6. Oar attack does 25 fire damage. Creepy AF. Long arms that seem to stretch out. 
	1. Voleth either wants a soul coin, or they want the party to kill an elf wizard named Obaris. They won't say why, but Obaris obtained a ride down the Styx, then refused to pay. Voleth isn't strong enough to exact punishment.
	2. Voleth is **the captain** to hire. They're stealthy, powerful, and they've never, ever lost a passenger. Nor are they types to lawyer up. 
3. Essentius The Ferryman - a lower-level Merrenoloth who can take the party along the river. They'll do so for 1000 platinum or a soul coin. Use Essentius and the party will face skill checks on INT, WIS, and Survival to successfully hire and navigate the Styx.
