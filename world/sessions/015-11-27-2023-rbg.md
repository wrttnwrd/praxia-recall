This starts in [Ironstead](/world/places/ironstead.md). It may move on from there, depending on what the players decide to do.

## The Plot
RBG is in Ironstead, which is currently surrounded by Duergar. Opens with players in the library.

Zheilhurd is there, showing the players the Tome Of The First Gods. 

At this point, Zheilthurd is quite agitated. He's got four mythic figures standing in his office. Not how he expected his day to go.

_Zheilthurd sits heavily in his chair, looking pale. After a moment, he mops his brow. "Sorry, sorry, I'm a little taken aback. This is most unusual. Not how I expected my day to go at all. This isn't a coincidence, what with all the happenings of late._

The happenings include the cracking of the Veda seal. Here, that was felt in the disappearance of the Ley Line, but also the diminishing of some divine protections on Ironstead and this place. That led to the stuff with the Avatar of Zuggtmoy. 

_Thelwald told me what you did. You did us all a service. Bad business, that one._

Zheilthurd can give them several tidbits:

1. The Tome also contains the location of all four Seals. Including the one at the foot of the Gloomspire, which is five days from Ironstead
2. The Seals are apparently attractors. Different Seals attract different things, but it doesn't always add up
3. The Tome contains what he THINKS are instructions for cracking the Seals. The instructions call for some kind of coin or disk, but he's not sure what
4. The players are free to read it, but it's written in Celestial.

Now, RBG has to decide what they want to do:

1. Try to persuade the deep gnomes to turn the Tome over to the Duergar
	1. They will be **very** resistant, but it could work. If persuaded, they'll ask the players to carry it out to them. The Duergar will then take it to Nigh Darul and ask the players to help escort it
2. Steal the Tome
	1. Then they have to decide what to do with it
3. Go talk to the Duergar

The craftsman they need (Zerla Pelletweaver) is actually in Nigh Darul. She **used** to live in Ironstead but left shortly before the blockade and is now stuck. If the party wants to create the Hand That Strikes, they will have to go to Nigh Darul. 

### What's Happening
Agrona Sef has escaped. They are seeking for the next Seal, unaware (as of yet) that RBG has it. They **do** know that the Tome is in Ironstead, where it has rested for decades. They don't know where the next Seal is, but the Tome would tell them.

There is an attacking force comprised of Drow and Duergar heading for Ironstead. They are allied with the Sef and want to seize the Tome. 


## If Outside
The force is per this encounter: https://www.dndbeyond.com/encounters/37d4b722-a563-49ff-9fa2-73978ef20882

## If in the city
But, if it's via infiltration, then it's 2 Gnome Assassins, 2 Duergar assassins, and the Drow Mage, no Driders. If they infiltrate, they come in via a passage opened by Thelwald. Thelwald's family is under threat from The Sef. RBG has seen what she can do, so they may be sympathetic. The five will attack (preferably) while the players are outside the library.

The Duergar will be invisible, as will the mage. The plan: Snatch the book and run. But Thelwald has second thoughts and raises the alarm. 



After the fight, Thelwald is badly wounded and ready to tell the party everything.

# Recap
**Background** RBG spent some time in Ironstead, studying the Tome Of The First Gods and talking with Zheilhurd, the librarian. Zheilhurd was understandably shellshocked after seeing people from the Tome walk into his library.

- You learned the location of all four Seals.
- You learned that each Seal attracts a certain kind of... thing... that doesn't always add up, but you can expect weird stuff when you're around them.
- The Tome is written in Celestial, so it's a bit difficult to parse.

The craftsperson who can create the Hand That Strikes is named Zerla Pelletweaver. She currently resides in the Duergar city of Nigh Darul. You'll have to go there if you want to meet her. 

## The Council Meeting
The party has worked out a meeting between the Duergar and Sverfneblin, but it's complicated, and I'll let you all manage that. 

## The Fight In The Library
A small force of Drow and Duergar attacked the library and attempted to steal the Tome. Thelwald apparently let them in after Agrona Sef (who has escaped) abducted and threatened his family. At the last minute, Thelwald tripped the alarm and locked the book. RBG showed up and took care of the attackers. Now, you're considering saving Thelwald's family. You've got some info about where they may be located. We can revisit that tonight.
