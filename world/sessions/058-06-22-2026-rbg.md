## Escort Mission

### Recap
With the aid of multiple low-level NPCs, you escorted a mob of terrified civilians through the ongoing battle for Silgorin Below. Devils and demons fought around you, and while devils saw the Badge Of Zariel and avoided you, demons did not hesitate. Your biggest challenge was the chasme demons - their Drone ability turned out to be a real problem for large groups of inexperienced humanoids.

You made it to the bunker, using a Spike Growth to stop a cresting demonic wave from blocking your escape. Brona expressed her gratitude to you as you all led the citizens to relative safety.

But the problem remains: The city is being drawn into the River Styx. If drowned, most if not all of the citizenry will be damned, providing more devils for Zariel and Asmodeus in the Blood War. That's Asmodeus's goal. Well, half his goal, the other half is to forever corrupt Silgorin, which was once Amoth's seat of power on Praxia. 

You faced a tough decision: Stop the chains and machinery that's going to drown the city, or head straight to Tiamat's prison. Because of the different passage of time in Avernus, a few more days down here could mean Praxian months (years?). In the end, you elected to stay and deal with the chains first, then head for Tiamat.

Reminders: 

- In an effort to rise to godhood, Asmodeus murdered Amoth, which led to The Fall, as well as Silgorin's sundering from Praxia and the forming of Silgorin Below. 
- Brona is the city guard lieutenant and commander of your NPCs. With Enthraxis's betrayal, she's now the leader of the citizenry and surviving Black Helms (the city guard). She's said that the bunker to which you led everyone is the safest place in the city and buys everyone some time.
- The chains being used to drag Silgorin Below into the Styx are anchored at eight points around the city. You've decided (I think) to head for the chain behind the Temple Of Asmodeus (previously the Temple Of Amoth). That's chain II, right behind #6 (the Temple), which is a relatively short trip.




### A Critical Moment
The players can choose to have one of their low-level characters make a heroic sacrifice. If they do, the NPC throws themselves in front of the demon, or pulls a civilian from burning wreckage, or something else. In that case, the low-level character gets **one** death save. Fail and they're dead.

### Encounters
These are various surge versions. In each case, roll 1d10 / 2 to determine which mob is hit.

#### The howling dark (dread wave)
A huge fanged visage manifests. It's Yeenoguh, exorting his troops to battle. While not physically threatening, it terrifies a mob. DC 13 Morale save vs Wisdom. The Shepherd can make use their action to make their check and add +5 to the save.

#### Chasme drones (dread wave)
Three Chasme demons fly overhead. One mob makes a DC 13 WIS save. On fail, lose one Morale and stunned. Any player can use their action to make the save and give the mob a +5.

#### A rain of arrows (crossfire)
A shadow falls over the column. Dozens of arrows rain down over a mob. One player can divert and stop this with a spell or an Athletics check (difficult). 

If they fail, that mob takes 2d6 damage as arrows pierce flesh.

#### Raking fire (crossfire)
A party of devils runs past the column, ignoring them because of the badge of Zariel. A group of demons waits in ambush, though, and lets loose a hail of stones, projectiles, and spells at the devils. The column, caught in between, must make an Acrobatics (DEX) check DC 14. If they fail, the mob takes 3d6 damage.

#### Collapsing Road (collapse)
A huge chunk of the road cracks open. A swarm of quasits boils out, ignoring the column as they fling themselves into the battle against a disciplined team of bearded devils. One mob must make a roll to avoid falling into the crack. DC 12 DEX save. 1d10 damage as one or more people fall into the crack and are either wedged and injured or fall to the River Styx below. Advantage conferred if successful Scout.

#### Crescendo (Breach)
Nearby demon and devil forces clash. Total confusion reigns as the two sides tear at each other. The column can sneak by, advancing one zone.

#### Recruitment (Wildcard)
The column happens upon a battle between devils and demons. The devils are losing. Seeing the Badge Of Zariel, they offer favors if one or more party members will join the fight. If the players join and the devils win, they might provide the players escort or, more likely, spread the world to leave the column alone.

### Final Encounter
Urguros, having routed Enthraxis and his troops, confronts the PCs in front of the entrance to the bunker. 

This fight is pure chaos. The players should have almost no problem dealing with the attack, but be sure to describe the quasits swarming on the players, dretches flinging themselves at them, and Urguros staying back.
