## Recap
Players decided to go speak to G'ul, not realizing that [the vaults](/world/places/the-vaults.md) they wanted to break into were actually his, and part of his business.

G'ul made it pretty clear that he couldn't give them permission, but he really doesn't like the Bourniers, so if he had plausible deniability, he'd let them go.

RBG has to get in and out without getting caught, within the next 24 hours.
