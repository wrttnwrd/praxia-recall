## Recap
This starts in battle with Sulomorn The Tidebender. The party defeated them, then proceeded to the Gloomspire.

They found the room with the Lida (Life) Seal in it. They fought and defeated a Kraken Priest and his minions. Just as they won, though, a giant tentacle reached into the chamber. They were all struck by lightning. Leviathus has arrived.

With Mossy down, the party has to regroup and deal with this new threat - the most challenging creature they've ever fought.

Fade to black.

## The Plot
RBG, on their way to the Gloomspire, got tangled up in a nasty fight against an ancient aboleth.

## Travel
RBG is now about 1/2 day from the Gloomspire. They may need to rest after this battle.

If they reach the Gloomspire, accomplish their mission, and then head to Nigh Darul, that's another 2 days southwest.

Note that the Underdark beyond the Whispering Abyss is completely collapsed. The party can't go further West without either:

- Returning to the surface
	- If they somehow fly out through the crack, they are in the middle of The Gap, where screaming winds and ocean floor uplift have created a long, narrow bit of land they can stand on
	- They can go back the way they came
- Picking through the rubble
	- There are narrow ways through the rockfall. It will take two days in the dark, with survival checks to not dislodge rocks or run afoul of whatever creatures are lurking about

## Encounters:

Roll d6. On a 4-6, roll below:

1. Giant Cave Toad (CR 13) hides in the mud under a small pond
2. Sandstorm. Lose 1 day. Make a DC 20 survival check. If succeeded, find shelter, and all players make a DC 15 CON save or gain a point of exhaustion. If failed, as previous but save at disadvantage and take 2d6 damage as sand and rock scrapes and batters the party
3. Displacer Beast Pride (CR 13) - they're hunting and will avoid the players if left alone
4. Two Behirs - https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/16804-behir - (CR 13) - they're hiding in a rocky area and attack as the players move through a ravine
5. Roper and Darkmantle Combo (CR 13)
6. Froghemoth (CR 13)

As the storm diminishes, the players must make a DC 25 Survival check to not get lost. If they fail, add 1 day to the journey.

## Scenes & Settings

### Approaching The Whispering Abyss
Conditions: As the players near the Gloomspire, the "weather" worsens. First, it's a low, wispy fog. Then it's a thick fog. Then rain. Then a driving rain, and finally, as they get to the Spire, it's a raging thunderstorm with high winds. This is all thanks to Leviathus, and an enormous crack in the ceiling of the Underdark. 

1. Ground: A half-day from the Whispering Abyss, the ground begins to change from sand and rock to a slimy, seaweed-covered mass. This is because of Sulomorn (see The First Aberration, below)
2. Weather: Six miles from the Abyss, the weather turns to a low-wispy fog. The players begin to notice a sound that's not so much a sound as a steady roar that feels as if it's making the air vibrate. It's not loud at first, but grows steadily in volume as they move closer
3. Weather: Three miles from the Abyss, the players are in a driving, salty rain. It blows in their faces from the direction of the lake. The shores of the Abyss are obscured by a dense curtain of fox and rain. Lightning flashes in the distance, and the roar is now loud enough that any speaking has to raise their voice to be heard
4. Weather: One mile from the Abyss the weather turns to a full-fledged gale. The wind blasts in 40 mile per hour gusts, and the rain becomes painful. Movement is slowed to 1/2 and the terrain is difficult
5. Weather: Once the players reach the shore of the lake, describe this:


>The ground under your feet trembles. The wind is an unrelenting howl that makes it hard to think, never mind speak. It feels like it's whipping the breath from your body.

>You shake the water from your eyes, shield your faces and in the near-constant strobes of lightning see that you stand on the shore of a massive underground sea. The surface is whipped into a madness of tall, white-capped waves. 

>Another flash of lightning and you understand the source of the weather: Overhead, a massive crack in the roof of the chamber reveals the open sky. The salty water of The Gap flows steadily through the wound and is whipped into rain by cyclonic winds that rage from above. 

>What exactly prevents the sea from flooding into the chamber, flooding both the cave and the rest of the Underdark, you have no idea.

>Ahead and to the right, partly obscured in the deluge, is a massive stone tower - the Gloomspire.

### The First Abberation
An Aboleth - https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/16762-aboleth - has taken up residence a mile from the Gloomspire. It enslaved a Sverfneblin (long dead) to build it a complex trap. Anyone falling into the trap is dropped from the surface into an underground lake, where the Aboleth attacks. Players w/ a passive PER > 18 notice something off about the ground up ahead.

If the players notice the trap, they avoid it and pass what appears to be a small pond. The pond is actually a narrow opening at the top of the underground lake where water has pushed up to the surface. The aboleth will use its lair and regional affects to attack the party.

Reason: The Aboleth, in spite of its great power, has fallen under the influence of Leviathus. It feels compelled to defend this area. It also seeks a way out of its prison and up to the surface.
### The Whispering Abyss
The Abyss is a deep, dark underground lake that's been there for thousands of years. [Leviathus](/world/people/leviathus.md), the ancient Kraken, traveled to and from the lake using an underwater tunnel that exited into The Gap. It was drawn there by the Lida Seal at the base of the Gloomspire. But the beast was trapped by The Fall, which collapsed the tunnel. 


#### Leviathus, The Desperate Titan
Per notes, proximity to the Lida Seal has tortured the titan. For all its power, it cannot traverse the space between the lake and the ceiling. It will do anything to escape. 

## The Gloomspire
The only way into the Gloomspire is the top. The party must somehow reach the roof, 90 feet off the ground, in the wind and rain.

Then they can make their way down through the tower. Have everyone roll a D20 at the start, just to build suspense. Then have the following:

1. A stair gives way underneath someone. Make a DC 20 DEX save or fall 20-30 feet to the next level, taking 2d6 falling damage. Then make a DC 15 CHAR save. A fail means you were unlucky, and you fall another 20-30 feet. And so on
2. The Lida stone pulses, sending a burst of aberrant life force up through the spire. All players make a DC 18 WIS save. On a fail, one of these happens:
	1. The player grows scales. AC goes up 2 and they lose 4 DEX (no lower than 5), with all attacks, etc. falling accordingly
	2. The player picks up a point of exhaustion
	3. All temporary magical effects created by spells or items are removed
	   All of these can be fixed with a lesser restoration, a short rest, or a remove curse.
3. The players here the cry of the Kraken. No specific effect, but the rumbling, wailing sound is utterly horrifying

At the base of the Spire is a single trap door, bound by a complex lock. 

It requires two rolls:

1. A DC 25 lock picking roll to disarm a lightning trap. On fail everyone in a 20' radius sphere takes 2d10 lightning damage and is paralyzed for 1 round. The Deep scions hear the 
2. A DC 20 lock picking roll to open the lock
### The Lower Chamber
Attracted and charmed by [Leviathus](/world/people/leviathus.md), four Deep Scions - https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/2560769-deep-scion -  have taken up residence. All have 120hp, do 14pts/claw attack, 16/19 Battleaxe damage. All have Psychic Screech but require a DC 18 WIS save, not DC 13. They are led by one greater Scion, which is also a Kraken Priest with 150hp (https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/2560858-kraken-priest).

They are all concealed in the cave and are ready. One of them followed the party down through the Spire. 

Their job: Prevent the removal of the Seal or, if that becomes impossible, cast it into the Abyss.

Once the fight is over, everything pauses. Leviathus is approaching. If the players do nothing, Leviathus leaves. If they remove the Seal, it thrusts tentacles through the doorway and menaces the party. They can flee, fight, or something else. Their call.

It **wants** to intimidate someone into helping it leave the Underdark.

### Nigh Darul
A 4-5 day journey from Ironstead.

There, they can find Zerla Pelletweaver and learn the secrets of the remaining blueprints. She can also create the Hand That Strikes, and provide some ideas where to go next.

- A Hag named Silver Gravemingle lived on Essedria in an ancient forest. She can make the Door
- A creature known as Rave The Howler lives high in the mountains above the Eastern Shore of Essedria. Her jawbone can yield the Mouth That Roars

Also in the city is a Duergar named Tysmora. She apparently possesses a length of Kraken gut. But she won't part with it unless someone helps her retrieve a small chest that's been hidden at the opposite end of her family vault for centuries. It's the balance and ruin room from the Deck (page 29)
