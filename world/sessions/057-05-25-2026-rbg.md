## Silgorin Below

### Recap (so far)
Notorious RBG boarded the vessel of Voleth Of The Fog and took the treacherous journey down [the Gurges](/world/places/the-gurges.md) (the enormous waterfall that plunges from the surface of Praxia to [Avernus](/world/places/avernus.md)). You learned that the toxic waters of the River Styx have affected [the Gurges](/world/places/the-gurges.md) themselves, and that various denizens of [Avernus](/world/places/avernus.md) also live in the falls.

You sailed down the falls for some time until the falls dwindled to a curtain of mist. There, Voleth had to stop in a fallen city to reconfigure his craft for sailing on the River itself. 

You found a city under siege. Demons and devils battle for control as the city itself is slowly dragged down towards the waters of the River Styx. Devils try to get citizens to sell their souls. Demons try to kill citizens before they have an opportunity to sell their souls.

Voleth implored you to stay with the boat for the two hour stop, but you heard screaming from some destroyed buildings and went to investigate.

You found Hornsby, TPT418, and Cravel fending off two chasme demons, protecting a small crowd of civilians.

After dispatching the demons, you talked to the Black Helms (Hornsby & co). They're trying to get the civilians to the city armory. It's the most defensible part of the city, and the Black Helms are gathering civilians there for a rescue operation they seem to think is on its way.

You're now guiding them all towards the armory. You came to the center of the city. There, from a huge, scarred, blackened stump of a tree stepped a twisted, demonic ent that, letting out a roar, charged towards you. That's where we left off.


Reminders: 

1. The fallen city is [Silgorin](/world/places/silgorin.md), which Asmodeus chained to [Avernus](/world/places/avernus.md) and is winching slowly into the River Styx. He wants to convert as many surviving citizens to evil as possible to provide more troops for the Blood War (the war between devils and demons that rages across [Avernus](/world/places/avernus.md)). He particularly enjoyed doing this to [Silgorin](/world/places/silgorin.md), which was the Praxian seat of Amoth's faith. He considers this his final revenge upon Amoth, whose murder triggered The Fall.
2. Zariel, the fallen angel, is the archdevil in charge of Avernus. Just a bit of regional trivia
3. You are immune to fire damage because of the boon granted to you by Urush Ghul. But he also exacted an as-yet unknown price
4. You have the Badge Of Zariel, which you must display to avoid being attacked on sight by Zariel's devils while in Avernus. This will not help you with demons, who are chaotic and attack everything 


### Descending the Gurges

Even with Voleth's help, descending the Gurges is chaotic and dangerous. The water begins to turn to the toxic waters of the Styx.

### Encounters

1. Landing place. Voleth tells the party they need to pause there so that he can arrange safe passage to the River Styx below. The party hears screaming at location 2.
2. Two [Chasme](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5194942-chasme) are hunting a group of eight and have cornered them at the end of an alley. If the party does nothing, two adult elves use potions to render their two children invisible. Then the rest of them attempt to stop the chasme. Included in the group is TPT-418, Crayvel, and Hornsby Willow. If the PCs ask, the three are trying to escort all survivors to the Armory where (apparently?) someone's coming to get them out
3. A party of eight [bearded devils](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5194920-bearded-devil) face off against 3 Barlgura. If the party does't intervene, the devils are defeated by the Barlgura, but one of them dies. The Barlgura then head for area 2, where they quickly find and consume the elf children and the adults, if they're still alive
4. Soul coin auction
5. The [Gulthias Blight](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195067-gulthias-blight). Once an enormous oak at the center of Silgorin, the tree has been infected by the evil around it and has only hatred for its surroundings. If the party moves across the commons, a swarm of [Razorvine Blights](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/3883559-razorvine-blight) and the Gulthias Blight attack. Note that there are, oddly enough, no demons or devils moving here. There are various puddles of ichor indicating a demon has died. After the battle, the players may notice a line of people stretching back from the temple (6)
6. Temple of Asmodeus. Players can interact with Entraxis's lieutenant Xalis, who is stuck minding the store and basically irritated. Once he sees the Badge, he's happy to explain, and the party can learn what's going on
7. Pitched battle between devils and demons. Nothing to see here
8. City Armory and the confrontation between Entraxis's forces and Urgora's horde

### Chain Stations
There are seven chain stations. Each is crewed by four bearded devils turning the gear, and one chain devil in charge. 

Whichever station the party goes to first is under attack from three vrock and one glabrezu. If the players don't intervene, then the demons win and the city tilts upward as that chain is released.

If the players don't visit a station, let them see the battle at station one, before they re-board Voleth's craft.

## Places

### Temple Of Asmodeus

A black slab of a building. The only thing unscathed in the ruins. Here, Xalis leads a team of imps, ice devils, and other devils that are collecting contracts from citizens of Silgorin. Citizens are lined up at the doors, with the line stretching halfway back to The Commons.

In the line they may meet:

**Fivuc**, an aging dwarf who has decided to sell his soul for safe passage. He doesn't know that save passage means a swift, painless death.

**Agis Qinric**, a young elf who was always a bit of a nihilist and believes there's no reason not to trade. She's trading her soul for a few items of comfort, like nice clothes and an apartment.

**Troy**, a human adult who's selling his soul for food for his children.


### The City Armory

Largely destroyed but defensible, Brona has a large force of Black Helms here protecting citizens who have made their way.

She's been led to believe there is a rescue coming. That is a lie. Entraxis is collecting everyone here so that he can "encourage" them to join the legions of Hell to save themselves.

At the armory, the players will meet:

**Brona** - captain of the Black Helms

**Guldius** - a Goliath bartender drafted by Brona into protecting the entryway. She has an enormous warhammer

**Burguk** - a half-orc sergeant in the Black Helms. He doesn't believe they're going to get rescued

**Leonard** - father of three small children who won't speak. Lost his wife in the fall and is just trying to get his children out

#### Enthraxis's Betrayal
Enthraxis, (an an [Ultraloth](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195244-ultroloth)  general, AC25) dressed as a member of the Hellriders, the city's elite battleforce, arrives to speak to Brona. He appears displeased with the presence of RBG.

He tells the party he's there to prepare the group to move. There are several wizards standing ready to magically transport survivors back to Praxia. He's already taken several groups.

In reality, Enthraxis is taking the people away eight at a time, then teleporting them to be tortured and turned. If pressed, his story doesn't hold up. He becomes angry and summons his troops: Two [Bone Devils](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5194930-bone-devil) as lieutenants, who then lead regimented units of 10 [Bearded Devils](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5194920-bearded-devil). As they come in, the party may or may not realize they're screwed. Brona looks grim and forms her troops up for a last stand. Then Urguros, a [Nalfeshne](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/16963-nalfeshnee) in service to an unnamed demon lord, arrives with her horde, a mix of [Quasits](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4775835-quasit) (20), [Dretches](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5194974-dretch) (10), and a few [Barlgura](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5194918-barlgura) (3). Urguros and Enthraxis have a history. They've been battling back and forth across Avernus for a long time. 

Urguros is there to stop Enthraxis from recruiting more souls by, at the very least, killing all the mortals. She'd also like to kill Enthraxis, but that's secondary. Enthraxis wants to continue his work, but death here is, for him, permanent. So he'll try to negotiate briefly.

This will be a battlefield crossing. Brona and her team will protect the flanks while the players take on several different roles:

1. Scout: Scouts ahead. Successes = advantage for Vanguard, negate some surges
2. Vanguard: Leads the way. Successes = +1 progress
3. Shepherd: Successes = +1 morale, fail = -1 morale
4. Rearguard: Protects against attacks during the attack beat

They can pick which roles have more people. More chances to roll means more chances to succeed (or fail).

**Where are they going?** The players can figure this out, but there are a few options you can present: They can head to the docks and try to persuade Voleth to help; Brona knows a safer place deep under the city where they could hold out far longer; they could try to find a teleportation resource

Actual combats as makes sense. 

Party tells me each what skill they want to use. All checks will be performed using that skill. All checks are DC 15.


##### Surge Table

1. Direct threat: Attack from total of CR 7 demons/devils
2. Crossfire: Scout rolls check. Success = nothing happens. Failure = -1 morale for 1d10 groups
3. Stragglers: 1 mob wanders off and are lost - shepherds roll to check 
4. Ambush: Direct threat, but no surprise - scout rolls to save
5. Horror: Witness something awful. Scout rolls check. Success = negated. Failure = -1 morale for 1d10 groups
6. Opportunity: +1 progress - scout rolls to succeed
7. The wrong attention: Group roll/choose who rolls. Failure = roll again
8. Attack at the rear: Rearguard rolls check - success = negated, failure = roll again
