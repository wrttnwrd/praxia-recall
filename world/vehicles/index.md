# vehicles

* [Marta's Fury](martas-fury.md) - Oriana Valtova's airship, bought with her share of the slaad's control gem and named for Marta Valtova.
* [The Albion](the-albion.md) - A Constitution-class warship out of Earth, 1789 — fought at the Battle of Urdun under Obadiah's gun crews, then returned home.
* [The Gray Foam](the-gray-foam.md) - Bournier smuggling ship RBG were hired onto, and which Elissa meant to see destroyed with them aboard.
* [The Villain's Discontent](the-villains-discontent.md) - RBG's airship — a Zephyr Assault Frigate, half the size of the Wave Serpent, carrying three ballistae and the BFG.
* [The Wave Serpent](the-wave-serpent.md) - Grimshaw Shadowplank's airship — 450hp, 65,000gp, crew of ten; wrecked in the attack Sango survived.
