# Magic Item: Soul Coins

## Description
Dull disk-shaped coins, roughly 1 inch in diameter and 1/3rd of an inch thick. These unadorned, plain coins coins appear to be made of blue-green stone and are far heavier than they look. They seem to absorb the light around them.

## Details
Those who make deals with devils are damned. Those break their pacts with devils are punished. Some, though, are so useless, or so untrustworthy, that their captors use their souls to mint coinage. The resulting soul coins are prized by fiends of both Hell and The Abyss.

They appear to be plain, gold coins, with two key differences:

- When exposed to moonlight, they glow dully 
- Any non-fiend touching a soul coin hears distant screaming so faint they think they are imagining it

Destroying a soul coin releases the trapped soul. Soul coins can only be destroyed using a Wish, or by smelting the coin in the smithy where it was forged.

Fitted into the indentation in the back of a Seal, a soul coin cracks the Seal and releases the energy within.
