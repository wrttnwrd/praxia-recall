Unbeknownst to the players, Tiamat cursed them. They bear the Five Fold Brand.

She's been biding her time, waiting for them to be in proximity w/ dragonfolk at a bad time.

TLDR: The players are slowly separated from those they love the most, all while they serve as unwitting messiahs for Tiamat's horrifying return.

## Key Facts

1. Simply doing damage won't kill Tiamat. She is not native to Avernus. Dying there won't erase her
2. You have to use the Dagger Of Amoth - used to (ironically) slay Amoth

## Lore
Tiamat has been a prisoner on Avernus for centuries.

## The Big Reveal
The room goes dark. You are all standing in an empty void beneath five malevolent, watching stars, each a different color. You hear a triumphant, sibilant whisper that echoes in your very soul: 

> **You thought you won?! Mortal idiots. All of you. Before, I planned simply to escape my prison. Now, I shall carve my likeness upon the very rock of your world. My armies will raze Praxia from pole to pole. And you? You shall be my banner.** You should have killed me when you had the chance.

You feel a searing heat on your skin as a five-pointed brand appears on your dream-self. Five colors: Red. Green. White. Blue. Black.

At that moment, the nearest dragonfolk go mad and attack everyone EXCEPT the party.
