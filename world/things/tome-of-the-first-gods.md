## Description
An ancient text, inscribed on pages of ancient paper, bound in hide.

## Details
There were five First Gods. 

Together, they formed the fabric of the Cosmos itself: The Void. The Five Aran Deones (First Gods) walked the Void. They combined their elements and created the first world. But their elements, left to mix freely, tore the young planet apart. In the calamity, the Fifth God was lost to time.

The four remaining gods mourned their fifth and were called:

**Veda** (faith, divinity)
**Lida** (life, naturum)
**Mist** (magic, arcanum) 
**Flux** (chaos, change)

## The Creation Of Praxia
Of the four remaining, Lida, Goddess of Life, most wanted to create another world to fill with living things. So, the Four First combined their elements again. But before they did, Mist wove together four Seals. When the planet was formed, she placed the seals along a single line and called it the Ley Line. There placed, the Seals controlled the combination of the elements, carefully combining chaos, arcanum, naturum, and divinity in perfect balance. 

## The Biran Deones (Second Gods)
As Praxia formed, Life, Faith, Magic, and Chaos found their place and gave rise to the Biran Deones - these are the gods most of us know: Amoth, Bahamut, Mystra, and others. 

Those gods loved and strove against each other, creating more deities, large and small. These were Those That Followed, or the Sequar Deones.

And all of these generations of gods embodied one or more of the Aran Deones: The Raven Queen (Life), Sar (chaos), Asmodeus (faith, magic), Callistra (chaos), and others.

## The Peoples
[Praxia](/world/places/praxia.md) saw new peoples arise: Orcs, humans, elves, dwarves, and countless others.

These peoples also loved and strove against each other. Some waxed. Others waned. They lived across many planes: The Prime Material, The Nine Hells, The Abyss, Pandemonium, to name a few.

## The Seals
Through all of this, [the Seals](/world/things/the-seals.md) balanced the four elements and protected the fragile world.

With the Aren Deones, the Seals prevented the end of all things.

With the Biran Deones, the Seals prevented catastrophe. 

With the Sequar Deones, the Seals prevented disaster.

And, as the peoples grew, the Seals kept the many planes separate, allowing the various peoples to grow and learn until they found their own way to each other.

So the Seals work today, along the Ley Line, separating the planes until all is ready.

## Settling The Void
The First Four left [Praxia](/world/places/praxia.md) and created more worlds, and stars, and sought for their Fifth, who they mourned and hoped could return to the fold.

## The First War
As time passed, the later gods, who had never known the First, became jealous of the Second. They resolved to find the First and . They made weapons of war and gathered armies of humans, elves, and dwarves, and marched on the temples of the Second Gods.

The Second strove against the armies and cast them down, saddened by the death of so many. The Later Gods gave no pause and attacked directly. Many of the Second fell, and of the Later Gods, *names illegible* approached the very gates of the Second City.  

They could not enter the City. The Second Gods were too powerful, But the Later Gods brought with them a powerful weapon: The Stygian Gate, or the Door That Opens. With it, they could summon abominations from other planes and unleash them upon Praxia. They demanded the locations of the Seals. If the Second Gods left their city or refused the locations, they would open the Gate.

The Second Gods selected five heroes - a tabaxi, two humans, and elf, and another of a race as yet unseen - to battle the Later Gods. They gave the Pentar Virones weapons of great power, and skills and strength of body and mind greater than others of their kind. The five heroes fought their way through the Later Gods' strongest minions and, before the Later Gods could stop them, closed the Stygian Gate.

*In the battle, the leader of the Later Gods, Thargorax, was flung down and hurled, broken, through the Gate.*

With the closing of the Gate the Later Gods were dismayed and fled before the anger of the Second Gods.

## DM Only
These Seals may be destroyed in many ways: Great force, annihilation, a Wish, or in some cases, take a Seal to ruin by using a soul coin. 

1. A  Legendary item created on and imbued with a fundamental element from its home plane. [Soul coins](/world/things/soul-coins.md) are one example, as they are pure divinity. The item must be placed on the Seal. Then someone must read The Sundering Ritual from the Tome Of The First Gods
2. A great deal of force. >1000 HP of force damage, inflicted instantly
3. Holding it in a Sphere of Annihilation for 10 seconds
4. A Wish, correctly worded

Each of the Seals is secreted around this world. The Tome has the locations:

1. The first in solid rock at the southern tip of Sillidria - Arcanum
2. The second in a globe on half of the twin islands in The Gap - Flux
3. The third deep under an unnamed island in The Gap, at the base of the Gloomspire, which is 5 days' journey from Ironstead under good conditions, but no one's been there since before The Fall - Veda
4. The fourth is embedded in the goddess's forehead on Essedria's Plains Of The Mother - Lida

The Seals attract all manner of nastiness around them. [Leviathus](/world/people/leviathus.md) is near the Gloomspire, for example.
