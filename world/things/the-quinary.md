Recipes for these items once created by the avatars of each Founding.

Once four items exist, the others gain power. This is true whether a single party possesses them all or not.

**If all five items exist, all holders of the items gain 20 temporary hit points if all items are within 1 mile of each other. Temp HP do not stack.**

If an item is destroyed, the power of other items declines accordingly.

## The **Eye** That Sees (Mist) 
These goggles provide the wearer with uncanny vision.

A pair of golden goggles with glittering lenses and a small switch on the left-hand side of the left eye. Flipping the switch activates a power.

1. Awards darkvision
2. Protects against conditions that might impact sight (DM discretion)
3. Awards Truesight for 2d8 minutes every day

Starting at 4 items
3. Awards darkvision to 120 feet
4. Heavily obscuring effects (like heavy fog or dense foliage) cause disadvantage but you can see
5. Obscuring effects cause no disadvantage

Sacrifice Power:
6. Foresight 1/day. Maximum hp reduced 50% (can't be healed beyond 1/2 your maximum) (Long Rest or Greater Restoration required to reverse)

Ingredients:

- Worked iron from the Plane Of Fire
- Arcane Globe
- Leaded glass
- Worked at super-high temperature

## The Hand That Strikes (Lida)
A long, graceful bow with simple markings and a nearly-invisible string. A trained archer need only attune and use the item.

1. +3 longbow
2. Truestrike 1x/day as a bonus action

Starting at 4 items
3. You can now fire a blurring volley: You can make a single ranged attack against all targets within 60 feet at -8 to each attack

Sacrifice power:
4. A single arrow of slaying. Make a normal attack. 1-3 it does 50% of the targets maximum hp; 4-6 is slays the target instantly. **When fired** (even if it doesn't hit) instantly kills creatures elsewhere on Praxia w/ hp totaling the target's HP. So, if you fire at a creature w/ 1000 hp, then 1000hp worth of creatures elsewhere die

Ingredients:

- The limb of an Ent
- Nature Globe
- Gut from a Kraken
- Worked by a gnomish crafter of great repute

## The Light That Sparks (Veda)
A simple, highly-polished platinum ring. Pressing a small diamond stud that's concealed on the underside of the ring activates powers of the user's choice:

1. Bless 2x/day
2. Heal 2x/day

Starting at 4 items:

3. Greater Restoration, 1x, recharge each day on 5-6
4. Revivify 1x, recharge each day on 5-6

Sacrifice Power:
5. Mass Heal. Caster takes 2x the healed damage as necrotic

Offers protection from even the most potent enemies

Ingredients

- Pure mithril ingot
- Divinity globe

## The Mouth That Calls (Flux)
A horn made of intricately worked bone.

1. Possession gives you advantage on all hearing checks

Starting at 4 items:

2. Blowing the horn requires up to 3 selected targets to make DC15 WIS saves or be frightened for 5 minutes (w/in 5 face time?)
3. Cast Divine Word, 1x/day

Sacrifice power:

4. True resurrection as it summons the soul of the dead being, regardless of where it is. Target revived for 1-10 years. Then irrevocably dead, sent to wherever "heaven" is for that person. Caster dies at the same time.

Said to shout across the planes themselves.

Ingredients:

- Flux globe
- Jawbone of Rave The Howler - haunts the peaks on the Eastern Shore of Essedria
- Place them near each other and it should form

## The Door That Opens

This actually appears to be a large, wooden key. Speak the power word and use as if you're opening a lock, though, and the cloth unfurls, opening a gate.

One power: Gate, one time, no concentration required. Caster is rendered unconscious w/ 2 failed Death saves. All damage is necrotic. 55% chance caster is instantly killed. Can be used to summon deities, but if so, Caster is instantly and irrevocably killed.

Ingredients:

- An undamaged _pwafi_
- Unknown globe
- All four of the other items must be in existence

Woven by a hag of fate under the two new moons.

Ignomantic magic

## Assembling the Quinary

The svervneblin Zerla Pelletweaver (currently living in Nigh Darul) is one of the few artificers on the planet who can read and decipher these blueprints. She can also make the Hand.

Other artificers need a DC 20 check to interpret the blueprints.

When making them, the artificer must make four DC 20 successes. Each failure removes a success. If they go negative, then the attempt fails, everyone within 10 feet takes 2d6 force damage, and they have to start over with new materials (the globe survives). Other artificers can assist but must describe how.
