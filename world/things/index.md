# things

* [Soul coins](soul-coins.md) - Coins minted from the souls of those too useless or untrustworthy to keep; fitted into the back of a Seal, one cracks it.
* [The Astrolabe Of Three Wishes](astrolabe-of-three-wishes.md) - A platinum astrolabe that casts Wish when correctly configured; once spent, it is an ordinary astrolabe forever.
* [The Chains Of Gleynir](chains-of-gleynir.md) - What binds Tiamat to Avernus; they demand repair, and Arkhan The Cruel works to break them.
* [The Dagger Of Amoth](dagger-of-amoth.md) - The blade used to slay Amoth — the only thing that can permanently kill Tiamat.
* [The Door That Opens](the-door-that-opens.md) - The Quinary piece the Hag must weave; without it the Gate cannot be opened wide enough for Thargorax's horde.
* [The Eye That Sees](the-eye-that-sees.md) - Golden goggles of Mist granting darkvision, protection from blinding, and truesight; its sacrifice power is Foresight at the cost of half maximum HP.
* [The Fivefold Brand (curse)](the-brand.md) - Tiamat's curse on Notorious RBG — a five-pointed brand in red, green, white, blue and black, marking them as her banner.
* [The Hand That Strikes](the-hand-that-strikes.md) - A long, graceful bow of Lida with a nearly-invisible string — a +3 longbow with Truestrike once a day.
* [The Ley Line](the-ley-line.md) - The single line along which Mist set the Seals; airships tie to it to move, and breaking a seal breaks the line.
* [The Light That Sparks](the-light-that-sparks.md) - Quinary piece with a sacrifice power; Rikterr used it at Mistwharf and died.
* [The Lor Manádh](lor-manadh.md) - A gigantic engine of the Ancients, wrecked when it crashed travelling the planes and lying dormant high in the Mournfang Range.
* [The Quinary](the-quinary.md) - Five items, one per Founding; once four exist the others gain power, and all five within a mile grant their holders 20 temporary hit points.
* [The Seals](the-seals.md) - Four seals woven by Mist along the Ley Line, holding chaos, arcanum, naturum and divinity in balance and keeping the planes apart.
* [The Searing Brand](the-searing-brand.md) - Urush Gûl's boon granting immunity to fire damage, for an unspecified price.
* [The Stygian Gate](the-stygian-gate.md) - The rift on the Field of Bone that can bridge Avernus to the Prime Material plane; Thargorax was cast through it.
* [The Tome Of The First Gods](tome-of-the-first-gods.md) - An ancient text bound in hide giving the making of the world, the Aran Deones, and the Seals; one of three places holding the binding ritual.
* [The Voice That Calls](the-voice-that-calls.md) - The Quinary piece made from the jawbone of Rave The Howler; the reason Cregyn and Sango have only five years to live.
