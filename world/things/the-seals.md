## Chaos - Flux
Was located at East Godelis but has been removed by RBG. Long term exposure can lead to chaotic behavior.

## Life - Lida
Located near the Gloomspire in the Underdark. Long term exposure can lead to random effects:

- De-aging
- Sudden (uneven) growth
- Aging/loss of HP (necrotic)

(Destroyed by Thargorax)

## Divine

## Arcane - Veda
Was located near Porthmoor, now destroyed by [Agrona Sef](/world/people/agrona-sef.md).

## What To Do With The Seals?
As long as the Seals are accessible, they're a risk, and the destruction of even one more could cause catastrophe rivaling The Fall.

A few possibilities:

1. Hide them on Praxia
2. Hide them in a demiplane or on another plane
3. Destroy them all and let the chips fall where they may
4. Bind with them: A being can bind with each Seal. In so doing, they will pass beyond this existence, carrying the seals back to the First Gods. This is close to a permanent solution, although the First Five could return them. The binding ritual is involved and requires 2 days, plus a being with sufficient constitution/HP to survive the ritual. The being does not have to be a willing participant.
	1. Knowledge of this ritual is contained deep w/in the Temple Of Helm, in a document in House Umbra, and hidden in the writings of the Tome Of The First Gods
