# Magic Item: The Astrolabe Of Three Wishes

A beautifully-built astrolabe of platinum. If adjusted to the correct configuration, it allows the user to cast the Wish spell, with all the attendant dangers. 

Once all charges are expended, reverts to an ordinary astrolabe. Charges cannot be restored.
