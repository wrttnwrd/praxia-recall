Sango was banished into a small demiplane by a sahuagin priestess and spent
the battle there. Mossy rained fire and ballista bolts from the Discontent;
Drak fought sahuagin under the Albion's hull; Rikterr held the docks. The
Albion, rearmed and repaired, weathered the Xebec assault and returned to
Earth afterwards.

Unresolved from the fight: who the mysterious Abishai that waylaid Rikterr
were, and what they wanted.
