## Story
*What we all know is this: In 0 FR, an immense object fell from the sky and struck Essedria just East of the Spine Of The World, cracking the very skin of Praxia herself. Much of Essedria east of the Spine and north of the Helgoril was lost in moments. South of the Helgoril was engulfed in a firestorm. Enormous waves swept across The Gap and around to the Sedruth Sea. These waves raced up the Ibril, Helgoril, and other rivers, scouring all but the highlands of both Silidria and Essedria of life. In days, the Elven empires of Essedria were destroyed. The  Dwarven strongholds were not heard from again. Then the world was plunged into an ashen dusk.*

*But we know more: At the moment of The Fall, the priests, priestesses, and holy warriors of Amoth lost their powers. Some died. Others went mad. Survivors said they heard a great noise, like a howling or a shrill death rattle, and then nothing. We know that followers of Asmodeus gained great power after the Fall. And we know that the Fall cracked the Orathac, nearly freeing Zathustra, Asmodeus's traitorous consort. Only the heroics of The Spear saved us from a ruin that would have made the Fall look like bad weather.*

*I believe our world was stricken as one of the Second Gods fell. Asmodeus of the Sequar Deones (the gods that followed) betrayed and slew Amoth of the Biran Deones (second gods). He did so to gain power, and he succeeded. Amoth perished. He fell, struck Essedria, and brought ruin to the world.* 

*You "scholars" scoff at the idea, but open your eyes to what has befallen us. Aventine thrives, it is true, high above the sea, on cliffs of bedrock. Elsewhere, millions have died. An entire continent burned. What explanation do you offer? A "natural disaster?" Half true. This was not natural. We were trodden down by the greed of a god that followed. If we mean to avoid a repeat, we must commit these events to memory. And plan.*

Anara Lorebinder, historian to Feyd Azari, speaking at the University of Aventine, 1 FR
