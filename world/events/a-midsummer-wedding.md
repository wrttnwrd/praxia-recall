Fidelia Azari and Gallias (GAL-ee-yus) are to be wed during the Midsummer Festival.

1. Invite

## Story
Cetranah and Veles have broken with their son, Gallias, over his engagement to Fidelia Azari. They feel she is beneath his station and would prefer he marry another member of the Summer Court.

This has changed Gallias from a Spring to a Summer Eladrin. 

In an attempt to break off the marriage, the parents have retained Diskuss, who's mission is to place an enchantment on Fidelia that will cause her to fall in love with the first person she sees. 

They foolishly believe Gallias will simply abandon his lover if she appears to betray him. He, on the other hand, is far too sophisticated for that.

Have the party roll D20. Highest roll "wins." 

## Strong Start
The PCs are spending time at the mansion when a messenger in royal regalia appears. She invites them to attend the wedding of Fidelia Azari and Gallias on the morrow (which is also Midsummer).

It is a somewhat small affair, quickly organized in the aftermath of the confrontation with Thargorax.

It would mean a great deal to Feyd and Fidelia if they were there.

*For a moment, the entire area carries the overwhelming scent of summer flowers. A column of silvery light appears to spring from the ground, and the guards grip their swords. Gallias looks pained, and a pair of Eladrin appear, floating over a shimmering pool of silver. The Eladrin are fabulously dressed in warm-colored silks and soft boots. The woman wears a finely-styled tiara of wrought mithril and carries a gift. The man wears a rapier and holds his partner's hand in a delicate, courtly clasp.*

*While everyone else freezes in shock, Gallias steps forward. "Hello father, mother."*

They ask him to introduce his bride, which he does. They exchange some words w/ the two of them, then guide their son away. Fidelia cocks an eyebrow, then walks towards the win table.

### The Wedding Itself
Before the ceremony, the party gets to mingle a bit.

They're presented to Fidelia and Gallias and get a description.

They can chat with Saroya, who approaches them.

Or they can seek out other personages: Hexxam, Feyndal, Palmer, heads of houses, whatever

### A Romantic Fumble
Fidelia, freshly enchanted, sees (party rolls d20). She is immediately besotted and throws herself on the PC.

She is behaving utterly unlike herself.

A player w/ passive PER > 20 may notice a gnomish waiter hustling away from the table where Fidelia had been standing. That waiter is Diskuss, who just cast an enchantment on Fidelia.

*Diskuss (disguised): A hideous, gnome-like creature with an enormous hooked nose, bushy, uneven eyebrows, ears that nearly scrape his shoulders, small, meaty hands, and a single long hair growing from the shelter of an enormous mole on his left nostril.*

*Diskuss (revealed): A medium humanoid with gossamer wings, dark gray skin, solid black eyes and pointed teeth that simultaneously communicate malevolence and mirth. They smell fragrantly of lily's and hemlock.*

There is a 2 in 20 chance Gallias notices this, but he is deep in conversation with his parents.

To resolve this, they must find Diskuss and get them to remove the enchantment. But Diskuss did this on the orders of the Gloaming Court. They will need to hear from the Baroness Of Air And Darkness.

Note that, if the party does nothing, Fidelia will return to herself in a few days, but it's a very embarrassing incident and the wedding is postponed.

### Saroya
The Salmuirian diplomat would like a word. She could use the party's help with a small matter in Salmuir. She can't speak much of it here (she sends a brief glare towards Xavis, who nods and smiles back) but, if they're agreeable to giving her a ride once more, could go with them to Salmuir in two weeks (she has business in Aventine first).

### Midsummer Race Of Wheels
Teams form up at a start line. At the other end are four tables, each with a set of ingredients hidden from view. At the start, they run to the tables and get to work.

All teams are given the same tools and ingredients:

1. A large wagon wheel
2. A small quantity of tar
4. One flask oil
5. Three 5' x 5' pieces of burlap
6. Several blocks of wood
7. Assorted dyes: Red, green, yellow, orange
8. 20 iron nails
9. 5' of thick hempen rope
10. Two leather cylinders, 6" in length and 2" in diameter
11. Two wineskins (empty) 
13. Four candles (tallow, 6" long)
14. Two mallets, two knives, pliers, and other tinker's tools 
15. Flint and tinder

No magic, no other tools allowed.

The objective is to build a wheel that will roll down the hill from the Northern Gate, reaching the bottom first. But there are points given for the most spectacular wheel, as well.

Seven judges vote after.

Competitors:

- A team of University students headed up by Palmer
- Five individuals from Bluestrike
- Siliib and four friends
- RBG

## Fantastic Locations

### The Wedding
A large tent in a beautiful setting high on a hill at the center of the University, looking out over The Gap. Many of Aisha's students and fellow faculty are there, as well as members of the houses and her family.

A gorgeous table laden with summer delicacies, including iced fruits, wine, bread baked by the Hull Ward bakery, elaborate pastries from the palace kitchens, and carefully prepared, single-serving quail.

For a royal wedding, this looks to be a modest affair.

Guests mill about, chattering away.

Siliib is off to the side, wine glass in hand, looking slightly drunk and very awkward.

It's a beautiful, sunny day.

Guards are posted around the tent.

Nearby, preparations are underway for tomorrow's Midsummer celebration, scheduled to take place the morning after the wedding feast.

### Fey Crossing
Temporarily created when Cetranah and Veles appear. It is a shimmering pool of silver liquid sitting about 50 feet from the wedding tent.

### The Gloaming Court
Page 195, DMG

Players will need to persuade the Gamanoc, Baroness of Air And Darkness, to lift the enchantment. She won't be that hard to persuade, depending on what the players ask for.

She prizes instinct and intuition. She does **not** like the fancy, highborn antics of the Summer Court and only did this because she owed Cetranah and Veles a favor. 

See image below. She smells of peat, rotting leaves, and heavy dew on flowers.

She can be:

- Persuaded that she's done her job
- Convinced that she's resisting the obvious attraction of Fidelia and Gallius and submitting them to the will of the Summer Court
- Bargained with, if the party offers her something of sufficient value
