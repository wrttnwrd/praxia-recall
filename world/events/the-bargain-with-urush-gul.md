Gûl's predicament, explained in his lair: Tiamat means to escape and return
the Prime Material to its former glory as the domain of chromatic dragons,
wiping out most other sentient species. Gûl prefers having humanoids around
to serve him — but he has sworn fealty to her, and even imprisoned her power
is such that he cannot disobey. So he must thread the tiniest of needles:
deliver a weakened party, while granting them a boon that ensures Tiamat does
not get her way.

> "You think you're safe because of the mumblings of an old man? Avernus has
> changed. The fires will burn you alive before you can speak. My brand is the
> only thing that will keep your flesh on your bones."

He offered the **Searing Brand** — immunity to fire damage — and took an
unspecified price.
