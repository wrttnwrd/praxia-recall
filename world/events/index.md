# events

* [A Midsummer Wedding](a-midsummer-wedding.md) - 15 Summertide 07 — Fidelia Azari weds Gallias amid an enchantment planted by the Gloaming Court's jester.
* [The Ambush at Mistwharf](ambush-at-mistwharf.md) - 20 Flowering 07 — Kergek revealed as Celdastrus; Agrona disintegrated him; Rikterr died using The Light That Sparks and was revivified.
* [The Avernus Expedition](the-avernus-expedition.md) - -80 FR — eleven of the Raven Queen's order went to Avernus to disrupt fiendish incursions into Krag M'goth; three returned.
* [The Bargain with Urush Gûl](the-bargain-with-urush-gul.md) - 23 Leaffall 07 — the dragon crippled the Villain's Discontent, then sold RBG the Searing Brand and passage toward Silgorin for an unspecified price.
* [The Battle of Urdun](battle-of-urdun.md) - 8-10 Leaffall 07 — the Blood Corsairs and the Sahuagin of the Dark Reach assaulted Urdun and were beaten by the orcs, RBG and the Albion.
* [The Fall](the-fall.md) - The cataclysm that resets the calendar; Amoth's impact scoured Essedria and changed the climate of the world.
* [The Fall of the Chain](the-fall-of-the-chain.md) - 4 The Rotting 07 — Rikterr's sacrifice at the quay in Avernus, and the wave that followed.
* [The First War](the-first-war.md) - The war in which Thargorax led the mortal hosts against the Second Gods, was felled, and was cast through the Stygian Gate.
* [The Pandora Crisis](pandora-crisis.md) - 4 FR — the crisis during which Guthra G'hul started the Court; Team Spear's saving of Pandora returned the Gate to its original site.
* [The Silence of Amoth](the-silence-of-amoth.md) - The night Asmodeus finished killing Amoth — the shock tore a hole to Avernus and dragged the city of Silgorin through it, leaving the crater that became the Gurges.
* [The Ten Years War](ten-years-war.md) - BR 95 — the houses' decade-long campaign to destroy Fel House after Vek Azar tried to create The Monster.
