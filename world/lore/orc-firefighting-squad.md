# **Orc Firefighting Squad (CR 4)**

_Medium Humanoid (Orc), LN — unit of 6 orcs acting as one creature_  
**AC** 20 • **HP** 100 (10d8+40) • **Speed** 30 ft.  
**STR** 17 (+3) • **DEX** 12 (+1) • **CON** 18 (+4) • **INT** 10 (+0) • **WIS** 12 (+1) • **CHA** 10 (+0)  
**Saves** Str +6, Con +7 • **Skills** Ath +6, Per +4  
**Senses** darkvision 60 ft., PP 14 • **Lang** Orc, Common  
**PB** +3
### **Traits**

**Coordinated Unit.** Occupies 10×10 ft.; attacks deal half dmg below half HP.  
**Shield Wall.** Resist nonmagical B/P/S while above half HP.  
**Bucket Relay (3/day).** Bonus action: squad heals 10 HP.  
**Fire Response Team.** Squad + allies w/in 10 ft. have adv. on saves vs fire.
### **Actions**
**Multiattack.** Two _Shield-bash_ or one _Water Discharge_ + one _Shield-bash_.  
**Shield-bash.** +6 to hit, 5 ft.; 12 (2d6+3) bludgeoning; DC 14 Str save or push 5 ft.  
**Water Discharge (Recharge 5–6).**  
• _Single Target:_ +4 to hit, 20/60 ft.; 14 (4d6) bludgeoning; extinguishes flames; fire creatures take +7 (2d6) cold.  
• _Area Spray:_ 15-ft. cone; DC 14 Str save; 14 (4d6) bludgeoning (half on success); extinguishes flames.

### **Reaction**

**Interpose Shields.** Disadvantage on attack vs squad (only above half HP).

### **Notes**

Specialized orc team: 2 firefighters (water tanks), 2 shield-bearers, 2 runners. Highly coordinated; functions like a disciplined unit with anti-fire utility.
