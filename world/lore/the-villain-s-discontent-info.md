Boiling Oil X 2 AC 19 HP 20 Pours oil on a 10ft square area

The BFG
Lightning cannon doing 10d12 siege damage to a structure or 6d12 to a creature.

- +1 to hit
- 50 foot range
- Two charges, recharges at end of day

On a 1, misfires and can't be fired again.

On a 20, does max damage but also does 5d10 to whoever is using the gun.
