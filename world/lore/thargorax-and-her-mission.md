One aspect of Tiamat, and one of the Later Gods, Thargorax was a lesser god who spent a great deal of time among the peoples of Praxia.

The scholar Vogrel Stonebeard's lecture [On The Fall of Thargorax](/world/lore/on-the-fall-of-thargorax.md) is the best-known profile of this most interesting of the Sequar Deones.

Thargorax led the mortal hosts against the Second Gods in the First War. She was felled by a blow from ___ and cast through the Stygian Gate. Her five lieutenants fell with her but perished.

She has been imprisoned on Avernus for two millennia. She is a being of pure hate, bent on one thing: Returning to Praxia and throwing down the Biran Deones (second gods).

Her plan: Wait for a time when the barrier between planes is weakest, then tear the Stygian Gate open, allowing her and her hordes to follow her onto Praxia, then use the world to mount her assault on the Biran Deones.

Play Thargorax as the [aspect of tiamat](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/21927-tiamat), but her power is limited. She does not have her limited magic immunity. She does not have Divine Word. Her saves are a bit easier. 

If the players encounter her, it will be while she's partially in and out of the Stygian Gate, so she won't be able to fly, either.

## Limitations
Thargorax has one terrible limitation: She's an evil demi god. She's used to scheming, plotting, and opportunism. She can't imagine RBG would turn down the chance to become part of her army.

## What She's Doing

## The Story
Thargorax wants the Pentar Virones (RBG). Only they, with Quinary, can open the Stygian Gate wide enough to let her through. They can crack the Seals with the Quinary, then cast all of it into the Pit, opening the Gate wide.

The histories RBG has seen are partly modified to make them believe that during the First War the Pentar Virones actually sided with Thargorax, but that's not true. The Pentar Virones battled Thargorax with weapons provided them by the Second Gods. 

History isn't repeating itself. Thargorax is manipulating events to set up a final confrontation on the Field Of Bone, near the latest manifestation of the Stygian Gate, aka the pit.

Thargorax will attempt to convince them to side with her. Her pitch:

The Biran Deones inflicted great harm upon Praxia. The Seals are keeping the First Gods from returning and providing choice. Until now, they've all been playthings on the strings of the Second Gods. With the return of the First would come something new: Choice. No more reliving the same histories again and again.

This is, of course, all lies. Thargorax wants to come through with her hordes, conquer Praxia, then set Praxia up as a base from which she can attack the Second Gods. But the only way to open the Stygian Gate wide enough is with the Door That Opens. That will let her and her hordes loose.

To fully open the gate, she needs to cast the remaining Seals and the rest of the Quinary into the Door That Opens. That will release enough energy in just the right way. That means RBG has to get Hexxam and Umbra to return the Seals to the Field of Bone. 

There are two possible ways this can go.
### Setting The Scene
 Agrona Sef immediately attacks, silently using Ignomancy to bind the players in place. Have the players make DC 25 DEX saves. If they fail, they're immobilized and at disadvantage for all melee attacks. They can't make DEX saves against AOE attacks, and they can't move.
 
Thargorax appears as a beautiful woman, hovering above the Pit. The area around the Pit appears to be an oasis amidst the ruins of the Field.

If she's given the chance, she explains her plan.

### If the Party Sides With Thargorax
The party attempts to get Umbra's soldiers to return the Seals. If they succeed, Agrona Sef opens the Gate with the party's help. Thargorax fully emerges and lays waste. 

If they fail and Umbra refuses (very likely), Thargorax flies into a rage and attempts to tear the Gate open. See The Battle, below.

### If the Party Opposes Thargorax
More likely, RBG will refuse Thargorax's offer.

Somehow, Thargorax needs to get the Seals and the Quinary. She's now gotten everyone in one place. She doesn't know the party doesn't have all Seals.

### The Battle
Either way, it's very likely the party ends up in a confrontation with Agrona and Thargorax. Thargorax has two ways to win:

#### Tear the Pit open and come through without her horde, then grab the Quinary and try to do the work herself
She **could** pull it off. She'll have to inflict 5000 HP of damage on the pit. That would open the gate far enough to let her through. The gate will immediately slam shut, so the horde can't follow her.

But Thargorax at full power is enough to completely shift the balance of power on Praxia. Essedria would become her domain, and she'd almost immediately lay waste to the continent. Then should could raise her dragon army and attempt to attack the Second Gods. War would ensue and bring Praxia to ruin for centuries.

It would also give her the Quinary and almost enough power to tear the Gate open.

She'll have to kill or disable the entire party and take their items, then somehow get the Seals. 

This will be very, very difficult and very, very dangerous for her. If she comes through the Gate, every warrior on the planet will come looking for her, and if House Umbra organized they could give her a real fight.

#### Force The Party To Cooperate
She could threaten the party, the party's friends, etc. with swift death and try to cow them into joining her. She'll show the party the Green Slaad, Porthmoor, Sango's village, Salmuir, all w/ dragons circling overhead. The party does what she says or she destroys everything.

Why not just use the dragons to kill the party? They're young dragons and nothing's a sure thing. Better to get them to switch sides.
