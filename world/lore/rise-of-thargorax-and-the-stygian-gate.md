### The Setup
The four Fouding Runes the party saw during the Amenopophis campaign refer to the four elements of the Founding:

Divine (divinity) - plateau of bone
Nature (naturum) - gloomspire
Arcane (arcanum) - porthmoor
Chaos (flux) - East Godelis 

On the map
!Pasted image 20230122145728.png
Each of the four runes represents a seal. The seals contain magical forces that lie along a critical line of magical energy across Praxia.

That line of energy points directly at the original location of The Stygian Gate. When Team Spear saved Pandora, the gate returned to that location.

Cracking each of the seals unlocks the gate. Which will allow Tharogax And Her Mission to complete a ritual opening the gate...

## The Gate
- Lies on the Plateau of Bone.
- Tharogax's servant, Agrona Sef, moves from seal to seal, opening them
- One of the missing members of Sango's order is attempting to do the same
