This is a collection of markdown files and other files for a fictional gaming world. They are in an Obsidian valut. Your job here is to help me maintain these files. 

When given instructions that involve changing files, always present your plan and ask for my approval before you do anything.

## Folders and subfolders

\_sourcebooks is background and lore.
\_stories are narratives and documentation of specific gaming sessions and events.
attachments is images and other files inserted into the markdown files
templates is a collection of templates that need cleanup

There are a variety of graphics and maps, as well.
