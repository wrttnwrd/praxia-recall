# The Plot
Jazelle, illegitimate daughter of Sulba, bitter about what magic did to her father and denied her, wants to end traditional magic. Her first target: House Umbra.

>> Jazelle is a CHR 18 INT 20 artificer (?) wizard. She has dark hair and pale skin, and her father's pale green eyes. She lives alone in her High Ward house.

The only weapon she knows that has sufficient power is the Lor Manádh, a gigantic engine of the Ancients. But the Lor Manádh lies dormant high in the Mournfang Rang. It was wrecked when it crashed as it traveled the planes.

To fix it, she must accumulate a series of devices and creatures. This adventure is about her quest.

>> Playing Jazelle: Unlike her father, she is not a seeker of power. She is on a mission and will stay behind the scenes as long as possible. She doesn't want to hurt anyone except House Umbra and will unwittingly commit atrocities in the process.

## Grim Portents

1. Robberies around Aventine, stealing items needed to restart Lor Manàdh
2. Rumors of weird happenings north of the Mournfang Range, near the orc lands
3. There's a sickness in Diaspora (Taelian)

## The Setting
Time: The 2th day of Deepwinter, just 5 FR. It's bitterly cold.

Place: Aventine

## Jazelle's Quest
She must:

- Release the Primal Automata. There are three she must gather to her. They are in the mines of the Wailing Cairn. They need several items to do this, including the Perfect Timepiece, Oil of Mekanikum, and a lot of Mithril. She can attempt to raise them w/o the Timepiece. If she does, the Automata will run wild. But they're required to start the Necropolis' engines, so she may take the risk.
- Start the Necropolis. That is what the Lor is. Unbeknownst to her, ancient evil controls the Mind Flayers, who in turn have manipulated her to restart the Necropolis. The characters must stop her or, if she succeeds, stop the Necropolis.

## The Characters' Roles
At the start, Jazelle attempts to manipulate the characters into helping her steal the plans for and build The Master Key. Once she has that and the Perfect Timepiece, she will attempt to steal the map, then head for the mountains and the first Automaton.

The players will probably figure out her designs after the key is built. Clues will accumulate along the way until it's hard to ignore the fact that she's behind most of the thefts. Or the players may research the Master Key. See "clues" below.

The adventure assumes players will attempt to stop Jazelle, but they may decide to aid her.

### Clues
Along the way, the players may happen upon clues to Jazelle's designs:

1. The History Of The Shards War contains a reference to Sulba's true nature and his adopted daughter. It doesn't name her
2. The rogues that attempt to steal the Oil from Artemis's shop will identify Jazelle. Since this is the second time someone's tried to steal the Oil, that might be enough
3. She'll want the Timepiece, too

## Part One: Introductions
Jazelle hires the party to steal the plans from a High Ward Mansion known as the Bournier Chateau, recently purchased by the Bournier family who comes from new money.

Jazelle invites the characters to meet her at her mansion. She is courteous and likeable in a pleasantly snobby way.

She needs the party to retrieve a scroll from the mansion. She is concerned the Bourniers do not understand what they have in their possession and wants it for herself. She can learn a lot from it, she says (honestly).

She previously hired a more conventional group of thieves who did not return. Witnesses reported hearing screaming and animal sounds from inside the walls of the estate.

Based on this, she decided she needs a team of more unique skills. House Belos gave her references, and here they are.

_Organized Crime: Jazelle's robbery is sanctioned by House Belos, who agreed to stand aside. They don't like the Bourniers._

Jazelle expresses guilt at the fate of the last team (she is sincere) and suggests the party gather more information by visiting the Green Slaad.

### The Green Slaad
Describe the Green Slaad to the party. Jazelle has suggested they talk to Blong Hrusto, the manager and bartender, about the sounds of beasts. She's hoping Blong can help them prepare.

She also suggests talking to an ex-servant, a gnome named Warben, about details of the estate. Warben is bitter after being fired for sleeping on the job. He's at the Slaad drinking away his sorrows.

Blong will tell them that from the calls he's been told about, the creatures on the grounds were some kind of reptile. He doesn't know more.

Warben can tell them the scroll is probably in a vault at the back of the observatory. He says he knows beasts patrol the grounds but isn't sure of their nature or number. He knows that Mercedes fancies herself a wizard and herbalist (she's actually pretty respectable).

While the players are there, Remmie stumbles in, looking singed and the worse for wear.

### The Robbery
Three thieves have attempted to rob Remmie's shop. If there's a rogue in the party, they know Remmie. If the party goes to help, they find three rogues attempting to carry out an unsantioned robbery.

In the process, though, the rogues overturned several potions, affecting a few little beasts in the place. When the party enters, they find one of the would-be criminals webbed in a corner, being menaced by a giant house spider. Another rogue is struggling with a chimpanzee that's barking loudly. The third is desperately trying to get out the rear window.

If the party captures any of them, they learn that the three were hired to steal four vials, all pricey specialties:

- Oil of mekanikum (what Jazelle needs)
- Potion of vision
- Starseer's Scent
- Augurer's Oil

Total, that's more than 20k gp.

The thieves will be turned over to House Belos for punishment. The various creatures will return to normal form/size after an hour.

### The Bournier Chateau

Husband: Drake Bournier
Wife: Mercedes Bournier
Son: Drake Jr.

- The grounds: 2 guards + 2 allosaurus. See [the encounter](https://www.dndbeyond.com/encounters/88074def-0f6e-48ae-827e-8f30c98ab56a). There are four more guards on the premises. They will arrive at any commotion after a few rounds.

#### The observatory
A beautiful dome of glass, the observatory contains a telescope, a mechanical model of the cosmos, and various books and statuary.

It's quite dusty - no one comes up here very often.

The scroll is in the vault, which is made from garishly carved stone. It is locked and has a keyhole in the center of an eyeball. It requires a DC 25 Thieves Tools check--only proficient characters can try. Or, they can attempt to suss out the puzzle: On the front of the vault, around the keyhole, is drawn a single eye. The eye looks across the room to a telescope that's aimed upwards. There's a cover on the end of the telescope that points at the eye. Uncover that and a beam of starlight hits the vault, opening the door.

The vault has a nasty surprise: The scroll and all other vault contents are in a small gelatinous cube. Normal perception to see the cube. The cube attacks as soon as the party attempts to retrieve an item.

[https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/gelatinous-cube](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/gelatinous-cube) but with 30hp, does 5hp acid damage, can hold one medium sized creature.

Observatory Loot:
- Gems worth 50gp
- Hideous small statuary
- A _scroll of invisibility_
- One book: The Hero's Chronicles, Part 6: Belle & Borgia In The Underdark (mint condition, in envelope)
- The History Of The Shards War
- Also in the cube: A small, shiny amulet on a chain. It weakly radiates magic. This is a Perfect Timepiece: It will always keep the time, indoors or out, no matter what dimension or plane you're on. The players are too low-level to figure it out, but this _is also an item that can awaken a Primal Automaton_. If this is stolen the Bourniers **will** be furious, and will start hunting for it. They'll bring in a low-level Wizard with Locate Object and start combing the city. This is how the Bourniers may find the party. They have a 1 in 10 chance of picking up the party's scent. Jazelle figures out what it is, too, and may send the party on a mission outside the city if she fears the Bourniers are getting too close. This is another bit of Old One manipulation: They made sure the Timepiece was near the scroll, thereby delivering two components to Jazelle, instead of one.

#### The Grounds
There's nothing remarkable about the grounds except the way they're absolutely littered with kitsch.

#### The Dinosaur Pen
Just north of the main building is a heavily barred gate leading to a shallow underground paddock. This is where the allosaurs are kept. In the back of the pen are two more eggs.

The pen is cramped: Maybe 50 feet on a side. It runs under the house with a trap door and a ladder in the kitchen, from where they feed the beasts and retrieve eggs.

#### The Escape
City Guard will come in the front gate within a few minutes. If either Allosaurus is still loose, the Guard will be completely flummoxed, and the players might slip away. If not, the players can still try to get away, or go out the back.

### Through The Tunnels
Travel through the tunnels to emerge either near the Green Slaad (if they go East) or under the destroyed Temple of Amoth (if they go North). Remember the Temple of Shar now occupies part of the grounds of the old temple.

### Captured!
The Guard might capture the characters and take them to a small holdiiing cell in the High Ward guard station. A harried guard named Gwenlei books them in and leaves them to their devices.

Gwenlei confiscates their belongings.

If the players don't do anything, Jazelle shows up the next day and bails them out.

### Back To The Green Slaad
If the players are being pursued, Blong either hides them in the Belos tunnels under the Slaad or sends them to the Cauldron. At the Cauldren, Panovar Flynt can hide them in the contraband lockers under the fighter locker rooms.

### Other Locations
The players might flee up towards the Temple District. There's also a tunnel in the alley behind the estate. The tunnel leads into the Catacombs, where they might get lost and end up under the ruins of the Temple of Amoth, or find their way back to Hull Ward (under the Green Slaad), or unexpectedly pop up in the Zuppek Bazaar.

Have them make survival checks to go the right way.

Or, they might flee down the hill to Hull Ward, in which case they'll end up in Heroes' Square.

### Meeting Jazelle
Jazelle lets the party know she'll meet with them at the Green Slaad the next evening, leaving the characters to do what they wish during that day.

If Jazelle sees the Timepiece, she offers them 300gp for it. She says she's fascinated by such a unique piece of work, but in truth she's desperate to get her hands on the item.

### The Scroll
If the players look at the scroll, it's an incomprehensible diagram of gears, axles and mekanikal formulae. If they show it to Artemis Hasp, he's fascinated. He memorizes the scroll and asks them to stop by his shop in the morning.

### More
If the players need more to do, they can visit The Mekanikum and talk to Hasp, who explains the scroll is a plan for something called The Master Key. He doesn't know what the Key does.

End of Part One.

## Part Two: Too Hot To Handle
Jazelle offers the players employment. They can accept, or not. If they do not, House Belos approaches them. If the party doesn't affiliate themselves w/ anyone, that's fine, but they may have to leave the city fairly soon. The Bourniers may be after the Timepiece, or people who see them as competitors come knocking.

If the players take employment w/ Belos, skip to Trouble At The Hull Ward Bakery.

### Jazelle's Next Job
Jazelle needs the party to find and take a message to an elf named Menandré. She is an ex-lover of Jazelle's. Jazelle hasn't heard from her in quite a while. She doesn't usually hire people to do this kind of thing, she says, but she's very worried, and there have been mutterings of bad things happening in Taelian (where Menandré lives).

### Protecting The Mekanikum
Artemis is concerned someone's going to burglarize his shop. He's the only other person who has the Oil Of Mekanikus.

Jazelle has hired these rogues and then asked the party to defend the shop as a distraction.

### Lumpkin's Meats
The Cult of Eight is meeting in a warehouse next door to Lumpkin's Meats. That has spawned cranium rats (weak ones, since there aren't any real Illithid in the group) which are spoiling and eating the meat at Lumpkins. Lumpkin hires the party to help.

He'll pay them 25gp. More important, he'll get them a relationship within Bluestrike.

(the players have helped out Lumpkin)

#### The Warehouse
In the warehouse, the players will find evidence of a meeting: Boxes pushed together, chairs, and an odd looking symbol drawn on the floor (see encounters deck). This is an emblem of the Cult Of Eight.

In the rats nest is 50gp and a small scrap of chewed-up paper with the Cult Of Eight symbol on it.

### Fidelia's Connection
Fidelia's half-brother, Siliib Azari, is missing. He's young and impulsive, and Fidelia's worried about him. They are very close.

Fidelia remembers the party and knows they're probably, er, discrete, as well as capable. So she talked to Grimshaw, who steered her to them (since they showed up on the Wave Serpent anyway).

Siliib has joined the Cult Of Eight. He had **no** idea what they were about, but liked being part of the group, and Mevvic was very flattering.

Fidelia tells the party Siliib is a student at the University of Aventine.

If they go there, they'll probably talk to Wendell, Siliib's roommate. Wendell knows Siliib has spent a lot of time w/ Professor Mevvic, a Professor of Commerce. Mevvic took Siliib under his wing supposedly due to his aptitude with logistics.

If questioned, Mevvic says he hasn't seen Siliib in some time. But Wendell will say he saw Siliib w/ Mevvic the last time he saw Siliib.

Mevvic has taken Siliib to a small safehouse, purportedly to meditate while he prepares for formal induction into the Cult.

Mevvic actually recruited Siliib for his connections and the prestige.

The safehouse is just outside the small Southern Gate of the city.

Mevvic goes to the safehouse every night. He's terrible at hiding himself and his tracks.

_Might be legit. No need to worry about it now._

### Trouble At The Hull Ward Bakery
Someone's been raiding the bakery at night and vandalizing the place.
