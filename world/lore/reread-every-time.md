# Goals
## Rikterr
Childhood best friends: Ger'u'do Durtbourne, a half-orc, and Schala Zeal, a half-elf

When he was younger, Rikterr lived with the only friends he had: a half-orc named Ger’u’do Durtbourne, who he’s mentioned to RBG, and Schala Zeal, a half-elf. All three grew up destitute and started working together to survive [Salmuir](/world/places/salmuir.md). Both Rikterr and Ger’u’do fell in love with Schala. One night Rikterr got drunk and let his jealousy rage. He beat Ger’u’do nearly to death.

The next day he left their small home to find the nearest place to kill himself. He found a steep cliff, and right before he jumped, a paladin named Alphonse Horn stopped him. In exchange for saving his life, Horn made Rikterr pledge it in service to others.

Horn led Rikterr on the path to become a paladin.

Rikterr didn’t take a god before the Raven Queen because he never felt worthy due to his rage and what he did to Ger’u’do. Rikterr felt no better than the people who bullied him for being poor. For being an orphan. His training didn’t get rid of his rage: it allowed him to use it to help others.
