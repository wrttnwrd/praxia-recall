The Haunts are under the effects of The Blight Of Amenopophis, or The Blight. These are regional effects created by Amenopophis's long residence here.

### Lost Memory
The Haunts slowly leech away the memories and agency of sentient creatures. Every day, any creature with an INT over 8 must make a DC12 WIS save. On a failure, they forget one thing. DM's choice. Examples include:

- A party member's name
- Handedness: They can't remember if they're right- or left-handed
- They forget their own name
- They lose an item. It's somewhere on their person or on another party member, but they can't remember where. Roll 1-20 and choose from their inventory.

They will also roll their next WIS or CHR save with disadvantage.

Fey and undead are unaffected.

Most sentient creatures the party encounters feel no need to leave the Haunts. If asked, they seem downcast but just can't get the energy to do anything about it.

### Tracking
The ground everywhere is spongy. Any efforts to track are -4. In the swamp, tracking is at disadvantage.

### Green Mists

^20373b

At night, players will often see swirling green mist. It appears to take the form of living creatures.

It isn't harmful, and no one knows why it does this.

### Grafting Grasses & Vines

^078c8e

In the swamps, living creatures of all types are grafting bits of other living creatures to themselves.

Grasses and vines are particularly nasty: If players rest in the wrong place, grass and/or vines slowly twine themselves around them, removing bits of uncovered flesh. They excrete an anesthetic at the same time, so players don't notice unless they see it happening to another character, or the process goes on for more than 5 minutes.

The character suffers 5 points of damage. A small bit of their skin appears on the vine or grass. Players can pull themselves free with a sickening popping sensation.

After a character has been "bitten" once, a PER DC 15 spots the grasses/vines.

### Quicksand

^0e6786

The swamp is dotted with patches of quicksand. An unwary traveler will become stuck. Players need a DC 18 STR check to get out. If they fail three rolls, they begin to sink.
