# Tasha Quann
*House*: Belos

Half-elf rogue, lawful neutral

In charge of the Hull Ward territory.

Tasha is a brilliant businesswoman, a ruthless political player, and a sharp manager. She has rapidly risen through the ranks of House Belos and wants leadership of the house itself.

Appearance: A small, unremarkable pale-skinned half-elf woman with dark hair. She wears no makeup but is immaculately groomed. Wears the red cloak of Belos, leather armor, a rapier and a long dagger. Hidden in her boot is another dagger and a small vial of paralysis poison.

# Mevvik
*House*: Belos
Changeling rogue, lawful w/ evil tendencies

Tasha's right-hand and bodyguard. He is fiercely loyal to Tasha. Only has two settings: Not a threat or kill.

Appearance: It's hard to get a clear look at Mevvik, really, and he seems to materialize out of nowhere.

# Ranvar Flynt
*House*: Belos
Human rogue, chaotic neutral

A small-time enforcer working in Hull Ward. He'd like to someday run the Ward but isn't up to the task. Ranvar is jealous of anyone of higher station and assumes they got there because of unfair advantages or cheating.

Appearance: Slighly overweight, the broken veins and sallow complexion of an alcoholic. His fingernails and hands are filthy, and his breath reeks of rotting teeth. Ranvar is twitchy, constantly fiddling with a pair of daggers he wears in his belt. He wears a tattered cloak that may have once been red.

# Amity Longbane (KIA)
*House*: None
Werewolf barbarian.

Amity is the vicious leader of the Longbane bandits. At her direction, they're raiding up and down the southern Ibril, stealing from villages and farms.

She sees herself as someday becoming leader of a larger band capable of storming entire villages.

Amity is blinded by greed and desire for mayhem. If pushed, she answers all challenges with violence, and has assumed leadership of the bandits through fear and intimidation.

Appearance: A stocky woman with long fingernails and a toothy grin.
