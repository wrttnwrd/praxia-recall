# **Corsair Raider Unit — CR 8 (Ultra-Minimal)**

_Large humanoid war unit (CE)_  
**AC** 17 • **HP** 150 • **Speed** 30 ft.  
**STR** 16 • **DEX** 14 • **CON** 17 • **WIS** 14  
**Saves** Dex +5, Wis +5 • **PB** +4

### **Traits**

- **War Band:** 15×15 ft space; half dmg when below half HP.
    
- **Shield Screen:** Resist nonmagical B/P/S above half HP.
    
- **Volley:** Ranged attacks ignore half cover.
    
- **Healers (3/day):** Bonus action heal unit 12 HP.
    
- **War Caster:** Adv. on concentration.
    

### **Actions**

- **Multiattack:** Oil-Arrow Volley + Blade, or Spell + Blade.
    
- **Oil-Arrow Volley:** +6 to hit, 120 ft. Single: 18 (4d8)+7 fire. Area (10-ft rad): DC 15 Dex, same dmg (half on save).
    
- **Blade Assault:** +7 to hit, 5 ft., 16 (3d6+3) slashing.
    
- **Spells (DC 13, +5):**  
    • _Fire Bolt_ (at-will) 10 fire  
    • _Magic Missile_ (2/day) 10 force  
    • _Scorching Ray_ (1/day) three rays, 7 fire each  
    • _Burning Hands_ (1/day) 15-ft cone, 10 fire (Dex half)
    

### **Reaction**

- **Close Ranks:** Impose disadvantage on an attack (above half HP).
