[The Obsidian Wing](/world/groups/the-obsidian-wing.md) seeks to gain a hold over [the Haddad Combine](/world/groups/haddad-combine.md) and therefore a foothold in [Salmuir](/world/places/salmuir.md). By doing so, they can tap Salmuir's wealth and use it to gain greater control over their own city.

First, they tried a campaign of disinformation in hopes of dislodging the Combine, but that didn't work. Now, they're turning to intimidation and assassination.

The Wing uses two portals: One in [the Blighted Haunts](/world/places/the-blighted-haunts.md) just outside Salmuir, and another in the cellar of a ruined Temple Of Shar in The Mists.

The mysterious death of one of the Haddad has led the Combine to seek help.

## Kozani's plan
[Kozani Noctis](/world/people/kozani-noctis.md) understands that the portal to this domain opened because of a catastrophe (The Fall). She's half-right. What really happened was the catastrophe **created** the dark domain.

So, she's hoping to cause a smaller catastrophe in [Salmuir](/world/places/salmuir.md) by flooding the city with tainted Elfcap and Orguar. Any Elfcap or Orugar created in the Shadowfell and taken on the Prime Material can cause psychosis, violence, and death. The ensuing chaos might be enough (according to Noctis) to open a stable portal and let her take control of Salmuir.

For info on Elfcap and Orugar, see [Salmuir](/world/places/salmuir.md).

She's wrong - it won't work. But that won't stop her from pushing her tainted drugs into [Salmuir](/world/places/salmuir.md).

## Tainted Drugs
Anyone consuming Shadowfell-brewed Elfcap or Orugar immediately suffers the following effects:

15WIS. On a fail, roll a d6
1. Immediately attack the nearest creature or person, using all spells and abilities to attack
2. Run to the nearest flammable object and try to set fire to it
3. Carry out a single attack against the nearest creature or person, then fall to the ground unconscious
4. Fall unconscious
5. Laugh hysterically and run in random directions
6. Try to persuade those around you to try the tainted orugar/elfcap. Give your best argument



## What Saroya Wants
[Saroya Demiarma](/world/people/saroya-demiarma.md) knows the assassination, disinformation, and other stuff aren't a coincidence. She feels there's a larger organization at work, trying to destabilize [Salmuir](/world/places/salmuir.md).

So, she has enlisted the aid of Notorious RBG to discover what organization is behind this and put a stop to them.

Saroya fears for her city. Whoever is doing this has resources and connections. Salmuir hasn't had a criminal underworld since the Fall because [the Haddad Combine](/world/groups/haddad-combine.md) **was** the criminal underworld. With them gone, there shouldn't be any concerted attacks on the city's leadership.

## What's Happened So far
[The Obsidian Wing](/world/groups/the-obsidian-wing.md) started w/ subtler stuff but failed. They then dispatched an assassin to kill a member of [The Haddad Combine](/world/groups/haddad-combine.md). They succeeded.

The murder has been hushed up in [Salmuir](/world/places/salmuir.md) so as to avoid a scramble. There's no easy way to replace this Haddad, and in fact it's hard to figure out exactly how they were killed. There are no apparent wounds. The Haddad was reduced to a dessicated corpse in complete silence during the night.

The Haddad was, in fact, killed and drained by a Nosferatu vampire. The vampire licked the wound after feeding, hiding the bite.

The Haddad will rise again 4 hours after RBG arrives in [Salmuir](/world/places/salmuir.md), preferably when they're inspecting the body.

## The Primary Portal
The primary portal is in cellar of the Temple Of Shar, which has been constructed in the rubble of the Mists.

The portal emerges into a Temple Of Shar in the Shadowfell, in [Shadowmuir](/world/places/shadowmuir.md). The temple is located in Darktome Cross:!Pasted image 20250119142350.png

The portal only opens at night, and only at a new moon. The next New Moon is due 28 Summertide.
