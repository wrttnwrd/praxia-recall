# Silgorin Below — 20 Random Encounters (CR 10–15)

A d20 table of encounters tuned for a party of 4 PCs at tier 3. Mix is roughly half combat, half social / exploration / mystery — Silgorin Below is a battleground, but it is also a city where mortals are being bartered for, lied to, and slowly remade by both sides of the Blood War.

Combat CRs assume the listed creatures; substitute as you like for your party size. Social encounters list a **Difficulty** rather than CR.

---

## 1. The Stolen Winch  *(Combat — CR ~13)*
Demonic forces have overrun the lemure crew of Chain II. A **Glabrezu** (CR 9) directs **two Vrocks** (CR 6 ea.) as they tear into the swarm and lever the great cog backward. A surviving **Bearded Devil sergeant** is barricaded behind the gear housing, screaming for relief. Stop the demons, choose whether to save the sergeant, and the chain holds. Walk away and the city lists three feet toward the Styx within the hour.

## 2. The Arcanaloth's Offer  *(Social — Difficulty: Hard)*
**Veshara, Quill of the Brazen Compact** (Arcanaloth, CR 12) descends from Silgorin Above in elven funeral silks. She offers the party a sealed Soul Coin in exchange for delivering an unread contract to Enthraxis. The contract is, of course, an extradition order. If the party reads it she'll know — but she'll also bargain. Veshara never lies in the literal sense; everything she says is technically true and morally rancid.

## 3. The Eternal Flame  *(Exploration / Mystery — Difficulty: Medium)*
Beneath the cracked western half of the old Temple of Amoth, in a chamber filled with broken statuary, a **single tongue of silver-white flame** still flickers. Beside it kneels **Brother Cossic**, an Amothi acolyte who has been kept alive — only barely — by the flame's last breath. He has heard what Veyrin could not: Amoth's final word. He will share it only with someone who swears, on something dear, to carry it back to the surface.

## 4. Chain Inspection Patrol  *(Combat — CR ~13)*
A **Horned Devil** (CR 11) leading **four Bearded Devils** (CR 4 ea.) on a chain inspection rounds a ruined corner and sees the party. They are jumpy. They will assume the party are demon infiltrators in disguise unless a PC can produce an authorization sigil (only obtainable from previous encounters) or pass a Charisma (Deception/Persuasion) DC 18.

## 5. The Refugee Child  *(Social / Moral — Difficulty: Variable)*
A small child clutching a soot-stained pendant of Amoth begs to be escorted to the City Armory. Two strange details: her shadow lags half a second behind her, and passing devils give her **a wide and deliberate berth.** She is either (DM's choice / roll d6):
1–3: a genuinely pure mortal soul devils cannot touch
4–5: a **Quasit** in disguise (CR 1) baiting the party into an ambush by a **Chain Devil** and **Erinyes**
6: the reborn soul of a slain Amothi priest, no memory of it, but a powerful long-term ally if delivered safely

## 6. The Time-Slip  *(Exploration / Mystery — Difficulty: Hard)*
Rounding a corner of the third ring, the party sees **themselves** walking the other way, ten minutes in the past. The earlier party does not see them. Interaction risks paradox (PC must make a DC 17 Wis save or take 4d10 psychic damage and lose 10 minutes of memory). Observation grants advantage on the next Insight check against an NPC they recently met. Leaving a message in chalk on a wall *does* work — but the message has always been there.

## 7. The Soul Auction  *(Social — Difficulty: Medium)*
In a broken plaza on the eastern ring, **Skrizzix the Honest** (an Imp with a bone gavel) is auctioning soul coins of dead Silgorin citizens. Bidders: a Bone Devil in regalia, a hooded Arcanaloth, a Cambion swordmaiden, and one cloaked figure who never speaks. The party can interfere, bid (each coin costs 500–5000 gp or a memory), or hide and follow the silent bidder home.

## 8. The Gulthias Whispers  *(Exploration / Mystery — Difficulty: Medium, with cost)*
Resting anywhere within 300 ft of the Commons exposes the sleeper to the **Gulthias Blight's** dreams. It offers a single true vision — one accurate answer to one specific question about the city, Amoth, or the chains — in exchange for **a drop of blood watered into its roots**. Each such bargain marks the sleeper (a black vein, a craving, a tendency to grin at suffering). After three marks, save DC 16 Wisdom on each long rest or wake up at the tree's foot, hands dirty.

## 9. The Severed General  *(Combat / Trap — CR ~14)*
The party finds the still-shrieking severed head of a **Nalfeshnee** lashed atop a pile of devil corpses. It curses, taunts, and begs. It is bait. As the party approaches within 60 ft, a **Marilith** (CR 16) and **two Mezzoloths** (CR 5 ea.) emerge from beneath a collapsed market awning. The Nalfeshnee head will switch sides depending on who looks like winning.

## 10. The Demon Diplomat  *(Social — Difficulty: Hard)*
A **Glabrezu** named **Vraxen** stumbles into the party's path, hands raised in mortal pantomime of surrender. He claims sanctuary and offers intel: the location of the next demon assault, the name of a corrupt devil officer, the route through the Gurges vapor that the swarms use. Every piece of intel he gives is half-true. He is buying time so a strike team can locate his actual target. The party has roughly an hour to figure out what he really wants.

## 11. The Rogue Lemure  *(Social / Exploration — Difficulty: Easy)*
A single **lemure** has wandered away from Chain V. It is clutching a small bronze ring engraved with a Silgorin family crest. Touching it produces a flash of who it once was — a baker, a young man named Telim, murdered in his shop the night of the Fall. If the party can speak its true name within five minutes (Investigation DC 15, or interaction with the right NPC elsewhere), the lemure dissolves into a wisp of warm light and ascends. Their reputation in the Armory rises sharply.

## 12. The Eternal Skirmish  *(Combat — CR ~12)*
Two opposing units have been fighting in this plaza for what looks like days. **Three Bearded Devils** and **two Chasme** (CR 6 ea.) rise, die, rise, die again. None of them notice the party. A localized time-eddy from the Gurges is keeping them in a loop. Breaking the loop (dispel magic at 5th+, a Religion DC 18 ritual, or destroying the carved focus stone in the fountain's base) frees the souls — but all combatants will turn on the party for one final round.

## 13. The Astral Anchorite  *(Social / Mystery — Difficulty: Medium)*
A blind elven monk, **Iremu of the Quiet Hand**, sits cross-legged on an exposed rooftop. Battles pass within feet of her and never touch. Her body has been here since the Fall, sustained by her own mind, which is elsewhere. If a PC sits with her in silence for ten minutes and succeeds on a DC 15 Wisdom save, they share a vision of the *moment Amoth died*. The vision grants one-time advantage on any check involving the god, the temple, or the Hierophant. Failure: 1d6 psychic damage and a haunting dream for the next week.

## 14. The Failing Chain  *(Combat + Skill Challenge — CR ~13)*
Demonic forces have nearly succeeded — **Chain IV** groans, and three of its great rings show stress fractures. The party has roughly six rounds before catastrophic failure. They must simultaneously:
- Hold off **two Hezrou** (CR 8 ea.) and **a swarm of Chasme** climbing the chain
- Make three successful checks (Athletics DC 16, Smith's Tools DC 17, or Religion DC 18) to brace the anchor
- Signal the Bearded Devil garrison on the Avernus side (Perception, Persuasion, or a creative spell)
Failure tips the city; success earns a marker of debt from Zariel herself.

## 15. Don't Look Down  *(Hazard — DC 15 Dex)*
A large section of street falls away beneath the party, leaving a gaping hole through which you can see the **River Styx**, churning hundreds of feet below. Each PC in the affected area must make a **DC 15 Dexterity save** or fall hundreds of feet into the river. (DM: a Styx plunge should be more than fall damage — exposure to the river risks short-term memory loss; a failed Charisma save on contact wipes the PC's last 1d4 hours, and full submersion threatens permanent loss until restoration.) Survivors must improvise a way back up to a city already pulling slowly toward the Styx.

## 16. The Pact Paper  *(Exploration / Hook — Difficulty: Medium)*
A signed contract tumbles past on the wind, somehow uncharred. The script is fine infernal. It bears the signature of **someone close to a PC** — a parent, mentor, lost lover, dead friend. The contract is for that person's soul, signed roughly thirty years ago. Reading it (Arcana DC 16) reveals it is voidable, but only by completing its named clause: **kill the contracted devil**, who is currently somewhere in Silgorin Below. The contract's holder is named in fiendish script on the reverse.

## 17. Krystos's Wager  *(Social, possible Combat — CR 14 if it turns)*
An **Ice Devil** (CR 14) named **Krystos** blocks a vital street, leaning on a halberd of black ice. He does not want to fight, he says. He proposes three riddles. Win all three: pass freely, plus a single useful piece of intel ("The Ultroloth visits the Temple every third dusk"). Lose any: he stalks the party for 24 hours, attacking at the worst tactical moment. Refuse to play: combat now.

## 18. The Silent Choir  *(Exploration / Mystery — Difficulty: Hard)*
In what was the Choir Hall of Amoth, **fifty Amothi clerics stand frozen mid-song**, perfectly preserved. None breathe. Their eyes track the party. Touch one and the touching PC hears, for the space of a heartbeat, **the last note Amoth heard before he died**. They gain inspiration and a permanent psychic scar (DC 16 Wis save; on failure, disadvantage on saves vs. madness until the next sunrise the PC sees with their own eyes — which, in Silgorin Below, may be a while). Up to four PCs can touch a chorister. The fifth and onward trigger something far worse.

## 19. The Defectors  *(Combat — CR ~11, or Social if PCs intervene)*
A **Bone Devil** (CR 9) and **four Bearded Devils** are trying to flee Silgorin Below via Chain VI. They are not heroes, they are afraid. A loyal patrol — **a Horned Devil** (CR 11) and **two Bearded Devils** — arrives to execute them. The party can pick a side, broker a deal (DC 19 Persuasion or a creative bribe), or hide and let it play out. Saved defectors become a recurring favor in Avernus; loyalists' favor moves the party closer to Enthraxis's confidences.

## 20. The Last Confessor  *(Social / Roleplay — Difficulty: Easy in mechanics, hard in heart)*
In a curtained alcove of the Armory infirmary, **Old Cossen Marrow** — the last living survivor who personally knew Veyrin Soldain — is dying of a wound that should already have killed him. He has been holding on for someone to confess to. He will speak only to a person who can prove they do not worship Asmodeus (a sliver of holy ash, an honest oath, refusal of an obvious deal in front of him). What he says is up to the DM: it could be Veyrin's true motivation, the name of the demon Asmodeus killed Amoth for, or the hiding place of the eternal flame in encounter #3. He dies mid-sentence; the last syllable is for the listener to fill in.

### Tone Notes
- Both sides — devil and demon — should always be present somewhere on the horizon. Silgorin Below is a *war*, not a haunt.
- **Mortals matter.** Every devil and demon is calculating mortal soul-value at all times. Encounters should make the party feel watched, weighed, and offered to.
- **Time is wrong.** When in doubt, distort it — a conversation lasts longer than it should, a wound from yesterday isn't there today, a building is one street over from where it was.
- Encounters #2, #16, and #20 all leak hooks back to Silgorin Above and to The Fall — pull on whichever thread is hottest for your table.
