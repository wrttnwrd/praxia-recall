Alpip Copperpipe: Gnome inventor and tinkerer, next door to the blacksmith’s

Astrid - 1st level sorcerer, Ulric’s wife

Asmund Grundhammer: Owner of Wayhouse Byfrost; Dwarf fighter

Aethelred: Head of the town militia. [Use the Veteran from the back of the MM](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/veteran)

Brother Valon: Cleric in charge of the Temple of Heimdall. 2nd-level tempest cleric. He enjoys kicking ass. But Vanya has to pull him out of the fire all the time.

Dagny Skargard (daughter of Sven)

Erik: Ranger 1, leader of the Rangers of Meilikki. Has a crush on Mildrith. Aethelred and Wilma think he's cool. They're frequently seen having an ale at the Byfrost. Valon and Vanya despise him. He doesn't care. If push came to shove, they'd work together.

Godtfred Frey - stables owner. Lawful evil. Actually a deep scion enslaved to Sycorax. Careful about what he does. Covers his tracks and benefits from a stable town.

Mildrith: Elf female commoner/1st level monk/cleric stuck in town since The Fall. Training w/ Valon as a cleric. Works at the Byfrost

Ghorza Rockfang: New shaman leader of the orc clans around Dirion. Has united the clans, and presents a major danger to the region

Ragnar Ansson (the Young): Jarl - good guy. Fighter/Bard 3/1

Skar: Leader of the were creatures

Skorn: Goliath fighter

Sven Skargard: Town wizard level 1

Sycorax: Beholder under the city

Keldar the Mad: Insane Duergar artificer who's building the Apparatus

Dagny - warlock of the fiend. Bald. Tatooed.

Ulfgrim Ansson: Bandit leader, Ragnar’s cousin

Ulric the Smith - 1st level fighter, Astrid’s husband

Vanya: 1st level half-elf monk, protector of the Temple of Heimdall

Wilma: 2nd in command of the militia. Loyal to Ragnar. Doesn't like Aethelred but follows him because he's Ragnar's choice
