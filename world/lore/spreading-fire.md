# **Spreading Fire (Hazard, CR —)**

_Large elemental hazard (fire)_  
**AC** 10 • **HP** 40 (immune to nonmagical physical dmg)  
**Speed** — (see Spread)

**STR** — • **DEX** 14 (+2) • **CON** 12 (+1) • **WIS** 10  
**Saves** Dex +4  
**Damage Immunities** fire, poison; nonmagical bludgeoning/piercing/slashing  
**Condition Immunities** prone, stunned, grappled, restrained, poisoned  
**Senses** blindsight 10 ft.  
**PB** —

---

### **Traits**

**Living Flame.** The fire fills a 10×10 ft space. It is destroyed at 0 HP. The fire cannot be healed.

**Vulnerable to Water.** Water-based effects deal double damage and can forcibly extinguish the fire via a saving throw.

**Water Save (Extinguish).** Whenever the fire takes water damage (such as _Water Discharge_), it must make a **DC 14 Dexterity saving throw**.  
• **Fail:** The fire is extinguished immediately.  
• **Success:** It remains burning and takes damage normally.

---

### **Actions (Environmental)**

**Burn.** Any creature that enters or starts its turn in the fire takes **10 (3d6)** fire damage.

---

### **Spread (End of Round).**

If the fire is above half HP and has access to flammable material, it spreads:

- Choose a random adjacent 5-ft square containing flammable terrain.
    
- That square ignites, increasing the fire’s occupied area by 5 ft.
    
- If the fire exceeds a 20×20 ft (four-square) area, it splits into two independent fires with an equal share of the HP.
    

If the fire is **at or below half HP**, it **does not spread**.

---

### **Water Damage Guidelines (for your firefighting squad)**

- _Typical bucket / splash:_ 5 (1d10) water dmg
    
- _Water Discharge (single-target hit):_ 14 (4d6) doubled to **28**
    
- _Area Spray:_ Average damage before doubling works fine (double the final total)
    

Each instance forces a single Water Save.

---

### **Notes**

This fire is intentionally statted as a “creature-like hazard” to give players tactical decisions: containment, targeted water bursts, and quick response all matter.
