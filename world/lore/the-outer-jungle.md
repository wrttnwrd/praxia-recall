# The Outer Jungle
Entering the outer band of the Haunts, adventurers encounter increasingly twisted vegetation.

The normal smell of rotting vegetation becomes increasingly like rotting flesh. Trees drip with a black, ichor-like substance.

The jungle becomes quieter and quieter. The air is hotter, thicker, and more oppressive.

Players start to see things like:

1. Bloated white crawfish in stagnant pools of water
2. Howling mushroom

There are also unique Encounters to be had.


## Areas

### 1. The Grafted Grove
A small ring of trees that seem healthier than the surrounding jungle. In the center is a pool of what appears to be a small spring. The air is cooler. it's a great place to make camp.

If the players make a DC24 PER, they'll notice that some of the trees have humanoid faces, barely visible in their trunks:

- The face of a sleeping goblin
- The visage of a human woman, staring with a small smile on her face
- An orc, also sleeping

If a PC gets within 5 feet of a tree, the face contorts in a shrieking scream. All players have to make a DC 15 CHAR save or be stunned for one round.

5-6 assassin vines then attack. This shouldn't be a difficult fight, but the noise may attract other creatures. Roll against the random encounters table.

### 2. Kenku Swamp Fishers
Towards the inner-most band of the outer jungle, the players may see Kenku on strange stilts. They're fishing dangerous, boggy areas for the oversized, bloated crawfish that live in the waters. They taste disgusting, but the Kenku have established small, run-down villages and rely on the big bugs.

The Kenku will not bring the players to their burrows and won't engage them in conversation, instead staring at the players from their stilts and, if the players speak to them, echo their questions mockingly.

### 3. The Gully
At the edge of the jungle, the land has dropped 10 feet, forming a gully between the jungle and swamp.

The players have to fight their way through the gully. A DC 15 SURV check will help them find a better route, which translates to less slogging.

Waiting in the gully are two beholder zombies. Note that these zombies are +100hp, and all saves are DC17, not 14. Powers are delivered by an eerie howling that comes from their gaping, rotting mouths.

### 4. The Cursed Warriors
Two cursed warriors - sword and  hammerspawn, are wandering the area. They detect the players via Tremorsense and stalk them, attacking if it seems like they might win.

### 5. Ruined Village
In the last 50 years, a small village was engulfed by the Haunts. It's deserted now, but there are buildings half-pulled down by vines, a fallen barn, and a well. The well is infested by a swarm of giant constrictor snakes. Very little danger to the party, but a nasty shock. If the players look, they'll see various bones scattered around the well.

When the village was first engulfed, the snakes took up residence, stealthily killing several hapless villagers and driving the rest out.

Also in the village: Several Yuan-ti are scratching out a meagre living. They're not doing well, and they leave the party alone. They're under the effect of [The Blight](/world/lore/the-blight.md), have lost their memories, and have no desire to leave.

### Slurt
The players encounter a giant boa constrictor with one leg, random eyes, and a single arm. It is currently devouring a goblin boss (https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/17155-goblin-boss) 

The goblin's name is Slurt. He's been trapped in the Haunts for weeks and has forgotten the way out, how he got there, or what the hell he's doing there in the first place.

(Slurt was one of Rake's retainers. He slipped away one night but went the wrong way.)

He's survived because of a grafted, replacement foot from a halfling (easily visible) that has, for some reason, given him the Lucky Feat. 

Slurt is very, very greatful at being rescued. He (?) immediately offers his services as a guide. Until he finds out the party is going deeper into the haunts, rather than out. At that point, he's terrified. But he knows his chances of survival are far higher if he stays with the party. 

He can't do much, but he'll help the party find their way around stuff. Give them +2 on rolls to avoid dangers, track, and do other non-combat activities. 

He is a noncombatant and flees and hides if there's any danger.

### The Tree
Remember that the tree is the lair of an adult hemophage dracolich (https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/2135703-young-hemophage-dracolich). That means that:

1. Within 6 miles, movement is halved, as underbrush is wiry and oozes blood
2. A thick, red-tinged mist lies over the ground. All vision is reduced to 30 feet
3. There is a 2/20 chance the players disturb swarm of stirges (https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/17023-stirge) that will attack. They're mostly harmless, but nasty

*You see a giant oak that stretches into the mists (DM's note: the oak is 100' high). Its limbs are twisted and gnarled, bark is a sickly grey-green. Entangled in its roots is what appears to be a stone pyramid, 15 feet high. On the pyramid in ancient Shamharan script (DC 18 Religion to read) that reads "Behold the crypt of Amenophis. Here he sleeps, ready to awaken a new world."*

As the players approach, a dracolich lazily uncurls from the roots of the tree. The lich is Sissun, guardian of the city. 

*Enter this place by besting me in battle, or by answering three riddles:*

*I am a room you cannot enter. What am I?* (Mushroom)
*I have towns without people. Forests without trees. Lakes without water. What am I?* (A Map)
*Once noble, now a shadow. Once vital, now rotted. Who am I?* (Sissun)

If the players answer Sissun's riddles, the dracolich is enraged, beating its wings and shrieking angrily. It knows that Amenopophis will punish it. It is bound from attacking but will let them past. It hopes, though, that the party will attack and kill it.

The ground is broken upward from underneath from when the ground fell.

#### The Door To The Crypt
The door to the Crypt is shut by a complex set of locks. Only a player proficient w/ thieves tools can open it (DC 20), or it can be battered/pried open with a DC20 STR check or 150hp of damage.
