# Summary

The party arrives in Silgorin, a city carved from bedrock on the River Styx and strung with bridges, and looks to hire a marraenoloth ferryman for the Dock of Fallen Cities. A local tiefling points them to Balthus the Quill, Voleth of the Fog and a third option. Among elves, devils and a bone devil they find a snack that contains a lemure and return it to the bartender. Voleth offers passage for two soul coins, or for a theft from the necromancer Obaris. Vals the Quill acts as agent at 15 percent, setting terms of safe passage and no outside deals, and the party agrees to steal Obaris's purse.
