# Summary

The session picks up exactly where 5 Oct stopped: the party and Bazett are tucked into a dead-end passage in the castle ruins, among three huge, gutted towers threaded with unexplained crystals, with the bridge half a mile off. Urush Gûl circles a hundred feet up. One side of his face is scarred and melted as if by acid, a wing is twisted and a limb half-melted. He rages that all they had to do was his bidding; by dealing with Silgorin Below instead they wrecked his plans, and they will pay with their lives.

He never breathes fire. He knows the boon he gave them makes them immune, so he fights with the ruins instead: hurling tower chunks that drop as columns of rubble (DC 24 Dexterity or Strength saves, 40 to 90-odd bludgeoning, and anyone who fails is buried, restrained and blinded), beating up cylinders of red-hot dust that block sight, and staying out of reach in the air. His Frightful Presence (DC 21) catches Mossy.

Drak goes invisible, sprints to a tower and shoots from its windows with Hunter's Mark, taking two tower-drops for 42 and 41. Cregyn raises Twilight Sanctuary (19 temporary hit points a round), Death Wards himself and later Sango, and turns invisible; his Guiding Bolts mostly miss all night. Sango, in Starry Form with Bless and Hex up, lands most of the party's radiant damage and is mauled twice for it. Mossy is frightened, then buried for much of the fight. Bazett first leaves ("above my pay grade"), then comes back invisible to dig Mossy out.

The worst moment is the top of a tower dropped on the whole group: 93 to Cregyn and Mossy, 46 to Sango, and everyone but Drak buried, Bazett included. Gûl comes down to gloat, then cocks his head and snarls "No, they're mine": Drak spots distant dots, an abishai patrol drawn by the noise, and the dragon turns frantic to finish the kill himself. He claws Sango out of the rubble and savages him; another column buries Cregyn for 90 and knocks Bazett unconscious. By then Gûl is bloodied.

Drak answers with Swift Quiver (four hits, 52), and Gûl lands on him. From there it is a duel: Drak drops to his daggers, taunts him ("a campfire with an ego"), and trades blows with Defensive Duelist, luck points and Cregyn's Silvery Barbs holding off the worst. Mossy digs Bazett out with her last Flash of Genius and stabilises him. Sango uses the sacrifice power of his ring for a mass heal of more than 350 hit points (Drak 119, Bazett 142, Mossy 74, Cregyn 45), which drops him to 1 hit point behind the Death Ward. Drak is buried once more, clears his eyes and Misty Steps out, then lands a double critical.

Mossy's sneak-attack crossbow bolt is the killing blow. She describes it catching his tail mid-swing so that it smashes the tower beside them, which buckles and crushes him; Urush Gûl dies trapped under rubble, the fire in his eyes going out. Drak ends on 60 hit points after taking 388.

There is no time to pull teeth for daggers: the patrol is a few rounds out. Bazett urges them into the tunnel he recommended, the route that runs under everything and comes up beside Arkhan's Tower. Sango casts Pass without Trace and they run. The DM calls the fight a major waypoint in the campaign and the party levels up.
