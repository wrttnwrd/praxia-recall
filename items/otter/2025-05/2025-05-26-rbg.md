# Summary

After resting in a secret room and setting distractions, the party confronts Kazani Noctis and opens with a performance. They negotiate a truce: Noctis, who appears as a doppelganger of Soroya, is granted a seat on the council of Salmuir on condition that she abides by its rulings and uses no arcane or divine means to sway votes, and in return she stops threatening Salmuir. Her sandwich-shop franchise idea is declined. The party returns to Salmuir to prepare a summit between Noctis and Soroya at their Bastion in Aventine.
