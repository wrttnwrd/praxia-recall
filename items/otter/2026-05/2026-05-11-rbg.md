# Summary

A one-shot with different characters: a warforged, a gnome, a halfling and a tiefling investigator are sent quietly into the Temple of Amoth after three murders in three nights, the latest in the bell tower. Blood on the Hand of Amoth, a sacred mace, and flecks of silver and gold in the wounds lead to the high hierophant Varon, who silenced his own followers to hear his dying god's last words. Varon kills investigators including Jaxxson and TP. Then a fiery object falls from the sky, the shock wave blinds and flattens everyone, Silgorin buckles and begins to descend, and the temple is left perched on the crater's rim.
