# Summary

Opening on the abishai that spotted them on 21 Sep: five abishai with a green leader. Mossy vortex-warps the green into a rock face, Drak's steel wind strike hits all five, Sango stuns one and follows with guiding bolt, and Cregan's twilight sanctuary and spirit guardians grind them down; the green dies and the whites retreat. A guide points out Arkhan's Tower on the hex map and two places to avoid — the Watchtower and an unexplained danger — and mentions someone called Bazett who knows the safest route. Bazett turns out to be an ultroloth, chained and caged over an immense lake of demon ichor and slightly unhinged from the long suspension; Mossy saws through his manacles with tinker's tools and he agrees to guide them in exchange for freedom, recommending the Darking Way, a tunnel running under the River Styx, over air or open road. Urush Gûl gives chase; the party runs for an immense ruined castle, half a mile wide, at the edge of the bridge, and spends the end of the night working out how to pin a flying ancient red dragon — dropping rubble or part of the bridge on it, with the 'hand that strikes' held back as a break-glass option because of its cost in innocent lives. The group pauses for four weeks while Ian is in Australia; next session 9 Nov.
