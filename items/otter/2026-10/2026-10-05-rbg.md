# Summary

Opening recap: after losing their guide Bitter to a bone storm, the party has been crossing Avernus on their own, hiding from Urush Gûl and watching Ardmon's Wandering Emporium travel the Styx shore, until one of the many hunting parties caught them in a clearing.

That party was four white abishai and a green leader. Mossy, hidden, used Magical Ambush to vortex-warp the green into a rock pillar at flying speed. Drak's first Steel Wind Strike hit all five (a crit on the green), and Defensive Duelist pushed his AC to 31. Sango stunned a white and broke its neck; Cregyn opened with Twilight Sanctuary, flew up with Steps of Night and caught the fliers in Spirit Guardians (snapping turtles); Mossy dagger-sneak-attacked a faerie-fired white to death. Drak killed the green and shot one fleeing white down; Sango's Starry Form arrow dropped the last. No survivors to report back. Mossy bottled a vial of demon ichor from the plants that burst over the field, to study with alchemy.

Cregyn then rolled 08 on Divine Intervention. His standoffish weather god parted the clouds: white beams over Tiamat's Monument, Arkhan's Tower and someone named Bazett; red beams over the Watchtower and an unidentified danger. The party travelled and rested in Drak's vault beetle. On Sango's watch Urush Gûl landed and snuffed right over the hatch, but Sango, wild-shaped into a rock-coloured giant flying squirrel spread across it, went unseen.

Bazett is an ultroloth caged 200 ft above a vast lake of demon ichor. The lake is Shumroth, a pit fiend Bel drowned in Styx water after Shumroth betrayed him; it has forgotten itself and is now a garbled telepathic voice craving revenge on Bel and mentioning a hammer. Mordenkainen imprisoned Bazett there about 252 years ago for trying to rob him. Cregyn flew Mossy out and she sawed the welded chain binding Bazett to the cage; his anti-teleport manacles stay on until Mossy can study them for a couple of hours. Insight: a manipulator by nature, but loopy after two and a half centuries and with no current scheme. He carries a magic staff and ring and can hover.

The deal: Bazett guides them to Arkhan's Tower (but will not enter it) in exchange for removing the manacles there and a split of treasure found on the way; it is not a binding contract. He says only reality-altering magic can strengthen the Chains of Gleynir; the party privately agrees not to let him learn about the Astrolabe of Three Wishes. He warns that the Watchtower holds two narzugon lookouts serving Tiamat. Of three routes (hijack Zariel's army airship; Dimension Door hops over two or three days, which leave a detectable magical trail; or the Darking Way, a neglected tunnel from beyond the bridge, under the Styx, to Arkhan's Tower, full of things surface fiends avoid), the party chose the Darking Way.

Pushing past hour 16 toward the tunnel, they reached a half-mile-wide ruined castle at the head of the bridge, and Urush Gûl found them. They sprinted into the ruins; his fire breath did nothing to the fire-immune party but took 46 off Bazett, and everyone hid in a dead-end passage while the dragon pounded the stones. Reasoning that the dragon is too proud, and too wary of Tiamat after being punished for helping them, to call reinforcements, they began planning. Mossy scouted: two quarter-mile towers are too big to topple, but rubble piles could be rigged to fall on a grounded dragon, and part of the bridge might drop if he flew beneath it. Drak offered to bait him, since Urush Gûl hates him most, and raised the Hand That Strikes, which halves or slays the target at the cost of equivalent lives on Praxia; it was agreed as a break-glass last resort. The party is level 17; the next session is 9 Nov.
