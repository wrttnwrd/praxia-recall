# Summary

The party plans to defend Urdun from the Blood Corsairs, using the airship Villain's Discontent to draw the fleet into a trap and Drak's plant growth to foul the bay. Mossy and Obadiah find their powder inert without saltpetre and set out to get it from bat guano. Rikterr and Sango weigh scouting the Corsairs' island and settle on the Albion's fast boats. The cutter runs into sahuagin underwater and later threads a fleet of warships to warn the village. In the guano cave Mossy and Rikterr fight a giant bat and a sonic creature, escaping with a web spell and Flash of Genius, and come out bleeding from the ears but with guano for gunpowder.
