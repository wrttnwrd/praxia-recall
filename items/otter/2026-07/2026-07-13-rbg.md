# Summary

The party studies the chains anchoring Silgorin over Avernus and meets a black abishai named Vaxith. They find a single point of failure, an iron bolt kept under constant repair by lemures and imps, which Mossy identifies as iron and therefore vulnerable to acid or explosives; spike growth and magic circle are considered, and a vault beetle proposed to loosen the ground around the anchor. They also discover a market dealing in souls holding a slip of paper like one seen before, possibly tied to Mossy's aunt Vanity, and plan to investigate while staying within the protection of the Badge of Zariel. They also discuss hiring a ferry and the small square coins used as currency.
