# Summary

The party leaves Bel's forge guided by the imp Bitterness ('Please call me bitter. Bitterness is my father'), crossing a sandy plain that recedes behind them unnaturally fast, toward the River Styx and Arkhan's Tower. Over the Styx they watch a soul shower — damned souls, humanoid and terrified through Mossy's goggles, falling into the river. An invisible orthon sniper shoots Drak and runs; the party chases it down with faerie fire, stunning strike and a volley of arrows that cracks it open. They shelter from a heat wave inside the vault beetle, Bitterness included. When a red dragon comes low over them they pop the eversmoking bottle as a decoy and recognize Urush Gûl, scarred and twisted, who torches the smoke and moves on. Crossing an ichor field of hovering, stalked bladders, they hide from a caravan of six-legged beasts pulling tents of wares: the sign reads Ardman Lacqui's Wandering Emporium, the rakshasa they fought on the Elemental Plane of Fire. A bone storm hits; Cregan drops a Leomund's tiny hut from a scroll, but Bitterness is killed outright — no death saves on Avernus for an imp. Picking their way through cracks in the ground, they are finally spotted: four abishai dive on them as the session ends.
