# Summary

Played partly from the deck of the Albion, the session opens with a naval battle in which the doctor Jedediah amputates a limb and the gunner Obadiah scores a cannon hit. The party plans research into the Astral Sea and Avernus and resolves to find Rugerim. Mossy is missing Kiki, and Drak backs Rikterr's plan to face Tiamat once Kiki is safe. In the orcish city of Urdun they meet Voda, hear of the Corsair raids, discuss trade with Aventine and an airship strike, and listen to Regorum, a 150-year-old paladin, describe Avernus. A fleet of small ships approaches and a huge explosion devastates ships and town, damaging the great frigate in the harbour.
