# Summary

Seeking the badge of Zariel, the party learns Surtur holds a grudge against Tiamat and hears what surviving Avernus demands. They buy potions, pay Ember Forge thirty gold each way for passage to Rathskald, and receive amulets of comfort from Sierra Crest against the heat. In Skullsguard, Mayor Ulos asks them to stop the sale of counterfeit fire-protection gear. The rakshasa Ardmon defends his business and shows off a machine, worked by a salamander, that multiplies items. The party resolves to shut it down.
