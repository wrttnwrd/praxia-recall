# Summary

Drak wakes badly wounded in a volcanic crater on the mainland, kept alive behind a force field by the red dragon Urush Gûl. The dragon explains that Tiamat has ordered him to weaken the party, not kill them, and demands that all of them come to him voluntarily to pay his price; he gives Drak a day, food and drink. Drak explores the field's odd properties, learns the Bastion's word of recall is beyond him, and after being worn down agrees to bring the party, submitting to scrying so the dragon can track him. He is flown out and set down about five miles east of the impact crater near the barrier islands.
