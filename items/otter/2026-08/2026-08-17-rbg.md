# Summary

Travelling the River Styx through Avernus, Sango receives a boon from the Raven Queen: the ability to summon four shadar-kai soulmongers once in a 28-day period. The party encounters abyssal chickens, fletches ichor-tipped arrows with an artificer's skill, and fights hydroloths trying to steal their memories while Drak shelters in a Tiny Hut; a potion of stone giant strength is used. They meet Grandma Valar, a green hag on a barge, and buy a potion of gaseous form and an elixir of health for 750 gold each, and deal with a crew of kenku mechanics.
