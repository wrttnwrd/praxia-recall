# Summary

The party prepares the summit, moving Soroya and her entourage to Aventine by teleportation circle and coordinating with Shadowmuir's representative Magog. Negotiations trade Salmuir's wish to monitor Kazani Noctis against Shadowmuir's desire for drug trade, settling on a sliding-scale tax and a possible exchange of spies. Sango and Draknev engage, Drak grapples the blade, Rikterr's Remove Curse drops the attacker, and Kiki is grappled and later revived with Spare the Dying. A breath weapon lands and a lingering curse remains.
