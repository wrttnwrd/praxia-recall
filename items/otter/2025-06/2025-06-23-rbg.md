# Summary

Two dragonborn, West Ellis and Kiki, are possessed by Tiamat. Eleanor Silverleaf at the Temple of Bahamut casts Greater Restoration on West Ellis. To restore Kiki's soul the party must win a contest against Tiamat's summoned creatures, choosing their own creatures round by round from a shrinking pool of hit points; a stone giant Dreamwalker faces a copper dragon and falls to its acid breath. Afterwards the party plans crafting: a ring of enlarge/reduce and a bowl of infinite soup, twelve days each.
