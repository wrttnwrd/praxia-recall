# Read notes — the Thargorax arc (30 sessions, 2023-02-20 → 2024-09-30)

## Thargorax (aka Tharogax — both spellings in source; ONE being)
- An aspect of **Tiamat**; one of the Later Gods (**Sequar Deones**); a lesser god who walked Praxia.
- Etymology: thargo (many) + rax (dragon heads) = "many-headed dragon".
- Led the mortal hosts against the Second Gods (**Biran Deones**) in the **First War**; was felled
  and cast through the **Stygian Gate**. Her five lieutenants fell with her and perished.
- Imprisoned on **Avernus** for two millennia. Pure hate. Wants to tear the Gate open, bring her
  hordes to Praxia, and use it as a base to attack the Biran Deones.
- Needs the **Pentar Virones** (= Notorious RBG) + the **Quinary** to crack the Seals and open
  the **Door That Opens**. Manipulating history so RBG believes the Pentar Virones once sided
  with her (a lie — they fought her with weapons from the Second Gods).
- Profiled in "On The Fall of Thargorax", a lecture by the scholar **Vogrel Stonebeard**.
- Still alive as of _Reread Every Time: "plotting a comeback, and she is very, very angry."

## Agrona Sef — her servant
NE Cleric/Wizard. "Sef" = servant, a servitor of the Abyss's royalty; Agrona is one of the Sef.
Cult Of Thargorax. Tasked with breaking the four Seals. Collects **soul coins** (Abyss/Nine Hells
currency) for personal wealth. Thargorax does not trust her.

## The four Seals (Founding Runes) and the Stygian Gate
Gate lies on the **Plateau/Field of Bone**; returned there when **Team Spear saved Pandora**.
Cracking a seal = apply a soul coin + a ritual only the Sef know.
- Divine / divinity — Plateau of Bone
- Nature / naturum — **Gloomspire** (on the **Whispering Abyss**, Underdark)
- Arcane / arcanum — **Porthmoor** (cracked first; broke the ley line, hid Selune's stars,
  disrupted elemental magic — airships tie to ley lines to move)
- Chaos / flux — **East Godelis**
The Quinary items gathered so far: **The Hand That Strikes** (made by Zerla Pelletweaver at
Nigh Darûl), **The Mouth That Calls** (from the jawbone of **Rave The Howler**), **The Light That
Sparks** (has a sacrifice power), **The Eye That Sees**, **The Door That Opens** (needs the Hag).
Temple of Helm expects the Quinary returned (Rikterr promised).
